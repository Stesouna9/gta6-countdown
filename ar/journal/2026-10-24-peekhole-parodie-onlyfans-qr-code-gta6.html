GTA 6: a "Peekhole" QR code spotted, ComicBook calls it an OnlyFans parody

A fan spotted a "Peekhole" QR code on a poster outside the Jack of Hearts club, and ComicBook reads it as an OnlyFans parody. Rockstar has not commented.

مصادر comicbook.com

J-26 يصدر GTA 6 بعد 26 يومًا →

One tiny detail in a trailer can start an argument. According to ComicBook, a fan noticed in GTA 6's extended look a QR code labelled "Peekhole" on the "Make It Rain Mondays" poster outside the Jack of Hearts strip club.

What was actually seen?

ComicBook says the name and context point to an OnlyFans parody, and the logo resembles the one the platform used from 2016 to 2021. The code can be scanned, but it currently leads to the game's official website. The find comes from a Reddit post and ComicBook is our only source, so treat it as a clue, not an announcement.

Set dressing or unlockable content?

Nothing is said about the code's purpose. The top Reddit comment, quoted by ComicBook, imagines scannable in-game QR codes tied to Strangers and Freaks-style missions. That is speculation. The other explanation is one more satirical prop: the series has always parodied brands, from Cluckin' Bell to iFruit, and this is the first GTA since such platforms took off.

As for the label "most controversial parody in Rockstar history", that is ComicBook's own. Hot Coffee and the early 2000s panic around GTA III make the comparison debatable.
