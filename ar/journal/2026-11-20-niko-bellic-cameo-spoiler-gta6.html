GTA 6 spoiler: Niko Bellic from GTA IV cameos late in the story

Kotaku says Niko Bellic appears in two late-story cutscenes of GTA 6, voiced by Michael Hollick. Spoiler: skip this if you have not finished the game.

مصادر kotaku.com

Spoiler warning: this article reveals a late-story moment. Kotaku, which says it has seen the scenes itself, reports that GTA IV protagonist Niko Bellic returns as a supporting character in GTA 6. The cameo has been spreading across social media and YouTube for hours, and some players doubted it was real.

Where does Niko show up?

According to Kotaku, Jason and Lucia are then working for Giorgio, a wealthy shipping magnate tied to cartels and Eastern European criminals. After the Coast Guard seizes a cocaine shipment, he plans to rob it back. As the heist comes together, a figure from a Balkan criminal family arrives in Vice City to deal with Giorgio. It is an older, grey-haired Niko, seen in two cutscenes: one to threaten Giorgio, one to collect the drugs once the heist works.

Who voices him?

Kotaku writes that Rockstar brought back the original actor, Michael Hollick, and links this to the credits Rockstar published, where the character is simply listed as "The Hitman". We covered those credits yesterday. Kotaku also promises an interview with Rockstar head of production Rob Nelson on how the cameo came about. We have no comment from Rockstar itself yet.

Why it matters

Niko has been silent since the end of GTA IV in 2008. His return shows Rockstar is tying its new Vice City to older games without selling the game on it. For players, it is mostly a reason to finish the story before someone spoils it. More in the journal.
