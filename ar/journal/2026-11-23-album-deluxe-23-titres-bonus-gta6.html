GTA 6 album gets a Deluxe Edition with 23 bonus tracks

Rockstar announces on X the Deluxe Edition of GTA VI: The Album, with 23 bonus tracks from The Dare, Slayyyter, Denzel Curry, HEALTH and more, streaming now.

مصادر x.com

Rockstar has released the Deluxe Edition of GTA VI: The Album, announced on X and streaming now. It adds 23 bonus tracks from across the game, with contributions from The Dare, Slayyyter, Denzel Curry, HEALTH, Power Trip, Real Dimez x Sexyy Red "and many more", the studio writes. The post passed 139,000 views within about ten minutes.

What is in the GTA 6 album Deluxe Edition?

Rockstar gives no full tracklist in its post, only a handful of names and a listening link. According to Variety, whose headline also names Travis Scott and Offset, the 23 songs are originals. That wider list does not come from Rockstar and we have not checked it track by track. A community account, gtasix_, says Travis Scott's "Burn This Shit Down" is among the additions. Nothing official yet.

The arithmetic from the standard release: the album out on 19 November has 34 tracks, so 23 more are added. Rockstar has not said whether the Deluxe Edition bundles all 57 tracks together, or whether a physical release is planned.

An album already breaking records

The news follows a very strong start. According to the GTAVI_Countdown account, the album had the biggest streaming debut for a soundtrack in Spotify history, and Flower In A Hurricane hit number one on the global chart. Those figures come from that account, with no Spotify confirmation in our sources. They follow the Justice song's rise in the European charts, which we covered this morning.

Why it matters

For players, it means more material: songs heard in the game, or not yet spotted, now available outside the console. For Rockstar, music becomes a second commercial front four days after launch. Still open: whether more waves follow, and whether the in-game radio, already on Spotify, changes with these additions. We will update this piece once the official tracklist is out.
