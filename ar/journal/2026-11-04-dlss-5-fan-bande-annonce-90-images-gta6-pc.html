GTA 6 DLSS 5 test: a fan runs the trailer at 90 fps, unofficially

A Reddit user applied DLSS 5 to the GTA 6 Extended Look trailer, but the test is unofficial and says nothing about the PC version, which still has no date.

مصادر hardwarecooking.fr

J-15 يصدر GTA 6 بعد 15 يومًا →

No PC version announced, so fans tinker. According to HardwareCooking, citing Wccftech, Reddit user RevolutionaryBus8208 applied DLSS 5 and 3x frame generation to the GTA VI Extended Look trailer on r/GTA6unmoderated. The video goes from 30 to 90 frames per second, in real time according to the author.

How was the test done?

The setup is described as improvised: a PS5, a 4K HDR capture card or Remote Play, the Veyra 2.1.1 tool (neural rendering, upscaling, frame generation) on a GeForce RTX 4080, MFG Unlock to enable Multi Frame Generation, and a 4K 144 Hz TV. It is strictly unofficial: neither NVIDIA nor Rockstar is involved.

How does it look?

Impressions are mixed. Environments look impressive, faces a bit odd, which HardwareCooking blames on the missing data DLSS 5 normally relies on (geometry, lighting, textures), absent from flat video. At 90 fps, visible ghosting appears, expected from a 30 fps source. Playing through a capture card and external processing also adds latency.

It is not a preview of the PC version, which still has no date, as we summed up in our GTA 6 PC piece. Consoles come first on November 19, with a 212 GB preload from November 10.

Why it matters

Nothing for players: no DLSS 5 integration is announced for GTA 6. The demo shows the technology needs game data to deliver its best results. A real demonstration is needed before judging. The release page lists what is confirmed.
