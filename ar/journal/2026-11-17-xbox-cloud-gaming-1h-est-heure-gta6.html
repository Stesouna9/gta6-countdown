GTA 6 Xbox Cloud Gaming start time: 1 a.m. ET on November 19

GTA 6 starts streaming on Xbox Cloud Gaming at 1 a.m. Eastern on November 19, 7 a.m. in Paris, according to Yahoo Tech.

GTA 6 will stream on Xbox Cloud Gaming from 1 a.m. Eastern on November 19, which is 7 a.m. in Paris, reports Yahoo Tech. The cloud start is one single moment, while console copies unlock at local midnight in each time zone.

What time does GTA 6 start on Xbox Cloud Gaming?

At 1 a.m. Eastern on November 19. Xbox announced the cloud launch on November 10, as covered in our Xbox One cloud article. Yahoo Tech spells out the US effect: Mountain and Pacific players get the cloud before their consoles unlock, Central players start together, and East Coast players wait an extra hour.

What do you need to play?

The game is bought separately: $79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate, per Yahoo Tech. Nothing runs locally on Xbox One; it streams from Microsoft's servers with the Xbox Series S graphics preset. Cloud play has monthly allowances: 15 hours with Game Pass Ultimate, 10 with Premium, 5 with Essential, plus extra hours bought without a subscription. Xbox has not said how GTA 6 sessions count against those limits.

What is still unknown?

Rockstar has not confirmed console unlock times for France. Our country-by-country look is in the release time article. Also unknown: how allowances limit cloud play time.
