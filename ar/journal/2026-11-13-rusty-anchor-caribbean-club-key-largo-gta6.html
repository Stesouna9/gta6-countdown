GTA 6: Key Largo bar turned into the Rusty Anchor, two accounts say

GTAlphaintel and GTAVI_Countdown say the Caribbean Club in Key Largo, Florida, is dressed as GTA 6's Rusty Anchor; Rockstar has not confirmed it.

A Florida bar dressed as a game location: according to two tracking accounts, GTAlphaintel and GTAVI_Countdown, the Caribbean Club in Key Largo has been turned into the Rusty Anchor, the bar that appears in GTA 6. Rockstar has not confirmed it and no outlet had picked it up at the time of writing.

What do the photos show?

GTAlphaintel says Rockstar transformed the Caribbean Club into the Rusty Anchor. GTAVI_Countdown, whose post has passed 40,000 views, talks about "10 new screenshots" of the real venue filled with references to the game. The first sighting is credited to a Reddit user, u/Snak3D0ct0r. We could not verify the images ourselves.

What about Hurricane Roxy?

The same post quotes a line attributed to local press: "As local companies prepare for Hurricane Roxy, staff at the Rusty Anchor insist business will continue." No source is given. It could just as well be part of the marketing.

Why is the venue plausible?

Rockstar has already staged real-world stunts around the launch: see the paper Leonida map at Selfridges or the Spice City sandwich. A real bar in the Keys, which inspire the game's Leonida, would fit. A fan project or a simple commercial decoration is still possible.

Why it matters

Nothing yet: it is one more lead six days before launch, relayed by two accounts using the same source. It becomes a fact only if Rockstar, the bar or local media confirm it. We track it on the Buzz page.
