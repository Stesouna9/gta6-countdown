GTA 6: IGN identifies Kodak Black as an NPC in the new screenshots

IGN identifies Kodak Black in Rockstar’s new GTA 6 screenshots, but the scale of his role and other possible musician appearances remain unknown.

Kodak Black appears to have a character in GTA 6, according to IGN, which identifies the rapper in Rockstar's new screenshot collection. That reporting takes the story beyond a resemblance discussed by fan accounts. The source text available to us does not include a separate statement from Rockstar announcing his involvement, so the identification remains explicitly attributed to IGN.

What has IGN identified in the new screenshots?

Reporter Cade Onder describes Kodak Black as a non-player character. According to IGN, the figure holds a cup displaying the Jack of Hearts logo, placing the image in the orbit of one of GTA 6's clubs. That is a specific visual observation. It does not establish a character name, a speaking role, a mission or whether players can interact with him.

The identification had previously circulated through GTAVI Countdown on X, and was labelled a rumour in our Buzz coverage. IGN's report is the new development here. It gives the story a press source, while leaving a clear boundary around what the available material actually describes. A portrait is not a demonstration of a gameplay encounter.

Could more musicians appear at Jack of Hearts?

According to IGN, Travis Scott mentions Jack of Hearts in RHYNO, a song made for the game's soundtrack. The outlet connects that reference with the character it identifies as Kodak Black, then considers whether other performers might appear in the game. That wider possibility is speculation in the source itself. It supplies neither a confirmed guest list nor a schedule of performances inside the club.

IGN points to earlier Rockstar projects for context. Dr. Dre became a character in GTA Online, while real DJs including Solomun, Tale of Us and Dixon appeared in its nightclubs. Those examples explain why the idea attracts attention. They do not demonstrate that GTA 6 will use the same mission structure, club interactions or live-performance format. Each of those details would require its own evidence.

Why it matters

The launch screenshot gallery now offers a casting story alongside its views of Vice City. For readers following the game's music, it raises a useful distinction between artists heard on a soundtrack and artists represented on screen. IGN makes a specific identification in Kodak Black's case. How substantial that appearance might be, and whether more musicians will join him as characters, remain open questions.
