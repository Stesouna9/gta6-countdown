GTA 6: Cyberleek says it is back with a "RETURN OF CL" statement

Cyberleek posted a "RETURN OF CL" statement dated 21 October promising new GTA 6 reveals, a week after announcing the end of its leaks. No new authenticated footage so far.

It said it was done. Now it says it is back. According to GinjFo, Cyberleek has put a statement titled "RETURN OF CL", dated 21 October, on the site tied to its earlier releases, promising an especially aggressive comeback with new GTA 6 reveals.

What does the new Cyberleek statement say?

The text says the next releases will be detailed. About their nature, nothing: GinjFo writes that the content is unknown. The site also still shows the older posts, including those from 14 October.

That matters because on 14 October Cyberleek said it was giving up on releasing a so-called full build of the game. GinjFo says its financial goal, a 30 million dollar market cap for its cryptocurrency, was not reached, and that it also dropped the plan to reveal the story's ending. We covered the threats, videos and apologies of 19 October: this saga has had more U-turns than a getaway scene.

Is there a verified new leak?

No. GinjFo says it found no gameplay footage that can be independently authenticated since the statement. A promise is not a leak, and nothing shows that Cyberleek holds anything beyond what it has already shown.

The outlet notes that some earlier posts were examined by specialist media, which judged them probably authentic. Hence the caution: a return could come with serious disclosures, or be one more publicity stunt a month before launch.

Beware of fake "Cyberleek" games

GinjFo flags another risk: several sites now use the Cyberleek name to offer supposedly playable GTA 6 builds for payment. It urges extreme caution, and so do we. The game launches on 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, through official channels only. Our release page keeps the schedule up to date.

Why it matters

In practice, nothing changes until something is published. But the calendar favors Cyberleek: the closer launch gets, the louder a spoiler or clip becomes. Our line stays the same: we do not publish or relay leaked content, and we only cite what serious outlets have been able to cross-check.
