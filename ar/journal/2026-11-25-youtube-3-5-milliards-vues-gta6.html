GTA 6: 3.5 billion YouTube views on release day, per YouTube

YouTube says videos with GTA 6 in the title drew over 3.5 billion views on November 19, according to Mashable.

مصادر mashable.com

On November 19, YouTube recorded over 3.5 billion views of videos with "GTA 6" in the title. The figure comes from a blog post the platform published Tuesday, reported by Mashable.

How many YouTube views did GTA 6 generate?

Besides the 3.5 billion on release day, YouTube says the Grand Theft Auto series totals over 900 billion views of related content, and videos specifically about GTA 6 had reached 40 billion views before launch. The platform also added themed touches to YouTube Live, with on-screen Wanted stars, and a Shorts effect putting creators in front of Vice City backdrops.

What about Twitch and Kick?

Mashable relies on Streams Charts: 2.50 million viewers on Twitch, 1.31 million on Kick and 3.22 million on YouTube per that tool, for 7.91 million concurrent launch-day viewers. Streams Charts counts fewer YouTube views than the platform's official figure, so the methods differ.

How does it compare with other records?

The 24-hour record for one YouTube clip remains BTS's: 108.2 million for "Butter" in 2021. GTA 6 keeps the best 24-hour debut for a non-music video: 93 million views for the first trailer in December 2023. These figures add to the 39 million PS5 players reported by Sony and the official sales already published.
