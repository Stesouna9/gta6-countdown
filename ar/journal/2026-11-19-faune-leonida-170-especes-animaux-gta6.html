GTA 6: over 170 animal species in Leonida, from pettable pets to Jason's snake

Leonida has over 170 species versus 28 in GTA V, per Mashable: you can pet cats and dogs, and Jason owns a snake named Dolores.

الصورة: Rockstar Games

مصادر mashable.com

Leonida features more than 170 animal species, against about 28 in GTA V, according to Game Informer cited by Mashable, which publishes its wildlife guide on launch day. Players are already sharing their encounters.

Which animals can you meet in Leonida?

Florida style: alligators, manatees, flamingos, but also green iguanas, cane toads and even rhesus macaques. On Mount Kalaga, a Rockstar screenshot shows a cougar stalking deer. Players have also reported black bears, bobcats, boars, otters, foxes, turtles, snakes and several dog breeds. In the water: pelicans, herons, dolphins, manta rays, hammerhead sharks, whales, and glowing marine life off the Leonida Keys.

Sources disagree on some species: GTABase lists possums and rats while IGN left them out, notes Mashable.

Can you pet the animals?

Yes, cats and dogs. Petting a cat gives Jason a small focus boost. If a dog's owner allows it, you can give it a treat, sold at some gas stations according to The Guardian, and praise or scold the animal.

Unexpected moments

In a clip Mashable spotted, a cat lunges at Jason during a car theft: a timed prompt lets him fend it off, then release it or keep driving. One player found a dog sitting on a jet ski offshore. Jason also owns a pet, Dolores, a snake at his Leonida Keys home, fed with a mouse for a small focus boost.

Why it matters

Wildlife is more than decoration: bonuses, interactive scenes and collectibles to log. See also our piece on the Leonida map and the journal.
