GTA 6: six new screenshots leak one day before launch

Six unseen GTA 6 screenshots have circulated since Tuesday night, reportedly from a non-public game-testing site; neither Rockstar nor any outlet has confirmed their origin.

Six GTA 6 screenshots have been circulating since Tuesday night, less than 30 hours before release. GTAVI_Countdown shared them with a "JUST IN" label, crediting @grapebrainGTA; the post had already passed 95,000 views. Insider Gaming headlines new images showing "new weapons and scenes". I could not read its full piece, so treat that detail with caution.

Where do these GTA 6 screenshots come from?

According to GTA6 Hub, citing @GameRoll_, the six images came from the non-public section of a video game testing site. The account that posted them deleted them, and GTA6 Hub removed its copies too. The outlet's name is unknown. Nothing proves yet that they come from the final build.

Has Rockstar responded?

No. There is no Rockstar message on this at the time of writing, and the Newswire is silent. Images pulled from their original source often point to an embargo lifted too early.

Should you look before Thursday?

Your call, but they are unofficial, and official footage is due with launch. See our release page for the calendar.
