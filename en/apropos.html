About

Who makes this site, and how we check every piece of information.

01

Who

A fan site made in Nantes, France, by OKALAM Studio, an independent studio that also makes Vice Bay Radio and the game Vice Break. We are not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

02

Our method

  1. A fact only goes into our pages if it comes from Rockstar, Take-Two, an official store or a media outlet citing Rockstar.
  2. Every fact sheet shows the date it was checked.
  3. A rumor is always labeled as a rumor.
  4. Press news is collected automatically in real time, every minute and links to the original article.
  5. Found a mistake? Report it on Discord and we fix it within 24 hours.
03

What we will never do

  • Present a leak as a fact.
  • Publish fake reviews, fake giveaways or fake keys.
  • Hide that a link is an affiliate link.

How the site pays for itself
