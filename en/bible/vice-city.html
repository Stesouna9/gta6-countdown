GTA 6 Bible · Regions of Leonida

Vice City

Vice City is the big city of Leonida and the heart of GTA 6: beaches, neon, skyscrapers, clubs and yachts. It is the return of the city from GTA Vice City (2002), fully rebuilt for the present day.

Confirmed

What is confirmed

  • Capital of the fictional state of Leonida, described by Rockstar as neon-soaked streets
  • Already the setting of GTA Vice City (2002) and Vice City Stories (2006), in the 1980s
  • Home turf of Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest and the Real Dimez
  • Seen in Trailers 1 and 2 and in the Extended Look: highways, skyline, beach, clubs, skyscrapers
  • The Vintage Vice City Pack (pre-order bonus) pays tribute to Tommy Vercetti's era

What is Vice City in GTA 6?

The official site presents it as the heart of Leonida, the sunshine state. Official images show a bay and skyline from the air, a packed highway, beaches, purple nightclubs and basketball players in Manatees jerseys. When the sun fades and the neon glows, says Rockstar, everyone has something to gain and more to lose.

What the videos show

Trailer 2 (May 2025) opens on Vice City and its beaches. The Extended Look from August 2026 shows a cover shootout in a city skyscraper, a six-star wanted level and explorable interiors. According to Bloomberg, Rockstar artists have been working on the map since 2015, with scouting trips to Miami.

The inspiration

Vice City points to Miami, as it did in 2002: the press recalls the influence of the Miami Vice series on the city's look. Rockstar does not name Miami.

What we don't know

The list of districts, the size of the map and the exact mission locations have not been announced. Names circulating come from leaks and do not appear here.
