Cal Hampton

Cal Hampton is Jason's friend and an associate of Brian Heder. A laid-back paranoid, he prefers staying home, snooping on Coast Guard comms with a few beers within reach.

Confirmed

What is confirmed

  • Friend of Jason Duval and associate of Brian Heder in the Keys
  • Feels safest at home, listening to Coast Guard frequencies
  • Conspiracy fan: what if everything on the internet was true?
  • Rockstar describes him as happy at the low tide of America

Who is Cal Hampton?

In the official gallery, Cal is the supporting character closest to Jason. He lives in the Keys, also works for Brian, and spends his days monitoring Coast Guard comms with a few private browser tabs open. His paranoia is described as casual: he loves sharing it, but his friend Jason has bigger plans.

What is his role in the story?

Rockstar has not detailed his missions. His profile suggests an informant and a listening ear for the Keys' smuggling: who is at sea, who is being watched. Everything else is unknown.

What we don't know

No official information on his past, his voice actor or his weight in the campaign. We will update this entry as soon as Rockstar or the press says more.
