Jason Duval

Jason Duval is one of the two playable protagonists of GTA 6. A former soldier, he works for drug runners in the Leonida Keys when he meets Lucia Caminos.

Confirmed

What is confirmed

  • Playable character, alongside Lucia Caminos (Rockstar, Trailer 2, May 6, 2025)
  • Grew up around grifters and crooks, then joined the Army to shake off his troubled teens
  • Lives in the Keys and works for local drug runners
  • Lives rent-free at one of Brian Heder's properties, in exchange for help with local shakedowns
  • Friend of Cal Hampton, also one of Brian's associates

Who is Jason Duval?

Rockstar presents Jason as a man who wants an easy life while things keep getting harder. Born among grifters, he tried the Army to straighten out, then came back to the Keys to do what he knows best: muscle work for drug runners. His official tagline: it might be time to try something new.

What is his relationship with Lucia?

According to Rockstar, meeting Lucia could be the best or the worst thing to ever happen to him. The couple is at the heart of the story: an easy score goes wrong and drags them into a conspiracy across the state of Leonida. Both are playable and, judging by the Extended Look released in August 2026, switching between them happens mid-action.

What the press reports

Jason Schreier (Bloomberg, October 2026), hosted at Rockstar North, describes a story closer in tone to Red Dead Redemption 2 than to GTA V, focused on the duo, and a sweat system that marks clothes depending on effort. These details come from a journalist, not an official announcement.

What we don't know

Rockstar has not named the actor who voices and portrays Jason. The names circulating come from fan lists: we do not publish them.
