Lucia Caminos

Lucia Caminos is the first playable female protagonist of a modern-era GTA. Fresh out of the Leonida Penitentiary, she wants the good life her mother dreams of, and Jason could be her way out.

Confirmed

What is confirmed

  • Playable character, revealed in Trailer 1 (December 5, 2023)
  • Her father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk
  • Sent to the Leonida Penitentiary for fighting for her family; sheer luck got her out
  • She and her mother lived in Liberty City before Leonida
  • She intends to take matters into her own hands instead of dreaming

Who is Lucia Caminos?

Rockstar presents her as a woman who has always had to fight. Loyalty to her family sent her to prison; she leaves it determined to make only smart moves from here. Trailer 1 opens on her interview in custody, and official images later show her wearing an ankle monitor.

What does she want?

More than anything, the good life her mother has dreamed of since their days in Liberty City. But instead of dreaming, Lucia plans to act. In the official description, only one thing matters: who you know and what you've got. A life with Jason could be her way out.

Her role in the game

Lucia is one of the two playable characters of GTA 6. It is the first time since the original GTA and its anonymous character roster that a woman holds the lead role in the series. In the Extended Look from August 2026, she takes part in shootouts and heists just like Jason.

What we don't know

Rockstar has not announced who plays her. Actress Manni Perez is often cited by players, without official confirmation: we present this as a rumor, not a fact.
