Raul Bautista

Raul Bautista is a seasoned, charming and confident bank robber, always hunting for recruits ready to take the risks that bring the biggest rewards. His recklessness raises the stakes with every score.

Confirmed

What is confirmed

  • Experienced bank robber, according to the official description
  • Recruits talent ready to take big risks for big rewards
  • His recklessness raises the stakes with every score: his crew will have to double down or walk away

Who is Raul Bautista?

Confidence, charm and cunning: that is how Rockstar sums him up. Raul is the professional robber of the cast. He is always looking for new partners, and Jason and Lucia, a couple in need of money, tick every box. His official line: life is full of surprises, we'd all be wise to remember that.

Heists in GTA 6

Official images show Jason and Lucia masked inside a vault, cash in the air. According to the demo given to Bloomberg at Rockstar North (October 2026), the game leans on improvised crime: walk into a bank to count the guards, give up, fall back on a gas station. Raul looks like the natural link to the big organized scores, but Rockstar has not said so explicitly.

What we don't know

His actor, the number of heists and their structure have not been announced.
