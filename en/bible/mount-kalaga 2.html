Mount Kalaga

Mount Kalaga is the great natural park in the north of Leonida: forests, rivers, hunting and wide open spaces. One of the six official regions presented by Rockstar.

Confirmed

What is confirmed

  • Official region of Leonida, featured on Rockstar's site
  • Official image: river, dense forest and hills
  • Seen in Trailer 2 (May 2025)

What is Mount Kalaga?

Leonida's national park, in the north of the map. Official visuals show a forest-lined river and hills: the wild side of the state, the opposite of Vice City.

What we don't know

Hunting, fishing, animals and activities have not been detailed by Rockstar. We will update as soon as an official announcement arrives.
