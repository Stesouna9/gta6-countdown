Getting started in GTA 6

Before launching GTA 6 on November 19: what Rockstar has shown of the game (two playable leads, interiors, weapon wheel, radio on foot, six-star wanted level) and what the press learned at Rockstar North. A starter guide with no leaks.

Reported

What is confirmed

  • Two playable characters, Jason and Lucia, with mid-action switching (Extended Look, August 2026)
  • Six-star wanted level and cover shootouts inside buildings
  • Eight radio stations, also playable on foot through the phone's earphones
  • Preload from November 12, 2026, release on the 19th on PS5 and Xbox Series
  • Extended Look footage was captured on PS5

What does the Extended Look show?

The 26-minute video released in late August 2026, first on Netflix then on YouTube, is the only official gameplay footage. It shows a cover shootout in a Vice City skyscraper, an instant switch from Jason to Lucia, a six-star wanted level, conversations, dialogue choices and backyard wrestling as a side activity. Everything was captured on a standard PS5.

Interiors

According to Bloomberg and JeuxVideo.com (October 2026), Rob Nelson of Rockstar North speaks of hundreds of small interiors accessible from the street: banks, convenience stores, back rooms, a gym. Not every building. Scouting becomes a gameplay move: walk in, count the guards, walk away or act.

The weapon wheel

Jason Schreier reports that Rockstar spent years testing other menus, inspired by Red Dead Redemption 2 and its limited weapon count, before returning to the classic wheel. Expect the usual interface, with fewer weapons carried.

The radio

Six stations were confirmed by Rockstar on October 8, 2026 (Flash FM, Cocoteo, Back Country, Afro Bank FM, The Chamber, Dirty South Classics) and two more spotted by the press (Vice City FM, V-Rock FM). Radio and podcasts follow you on foot through earphones linked to Jason and Lucia's phone.

Before launch day

Preload from November 12. The official site lists the game on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; no PC. Pick your edition on our Editions and prices entry. Everything we don't know (exact time, download size) is stated as unknown, and updated as soon as it is announced.
