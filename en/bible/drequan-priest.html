Dre'Quan Priest

Dre'Quan Priest is a young, ambitious Vice City producer and former dealer who co-runs the Only Raw Records label with Boobie Ike and has just signed the Real Dimez duo.

Confirmed

What is confirmed

  • More of a hustler than a gangster, according to Rockstar: music was always the goal
  • Dealt on the streets to make ends meet
  • Co-runs Only Raw Records with Boobie Ike
  • Signed the Real Dimez and now targets the whole Vice City scene

Who is Dre'Quan Priest?

In the official description, Dre'Quan was never a gangster by vocation: even while dealing, breaking into music remained the goal. He books acts into Boobie's club, but those days seem numbered now that he has signed the Real Dimez.

His philosophy

His official line: dancers are his A&Rs. If a record works in the club, DJs will spin it. That is the whole logic of Only Raw Records, between the night scene and social media.

What we don't know

His exact link to Jason and Lucia, his missions and his actor have not been announced.
