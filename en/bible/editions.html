GTA 6 editions and prices

GTA 6 comes in two editions: Standard at $79.99 / €79.99 (boxed or digital) and Ultimate at $99.99 / €99.99 (digital only). Same story in both; the Ultimate adds vehicles, weapons and shops unlocked through the campaign.

Confirmed

What is confirmed

  • Release on November 19, 2026 on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and S; no PC version announced
  • Standard: $79.99 / €79.99, the full game, boxed or digital
  • Ultimate: $99.99 / €99.99, digital only, same story plus bonuses
  • Ultimate bonuses: Grotti Cheetah, Shitzu Squalo, 1967 Vapid Dominator Buggy, Dinka Enduro, Crest Kayak; Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers; Sara's Unisex Salon, Electric Fang, One-Eyed Willie's, Goodtime Gear, Stock 305; Wyman's Classic Car Collection
  • Pre-order (open since June 25, 2026): Vintage Vice City Pack for everyone, and one month of GTA+ for digital orders
  • The physical box contains a download code, no disc
  • Preload from November 12, 2026; PEGI 18 / ESRB M

Standard or Ultimate?

The Standard is enough to play the whole story: both editions share the same campaign. The Ultimate, sold digitally only, spreads its bonuses across the chapters of Jason and Lucia's story: cars and a boat, two revolvers, exclusive shops and a collection of classic cars to restore. Rockstar lets you upgrade from Standard to Ultimate later for the difference. PCMag (October 2026) advises starting with the Standard.

Pre-order bonuses

Every pre-order, Standard included, gets the Vintage Vice City Pack: a 1955 Vapid Stanier, a garage in Ocean Beach, 1980s outfits and weapon patterns inspired by Tommy Vercetti. The pack remains free for anyone buying before November 20. Digital pre-orders add one month of GTA+.

Box, preload, PC

No box contains a disc: only a code. Preload opens on November 12, 2026. Rockstar has announced neither a PC version nor a date for one; the dates circulating are rumors, tracked in our Verdict of the 19th.

What we don't know

The download size and the exact unlock time have not been announced.
