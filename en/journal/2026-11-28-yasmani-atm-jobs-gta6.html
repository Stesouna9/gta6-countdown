GTA 6: Yasmani's ATM Jobs, how to rip out the ATMs with a winch

Yasmani's first mission: rip out three ATMs with a winch vehicle and take the marked bills, for about $30,000 to $40,000.

Sources powerpyx.com · gtabase.com

Direct answer: Yasmani's ATM Jobs unlock after Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs and the Chapter 3 mission Credit Check, when Yasmani gets in touch. You need a vehicle with a winch: a stolen tow truck, or a truck whose rear bumper you customize.

Steps

  1. Wait for Yasmani's call, then go to the three marked ATMs on the map.
  2. Back the vehicle into the ATM to hook the winch, then drive forward to pull it out of the wall.
  3. Drag the ATM until it opens. Each ATM has four cash trays marked with $ on the minimap.
  4. Pick up the marked bills and take them to Yasmani, who pays the most.

Tips

  • A sticky bomb placed on or near the ATM opens it faster.
  • Missable: the ATMs are only full for one in-game day. Yasmani keeps offering tips afterwards.

Sources: PowerPyx and GTABase agree on the payout (about $30,000 to $40,000), the three ATMs and the winch method.
