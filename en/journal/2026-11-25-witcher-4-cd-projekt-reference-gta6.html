GTA 6 sets a benchmark for The Witcher 4, says CD Projekt chief

Pause Hardware reports that Michał Nowakowski expects broad improvements for The Witcher 4, using the leap between GTA V and GTA VI as a comparison.

Sources pausehardware.com

GTA 6 has become a reference point in CD Projekt’s investor discussions. According to Pause Hardware, joint chief executive Michał Nowakowski believes The Witcher 4 can deliver a leap over The Witcher 3 comparable to the change between Rockstar’s two latest mainline GTA games. This is a statement about the Polish studio’s ambitions, with no partnership between the companies announced in the account.

How did GTA 6 enter the discussion?

Pause Hardware reports that Erste analyst Krzysztof Tkocz raised the comparison during CD Projekt’s third-quarter results presentation. His question asked whether the next Witcher could achieve a similar step forward. Nowakowski answered positively and pointed to the time since the previous game. That exchange supplies the context for the headline: it was an answer about development ambition, without a published technical ranking of the two franchises.

The report describes a goal encompassing both better visuals and improvements across almost every part of the game. That breadth matters. A sharper screenshot can establish a visual difference; a claim covering an entire adventure invites players to consider what changes while they actually play. The account does not identify the individual mechanics that would demonstrate such a change.

What has CD Projekt actually committed to?

According to Pause Hardware, Nowakowski said the team had examined GTA 6 and the differences from its predecessor. He also said Rockstar’s release had not changed CD Projekt’s approach to development. Using a competitor as an example in an investor answer does not, by itself, establish that a production has been redirected around that competitor. The reported comments support a comparison of ambition, without announcing that kind of change in strategy.

Why it matters

For GTA players, the news is mainly about the place Rockstar’s release now occupies in industry conversations. For The Witcher 4, the comparison creates an expectation extending beyond presentation. The useful next step will be concrete examples from CD Projekt showing what that ambition means in practice. Until those arrive, the remarks provide a development goal to assess later, without adding a new GTA feature, a Rockstar announcement or evidence that the two games are being designed to work alike.
