GTA 6 Lombank robbery: IGN breaks down Sway’s $160,000 escape

IGN describes Sway taking $160,000 from Lombank and leaving without a prolonged chase. The sequence is an example, rather than a guaranteed payout.

Sources ign.com · fr.ign.com

A well-prepared exit is the striking part of a GTA 6 bank robbery described by IGN. The outlet follows streamer Sway through a Lombank run in Vice City that ends with $160,000 collected and no extended escape chase. This is a reported example of one attempt, rather than a fixed earnings promise. The mechanics discussed include chapter-four progression, but not the ending of the story.

How does the run reach the vault?

According to IGN, Sway follows a security van to the bank. The outlet says those vehicles can lead players to an open vault and describes information available from Yasmani for a $10,000 in-game payment. It places that option in chapter four, separately from the earlier ability to make use of marked banknotes.

The reported route goes through the rear of the building. Sway disables a camera with a suppressed pistol, opens a garage entrance and knocks out guards before reaching the cash. These details describe the approach visible in this particular attempt. They do not establish a required route that every player must use at every bank.

What happens once the cash is collected?

IGN describes Lucia filling a bag before Sway switches control to Jason to increase the total to $160,000. The account says collecting money triggers the alarm. That distinction matters: a tidy escape is not the same as a robbery that never alerts the security system in the first place.

Sway then leaves through the initial access point, removes the characters’ masks and uses a personal vehicle positioned nearby to change clothes. In IGN’s account, the pair can walk away despite police arriving at the bank. The arrangement outside the building is therefore part of the reported success, rather than an incidental detail after the money has been secured.

Why it matters

The useful lesson is the sequence of decisions: entry, collection, character switching and an exit that accounts for appearance. The $160,000 figure belongs to the result IGN describes. It should not be presented as a standard reward for every bank or a promise of repeatable income at a particular rate.

Our GTA 6 news journal separates examples of player discoveries from universal gameplay claims. This run is an illustration of why planning beyond the vault can matter. It does not prove that police behaviour is identical across attempts, that a route will always remain open or that changing clothes guarantees a peaceful exit. Those outcomes would require broader evidence than one successful sequence. The achievement reported here remains Sway’s specific run.
