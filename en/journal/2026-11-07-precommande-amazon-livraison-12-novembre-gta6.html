GTA 6 Amazon pre-order: delivery from November 12, how to check yours

Per Infinity Area, a June Amazon pre-order shows delivery on November 12 in France; dates vary between orders.

Sources infinity-area.com · 20minutes.fr

J-12 GTA 6 releases in 12 days →

Pre-ordered GTA 6 on Amazon and wondering when it ships? The Infinity Area newsroom bought its own copy on June 25 for 60 €, and its order shows delivery on Thursday, November 12 in France, a week before the November 19 launch.

Why November 12 matters

That is the day Rockstar plans to make boxed copies available, as we explained in our piece on the physical edition. According to 20 Minutes, the box holds no disc, only a download code to redeem on the PlayStation Store. Getting the parcel on the 12th lets you start pre-loading before launch day.

How do I check my delivery date?

Infinity Area's method is simple: open your Amazon account, go to orders, and read the estimated delivery date on the GTA 6 line. The outlet stresses that its date is an estimate for its own order, not a guarantee for every buyer. One player quoted in the article saw November 28 for a pre-order placed the same day, June 25. Gaps exist.

What if delivery lands after November 19?

The site advises watching the order over the coming days, as dates can move while stock is allocated. We found no statement from Amazon or Rockstar on this, so none of this is an official rule. If the parcel arrives too late, the digital edition remains an option at the standard 79.99 € price.

Leclerc at 60 € for the PS5 version

20 Minutes also reports that E. Leclerc has put the PS5 version back on pre-order at 60 € instead of 79.99 €, with deliveries from November 12 after several sell-outs. The paper does not say how many copies are available, so check stock before celebrating. Our price page tracks the offers.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the release itself: November 19 stands and the unlock time is still unknown. But a disc-less box raises the stakes of a late delivery: no code before the 19th, no pre-load. Eight days out, check your order.
