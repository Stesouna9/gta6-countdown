GTA 6 launch: Vanoss Crew rents a Miami mansion and Rockstar is in the loop

The Vanoss Crew will film the GTA 6 launch in a rented Miami mansion, and Rockstar has already asked for the guest list, GTA BOOM reports.

Sources gtaboom.com

J-22 GTA 6 releases in 22 days →

Some creators do not wait for 19 November in front of a screen. According to GTA BOOM, the Vanoss Crew is preparing an in-person shoot in Miami, in a mansion rented for the occasion. Neon and palm trees already, and any fan of the 2002 Vice City would smile.

Brian "Terroriser" Hanby told GamesBeat, as quoted by GTA BOOM, that it is the group's biggest up-front investment in a physical shoot so far. The plan the site describes is a two-week shoot with flights for the whole crew.

Does Rockstar know what the Vanoss Crew is planning?

Yes. "We have a good relationship with the guys from Rockstar, and they've already kind of asked," Hanby says, per GTA BOOM. The studio knew about the event and reportedly messaged him for the names of everyone attending. For a publisher known for its silence, that is a striking detail.

GTA BOOM also recalls that Rockstar reportedly followed a group of streamers quietly, and mentions unconfirmed rumours of parties or creator activations before launch. Nothing official: treat it as a lead, not a fact.

Why are creators betting so heavily on launch?

Because the audience is there. The site says GTA V has passed 225 million copies and topped Twitch in 2024 with more than a billion hours watched. Andre "Typical Gamer" Rebelo reportedly pulled more than 25,000 concurrent viewers replaying GTA V's story last month. The first weeks decide, GTA BOOM argues, who collects lasting traffic from guides, maps and walkthroughs.

It also cites a YouTube policy change: in-game violence and drug content after the opening seconds could now earn ad revenue. And Rockstar is reportedly building a creator platform, per unconfirmed information. A group shoot is also a shared bet: costs are split and audiences overlap.

On our side we have followed the launch build-up in the Miami trailer shoot story, and the community is organising too.

Why it matters

Nothing for the game itself, a lot for its echo: on 19 November some of the first accounts will come from creators settled in Miami, with the studio's at least tacit blessing. The wisest course is still to wait for what Rockstar says itself.
