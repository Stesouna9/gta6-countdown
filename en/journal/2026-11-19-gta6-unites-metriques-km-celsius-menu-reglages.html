GTA 6 settings: how to switch to kilometres, kilograms and Celsius

GTA 6 defaults to miles, pounds, Fahrenheit and AM/PM time; every unit can be changed in the Units of measurement block of the settings menu.

Sources journaldugeek.com

The first thing many European players look for at launch is the kilometre. By default GTA 6 shows distances in miles, weights in pounds, temperatures in Fahrenheit and time in AM/PM, Journal du Geek explains. Everything can be changed in a few clicks.

Where do you change the units?

In the settings menu, under the "Units of measurement" block. According to Journal du Geek you can switch distance to metric, weight to kilograms, temperature to Celsius and the clock to 24-hour format. Four settings, and the outlet mentions no restart.

Rockstar's logic holds up: Leonida is a thinly veiled Florida, and the studio carries American realism down to interface details. For a European player, reading the weather in Fahrenheit mid-chase means a mental conversion nobody needs.

Should you also turn off motion blur?

The same article suggests a second tweak if your setup allows it: motion blur, in the Display tab. The slider defaults to 3; Journal du Geek recommends setting it to 0, since combined with the already heavy depth of field it makes the picture look soft in cars or when panning the camera. That is the outlet's advice, not a Rockstar instruction.

Why it matters

Nothing changes in the game itself, but comfort improves: mission distances, driving speed and weather become readable at a glance.
