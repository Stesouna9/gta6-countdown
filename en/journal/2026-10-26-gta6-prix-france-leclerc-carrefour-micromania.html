GTA 6: where to pre-order it cheapest in France, and under what conditions?

Retailers show different prices and vouchers: Leclerc lists the game at €60, Carrefour offers a €10 voucher, and Micromania has a trade-in offer with conditions.

Sources ouest-france.fr · carrefour.fr · carrefour.fr · e.leclerc · e.leclerc · micromania.fr · micromania.fr

A GTA VI deal is not always an immediate discount. A comparison published by Ouest-France Shopping on October 26 highlights three retailers, but the prices need to be read alongside their terms. We checked the offers against the pages of E.Leclerc, Carrefour and Micromania. These prices were listed when we checked on October 26; stock and promotions can change.

Leclerc lists the game for €60, but check availability

E.Leclerc’s official listing shows the PS5 code-in-a-box version of GTA VI for €60, €19.99 below the €79.99 reference price for the digital Standard Edition. The box does not contain a disc; it contains an activation code. Leclerc has also said that online pre-orders may be sold out and that copies would be available for collection in stores from November 12. As stock information can differ between the category page, product listing and local stores, the displayed price does not guarantee an order can still be placed everywhere. Check the basket or call your local cultural goods department before travelling.

Carrefour: a €10 voucher, not €10 off at checkout

Carrefour lists the Standard Edition at €69.99 and advertises a €10 voucher. Its in-store reservation page says reservations are free until November 2, with the voucher issued when the game is collected from November 19. The voucher therefore does not reduce the amount paid when ordering; it is a later benefit. We also found an inconsistency in the small print on the general page, which still shows dates from before the currently advertised promotion. Before counting on the €10, check that the offer appears in your order summary and that its terms match what the page currently advertises.

Micromania: €39.99 requires an eligible trade-in

Micromania lists the game at €79.99 and advertises a €20 voucher for pre-orders. A separate “Jouez à” offer can bring the payment down to €39.99 in store, but only in exchange for a used game or accessory on the GTA VI-specific trade-in list. A staff member must approve the item, it must be accepted, and the offer may change. This is not the game’s standard price or a discount guaranteed to every customer. Micromania also says promotions are not necessarily cumulative, so do not subtract a second voucher from the €39.99 without confirmation at checkout.

In all three cases, the retail boxes contain a download code rather than a disc, to support preloading scheduled to begin on November 12. Our GTA VI price and editions page collects reference prices; our report on Leclerc’s code and delivery timing explains another detail to check before buying.

Why it matters

As of October 26, Leclerc’s €60 listing is the simplest price to compare, subject to availability. Carrefour offers a later voucher, while Micromania’s lowest price depends on a trade-in. The right choice depends on stock, delivery fees, whether you can use the voucher, and what you might sell. Read the terms at the final basket step: for these offers, the fine print is part of the price.
