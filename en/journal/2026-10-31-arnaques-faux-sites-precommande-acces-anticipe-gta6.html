GTA 6 scams surge: fake pre-order sites and early access offers

Franceinfo warns of a surge in GTA 6 scams, with fake pre-order sites and early-access offers Rockstar has never announced.

Sources franceinfo.fr

J-19 GTA 6 releases in 19 days →

Three weeks before launch, scammers are crowding around GTA 6. franceinfo reports this Saturday a surge of fake early-access offers and bogus pre-order sites, and cybersecurity expert Luis Corrons expects it to get worse.

What are experts saying about GTA 6 scams?

According to franceinfo, hackers are exploiting fan excitement ahead of the November 19 release. Luis Corrons told the broadcaster, in French: "We fear the situation will intensify. There is a strong chance we will see many more scams in the week before GTA VI comes out."

Franceinfo points to one domain, gta6now.org, among the game-related sites flagged as dangerous. It was last updated in 2013 and has nothing to do with Rockstar.

How can you spot a fake pre-order site?

Stick to what is confirmed: the game costs 79.99 € for the standard edition and 99.99 € for Ultimate (digital only), and the boxed version only contains a download code. A price far below that, an "early access" or a key delivered before the November 12 preload date is a scam. Rockstar has announced no early access.

Buy through your console's official store or a known retailer, and never enter PlayStation or Xbox credentials on a page opened from a message. We already covered the fake PS5 emulators stealing passwords, and our guide to cancelling a pre-order still applies if you paid in the wrong place. Official prices are on our price page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for Rockstar, but the calendar works against players: according to Luis Corrons, fake offers will keep growing as the date nears. Distrusting anything outside the official store is the first reflex to adopt before November 19.
