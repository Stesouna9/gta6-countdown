GTA 6 PS5 files differ in Australia and New Zealand, report says

A 704 KB component is reportedly absent from two regional PS5 packages. Gamekult raises censorship as a possibility, without identifying removed content.

Sources gamekult.com

J-1 GTA 6 releases in 1 days →

Australian and New Zealand PS5 versions of GTA 6 may be missing a small file found in other regional packages, according to Gamekult, citing dataminer TexFunz2. The outlet raises the possibility of censorship. Its report does not identify a removed scene or explain what the file actually does, so a regional packaging difference should remain exactly that until its effect is established.

What is different in the PS5 files?

Gamekult says TexFunz2 examined PlayGo data, part of Sony's system for organising and distributing game resources. The report describes thirty-two sections covering elements such as story content, minigames, animation, textures and audio. Looking at this structure can reveal differences between downloads before players can compare the games themselves.

The two relevant names are easy to confuse. According to the outlet, “core_restofworld” is roughly 1.28 GB, while “core_restofworld001” is about 704 KB. It is the latter, smaller component that reportedly does not appear in the Australian and New Zealand versions. The finding is therefore not a claim that a 1.28 GB section has been removed from those releases.

Does a missing component establish censorship?

It does not. Gamekult notes that less than a megabyte could still be enough to change text or an image. That explains why a small difference might deserve investigation; it does not demonstrate that such a change happened. The report does not establish which asset, if any, would look different to a player, or give a confirmed reason for the regional variation.

The outlet also discusses previous restrictions affecting games in Australia and New Zealand. Those examples provide background for its censorship hypothesis, rather than evidence about the contents of this particular file. Its article supplies no Rockstar confirmation linking the absent component to a classification requirement. Claims about removed missions or specific scenes would go beyond the material reported.

Why it matters

Preload analysis can expose technical details before launch, but filenames and package sizes cannot answer every question about the finished experience. For now, the useful finding is narrow: a reported difference in PS5 distribution data for two regions. It is not enough to establish an altered story, and it does not establish the same behaviour on Xbox.

Players can check our GTA 6 release guide for the launch basics: November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. A comparison of playable releases or an official explanation would be needed to turn this file observation into a reliable statement about regional content.
