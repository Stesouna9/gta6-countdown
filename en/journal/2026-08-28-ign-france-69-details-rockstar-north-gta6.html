GTA 6: IGN France comes back from Rockstar North with 69 gameplay details

Retrospective: written in October 2026 from the sources of the time

Invited to Rockstar North, IGN France watched Rob Nelson play an extended GTA 6 sequence and drew 69 gameplay points from it.

Photo: Rockstar Games

Sources fr.ign.com

IGN France is back from Rockstar North with a list of 69 previously unseen GTA 6 gameplay points. The outlet says it was received at the studio by Rob Nelson, co-head of Rockstar North, who played an extended stretch of the game in front of its team. The full list of 69 sits in IGN's article; its introduction already lets us pin down a few things.

A played demo, not a trailer

According to IGN France, the visit took place at the studio behind the game. Rob Nelson held the controller and talked through the action while the team watched. The outlet describes a "reimagined and very much alive" Vice City, explored in detail, and an open world the co-head walked them through step by step. This is not an edited video but a sequence played in front of witnesses, which changes what can be reported: mechanics, not just shots.

Two topics stand out in the introduction. First, how the relationship between Jason and Lucia evolves across the story, which the trailers only showed in flashes. Second, a complete overhaul of the car-theft system, a founding gesture of the series since GTA III. The passage we have does not detail either, and we will not invent anything in its place.

What to keep in mind

A list of 69 details gathered during a guided demo remains material supplied within a frame the studio chose: Rockstar decides what it shows and what it holds back. That does not take away from the exercise, because a journalist watching live notices movements, chains of actions and world reactions that a trailer never keeps.

Grey areas remain: the real size of the map, the length of the story or the place of Online are not settled by this one account. The best way to form an opinion is to read the 69 points in IGN France's article itself, then set them against other press accounts. The visit fits into the release calendar and lands right after the Extended Look aired on Netflix.

What we know since

The date held: GTA VI launches on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preload on November 12, a PEGI 18 rating, 79.99 € for the standard edition and 99.99 € for Ultimate. The Goodtime State collector costs 399.99 € without the game, GTA VI: The Album is out on November 19 and no PC version has been announced. Rob Nelson has since talked about mission design, covered in our journal.

Why it matters

A national outlet describes a sequence played live rather than a studio montage. IGN's 69 details give a baseline: each new preview can be read against them. For gtavifrance readers, the Jason and Lucia relationship and the new car theft become two topics to follow closely.
