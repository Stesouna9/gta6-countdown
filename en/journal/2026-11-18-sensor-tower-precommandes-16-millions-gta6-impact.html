GTA 6 preorders hit 15.88 million, Sensor Tower expects other games to drop 10%

According to Sensor Tower, relayed by 01net, GTA 6 had 15.88 million digital preorders on 17 November, worth $1.54 billion.

Sources 01net.com

A few hours before launch, Sensor Tower puts numbers on the phenomenon. According to 01net, which relays its report, GTA 6 had 15.88 million digital preorders on 17 November, net of refunds, for $1.54 billion in revenue. PS5 accounts for 75% of sales. The estimates exclude boxed copies and store fees. Rockstar does not publish these figures.

What happens to the rest of the catalog?

Sensor Tower measured other PlayStation games in the month after five big launches. Daily players fell 7.6% after Monster Hunter Wilds, 6% after Oblivion Remastered and 4.7% after Resident Evil Requiem. For GTA 6 it expects a drop of more than 10% across all other games, the largest ever measured. Fortnite and big solo games would be most exposed: 42% of PlayStation owners with GTA 5 also have Fortnite, against 22% of all PlayStation players. Roblox should hold up better.

Did the marketing really matter?

Yes, per the report. Preorders earned $2.4 to 3.8 million a day in August, then $16.3 million the day after "An Extended Look" on Netflix, six times more. It fits the preorderer profile Newzoo described: 72% men, 34 on average. For price context see our price page.

What about phones and work?

For US Android phones only, Sensor Tower expects 60 million extra hours on social media in the first month, 100 million more sessions on music and podcast apps and 50 million on food delivery. Against that: 20 million fewer hours on mobile games, 6 million on video streaming and 180 million fewer sessions on work apps, due to time off and "GTA sick days". These are modelled forecasts, not measurements. On leave taken, read our piece on Italy and France.

Why it matters

If the numbers hold, GTA 6 becomes the biggest launch in gaming before it is even out, and other publishers will feel it from Thursday. What Rockstar reports on real sales remains to be seen.
