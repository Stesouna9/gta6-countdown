GTA 6 PS5 Pro console given to streamers: Rockstar is not selling it

Rockstar is handing out at least 100 GTA 6 themed PS5 Pro consoles to Miami Heat players then streamers, none for sale, TweakTown reports.

Sources tweaktown.com

A GTA 6 themed PS5 Pro exists, but nobody can buy it. TweakTown reports that Rockstar is handing out at least 100 of the custom consoles, first to Miami Heat players, now to streamers and influencers.

What does the GTA 6 PS5 Pro look like?

According to TweakTown, the console has a dark gray and black finish, palm trees etched on the side plates, the GTA 6 logo and neon accents from teal to magenta, a nod to Vice City nightlife. Each unit carries a unique serial number under the Rockstar symbol.

The first wave went to Miami Heat players just before the 19 November release, as part of the team's Vice City collaboration. Packages came in fitted hard cases with matching DualSense controllers and a physical copy of the game.

Who gets the second wave?

A second batch is being delivered to streamers and influencers. TweakTown cites streamer Davy Jones, who showed one of the consoles, with images shared by the videotech account on X. The styling matches the earlier units, which the site reads as a deliberate small run rather than one-offs.

This comes from a single outlet and we have not found it in a Rockstar or Sony announcement: the "at least 100" figure is TweakTown's.

What about the controllers on sale?

Those are in shops. Sony and Rockstar released black and white limited-edition DualSense controllers at 84.99 dollars, per TweakTown, at major stores and PlayStation Direct. We track official items on our goodies page. Another pre-order item, the Vintage Vice City Pack, now resells for 5 dollars, as this article explains.

As for official hardware, the collector set remains The Goodtime State at 399.99 euros, which does not include the game. Editions and prices are on the price page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for buyers: no GTA 6 PS5 Pro is announced for sale, and TweakTown stresses that neither Sony nor Rockstar has offered the public anything despite years of requests for a bundle or official faceplates. A collectible to watch, but no sale is confirmed.
