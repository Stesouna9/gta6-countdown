Phantom Vice Auto: a Turkish clone lands on Steam on GTA 6 launch day

Retrospective: written in October 2026 from the sources of the time

With no PC version of GTA 6 on the table, a small Turkish studio is launching its own open-world game in early access on November 19. Polygon laughs, with a hint of sympathy.

Sources polygon.com

On November 19, PC players will not get GTA 6, but they may get Phantom Vice Auto. According to Polygon, the game from Turkish studio Brgames Interactive will launch in early access on Steam on the very day Rockstar's game arrives. The US site sees boldness in it and does not hide its amusement.

What the game promises

Per Polygon, Phantom Vice Auto offers driving in at least four types of car, shooting with five guns, pedestrians and parkour animations, and that is about all the trailer shows, which is more than a year old. The Steam page, meanwhile, promises three playable protagonists in the style of GTA 5 and a standalone 32-player online mode. Polygon suggests taking those claims lightly, especially as the page talks of a game "upgraded for a new generation", as if it were a remaster, when development started in 2024 according to the studio's own site.

The studio has three people on its website's latest count, notes Polygon. The writer points out that opportunistic copies are a blight on small developers and clutter storefronts, yet finds it hard not to root a little for three people taking on the biggest launch in entertainment. The date is also a snub: most publishers avoid the six weeks around GTA 6.

The gap Rockstar leaves

The launch exists because the slot is empty. Again according to Polygon, Rockstar explains the missing PC version by its wish to serve its core audience first on fixed hardware. The writer adds that he would be surprised if fear of piracy played no part, but that is his guess, not a studio statement. Official dates and platforms are on our release page.

What we know since

Rockstar's game holds its date: GTA VI launches on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preload on November 12, PEGI 18, and prices of 79.99 € for standard and 99.99 € for Ultimate. Still no PC version announced. We have no news on what Phantom Vice Auto actually contains.

Why it matters

For PC players, nothing concrete: a three-person early access game does not replace GTA 6. But the episode says something about the market: a platform without an official version instantly attracts imitators hoping to catch a little of the excitement. Buy with open eyes, after reading the first player reviews.
