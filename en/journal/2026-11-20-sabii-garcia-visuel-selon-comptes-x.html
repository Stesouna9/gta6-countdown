GTA 6 cover model: two accounts identify Sabii Garcia, verification pending

Two GTA fan accounts relay a reported identification, but the underlying Instagram statement has not been inspected directly.

Sources x.com · x.com

Two GTA-focused X accounts are relaying an identification of Sabii Garcia as the model associated with GTA 6's cover visual. GTAVI_Countdown names Garcia and attributes a confirmation to her, while gtasix_ points readers toward an Instagram post presented as the underlying announcement. This is a reported identification from fan accounts, rather than a Rockstar statement directly verified by this newsroom.

What do the posts actually claim?

In its November 19 message, GTAVI_Countdown describes Garcia as a Dominican model based in New York. It also says Cali Elizabeth Moore, identified in the post as Rockstar's casting director, confirmed her role. Those remain claims made by the account: the original statement attributed to the casting director has not been directly inspected for this report.

The separate post from gtasix_ says the real person behind the cover image has been revealed and supplies an Instagram lead. The overlap is enough to report a developing lead with clear attribution. It is not enough to count the two posts as two independent checks. Both accounts could be drawing on the same underlying announcement.

Does a cover appearance establish an acting role?

A connection to promotional imagery does not by itself establish voice work, motion capture or a playable character. The posts being reported concern the cover visual. They do not justify assigning Garcia Lucia's voice, or another specific story role. Our casting coverage treats those jobs and forms of participation separately, because a name attached to one cannot simply be transferred to another.

The official illustration accompanying this article provides visual context for the game. It is not a face comparison or evidence that establishes an identity. The relevant evidence would be the original announcement in its full context, together with the casting confirmation described by the fan account. That distinction avoids turning an illustrative image into a claim it cannot support.

Why it matters

The lead has a named person, a reported original post and more than one account circulating it, so it belongs in our community news watch. What remains missing here is direct inspection of the statements attributed to Garcia and Rockstar casting. Until that happens, the attribution stays qualified. It does not become an independently established official credit, and it supplies no basis for further claims about a character's identity, performance or place in the story.
