GTA 6 preorder: Vintage Vice City Pack, a month of GTA+ and Ultimate extras

<a href="https://mashable.com/entertainment/gta-6-preorders" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Mashable</a> lists the preorder perks: everyone who buys before Nov. 20 gets the Vintage Vice City Pack, digital buyers add a month of GTA+, and the Ultimate Edition is digital only.

Sources mashable.com

What does a GTA 6 preorder actually get you? Mashable has laid out the bonuses ahead of the November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with more detail than the store pages gave so far.

What does everyone get?

According to Mashable, anyone who buys the game before November 20 receives the Vintage Vice City Pack, which Rockstar describes as "a collection of items that flash back to when the neon burned brightest". It includes a yellow 1955 Vapid Stanier with its own garage, outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and a palm tree weapon pattern. Nothing essential, but the nod to Vice City is clear.

What does digital add?

The outlet says digital preorders include a free month of GTA+, Rockstar's subscription, which costs $7.99 a month. Physical copies do not get that month. Mashable adds that boxes carry a download code rather than a disc, something we already covered on our price page. Preloading starts on November 12, including for boxes shipped to retailers on that date.

And the Ultimate Edition?

The Ultimate Edition (€99.99 in France, $100 in the US) is digital only, on the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store and Rockstar Games Store. Mashable, citing Rockstar's website, lists access to two vehicle mod shops, a clothing store and a raid mission on the PTT Youngin$ Illegal Goods Store, a gang compound. The physical retailers named are Amazon, Best Buy, GameStop, Target and Walmart for the US market.

The piece also repeats that Rockstar calls the game "a single-player experience" and floats a multiplayer mode later, which Rockstar has not confirmed. It mentions a PC version in early 2027 attributed to GamesBeat: no PC version has been announced, so treat that as a rumor.

Why it matters

The rule is simple: the pack no longer depends on preordering, only on buying before November 20. The real choice is the GTA+ month and the digital-only Ultimate. Dates are on our release page. The exact unlock time is still unknown.
