GTA 6 sales: Sensor Tower estimates 24.58 million copies, $2.36B in a day

Sensor Tower estimates GTA 6 sold 24.58 million copies and earned $2.36 billion in its first 24 hours; Take-Two has not published official figures yet.

Sources gamekult.com

The figures that circulated on X now have a named source. Gamekult reports that research firm Sensor Tower estimates GTA 6 sold 24.58 million copies by the end of day one, November 19, 2026, for $2.36 billion in revenue. We had covered the numbers as an unconfirmed estimate; a publication now cites the analysis itself.

What does Sensor Tower actually say?

Per Gamekult, the PS5 version would account for 75% of sales, or 18.4 million copies. Just before launch the same firm had counted more than 17 million preorders on PS5 and Xbox Series. The game is download-only and exceeds 200 GB, hence the heavy preloading that, Gamekult writes, spared PlayStation's infrastructure.

How should the records be read?

For scale, Gamekult recalls GTA V reached $1 billion in three days in September 2013, and that the biggest film opening, Avengers: Endgame, reportedly took $1.22 billion in a weekend. Sensor Tower relies on user panels, public platform data and its own models, so this is an estimate, not an accounting result.

The scale fits the known prices: €79.99 standard, €99.99 Ultimate. The split by edition, region and preorders is not detailed in Gamekult's text.

What is still missing?

A Take-Two Interactive release, which Gamekult expects soon. Rockstar has published nothing on sales in our sources, so we report no official record or profit, only a dated, attributed estimate.

Why it matters

The launch now has a sourced order of magnitude, above anything the industry has seen according to Sensor Tower. The final number will come from Take-Two, and we will compare it with this estimate then.
