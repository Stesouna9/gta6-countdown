GTA 6: Miami's E11EVEN club becomes NINE1NINE with Flo Rida on November 6

Several X accounts, including GTAlphaintel and Vice Central, say E11EVEN Miami will take on GTA VI's NINE1NINE look on November 6 with Flo Rida; Rockstar has not confirmed.

Sources x.com · x.com · x.com

J-20 GTA 6 releases in 20 days →

A Miami nightclub may turn into a Vice City club on November 6. @gtasix_ started it, echoed by GTAlphaintel and Vice Central: E11EVEN Miami would become GTA VI's NINE1NINE for a night with Flo Rida. Rockstar has confirmed nothing.

What do we know about the NINE1NINE night?

Per those posts, the November 6 night would run under the banner of NINE1NINE, the club parodied in the game, with a Flo Rida performance to celebrate GTA 6. Vice Central adds a neon Testarossa at the entrance and says Rockstar is not involved.

What is verified?

Only part of it. The Speakeasy ticketing page, checked by our monitoring, confirms Flo Rida at E11EVEN that Friday, but its text does not mention NINE1NINE. The GTA makeover rests on X posts alone for now. Treat it as unconfirmed.

Why Miami?

Miami is the real-world backdrop of the game's marketing: see the $3.9 million Miami-Dade deal and the Miami Heat Vice City night on November 18. See our release page.

Why it matters

Nothing for the game itself. If real, one more launch event; if not, a club promo riding GTA. We will update if Rockstar, E11EVEN or the press confirms.
