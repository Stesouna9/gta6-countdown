GTA 6 nudity: Jason and Lucia are never fully naked on screen

Kotaku and Insider Gaming say Jason and Lucia are only seen from behind in GTA 6 showers, with no frontal nudity, though some NPCs are fully naked.

Sources kotaku.com · insider-gaming.com

Can you see Jason and Lucia fully naked in GTA 6? According to Kotaku and Insider Gaming, which have both played the game released on November 19, the answer is no: the two leads appear nude in some scenes, but never from the front. Both sites do report full frontal nudity on other characters.

What does the game actually show of Jason and Lucia?

Per Insider Gaming, the first hours show little: you can take a shower in the safehouse and see Jason from behind. Lucia becomes playable later and gets the same rear view. The site also mentions intimate scenes that unlock once the couple's relationship has grown, still without frontal nudity for either lead. You catch a glimpse of Lucia's upper body as she steps in the shower, "but that's about it".

Kotaku describes the same thing: the sex scenes are not "porn-level explicit", you may see a stray breast or buttock, but no full frontal. The shower camera angle is fixed and photo mode does not show more. Kotaku adds that Japanese players would not even get the rear view, which matches the Buzz item on a toned-down Japanese version.

Can you walk around naked in Leonida?

No, according to both sites. Kotaku writes that you can dress down to your skimpiest outfit, but there seems to be no way to go fully naked in public. The videos attributed to Cyberleek, which showed the leads undressed on the map, do not reflect the final game. Insider Gaming offers a reason: in those clips, bystanders did not react, which does not fit a world where police and witnesses do. It judges the footage was modded. That is its own analysis, not a Rockstar confirmation.

Cyberleek is back this week with another threat: our piece on the executable promised for November 23 details what he claims to hold.

Where is frontal nudity shown?

On other characters. Kotaku mentions nudist areas, including bars and beaches, where several NPCs are naked, plus missions with explicit imagery it does not spoil. Insider Gaming cites a nudist resort and a street scene with an elderly man, which suggests Rockstar accepts nudity as scenery but not for its leads. The game is rated PEGI 18, and the press has already urged parents not to buy it for a child, see our piece on the Guardian's warning.

It fits what the press noted before launch: prostitution is gone, as we wrote in this article, and Rockstar stressed the tone of Jason and Lucia's relationship. The debate is already on: see the controversy described by BFM.

Why it matters

For anyone wondering whether the game goes as far as the leaks: both sites say no, at least for Jason and Lucia. One unknown remains, flagged by Kotaku and Insider Gaming themselves: a late-game scene or post-story content could change that, and nothing says so yet. No Rockstar statement on the subject was found. Kotaku already imagines what modders will do with a PC version, which has not been announced.
