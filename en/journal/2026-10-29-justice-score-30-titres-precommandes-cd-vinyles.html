GTA 6: Justice score details 30 main tracks and physical pre-orders

The announcement reported by the press separates 30 main tracks from digital bonuses. CD and vinyl pre-orders are open ahead of December 11.

Sources xboxygen.com · rockstaractu.com · actugaming.net

J-20 GTA 6 releases in 20 days →

GTA VI’s composed soundtrack now has a clearer release plan. Reporting Rockstar’s announcement, Xboxygen says Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score contains 30 main tracks alongside digital bonuses. Pre-orders are open for a December 11, 2026 release on streaming services, double CD and triple vinyl. That main-track count updates the earlier Apple Music listing of 50 entries, which we covered yesterday.

The main album and the extras

The distinction matters more than a change of number on a store page. Xboxygen separates the 30 compositions on the main release from additional digital tracks. Its report does not establish a final total for those extras. There is consequently no sound basis for assuming that the bonuses automatically restore the original 50-entry listing. Thirty is the figure given for the principal album in the new announcement.

Rockstar Actu describes Justice’s contribution as adaptive music, responding to how players approach Jason and Lucia’s story. A standalone album offers a way to hear the compositions away from the game. Its track listing does not, by itself, catalogue every variation that might accompany a play session. Listening to a record and hearing a score respond to a player are different experiences.

CD and two vinyl editions

Xboxygen lists three physical options: a black triple-vinyl set, a limited Crystal Clear triple-vinyl set and a double CD. The outlet reports prices of €41, €45 and €17 respectively on Justice’s store, before delivery. It also notes slightly different offers from retailers. Those figures describe the listings checked by the publication; they should not be read as a fixed price across every shop or destination.

ActuGaming independently reports the December 11 physical release and the 30-track programme. The music therefore arrives after GTA VI’s November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. A soundtrack pre-order is a separate purchase with a separate release schedule, something worth checking when several GTA-branded products appear together.

Why it matters

The useful development is a firmer description of what listeners can buy, rather than another announcement of Justice’s involvement. Original Score should also be distinguished from GTA VI: The Album, the separate 34-song compilation scheduled for November 19. Our GTA VI music page covers that project too. Anyone choosing a physical edition should check the full album title, the format and the delivery charge before ordering. Sharing a game’s name does not make the two releases interchangeable, either in their contents or in the day they become available.
