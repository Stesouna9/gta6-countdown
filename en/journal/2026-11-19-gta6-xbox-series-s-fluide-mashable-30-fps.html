GTA 6 on Xbox Series S: how it runs, according to Mashable

Mashable says GTA 6 runs without stutter or lag on Xbox Series S after a few hours; the PS5 Pro comparison is still to come.

Sources mashable.com

No slowdown on the small Xbox. According to Mashable, GTA 6 "runs smooth as butter" on Xbox Series S: author Eric Miller reports "no stutters, no lag" after a few hours of play.

Is GTA 6 playable on Xbox Series S?

Yes, according to this hands-on. The writer went through the Jason and Lucia prologue, daytime, night and rain, then explored Leonida: busy streets, a sunset by the water, a swim among fish. Nothing, he writes, pulled him out of a AAA game experience, despite earlier fears, echoed on Reddit, about reduced crowd density or scaled-back visuals.

How does it compare with PS5?

Mashable cannot say yet: it is comparing the Series S version with PS5 Pro. It reminds readers of Rockstar's statements: the game runs best on PS5, with faster loading, and PS5 Pro adds ray tracing. Both stay locked at 30 frames per second. Rockstar has not said whether other sacrifices were needed on Series S. See also Digital Foundry's Series X analysis and our console comparison.

Why it matters

For players who never upgraded, the fear of an unplayable Series S version looks eased by this first report, though it is a single one with no performance measurements. Numbers are still to come. GTA 6 is out since last night: our release page.
