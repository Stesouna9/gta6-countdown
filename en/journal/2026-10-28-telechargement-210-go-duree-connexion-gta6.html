GTA 6 download time: 210 GB takes 30 minutes to over 46 hours

According to Les Numériques, downloading GTA 6 on PS5 takes under half an hour on fibre and over 46 hours on ADSL, based on the unconfirmed 210 GB figure.

Sources lesnumeriques.com · tomsguide.fr

Downloading GTA 6 on PS5 would take under half an hour on 1 Gb/s fibre and over 46 hours on 10 Mb/s ADSL, according to Les Numériques. The estimate starts from the 210 GB figure found by PlayStation Game Size, which Rockstar has not confirmed.

How long does it take to download GTA 6?

For 210 GB, Les Numériques gives three theoretical marks: under 30 minutes on 1 Gb/s fibre, over four and a half hours at 100 Mb/s, over 46 hours at 10 Mb/s. Our own maths agrees: 210 GB is 1,680 gigabits, so 28 minutes, about 4 h 40 and 46 h 40. Network congestion is ignored, and a day-one patch could add weight. The figure comes from the PlayStation Store database, see our 210 GB story.

How much space do you need on your console?

Per Les Numériques, a launch PS5 has about 667 GB usable: GTA 6 would take nearly a third. On an Xbox Series S, with about 364 GB free, it would take more than half. Tom's Guide puts the Series X at 802 GB free. The Xbox size is unknown.

Does the boxed version skip the download?

No. The physical edition holds a download code and no disc, a decision Rockstar made in June, notes Les Numériques. Boxes reach stores on 12 November, preload day. A Dutch shop even boycotts it over this, see our story.

Why it matters

Start the preload on 12 November. Tom's Guide notes it starts on its own if automatic downloads are enabled: check that beforehand. Rockstar still has to confirm the real size, especially on Xbox. Dates are on the release page.
