GTA 6 launch gallery: Rockstar releases 38 new images

Rockstar has put a GTA 6 launch gallery on its Newswire: 38 visuals of Lucia, Jason and Vice City, according to Urban Hit.

Sources urbanhit.fr · rockstargames.com

J-7 GTA 6 releases in 7 days →

Rockstar has published a launch gallery for GTA 6, titled "Explore the Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Gallery" on its Newswire. According to Urban Hit, it contains 38 new visuals, released eleven days before the November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

It fits the pattern of recent weeks: Rockstar is feeding its official site in step with the countdown, after announcing the pre-load on November 10 and the original score by Justice.

What do the 38 images show?

Urban Hit says they feature Lucia and Jason, the two protagonists, several non-playable characters, and scenes set in Vice City and the state of Leonida. The outlet singles out one quieter picture: a man hiding his face behind his hand, far from the usual action shots.

We could not cross-check that description with a second source. Only Rockstar's Newswire is authoritative on the gallery's exact content, so we do not go through it image by image.

Are these images new?

Urban Hit calls them new. Rockstar's headline does not say which images had never been shown. Some may overlap with visuals from earlier trailers and galleries.

Why it matters

Nothing changes on the known facts: the date, platforms and release schedule stay as announced. The interest lies elsewhere. Eleven days from launch, Rockstar is showing a realistic, character-driven game rather than dropping a new trailer. If you are avoiding spoilers, open the gallery with the same caution as anything else.
