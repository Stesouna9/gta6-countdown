GTA 6: Miami Festival doors open at 4 p.m., ticket proceeds go to charity

Rockstar says doors open at 4:00 p.m. Miami time at The Festival, with a GTAVI store on site; all ticket proceeds go to local charities.

Sources x.com · rockstargames.com

Rockstar published the set times for Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival on Wednesday, November 18. In a post on X (over 155,000 views within the hour), the studio says doors at Mana Wynwood open at 4:00 p.m. Miami time, which is 10 p.m. in France, and that the venue's store will be stocked with special GTAVI gear.

Rockstar adds that, "as a thank you to Miami," all proceeds from ticket sales will go to local Miami charities. The post points to the official festival page for the set times, the running order of the artists, which we could not read as plain text there at the time of writing.

What do we already know about the Festival?

According to Rockstar's official page, the event takes place on November 21 at Mana Wynwood, 318 NW 23rd St in Miami, in partnership with PlayStation and produced by the team behind III Points. Two stages, nonstop music, and a lineup billed as drawn from the game and its soundtrack: Cactus Jack presents Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black and Sofaygo, Cardi B, Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso, Justice (DJ set), Metro Boomin, Nick León b2b DJ Python, Prospa b2b Skream, Rauw & Friends, Suero, Tainy and The Dare b2b Fcukers. Billboard had already detailed the lineup in our album story.

How do you get in?

Tickets were allocated by lottery through DICE and Laylo, with sales to winners on November 11. The lottery is closed, but Rockstar offers a waitlist. Two tickets per buyer, non-transferable, 17+ only, photo ID required. There is no on-site parking and Rockstar strongly recommends rideshare. No re-entry, no refunds.

A launch week rooted in Miami

The city has worn the game's branding for days: the Heat's court turned into Vice City, the Nike and Cactus Jack collection, the Miami Heat night. The Festival lands two days after the November 19 release, and the pop-up store adds to the merchandise already known.

Why it matters

For fans outside Miami nothing changes in practice: the event is on site and Rockstar has not announced a broadcast so far. What is new is the opening time and the charity pledge. The detailed running order is still to be confirmed on the official page; we will update this piece if it appears in plain text. For the game's music, see our music page.
