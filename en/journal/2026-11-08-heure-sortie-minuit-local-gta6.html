GTA 6 release time: what time does it unlock on November 19?

GTA 6 would unlock at midnight local time on November 19, per IGN France and Journal du Geek; Rockstar has not confirmed the exact time.

Sources fr.ign.com · journaldugeek.com · rockstargames.com · notebookcheck.biz

J-7 GTA 6 releases in 7 days →

GTA 6 launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026, but the exact unlock time is not spelled out in the sources we have. According to IGN France and Journal du Geek, the game would unlock at midnight local time in most countries, which means 12:00 a.m. on November 19 in France. Rockstar has not confirmed it in the texts we read, so treat it as press reporting, not a guaranteed schedule.

What time does GTA 6 unlock?

IGN France writes that Rockstar plans to launch the game on November 19 at midnight local time in most countries. Journal du Geek adds that unlock times spotted on the PlayStation Store point to 15 time slots, from New Zealand through Europe to the United States. Neither Rockstar nor Sony or Microsoft has confirmed those slots in the texts we read, so we call them likely, not settled.

Why local time changes the night of November 18

A midnight-local unlock is not a single worldwide launch: New Zealand would start about 12 hours before France, per IGN, and over 20 hours before Mexico. Rockstar did the same with Red Dead Redemption 2, which unlocked at midnight local time on console. That is why Journal du Geek discusses the Xbox "New Zealand trick", changing the console region. On PS5 it says the workaround is much harder, with a New Zealand account and gift card. Nothing says Rockstar or the platform holders will allow it.

When does preloading start?

These times are official. Rockstar moved the preload to November 10, as covered in our article on the earlier preload, which replaces the November 12 schedule from our first article on the topic. According to Rockstar Newswire, it opens at local midnight on PS5 and 0:00 GMT on Xbox Series X|S, which is November 9 at 7 p.m. EST, per Notebookcheck. Plan for up to 212 GB of free space, depending on the console.

Why it matters

For anyone who wants to play in the first minute, the safest plan is to preload on November 10 and be ready before midnight on the evening of November 18. Confirmed details (date, price, editions) are gathered on the release page. If Rockstar publishes per-country times we will update this piece; until then, the unlock time is likely, not confirmed.
