GTA 6: Rockstar releases the Pre-Load Now trailer and a Tove Lo single

Rockstar Games posted a 30-second GTA 6 spot on Tuesday for the pre-load launch, and revealed Blood In Your Eyes, a new single by Tove Lo.

Sources youtube.com · gtavi-thealbum.com

Rockstar Games released a new GTA 6 video on Tuesday, November 10, shortly before 3 pm Paris time, on its YouTube channel. Titled "Grand Theft Auto VI: Pre-Load Now", the 30-second spot marks the opening of pre-loading on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, nine days before the November 19 release.

Who can pre-load

According to the video description, pre-loading is available to everyone who pre-ordered the game digitally. Players who bought a code-in-box version can activate their code and start pre-loading as soon as they receive it. Rockstar urges anyone who has not pre-ordered yet to do it now to be ready on launch day. The game weighs 212 GB, so check your console's free space before starting the download.

Tove Lo delivers Blood In Your Eyes

The video also showcases the soundtrack. Rockstar says "Blood In Your Eyes", a new single by Swedish singer Tove Lo, is available on GTA VI: The Album, the official soundtrack. The 34-track album comes out on November 19, the same day as the game, and can already be pre-saved on major streaming services or pre-ordered on vinyl or CD on the official album site.

Communication is ramping up

After months of near silence broken by rare trailers, Rockstar is speaking up more often as launch approaches: a pre-load confirmation on X, then this spot mixing a practical reminder with music promotion. The takeaway is simple: if you pre-ordered digitally, you can start downloading now and play on November 19 without waiting.
