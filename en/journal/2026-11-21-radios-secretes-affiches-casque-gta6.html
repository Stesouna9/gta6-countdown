GTA 6 secret radio stations: how to unlock them with poster scans

Two hidden stations, The Sniper of La Perle and The Vault Radio, unlock by scanning posters in La Perle, PC Gamer reports.

Sources pcgamer.com · mashable.com · kotaku.com

Not every GTA 6 radio station is available from the start. PC Gamer explains that Leonida hides two secret playlists, both needed for 100% completion, and neither is hard to unlock once you know where to look: you scan posters with Jason's or Lucia's phone.

How do you unlock the two secret stations?

The first, The Sniper of La Perle, is a 22-track playlist by Kodak Black. It opens after scanning four posters in Vice City's Haitian district of La Perle: at TWA Ponya Tattoo, Drip Drops, Aunt Zola's Soul Food and the Jack of Hearts nightclub, where the poster sits in the upstairs recording studio. The second, The Vault Radio, needs a single scan, on the poster at the back of the entrance corridor of the nightclub of the same name.

The shops mark themselves on the map as you drive around La Perle and Crosstown, PC Gamer writes, and the phone alerts you to something to scan through the Waink It app.

Can you listen to the radio outside a car?

It is a series first, according to Mashable: you no longer need to be driving. Jason owns no headphones at the start; you buy them at an electronics store, marked by a headphones icon on the map. The outlet points to Zeke's Electronics, north of Hamlet: earbuds at $119 or over-ear headphones at about $200. Then hold left on the D-pad to listen on foot.

Kotaku adds shortcuts the game does not explain: double-tap left on the D-pad to switch the car radio on or off without opening a submenu. The writer says they learned it from Reddit and could not find it in the help menu.
