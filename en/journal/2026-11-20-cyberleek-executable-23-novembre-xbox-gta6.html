GTA 6 Xbox build leak: Cyberleek sets November 23 for the executable

Cyberleek says it will release the missing executable of its Xbox build on November 23, whatever its memecoin does; nothing is authenticated yet.

Sources fr.ign.com · ign.com · news.ssbcrack.com

Cyberleek file: everything we know about the GTA 6 leak →

The leak now has a date for its next chapter. IGN reports that Cyberleek threatens to release the executable of its alleged Xbox GTA 6 build "in just days", and SSBCrack gives the date: November 23, whether or not its memecoin reaches the stated target.

What is Cyberleek promising for November 23?

According to IGN, Cyberleek released an Xbox build without the executable on November 19, launch day, calling it a "full November 2026 build". The missing file was first tied to a $15 million market cap for the token, which IGN puts near $1.9 million at the time. The group now says it will publish the executable regardless.

It also claims PC emulation could follow "within a week" at well above 30 fps. SSBCrack stresses these claims are unverified and that nothing confirms the files are genuine or usable. Rockstar and Take-Two had not commented in the sources we read.

Read also GTA 6: Cyberleek claims to have released a 209 GB Xbox build

Why does the fixed date matter?

Until now the threat depended on a financial goal. A fixed deadline decouples it from the token price. IGN notes fans are urging people not to spend money, since the same persona reportedly cashed out in August and October, for roughly $270,000 according to blockchain analysis. Doubts about the contents remain too: IGN says the leading theory had been an older development build.

See our earlier pieces on the 209 GB Xbox build and on why no emulator runs GTA 6 on PC. Rockstar has announced no PC version.

Should you download these files?

No. Distributing or downloading unauthorized copies of commercial software is illegal, and files from unknown sources can carry malware, as SSBCrack points out. More on the launch is on our release page.

Why it matters

November 23 is a date to watch, with no proof yet that the file exists or runs. We will publish nothing leaked and will update once a confirmation, denial or Rockstar action is sourced.
