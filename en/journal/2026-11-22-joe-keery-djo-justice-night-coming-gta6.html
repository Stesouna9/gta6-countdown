GTA 6 soundtrack: Joe Keery (Djo) sings on Justice's Original Score

Stranger Things' Joe Keery, as Djo, features on The Night's Coming with Justice, per Eklecty-City. The track is due on the December 11 Original Score.

Sources eklecty-city.fr

Joe Keery, who plays Steve Harrington in Stranger Things, sings on the GTA 6 soundtrack. According to Eklecty-City, the actor lends his voice, under his stage name Djo, to The Night's Coming, produced by French duo Justice.

Which record is The Night's Coming on?

Not the album released with the game. Per Eklecty-City, the track is slated for Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score, due December 11, 2026 on streaming, CD and vinyl. That second record gathers 30 pieces by Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay, and also includes, the outlet says, the dark techno cut Pusher made with Gesaffelstein.

What is the song about?

Eklecty-City describes a nocturnal, melancholic mood: regret, the memory of a loss that cannot be undone, an old heart beating to a repeating voice. Nothing more precise has been published about the production or where the track plays in the game.

Who is Djo beyond Stranger Things?

Keery has run a music career for over a decade. He co-founded psychedelic rock band Post Animal, left in 2018, then launched Djo in 2019 with the single Roddy. His album Decide (2022) went international in 2024 when End of Beginning blew up on TikTok. The outlet also mentions a Grammy-nominated sleeve for The Crux and a Tame Impala tour slot.

The GTA 6 soundtrack leans heavily on the French electronic scene. We already covered Daft Punk on Flash FM in our Around the World piece, and the radio stations landing on Spotify in this one.

Why it matters

Nothing changes in the game itself, but the split between the two records gets clearer: the 34-track album out November 19, and Justice's score with guests. Whether Rockstar will publish the full Original Score tracklist before December 11 is still unknown. All the game's music lives on our music page.
