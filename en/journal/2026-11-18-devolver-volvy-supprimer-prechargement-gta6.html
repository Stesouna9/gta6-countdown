GTA 6: Devolver gives its game to people who delete their preload

Devolver Digital is giving away Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed, out November 19 like GTA 6, to people posting a preload deletion video, per IG News.

Sources news.instant-gaming.com · x.com

J-1 GTA 6 releases in 1 days →

An independent publisher offers to make room for its game by deleting Rockstar's. IG News reported on November 16 that Devolver Digital is giving away a copy of Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed, a platformer launching on November 19, the same day as GTA 6, to people who delete their GTA 6 preload.

How do you enter the Devolver giveaway?

According to IG News, you repost the publisher's official tweet with a video of yourself deleting your GTA 6 preload, with the hashtag #MakeRoomForVolvy. The prize is a copy of the game. Devolver asks in its post: "Want a free copy of November's hottest release?", and bills its game as the month's hottest release. The game is announced for PC on Steam. The GTAlphaintel account on X relayed the same contest on Wednesday.

Why delete a preload?

GTA 6 weighs 212 GB on PS5, a big share of a console SSD. The publisher plays on that: to start something else on November 19, you first have to free up space. It is open counter-programming, the kind small studios run against big release dates. Nothing suggests Rockstar will react, and the giveaway only concerns people willing to give up the game, at least for the length of a video.

Why it matters

For players, nothing changes: the GTA 6 preload stays open and the launch stays at local midnight. For Devolver, it is free visibility on the day all eyes turn to Vice City. Our release page covers times and preloads. On PC, Rockstar's game still has no announced version.
