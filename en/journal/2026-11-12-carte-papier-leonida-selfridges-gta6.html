GTA 6 physical map of Leonida spotted at Selfridges in Manchester

A physical map of Leonida was spotted at Selfridges in Manchester, per @gtasix_; Rockstar has announced nothing and its presence in the box is unconfirmed.

Sources x.com · x.com · x.com

A physical map of Leonida has been spotted at Selfridges in Manchester: according to @gtasix_ (via @GazzaMontana), it is the first look at the game's paper map. Rockstar has announced nothing on the matter.

What does the map show?

According to @GTAlphaintel, it sat in a Collector's Edition display and shows place names: Lummox County, Moreau, Manzana, Goose Key, Southwind, Pelican Valley and Key Lento. This comes from shoppers' photos shared on X; we have not checked it with Rockstar.

Is a paper map in the box?

Update, 3 pm: "full map" claim

Later in the day, @gtasix_ claimed the full Leonida map leaked from the Collector's Box. The post (238,000+ views) rests on that single account: no outlet or Rockstar has picked it up, and nothing shows the item is the entire map. Treat as unconfirmed.

Nothing confirms it. For the boxed edition, press reports mentioned an A3 poster and a downloadable map, no paper map. The item at Selfridges may simply be a store display piece, in a shop that already shows a real Grotti Cheetah. Release remains set for November 19.
