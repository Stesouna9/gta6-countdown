GTA 6 auto-drive: CBR explains Cinematic Drive and its two settings

CBR’s guide describes a waypoint-based Cinematic Drive feature, with road-restricted and unrestricted accessibility options.

Sources cbr.com

Comic Book Resources describes an automatic driving feature in GTA 6 called Cinematic Drive. Its guide says to mark a destination on the map, enter a vehicle and hold down on the directional pad until the activation prompt appears. These are the publication’s instructions, rather than findings from a hands-on test by our newsroom.

Which driving setting should you choose?

According to Comic Book Resources, the accessibility section of the gameplay menu contains restricted and unrestricted options. The first keeps automatic travel on standard routes, while the second allows off-road use as well. That distinction is more useful than treating automatic driving as a single, universal behaviour.

Think about the journey before choosing the setting. Getting between activities and wandering across unfamiliar terrain are different intentions. A player looking for a quiet transfer may not want the same behaviour as someone exploring a less direct route. Comic Book Resources also cautions that the unrestricted option can expose the vehicle to damage. Automatic steering should therefore not be read as a guarantee that nothing can go wrong.

Making room for the journey

The interesting possibility is a change of attention. With less need to manage each bend, a player can spend more time looking around. Travel can become part of the experience instead of simply the interval between objectives. That is an editorial observation about how such a feature might be used, rather than a claim that every player will find it preferable.

There is no need to abandon manual driving to find value in the option. Choosing when to take the wheel and when to watch the scenery can be a personal decision. A feature that is welcome during one trip may be less appealing during another, especially when the point of playing is the act of driving itself.

Readers can compare experiences in our community section. Useful reports would name the platform, selected setting and circumstances, so that an isolated experience does not become an unsupported general rule. Our news journal keeps the wider reporting and its sources together.

Why it matters

CBR’s instructions offer a practical starting point for trying a different pace of travel. They do not establish that every vehicle or sequence behaves identically. The useful takeaway is to choose the option deliberately, understand its settings and keep expectations proportionate to the evidence available. Automatic driving is an additional way to spend time in Leonida, with its appeal depending on what the player wants from that particular journey.
