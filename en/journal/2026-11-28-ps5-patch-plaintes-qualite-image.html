GTA 6 PS5 update: players report visual changes, downgrade unproven

Notebookcheck reports complaints about image quality after version 01.002.002. The cause and extent of any changes remain unconfirmed.

Sources notebookcheck.net

Some PS5 and PS5 Pro owners believe GTA 6 looks worse after update 01.002.002, according to Notebookcheck. The complaints cover sharpness, lighting and the appearance of objects. The publication does not establish a general downgrade through controlled measurements, and its report contains no Rockstar explanation confirming the suspected visual changes. The distinction between testimony and a demonstrated alteration remains central to this story.

What are players noticing?

Notebookcheck describes posts about blurrier textures, more visible object pop-in and less convincing illumination. Other complaints concern character faces and hair. The publication also makes clear that players do not all agree. These observations are useful leads for analysis, but they do not establish a result shared by every console or every scene.

Weather and lighting feature prominently in the accounts. Some players find sunrise and sunset scenes less striking, or rain reflections less convincing. Such comparisons need equivalent conditions to tell us much about a patch. A difference between two images does not by itself identify a changed graphics setting, particularly when lighting and weather are variable.

Has Rockstar confirmed a graphics reduction?

Notebookcheck dates the first post-launch update to November 23 and says it addressed crashes, static weather and other problems. It reports that the notes did not list performance changes. The suggestion that visual fidelity was deliberately reduced to ease the load is therefore an interpretation surrounding the complaints, rather than a studio decision documented by this source.

The report also mentions player speculation about image reconstruction on PS5 Pro. Its evidence does not establish that this technology caused the perceived loss of clarity. Even a real visual difference would require further testing to identify the mechanism and determine whether the base console and Pro model were affected in the same way.

Why it matters

The practical question is whether an apparent change can be measured under comparable conditions. Matching scenes, identifying the versions used and keeping the viewing setup consistent would make a stronger case than unrelated screenshots. Notebookcheck itself advises waiting for further analysis before drawing conclusions about what the studio has changed.

This issue is separate from frame rate experiments on a modified PS5 Pro. Those tests do not describe the official update running under ordinary conditions. Our GTA 6 coverage will track a documented studio response or controlled comparisons if they appear. For now, the supported conclusion is that complaints have been reported; their scale, cause and consistency across hardware remain unresolved.
