GTA 6 music: Justice to play a DJ set in Paris on December 3

Justice, the duo behind GTA 6's original score, will play a GTA 6 night at Mia Mao in Paris on December 3, with tickets going on sale November 27, according to Sortir à Paris.

Sources sortiraparis.com

Justice is stepping out of the soundtrack and onto a stage. According to Sortir à Paris, a GTA 6 night will take place on December 3, 2026 at Mia Mao in Paris, with a DJ set from the French duo. Tickets go on sale on November 27.

Who is playing, and where?

Justice is Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay. The outlet notes that the duo composed the game's original music, with an album release announced for December 11, 2026. Mia Mao is a former 3,000 m² industrial hall at La Villette turned into an electronic music club, with a no photos, no videos policy.

What do we know about the lineup?

Not much. Sortir à Paris says the exact musical programme remains to be revealed, and gives no ticket price or start time. The source does not say who organises the night either, and we found no Rockstar announcement about it in our data, so do not treat it as an official event yet.

The duo is already central to the game's music news: its Spotify chart-topper was covered by franceinfo today, and we spotted a nod to The Weeknd in the score. All the game's music is on our music page.

Why it matters

For Parisians, it is the first announced chance to hear Justice live in the GTA 6 universe, a week after launch. Elsewhere, nothing yet: no other date is mentioned. Watch for tomorrow's ticket sale.
