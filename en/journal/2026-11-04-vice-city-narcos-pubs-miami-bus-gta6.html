GTA 6: new Miami ads spotted on a tour bus, including the Vice City Narcos

Two X accounts share GTA 6 ads seen on a Miami tour bus: a basketball team, watches, shapewear and a taco chain, all parodies.

Sources x.com · x.com

J-12 GTA 6 releases in 12 days →

Rockstar keeps dressing Miami in Vice City colors. According to GTAVI_Countdown and gtasix_, four new parody ads were spotted on a tour bus in the city. Both accounts credit the same sighting, posted by user samchrme. Rockstar has said nothing on its Newswire: these are community photos, not a press release.

Which brands show up on the bus?

Both accounts list the same four. The Vice City Narcos, a basketball team echoing the Miami Heat, with the line "Get Your Balls Out, Vice City!". Crowex, a luxury watch brand nodding to Rolex, with "From Father Time to Daddy Time". Beige, a shapewear brand recalling SKIMS, with "The All-Natural Look". And Taco Bomb, a restaurant reminiscent of Taco Bell, with "Explosive Flavor!".

The basketball wink is not new. We already covered the Miami Heat "Night in Vice City" evening, and an official image already shows a player in a Manatees jersey.

Why does Rockstar run fake ads?

The series has parodied brands since its early days, and Leonida, modeled on Florida, suits it. Putting these posters in the real Miami two weeks before launch extends the joke: you meet the fictional brand before you even start the game.

What remains unknown: how many cities get this kind of placement, and whether the fictional brands matter in missions or only in the scenery. The photos come from a single observer relayed by two accounts, so we present them as a fan sighting, not confirmed by Rockstar.

Why it matters

Nothing about the game itself, but the street campaign is growing as the date nears. For release and preload details, see the release page: launch on November 19, preload moved up to November 10 according to Rockstar.
