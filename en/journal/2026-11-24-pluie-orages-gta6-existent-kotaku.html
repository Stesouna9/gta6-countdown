GTA 6 weather: thunderstorms do exist in Vice City, says Kotaku

Kotaku says GTA 6 does have thunderstorms: rain is local, and the first patch on Monday fixed a bug that froze the weather.

Sources kotaku.com

"It never rains in GTA 6": the complaint has spread on Reddit and X since launch. Kotaku answers that yes, storms do hit Vice City, but you can easily miss them. The writer says they saw rainstorms and fog before release.

Why do some players never see rain?

First reason: a real flaw. According to Rockstar's patch notes cited by Kotaku, the first fix, released Monday, solves an issue that kept the weather from changing for long stretches. We covered it in our patch notes article.

Second reason, per Kotaku: the system is local. In earlier GTA games, rain falls across the whole map. Here, storms, fog and other events hit only part of it. The writer describes flying a plane: clear sky, then big dark clouds ahead, rain inside them, and dry again after leaving. So it may be raining elsewhere in Leonida while you are not in the right spot.

Is the weather locked early in the story?

Some players say so. Kotaku finds it plausible but "not confirmed by Rockstar yet". The same writer reports several very rainy in-game days in chapter 3, to the point of missing the sun. One account proves nothing, so the question stays open.

Why it matters

Rockstar hyped dynamic weather before launch; this piece softens the "Rockstar lied" claims without dismissing them, since a bug did exist. If your game stays dry after the patch, nothing official explains why. More launch news is on the release page.
