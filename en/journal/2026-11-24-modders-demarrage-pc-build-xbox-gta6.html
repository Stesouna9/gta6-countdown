GTA 6 on PC: modders boot the leaked Xbox build, black screen for now

Eurogamer reports modders have GTA 6 booting on PC using Cyberleek's executable, but it stops at a black screen; about a week to playable, they say.

Sources eurogamer.net

Modders already have GTA 6 booting on PC from the Xbox build released by Cyberleek, Eurogamer reports. The game reaches the initial setup screen, then stops on a black screen: it is not playable.

What exactly happened?

After posting a near-complete Xbox build last week, Cyberleek published the executable (.exe) file it had deliberately held back. Eurogamer quotes the message relayed on Reddit: "The exe is out. Always free. An early Christmas gift from CL", adding "The torch passes to the open source re-comp and emulation projects". We covered that release in yesterday's article.

Hours later, members of the Project Leonida Discord server, the hub for recompilation efforts, posted footage of the game booting on PC. According to Eurogamer, the modders were helped by the Claude AI, and the Reddit video shows the launch up to the black screen. The outlet says it has not verified these claims itself.

When could a playable PC version arrive?

Shayder, a lead developer on Project Leonida, said in a since-deleted X post to expect a lot of static recompilation work. On Discord he clarified it could take about a week to reach a playable state. That is a modders' estimate, not a promise.

Cyberleek, meanwhile, keeps asking for donations for a secret project "for the gamers". The readme says nobody has to give anything and every release stays free, but every donation is a vote for more.

What about Rockstar?

Rockstar has not announced a PC version of GTA 6. Eurogamer notes the studio usually ships PC versions several months after consoles, and that an October report pointed to the first quarter of 2027. Earlier today another project called Leonida pulled out, with actions attributed to Rockstar. For platforms, see the release page.

Why it matters

Booting is not porting. The build may never reach the quality of an official port, but Eurogamer thinks these efforts could push Rockstar to speed up its own. A pirated PC version remains unauthorised content: we publish no link or file.
