GTA 6 cars, including a Coquette, spotted driving around Miami

Vehicles dressed as Vice City cars, among them a Coquette and a Lamborghini in Vice Beach Police livery, have been filmed in Miami. Rockstar has not confirmed it is behind them.

Sources insider-gaming.com · pushsquare.com · konbini.com

A Coquette wrapped in Vice City colors and a Lamborghini in Vice Beach Police livery are driving around Miami, Insider Gaming reports, based on images posted on X by the GTASixInfo account. Rockstar Games has not claimed anything so far.

What do the Miami images show?

According to Push Square, several real vehicles have been dressed up to look like the ones in the game: Vice City number plates, loud body kits, and even Vice Beach Police patrol cars. Videos seen on Instagram also show Vice City branding being installed, a blimp carrying the game's logo and planes towing banners.

Insider Gaming spells out the models. The Coquette is GTA's fictional take on the Corvette. The Lamborghini will not appear as such in the game: Rockstar draws on real brands to design its supercars without copying them.

Is Rockstar behind it?

There is no confirmation. Konbini stresses that the publisher's official involvement has not been established, and Push Square notes that none of these elements names GTA 6 explicitly. Caution applies: we do not know whether the cars belong to an orchestrated campaign or to very well-equipped fans. The context points to the former, since the city is already serving as a showcase for the game.

We have already covered the night the Miami Heat become Vice City's team against Milwaukee. The cars add to that backdrop, three and a half weeks before the November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Why does Miami play this role?

Because Vice City has been modeled on Miami since 2002, and Leonida, GTA 6's fictional state, is a stand-in for Florida. A Coquette parked on Ocean Drive is the moment the map meets the territory. The game promises a state where nature sits next to neon, which makes the comparison hard to ignore.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game itself: no information on content, date or price is altered, and the release stays set for November 19 (see our release page). What changes is the scale of the promotion, which now spills from screens into the street. Whether Rockstar owns these cars officially in the coming days remains to be seen.
