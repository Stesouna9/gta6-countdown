GTA 6: stealing the Coil Raiden, a Tesla copy, drives you to the police station

In GTA 6 the Coil Raiden, modelled on the Tesla Model S, closes its windows and drives itself to the police station once stolen, 01net reports. Breaking the window gets you out.

Sources 01net.com

Stealing a Coil Raiden in GTA 6 ends at the police station: the car, a copy of the Tesla Model S, drives the thief to the cops by itself, 01net reports.

What does the Coil Raiden do when stolen?

After you eject the owner, you will not get far, per 01net: the car shuts its windows and takes the wheel, as if self-driving had kicked in. Destination: the nearest police station. The site says you cannot turn it off, unlike the real FSD.

How do you get out?

Waiting is the worst option, since police collect you on arrival. The best answer, according to 01net, is to break the window. Other vehicles already resist thieves: recent ones have a hands-free key to hack, and a cat can even attack the driver of a freshly stolen car.

Elon Musk reacted

01net says Elon Musk reacted on X with a crying-laughing emoji. We have not seen the post ourselves. No real Tesla can pull off such a move yet, the outlet stresses.

Why it matters

Little for progression, but a reminder: stealing a car in GTA 6 is no longer always instant. Spot the Raiden before you grab it, and keep a way to break glass.
