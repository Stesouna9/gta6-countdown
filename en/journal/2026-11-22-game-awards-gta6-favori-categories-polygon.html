GTA 6 at The Game Awards: Game of the Year favorite, three categories at risk

Polygon calls GTA 6 a certainty for Game of the Year on December 10, but not a sure sweep of all nine categories.

Sources polygon.com

GTA 6 is "more than the favorite" for Game of the Year at The Game Awards on December 10: it is "a certainty", writes Polygon, which points to its 99 on Metacritic, described as the highest ever. But the site doubts a clean sweep: it says Rockstar's game could lose at least three categories.

How many nominations does GTA 6 have?

Eight so far, according to Polygon, which expects a ninth once the fan-voted Players' Voice nominees are announced. To match the nine wins Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 took in 2025, GTA 6 would need to win everything. The author does not expect that and predicts six wins out of nine, which would tie the game for third place all-time with Baldur's Gate 3 and God of War Ragnarök. The Last of Us Part 2 won seven.

Where is GTA 6 expected to win?

Polygon sees GTA 6 taking Best Game Direction: the gap with the field is called too wide, even against Remedy's Control Resonant. Narrative also looks good, as Rockstar won it with Red Dead Redemption 2 in 2018 and most reviews highlighted the love story between Jason and Lucia. Audio is tipped as a win too, as the jury tends to reward the busiest soundscapes. For performance, Manni Perez as Lucia is a strong contender but not a lock, with Polygon naming Angela Sant'Albano for Resident Evil: Requiem.

Which categories are at risk?

For art direction, Polygon notes the jury rarely rewards contemporary realism: The Last of Us Part 2 lost to Ghost of Tsushima in 2020. It sees Remedy as favorite, with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time as the dark horse. For music, the soundtrack leans on licensed songs while the jury prefers film-style scores, so it would come down to Justice's score against Koji Kondo's orchestrations. Finally, Players' Voice has often gone to Chinese games, and Polygon rates Phantom Blade Zero above its predecessors.

Why it matters

Nothing is decided before December 10, but the debate is no longer whether GTA 6 wins, only how many trophies it takes. The game, out November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, enters awards season from a position of strength. Music and art will test how much jurors value licensing versus original work.
