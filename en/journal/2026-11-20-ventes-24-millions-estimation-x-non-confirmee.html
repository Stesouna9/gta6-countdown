GTA 6 sales: 24 million claim circulates, no verified official total

Two accounts attribute huge launch figures to Sensor Tower, but the underlying report and its methodology have not been verified.

Sources x.com · x.com

Two GTA-focused accounts are circulating a claim that GTA 6 has sold more than 24 million copies. GTAVI_Countdown and gtasix_ attribute their figures to Sensor Tower and also describe revenue above $2.3 billion. The shared claim is newsworthy, but the corresponding primary report and any matching Rockstar or Take-Two sales announcement have not been inspected to validate it.

Which numbers are being shared?

The November 19 message from gtasix_ describes more than 24 million units and over $2.3 billion. A later post from GTAVI_Countdown gives more specific totals of 24.58 million copies and $2.36 billion in under 24 hours. These are not separate batches of sales that should be added together. They are presented as versions of a launch estimate attributed to the same analytics company.

Neither collected post provides a full methodology or a clear definition of the measurement. That leaves important questions unanswered, including the treatment of preorders, the mix of editions and the exact time window being counted. Decimal places can make a number look authoritative without supplying the evidence required to assess its coverage or accuracy.

Is this an official publisher total?

An estimate attributed to an analytics firm is different from a sales total released by the publisher. There is an additional gap here: the underlying Sensor Tower document has not been directly checked. Agreement between two accounts demonstrates that a claim is circulating, not that its arithmetic or sourcing has been independently validated. Our news coverage keeps those categories distinct.

Copies sold, revenue and profit also measure different things. The posts describe claimed units and revenue, without offering a calculation of costs or net earnings. The $2.36 billion figure therefore cannot be relabelled as profit. Looking at the edition prices alone would not reconstruct worldwide receipts either, especially without the regional and edition breakdown behind the estimate.

Why it matters

After the preorder estimates, a widely shared launch claim is now shaping discussion of the game's commercial scale. It deserves coverage with its provisional status visible. A firmer assessment requires the original source, its measurement period and methodology, followed by comparison with any publisher disclosure. Until then, the totals stay attributed and unconfirmed. They do not provide a verified foundation for declaring a new sales record, a net profit figure or a final first-day result.
