GTA 6: 9 games to play until November 19

Only 32 days until GTA 6. Nine games to get you to November 19, from GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 to the original Vice City and our neon brick breaker Vice Break.

Sources rockstargames.com · apps.apple.com

J-28 GTA 6 releases in 28 days →

November 19 is getting closer, but 32 days is a long time. Here are nine games to fill the wait, picked for a mood close to what awaits Lucia and Jason in Leonida: the city, crime, Florida sun and neon.

1. GTA V and GTA Online

The obvious pick. Released in 2013 and still available on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, GTA V is the best way to get back into Rockstar's world, and GTA Online is still being updated.

2. Red Dead Redemption 2

Rockstar's last big game before GTA 6, released in 2018. A rare level of detail and cinematic storytelling: the best preview of what the studio can do today.

3. GTA Vice City (Definitive Edition)

To revisit Vice City before going back: the 2002 classic, re-released in the Definitive Edition trilogy in 2021. The 80s, Tommy Vercetti, and Rockstar's dream version of Florida.

4. Mafia: The Old Country

Released in 2025, the Mafia series returns to early 20th-century Sicily. More linear than GTA, but a real organized-crime story.

5. Cyberpunk 2077

A big open city, gangs and very different districts: Night City is one of gaming's best cities, especially since the Phantom Liberty expansion.

6. Sleeping Dogs

A slightly forgotten 2012 classic: an undercover cop inside Hong Kong's triads, punchy fights and a living city.

7. Watch Dogs 2

Open-world San Francisco, released in 2016, lighter and funnier than GTA, with the same freedom to roam and cause chaos.

8. Hotline Miami

For the Miami 1989 mood and pink neon: a short, very hard game with a cult synthwave soundtrack.

9. Vice Break

And to kill time on your phone: Vice Break, our free neon brick breaker, 100 levels in a Miami-inspired city with Vice Bay radio playing. Full disclosure: it is made by OKALAM Studio, the publisher of this site. It comes out November 19, the same day as GTA 6, and pre-orders are open on the App Store.

To follow the countdown to the end, our release page has the date, the November 12 preload and the time where you live.
