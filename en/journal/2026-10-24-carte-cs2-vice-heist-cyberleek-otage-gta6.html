GTA 6: a Counter-Strike 2 map lets you rescue Cyberleek from a Rockstar mansion

Dexerto reports that The Vice Heist, a Steam Workshop map uploaded on October 17, turns the GTA 6 leaker Cyberleek into a hostage and has over 45,000 subscribers.

Sources dexerto.com

The Cyberleek saga now has its own game map. According to Dexerto, a community Counter-Strike 2 map called The Vice Heist asks players to rescue the GTA 6 leaker from a mansion supposedly belonging to Rockstar Games.

What is in The Vice Heist?

Dexerto says the map went up on the Steam Workshop on October 17, made by lmtlss and leander. Its description is one line: "Save Cyberleek from the Rockstar mansion!" Cyberleek plays the hostage in a classic Counter-Strike setup applied to a very real controversy. The site says over 45,000 people are subscribed.

Reception is positive: one player quoted by Dexerto writes "The Cyberleek hostage is 10/10", another compares it to Counter-Strike: Condition Zero's single-player missions and asks for more maps.

Who is Cyberleek, again?

Dexerto recalls that the anonymous leaker has been sharing apparent GTA 6 footage since August 2026 and making demands of Rockstar and Take-Two, including physical copies of the game. Their identity is unknown, and Take-Two has reportedly pursued legal action to identify those responsible. For the latest threats, see our pieces on the 200 GB build claim and the Lucia videos. We do not publish or describe any leaked footage.

The mansion is not official: it is players' fiction with no established link to a real Rockstar location.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for GTA 6 itself, still set for November 19. But the story has moved from forums into gamer culture, to the point of becoming a match scenario. More fan creations live on the community page.
