GTA 6 sales reach an estimated 53 million in a week, says Alinea

Alinea estimates 53 million copies and $4.7 billion in first-week revenue, with PS5 accounting for 75.7% of units.

Sources alineaanalytics.substack.com · n-gamz.com

Alinea Analytics estimates that GTA 6 sold 53 million copies during its first week, generating $4.7 billion in revenue. Rhys Elliott published the figures on November 27. The headline needs its qualifier: this is an independent market estimate, rather than a fresh sales announcement from Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

What does the 53 million estimate count?

N-Gamz, reporting on the same analysis, describes purchases reaching players rather than stock shipped to retailers. That distinction matters when comparing launch reports. Revenue also does not mean profit: the total cannot tell readers how much money the publisher retains after costs, distribution arrangements and other deductions.

Alinea puts PS5 at 75.7% of copies sold. Its Xbox estimate is 13 million copies, representing 24.3% of the total. The newsletter describes Xbox’s share shrinking over the opening days. These are estimates of the game’s platform split, rather than a count of consoles bought specifically to play it.

Why does PlayStation lead in the analysis?

Elliott points to PlayStation’s visibility, Sony’s joint marketing with Rockstar and a larger pool of casual buyers arriving after recommendations and mainstream coverage. Those are the analyst’s explanations for the pattern. The sales split alone cannot isolate the contribution of advertising, existing console ownership or word of mouth to each purchase.

The newsletter also examines spending on other premium PlayStation games. After removing Phantom Blade Zero from its sample, Alinea finds weekly revenue down 12% across ten titles. Its own discussion stresses that normal sales decay was already occurring before GTA 6 arrived. Treating that entire decline as damage caused by Rockstar would therefore overstate the finding.

Why it matters

This update supplies a useful checkpoint beyond the opening weekend. Future comparisons will need matching time periods and consistent definitions, with independent modelling kept distinct from official financial disclosures. A larger headline does not automatically describe a directly comparable measurement, particularly when reports mix shipments, purchases, player counts and revenue.

For readers considering a copy, our edition and pricing guide addresses the practical purchase decision. Market share cannot decide which platform suits an existing library or household. Nor does this sales estimate announce a Windows edition or establish a timetable for the online mode. Those questions still require their own announcements from Rockstar, regardless of how large the launch becomes.
