GTA 6: how to unlock storms and hurricanes with Freddie’s secret mission

According to Journal du Geek, tropical storms and hurricanes unlock through a hidden mission north of Lake Leonida involving a character named Freddie. Spoiler guide.

Sources journaldugeek.com

No tropical storm in your game yet? According to Journal du Geek, that is normal: these extreme conditions are rare and triggered by a secret mission. Spoiler warning: what follows reveals part of the game.

Where do you start the hurricane mission?

The guide says to head north of Lake Leonida, in North Leonard County, where a heavily watched compound warns you to leave. Follow the fence north, climb the tower and jump onto a small hangar roof to get in. The site is full of proximity mines and attack drones. At the bunker entrance you meet Freddie, “disappointed you are alive”; he takes your number and tells you to come back later.

How do you get the first storm?

The guide says to wait 4 to 6 in-game days until Freddie texts that his new compound is ready. On return, the ladder is blocked: a bulldozer on the building site to the right lets you push containers and find a secret tunnel. The compound is even more trapped. Freddie then invites you to tour his doomsday bunker, and a hurricane alert goes off mid-visit.

Leaving is recommended to face the first storm. Freddie pays $7,500 for the traps, and his Dubsta SUV, resold for $14,500 per the guide, brings the total to $22,000.

Why it matters

Extreme weather is therefore unlockable content, not a random event. This route comes from a press guide, and Rockstar has published nothing about it. For ordinary rain, see our article on weather according to Kotaku and the patch notes on stuck weather.
