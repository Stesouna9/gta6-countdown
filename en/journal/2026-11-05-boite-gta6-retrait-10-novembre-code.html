GTA 6 physical editions reportedly available from November 10, unconfirmed

GTA 6 physical editions would be available for pickup from November 10, two days earlier, per JVFrance and @gtasix_, with no Rockstar confirmation.

Sources jvfrance.com · x.com

J-13 GTA 6 releases in 13 days →

GTA 6 "box" editions would be available from November 10, two days earlier than planned, according to JVFrance and a post by the X account @gtasix_, which cites iGrandTheftAuto. We found no Rockstar statement on this point, so it remains unconfirmed officially.

When can you pick up the GTA 6 box?

According to @gtasix_, physical editions will be available for pickup from November 10 instead of November 12. Each copy contains a code to pre-load the game. JVFrance writes that Rockstar reportedly changed the box date to match the digital pre-load, moved up to November 10, as we explained in our pre-load article. The game itself only unlocks at the worldwide launch on November 19.

What is inside the GTA 6 box?

No disc: the box holds only a download code, according to Rockstar's support page reported at the time. The game weighs 212 GB, which partly explains the choice. La Crème du Gaming also recalls, citing Rockstar's November 3 Newswire, that the Leonida map is not in the box: it is offered as a free download at launch, or as an official poster at $0.99 on the Rockstar Store. See our piece on the poster and map.

JVFrance's editorial comment is harsh: buying a box to get a mere code, it says, removes the point of the physical format, since the buyer owns nothing more than a digital buyer.

Why it matters

For anyone who reserved a box, the 212 GB download could start the same day as digital, which matters on a slow connection. Confirm the exact day with your retailer, since stores set their own pickup hours. The November 19 unlock time remains unknown. Prices and editions are detailed on the price page.
