GTA 6: The Last Ride brings players back to Los Santos on November 18

GTA Online players are planning a peaceful farewell convoy on November 18, with no official start time or Rockstar involvement.

Sources numerama.com · complex.com

J-1 GTA 6 releases in 1 days →

Los Santos players are making room for one more shared journey before Vice City. Complex reports that a community convoy called The Last Ride is planned for November 18, the day before GTA 6 launches. Numerama also covers the farewell initiative. This is a player-led gathering, with no official Rockstar involvement, built around a simple request: drive together and leave the fighting behind.

Where does The Last Ride go?

The proposed route, as described by Complex, begins outside Los Santos International Airport. It continues to Del Perro Pier, heads north to Paleto Bay and finishes on Mount Chiliad. Drivers can bring whichever vehicle and outfit they prefer. The point is to share familiar roads rather than compete for the fastest finish or display a required car.

There is no official starting time, according to Complex. Individual groups can arrange their own November 18 departure, and some organizers recommend private lobbies to reduce disruption. Anyone hoping to join friends should therefore agree on a meeting place and schedule directly with that group. The reporting does not describe one synchronized session that every player must enter at the same moment.

What makes this convoy different?

Complex lists rules against shooting, looting, deliberately hitting other vehicles or bringing the police down on fellow participants. These restrictions change the purpose of the drive. Instead of using the city as the space between missions, players are being asked to spend time with its roads and landmarks, with the company taking priority over the destination.

Both Numerama and Complex trace the idea to creator CraftizyTV, whose nostalgic TikTok posts began in February 2025. References to DJ Cara and Non-Stop-Pop FM helped frame the farewell around memories of Los Santos. Our radio page follows that same connection between journeys and music, although this community event has no compulsory soundtrack stated in the reporting.

Why it matters

The farewell is symbolic. Complex says GTA Online will remain available after GTA 6 arrives, so the convoy should not be mistaken for a shutdown announcement. Players are marking a change in their routines rather than racing against the disappearance of the game. Taking part does not mean losing the option to return to Los Santos later.

That distinction leaves room for a quieter kind of launch preparation. Alongside checking the GTA 6 release details, friends can set aside an evening for the city they already know. The November 18 gathering gives those groups a common idea without requiring a worldwide countdown: pick a route, respect the other drivers and let familiar scenery become the reason for the trip.
