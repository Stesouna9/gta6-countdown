GTA 6 follow-up: L.A. Noire 2 rumor, unconfirmed by Rockstar

An anonymous leak claims Rockstar's next game is an L.A. Noire sequel aimed at 2030; neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed it.

Sources shattered.io

Rockstar may be working on a new L.A. Noire after GTA 6, not GTA 7, Red Dead Redemption 3 or Bully 2. That is a rumor, not an announcement. According to shattered.io, which cites ComicBook.com, GamesRadar and Dexerto, a leak that resurfaced on Sunday, three days after GTA 6 launched, claims the studio's next game is an L.A. Noire sequel.

Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed anything. No press release, trailer, job listing or regulatory filing backs it up.

What does the leak claim?

Per shattered.io, three details travel with the claim: the setting would move from 1940s Los Angeles to San Francisco, the plot would center on the Zodiac Killer, and the target window would be 2030. The site notes that none of it carries an official stamp. The leak would also rule out Bully 2.

Why caution is needed

The source is an anonymous 4chan or GTAForums poster no outlet has verified. Outlets even disagree on timing: Dexerto traces the claim to a December 2023 GTAForums post, while ComicBook.com describes it as resurfacing in September. It is back in the headlines because players think the same source described part of GTA 6's ending correctly, per ComicBook.com. shattered.io points out that one right detail does not validate a game planned years away.

What is confirmed at Rockstar?

Little. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, and Take-Two owns Rockstar. Remedy is making Max Payne 1 & 2 Remake, financed and published by Rockstar, with no release date. See our Metacritic piece for GTA 6's reception, and Buzz for other rumors.

Why it matters

Nothing changes yet. An L.A. Noire sequel remains an unverified claim with no official trace. We file it as a rumor to revisit when Rockstar talks about its next project.
