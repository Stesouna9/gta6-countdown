GTA 6 music: Justice tops Spotify chart, franceinfo reports

Franceinfo says Flower in a Hurricane has reached the top of Spotify’s global listening chart, prompting a surprised response from Xavier de Rosnay.

Sources franceinfo.fr

GTA 6 has given Justice a new chart story. Franceinfo reports that Flower in a Hurricane, the French electronic duo's track produced for the game, is leading Spotify's worldwide listening chart. Speaking on November 26, Xavier de Rosnay described the result as unexpected. He performs in Justice alongside Gaspard Augé.

Which Justice track is leading the chart?

The song identified in the report is Flower in a Hurricane. That distinction keeps the news useful for listeners trying to find a particular recording after playing. It also sets a boundary around the claim: a chart position for one track does not establish a ranking for the entire soundtrack or the duo's wider catalogue.

The collected franceinfo text does not provide a stream count for Justice, a percentage increase or a detailed run of daily chart positions. Those missing measurements matter when comparing different artists. Leading a chart and recording the largest weekly increase are separate achievements; the report supports the former description here, without supplying numbers for the latter.

From making the music to hearing the response

Our earlier coverage of Justice's track with The Weeknd concerned the music itself. Today's development shifts the focus to its reception outside the game. A comment from one of the musicians adds a direct human response to a story that might otherwise be reduced to a chart entry.

Why it matters

For players exploring GTA 6's music, the immediate takeaway is a specific song to look up. The report connects a track made for the game with listening beyond the console, while recording its creator's surprise at that response. It does not announce an extra radio station, another album edition or a game update. The new information is narrower and clearer: franceinfo reports a leading Spotify position for Flower in a Hurricane and a reaction from Xavier de Rosnay. Further comparisons would require additional chart data, rather than assumptions about how every player listens.
