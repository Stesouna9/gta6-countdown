GTA 5 leaves PS Plus on November 17: keep playing with GTA+?

GTA 5 leaves PlayStation Plus Extra and Premium on November 17, per La Crème du Gaming; a free GTA+ month with the GTA 6 digital pre-order keeps it playable.

Sources lacremedugaming.fr

J-3 GTA 6 releases in 3 days →

GTA 5 leaves the PlayStation Plus Extra and Premium catalog on November 17, 2026, two days before GTA 6, according to La Crème du Gaming. Subscribers who do not own the game lose access from November 18.

On the PlayStation Store, GTA 5 sits in the "last chance to play" row with November 17 as the deadline. The site describes a PS5 screen offering two options: buy GTA 5 or pre-order GTA 6. Red Dead Redemption 2 leaves the same catalog the same day, with no workaround.

What happens to subscribers on November 18?

A subscriber who has not bought GTA 5 can no longer launch Story Mode or GTA Online through PS Plus. The icon stays in the menu, but a payment screen blocks access. Local saves and PlayStation Plus cloud saves remain intact and reload if the game is bought later. Players who already own GTA 5, physical or digital, are not affected.

How can you keep playing GTA 5 without buying it?

Per the same source, any digital pre-order of GTA 6 on the PlayStation Store includes one month of GTA+, active from purchase. The GTA+ games library includes GTA 5, single player and online, so the game stays playable while that month runs. The outlet adds that the subscription then renews at the usual price and must be cancelled in PlayStation settings if you do not want it. We could not check this offer on the Store ourselves: it rests on the site's account.

Why it matters

For anyone relying on PS Plus to stay in Los Santos, the window closes before GTA 6 even launches. The free GTA+ month with a digital pre-order is a bridge, not a lasting fix. GTA 6 is not announced in any subscription so far. The standard edition costs 79.99 € (see prices) and the game launches November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S: see release.
