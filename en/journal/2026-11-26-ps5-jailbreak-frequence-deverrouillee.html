GTA 6 PS5 frame-rate unlock reported, but stable 60 FPS is not assured

A modification reported by PhonAndroid raises the ceiling under specific conditions, while hardware limits remain.

Sources phonandroid.com

A higher frame-rate ceiling is not the same thing as a stable performance mode. PhonAndroid reports a modification for jailbroken PS5 and PS5 Pro consoles that can allow GTA 6 to reach up to sixty frames per second under particular display conditions. The report also describes substantial drops. Nothing in it amounts to an official Rockstar announcement of a new graphics option for standard consoles.

Why does the display refresh rate matter?

PhonAndroid explains that the modified ceiling corresponds to half the television refresh rate. On a 120Hz display that means up to sixty frames per second. On a 60Hz display the limit remains thirty. The display therefore determines the permitted maximum, but cannot make the console produce enough frames to reach it during every moment of play.

The outlet reports that one of the patch authors characterises it primarily as a frame-rate unlock. That description is more useful than calling it a sixty-FPS patch. Removing a software restriction does not increase processor or graphics hardware capacity. A jailbroken console is another requirement, so this is not a hidden option that ordinary players can simply activate in the game settings.

How smoothly does it actually run?

According to PhonAndroid, a PS5 can fall to around twenty frames per second during driving and explosions. The report identifies PS5 Pro as the machine likely to provide the better experience with the modification, without establishing a constant sixty-frame result. We have not independently benchmarked the footage or reproduced its conditions.

The development follows reports of a build running on a jailbroken PS5. Its distinct contribution is the altered frame-rate limit. It should also be kept separate from the earlier console comparison, which addressed another testing context. Combining the two would obscure the conditions that give each number meaning.

Why it matters

This reported patch illustrates the gap between allowing a higher number and sustaining it. For someone deciding how to play, consistency matters as much as a peak frame rate. The account provides no basis for promising an official sixty-FPS update, and it cannot establish how a future console will perform. Our GTA 6 platform guide keeps public releases distinct from experiments on modified hardware. For now, the strongest conclusion is a narrower one: the ceiling can reportedly move, while the hardware constraints remain.
