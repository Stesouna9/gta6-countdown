Vice Break, the neon brick breaker set in Vice Bay, is up for free pre-order on the App Store

Vice Break, made by OKALAM Studio, the publisher of GTAVIFRANCE, is up for free pre-order on iPhone and iPad in 175 countries. It launches November 19, the same day as GTA 6.

Sources apps.apple.com

J-27 GTA 6 releases in 27 days →

Disclosure first: Vice Break is made by OKALAM Studio, the publisher of this site. Our neon brick breaker is now up for free pre-order on the App Store in 175 countries, for iPhone and iPad.

What's inside

A hundred levels across Vice Bay, an imaginary city inspired by 1980s Miami: the beach, downtown, the motels, the skyscrapers, with a boss in every area. Vice Bay radio plays in the background with its synthwave, salsa and jazz stations, and you can switch stations without leaving the game.

Free, no ads during levels

The game is free. It is funded by a small bonus shop and by ads outside of levels: no ad ever interrupts a game.

Out November 19

Vice Break will arrive automatically on iPhones that pre-ordered it on Thursday, November 19, 2026, the same day as GTA 6. The Android version is coming to Google Play a little later.
