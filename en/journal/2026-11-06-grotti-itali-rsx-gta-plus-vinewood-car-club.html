GTA Online: Grotti Itali RSX free for GTA+ members, Rockstar says

Per a Rockstar post on X, the Grotti Itali RSX is free for GTA+ members at the Vinewood Car Club, with new Chameleon Paints and a Mansion decoration.

Sources x.com

J-9 GTA 6 releases in 9 days →

Rockstar is giving GTA+ members the Grotti Itali RSX in GTA Online. According to a @RockstarGames post on X from Friday, November 6, the sports car is free to claim from the Vinewood Car Club showroom. The studio frames it as a way to "get ahead of the impending holiday rush".

What is in this week's GTA+ offer?

The post lists three items. First, the Grotti Itali RSX, free for GTA+ members through the Vinewood Car Club. Second, new Chameleon Paints, described as "mind-blowing". Third, a decoration for the Mansion, "and more", with no detail in the message itself. The post links to a short rsg.ms address for the full announcement; we only repeat what the message states. It had passed 185,000 views at the time of writing.

GTA Online keeps running before GTA 6

Weekly updates like this show Rockstar still supports GTA Online while GTA 6 launches November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Nothing in this post concerns GTA 6: no mention of the new game, its online mode or any link with GTA+. We draw no conclusion about the subscription's future, on which Rockstar has said nothing here.

For current players it is simple: the car is free, you need to be a GTA+ member and visit the showroom. Exact terms (how long the offer lasts, paint prices) are not in the post, so they remain to be confirmed on Rockstar's official site.
