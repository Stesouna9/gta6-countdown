GTA 6 Festival announced in Miami on November 21, ticket lottery open

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival takes place November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami, with Travis Scott, Cardi B and Justice. Tickets go through a lottery closing November 10.

Sources videogameschronicle.com · gamingbible.com · infinity-area.com

J-8 GTA 6 releases in 8 days →

Rockstar is throwing a party for the launch of GTA 6. According to Video Games Chronicle, Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival takes place on November 21, 2026 at Mana Wynwood in Miami, two days after the game's release. Rockstar calls it "a special live event" with artists from the game and its official soundtrack.

Update, November 10, 8:20 a.m. ET: Rockstar posted a reminder on X: the ticket lottery closes today at 4 p.m. ET (10 p.m. in France). An active dice.fm account is required. Winners are picked at random and contacted with access codes by 7 p.m. ET, with 2 tickets per code.

Who is playing?

Per GAMINGbible, two stages will host Cardi B, Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso, Justice (DJ set), Metro Boomin, Nick León B2B DJ Python, Prospa B2B Skream, Rauw & Friends, Suero, Tainy, The Dare B2B Fcukers (DJ set), plus Cactus Jack presents Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black and Sofaygo. It is a joint effort with PlayStation, produced by the team behind III Points, which already runs at Mana Wynwood.

How do you get a ticket?

There is no open sale: ticketing, run by DICE, goes through a lottery. INFINITY AREA reports that free sign-up closes Tuesday, November 10 at 10 pm French time (4 pm to 7 pm ET). Winners get an SMS code by early November 11, and sales start Wednesday, November 11 at 12 pm ET. Codes are single-use and cannot be sold or transferred.

Two tickets maximum per person, 17 and over. Winning the lottery does not guarantee a purchase. Ticket prices are not stated in the sources we read.

A link to the game's music

The game launches November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with GTA VI: The Album announced for the same day: see our music page. Miami inspired Vice City, and the festival sits in Wynwood, known for its murals.

Why it matters

Rockstar adds a real-world moment to its launch and brings the music of Vice City and Leonida to fans on site. Nothing so far points to a livestream, and we have not seen confirmation of one.
