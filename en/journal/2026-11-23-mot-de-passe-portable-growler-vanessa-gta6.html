GTA 6 laptop password: MuffinsQueen15 and the hidden pink Growler

The Chapter 3 apartment laptop password is MuffinsQueen15, and it leads to a free garage with a pink Growler in La Perle, per Polygon.

Sources polygon.com · polygon.com

What is the laptop password in GTA 6? Polygon has the answer: MuffinsQueen15. The laptop sits in the bedroom of the Chapter 3 apartment and opens a trail to a free garage.

How do you work out the password yourself?

At first none of the listed options looks right. Polygon says you must inspect two items. The first is a photo frame on the hallway table, dedicated to a dog named Muffins: pick it up, examine it, put it back. The second is the notepad on the dresser by the bed, in front of the photo wall. On the back of the drawing of Muffins on a throne, with a tiara and a drink, a note reads "Class of '15 reunion next Friday". The right options then appear: the dog's name, what she is (a queen) and the class year.

What is on the laptop?

Once open, it shows the windows left by Vanessa, the previous occupant: five WhatUp conversations, one of which leads to Auntie's Place, a photo gallery and her PeekHole chats, the game's OnlyFans parody.

Where is Auntie's Place and what does it hide?

The restaurant is not marked on the map. Per a second Polygon guide, it is off Del Rio Blvd in south La Perle, southeast of Aline's Thrift Store and east of TWA Ponya Tattoo. Don't confuse it with Aunt Zola's Soul Food just southwest. A bit further down the road, a white garage has a keypad with a small pink ribbon graffiti. Code 92665 opens it: inside is Vanessa's Growler, a pink Pfister Growler, and the garage can then store vehicles at no cost.
