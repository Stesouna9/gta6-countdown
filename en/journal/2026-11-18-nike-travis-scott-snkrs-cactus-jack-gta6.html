GTA 6 Nike Air Force 1: WWD reports Saturday and Monday releases

WWD lists a Saturday SNKRS release followed by a Monday Cactus Jack drop for Travis Scott’s GTA 6 Air Force 1 collaboration.

Sources wwd.com

Travis Scott's GTA 6 Air Force 1 collaboration now has a different timetable from the game's launch, according to WWD. The publication reports a Saturday release through Nike's SNKRS app, followed by a Monday opportunity on the Cactus Jack website. Its report explicitly revises the earlier expectation that the shoes would arrive on the same day as GTA 6.

Which dates should buyers watch?

In this week's November 2026 calendar, the Saturday and Monday mentioned by WWD fall on November 21 and November 23. Those dates are calendar conversions of the weekdays in the report. They do not establish a precise opening time or tell readers which national storefronts will participate.

That is the practical change for anyone who had put the game's release and the sneaker launch in the same diary entry. Our earlier Cactus Jack collection coverage described the wider promotion. This update concerns the footwear distribution timetable; it should not automatically move every related clothing item to a single new date.

Are all the colourways confirmed for both drops?

WWD describes black-dominant and white versions carrying GTA and Rockstar references. Its discussion of their SNKRS availability draws on product images. The report is more tentative about where a multicoloured pair could appear, and about how the two distributions might divide up the designs.

A photographed shoe, a pair worn by the artist and a listed retail product are different pieces of evidence. Keeping those distinctions visible is especially useful for limited collaborations, where pictures often travel faster than purchase details. WWD also mentions additional Cactus Jack exclusives without supplying a complete shopping catalogue in the collected text.

Why it matters

The useful takeaway is a two-stage distribution plan after the game's release, rather than a promise that every reader can buy every version. The material used here does not establish French stock, prices or exact sale times. Those details should come from the actual product listings rather than being inferred from the scale of the partnership.

Our merchandise page separates collectible products from the game purchase itself. The announced Miami festival is another distinct part of the campaign. Music, footwear and a game can share a promotional setting while still arriving through different channels and on different schedules.
