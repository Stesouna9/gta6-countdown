GTA 6 endings: every Jason and Lucia outcome and how to unlock it

Journal du Geek counts five to seven GTA 6 endings depending on variants, and the best one needs $2 million in the BuckMe account.

Sources journaldugeek.com

How many endings does GTA 6 have? There is no official answer. Journal du Geek reports that player accounts disagree: some count five main endings, others seven once variants are included. Rockstar has not given a number. Spoilers ahead.

How does Mendez's deal change everything?

According to Journal du Geek, the first fork comes in "Protective Custody", after Jason's escape. Facing agent Mendez, you can shoot him or wait about a minute for the option to accept his offer to appear. Accept, and Mendez betrays the couple: arrest, the Leonida swamps, and credits showing two skulls near an alligator. The outlet ranks it among the darkest endings.

A variant: accept the deal, then try to escape during the tower standoff. Either Lucia defends Jason and both die, or she stays down while Jason sacrifices himself. In the second case Lucia survives and reunites with her son, but is broke and back to crime.

What are the "End of the Road" endings?

Refuse the deal and you reach "The End of the Road", on a bridge surrounded by police. With "No Surrender", Jason and Lucia charge the officers and die. With "Swim For It", they try to escape by water, and the result depends on your progress.

If their relationship is developed enough and their criminal profiles stay reasonable, they step out unarmed. Jason lets himself be arrested so Lucia can flee, then sees his son in the prison visiting room. A bittersweet ending.

How do you get the best ending?

The happy version needs $2 million saved in the family BuckMe account: two years later, Lucia uses it to get Jason released. You also need the top "Hot Together" relationship level and clean criminal profiles. Only money deposited in BuckMe counts, not cash on hand. Journal du Geek advises a manual save before the finale, and says you can replay the ending after the credits to see other outcomes without redoing the campaign.

Why it matters

The count is provisional: the game launched last Thursday and more variants may surface. The ending is built during the campaign, not at the last choice. Our release page keeps the official facts.
