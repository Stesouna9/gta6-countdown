GTA 6 Xbox FPS glitch also speeds up gameplay, Pure Xbox reports

Pure Xbox reports accelerated gameplay and roughly 35–40 fps on highways, adding major caveats to the Xbox frame-rate glitch.

Sources purexbox.com

The Xbox frame-rate glitch comes with a significant catch: it speeds up much of the game, according to Pure Xbox. The outlet also says normal demanding gameplay is unlikely to reach 60 frames per second. This adds practical limitations to the two-account report we covered earlier today, shifting the question from whether a counter can rise to whether the game still behaves as intended.

Does an unlocked frame rate mean a proper 60 fps mode?

Not in the experience described by Pure Xbox. Its account says the game can look as though it is running in fast-forward. Faster animation is not evidence that the simulation preserves its intended timing while simply displaying additional frames. A short clip that appears smoother can therefore conceal a change that becomes disruptive during an ordinary play session.

The outlet gives an example of highway driving at approximately 35 to 40 fps at most. It says 60 fps and higher are reserved for scenes with very little graphical demand. These are the publication’s observations, not measurements made by our newsroom. They should not be read as guaranteed results for every console, location, display or particular stage of a player’s session.

An unintended behaviour has its own limits

Pure Xbox attributes the discovery to someone named Meowwie64 and describes an unintended frame-rate unlock. The collected text does not include the complete activation instructions advertised in the original article. We are therefore not reconstructing a sequence from incomplete material or combining unrelated social posts into an untested procedure for readers to follow.

The outlet also warns that using the game this way could introduce other problems because it was not designed for that behaviour. That is a caution rather than a measured failure rate: the available text does not establish how often additional issues occur. It announces neither an official Rockstar performance mode nor a patch. The subject remains a glitch with reported drawbacks, not a supported new graphics setting.

Why it matters

The relevant benchmark is a stable session with the intended timing, not just the highest number a counter briefly shows. Accelerating gameplay to gain frames is a different result from a purpose-built higher-frame-rate mode. Our release information page covers the broader game details. For this particular report, the useful update is Pure Xbox’s description of the limitations, alongside the absence of official validation in the available material.
