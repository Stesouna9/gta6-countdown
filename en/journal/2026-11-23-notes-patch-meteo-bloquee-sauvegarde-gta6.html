GTA 6 patch notes fix stuck weather, autosave and the cops that never left

Rockstar has published the first update's notes: crashes, frozen weather, autosave and phone calls that failed to trigger are fixed.

Sources support.rockstargames.com · x.com · x.com

Rockstar has published the patch notes for the first GTA 6 update on its support page, "Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Updates". According to the alert account gtasix_ on X, the patch fixes several causes of crashes, weather getting stuck for long periods, an autosave issue and phone calls failing to trigger.

What does the GTA 6 update fix?

Four items stand out in the list shared by gtasix_: game crashes, weather frozen for too long, an autosave problem, and calls that never fired, including those needed to progress the story or unlock content. GTAVI_Countdown adds that the notes also cover a bug that made it impossible to lose the cops, a line we could not read in the official text ourselves.

The patch from this morning

Update 01.002.002 has been rolling out in waves since Monday: 600 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox, first reported without patch notes. They have now arrived, hours after the first size readings. We could not read Rockstar's page in full, so the list above may be incomplete.

The police bug matters most to players: a chase that could not be shaken left the game stuck in a permanent wanted state. For how that system works, see our piece on wanted level, heat and witnesses.

Read also GTA 6 update 01.002.002: first patch is 600 MB on PS5, 1.6 GB on Xbox

Why it matters

Anyone who lost progress or got stuck on a mission because of a missing call gets the fix they were waiting for. Check that the patch is installed: the rollout is gradual and not every console is updated at the same time. The exact details of each fix remain to be confirmed on Rockstar's page.
