GTA 6: 40 Knife Flights to complete, total confirmed

The total number of Knife Flights is 40, per IGN and PowerPyx. Exact locations are not published here yet.

Sources ign.com · powerpyx.com

Direct answer: GTA 6 has 40 Knife Flights to complete. IGN and PowerPyx give the same total.

What is confirmed

  • Total: 40 Knife Flights (IGN, page updated 22/11/2026; PowerPyx, 26/11/2026).
  • These are side activities of the stunt type, counted toward the Cunning Stunts trophy according to PowerPyx.

Not yet published

  • The list of the 40 locations has not been checked by two matching sources for each point. It remains to be confirmed.

Tracking your progress

  1. Count the Knife Flights you complete out of the 40 total.
  2. Note your completions in the guide's progress list.
