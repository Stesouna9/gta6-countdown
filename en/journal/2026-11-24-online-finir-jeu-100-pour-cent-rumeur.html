GTA 6 Online: the 100% completion requirement is a satirical claim

Mashable traces the supposed multiplayer entry requirement to a satire account, rather than a Rockstar announcement.

Sources mashable.com

You do not need to treat full GTA 6 completion as an announced ticket to multiplayer. Mashable traces that circulating claim to a satirical X account and says Rockstar has confirmed no such requirement. The distinction matters for anyone considering a rushed completion run simply to avoid being locked out later.

Where did the claim originate?

The post identified by Mashable came from Chaos Crave. It asserted that players would have to complete the story, side missions and collectibles before accessing GTA 6 Online servers. By attributing this supposed requirement to Rockstar, the message made an invented condition look like established release information.

Mashable points out that Chaos Crave describes itself as a satire account. A Community Note attached to the post also identifies that context. Reposts stripped of that background can make the statement seem more credible, but repetition does not supply the missing publisher announcement. The source of the claim remains more useful than the number of accounts repeating it.

Should players hurry to reach 100%?

The material reviewed here gives no reason to change your single-player plans on that basis. Completing optional content can be a personal goal, whether you enjoy side missions, exploring or collecting everything. That is different from a confirmed requirement imposed on everyone who might want to play multiplayer later.

Mashable also says its reporting does not establish when an online version will become available. It would be misleading to replace the invented completion rule with another unsupported promise about launch timing or entry conditions. An unanswered question needs to remain unanswered until there is reliable information, rather than being filled with a more appealing guess.

Why it matters

A satirical post should not dictate how quickly you finish a game or whether you skip the parts you enjoy. The practical check is straightforward: can the alleged requirement be traced to an identifiable Rockstar announcement? In this case, Mashable says it cannot. Our community page and news journal distinguish popular discussion from confirmed information. For now, the circulating 100% claim supplies no confirmed official instruction players need to follow.
