GTA 6 Trailer 2: Jason and Lucia, Vice City and an official website

Retrospective: written in October 2026 from the sources of the time

Rockstar dropped the second GTA VI trailer without warning, captured on a base PS5. The Jason and Lucia couple moves to the front, surrounded by new faces and named locations.

Sources rockstargames.com · latam.ign.com · kotaku.com

Rockstar warned no one. On May 6, 2025, four days after the delay to May 26, 2026, Rockstar Games posted “Watch Trailer 2 Now” with a two-word introduction: “Vice City, USA.” According to IGN Latinoamérica, the footage was captured on a base PlayStation 5, which settles the question of a touched-up render from a workstation. Kotaku notes that it lands more than a year after the first trailer of December 2023.

Two leads and a list of names

IGN Latinoamérica lists what Rockstar puts forward: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos at the center, surrounded by Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. For places, the listing names Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Tisha-Wocka, VC Port, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Delights Cabaret, Ambrosia, the Allied Crystal refinery and Mount Kalaga. Nothing says what each of them does: these are names, not plots yet. I counted them twice, and what I really wonder is which ones will come back in later trailers.

A couple, not a punchline

The angle Kotaku keeps is the relationship. The writer sees a couple that behaves like a real couple: Jason half asleep in front of the TV, Lucia coming home, tossing her keys on the table and collapsing onto him, the pick-up outside prison, hands held. He reminds readers that the series long treated romance as a punchline, from Vice City to GTA III, and that Nico and Kate stayed at the edge of GTA IV. The real question, which he asks himself, is whether Rockstar can keep that promise across a game of thirty to seventy hours. Nobody has the answer, and the trailer does not pretend to give it.

What we know since

May 6 announced no new date: May 26, 2026 stayed on screen, until a second delay. Rockstar eventually set the release for November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preload on November 12, a PEGI 18 rating, €79.99 for the standard edition and €99.99 for the Ultimate. The Goodtime State collector costs €399.99 without the game, and GTA VI: The Album arrives the same November 19. No PC version has been announced.

Why it matters

This trailer set the couple up as GTA VI's central argument, well before prices and editions, and it handed players a first list of places to hunt for on the map. The full calendar is kept up to date on the release page.
