GTA 6: GTA+ subscription costs $7.99 a month, with free transit and one car

PC Gamer reports GTA+ in GTA 6 gives preorderers a free month, then $7.99 monthly for free public transport, discounts and an exclusive car.

Sources pcgamer.com

A subscription inside a singleplayer game: that is what PC Gamer found in the "store" page of GTA 6's main menu. Players who preordered get a free month of GTA+. Everyone else can subscribe for $7.99 a month.

What does GTA+ include in GTA 6?

According to Christopher Livingston, the offer is modest: free public transport, 10% off haircuts and a $10 scooter rental comped. There is also an exclusive car and an extra garage to store it in. With more than 500 vehicles already in the game, he doubts one more coupe will be missed.

He compares it to Bethesda's infamous horse armor, "but you keep paying for it month after month". That is his opinion; Rockstar has not detailed its plans for GTA+ in this game.

What about GTA Online?

PC Gamer says subscribing through GTA 6 also unlocks the GTA+ perks in GTA Online, including $500,000 of in-game cash each month, exclusive cars, mission multipliers and discounts. He expects the offer to grow if GTA 6 ever gets an online component, which Rockstar has not announced.

For context, the game already costs €79.99 standard. GTA+ comes on top and is optional.

Why it matters

Nothing is mandatory: you can finish the story without paying more. But the subscription shows Rockstar putting recurring monetisation in place from day one.
