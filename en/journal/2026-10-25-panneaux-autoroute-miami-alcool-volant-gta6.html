GTA 6 Miami highway signs warn "Don't Drink & Drive" a month before launch

Digital highway signs in Miami pair a drink-driving warning with a GTA 6 release reminder, The Source reports.

Sources thesource.com

J-25 GTA 6 releases in 25 days →

Digital highway signs in Miami read "Don't Drink & Drive. GTA 6 Is A Month Away," The Source reported on 24 October. The message pairs a road safety warning with a reminder of the 19 November 2026 release.

What do the Miami signs say?

According to The Source, the city's electronic signs combine a drink-driving warning with the game's release date. The outlet relies on a video posted on X and quotes no statement from Rockstar Games or Florida authorities, so who is behind the campaign is not stated.

The same piece says New York is getting attention too, with billboards across Manhattan and a large mural taking shape in Brooklyn, plus a TV spot built from trailer footage.

A campaign that leaves the screen

Vice City has already spilled into the real world before. We covered the estimated $500 million marketing budget and the giant Bagnolet poster with its weapons erased. Here the trick is borrowing an official format, the road information sign, to talk about a game where you drive, and badly.

Read also GTA 6: Burger Shot is taking over every Skinny Louie in Florida in November

The schedule is unchanged: release on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, preload on 12 November. See our release page for the countdown.

What remains unconfirmed

Nothing says how many signs are involved or how long they will stay. The Source relies on a social media video, not a press release. It also repeats player speculation about police, fuel and weather systems that Rockstar has not confirmed.

Why it matters

Nothing changes in the game. For the brand, it is a way to remind millions of drivers a month out that GTA 6 is coming, with a message nobody can fault. A month before launch, advertising is on the road.
