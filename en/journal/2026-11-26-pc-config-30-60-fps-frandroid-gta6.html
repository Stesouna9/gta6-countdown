GTA 6 PC requirements: what 30 and 60 FPS would take, per Frandroid

Frandroid estimates a PC costing around 2,000 euros could match the PS5 at 30 FPS, while 60 FPS would need far more, with the CPU the weak spot. No PC version is announced.

Sources frandroid.com

There is no GTA 6 on PC. Rockstar has announced neither a version nor a date, and the game launched on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Frandroid still ran the numbers: what machine would match the console, or double its frame rate? It is an estimate, not an official spec sheet.

What does the PS5 deliver today?

According to Frandroid, the console renders at 1440p and holds 30 frames per second, with dips into the mid-20s during big explosions per Digital Foundry's November 18 test. Chinese channel Geekerwan measured as low as 23 frames at the worst moment. There is no 60 FPS mode. Digital Foundry places the PS5 at about an RTX 4060 in console-matched settings.

Which graphics card for 30 FPS?

The outlet keeps console settings, ray tracing included. Its central estimate calls for a Radeon RX 9060 XT 16 GB or an RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB, at QHD or at 4K upscaled with DLSS or FSR. A complete PC, case, power supply and Windows included, would cost roughly 1,950 to 2,295 euros with AMD and 2,100 to 2,550 euros with NVIDIA. Video memory matters: with ray tracing, an 8 GB card is held back, hence the 16 GB pick.

Why is 60 FPS a problem?

At 60 frames per second the processor becomes the main obstacle, even with a capable graphics card. Frandroid also notes the pricing backdrop is poor, amid a memory shortage. It cites an unconfirmed leak reported by MP1st claiming PS5 and Xbox Series X run the same settings, mostly Medium and High. A PC set higher than the console would likely need more.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for players today: without an official version, these figures are orders of magnitude. They do hint at the budget to plan if Rockstar ever announced GTA 6 on PC, which it has not. For now, console is the only way to play.
