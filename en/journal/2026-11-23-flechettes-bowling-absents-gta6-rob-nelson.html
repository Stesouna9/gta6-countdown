GTA 6 darts missing: Rockstar's Rob Nelson explains why they were cut

Rob Nelson of Rockstar North told VGC darts could not be polished in time, Numerama reports: better not to do it than to do it badly.

Sources numerama.com

Darts are not in GTA 6, and Rockstar says why. According to Numerama, citing a Video Games Chronicle interview on November 19, Rob Nelson, co-director of Rockstar North and one of the game's three creative directors, points to time and quality.

Which mini-games are missing?

Numerama notes the game already offers plenty: weightlifting, motocross races, video games, pool, diving, fishing. But darts, present in GTA 4, GTA 5 and GTA Online, are gone, as is GTA 4's bowling.

What does Rob Nelson say?

Per Numerama, Nelson says each mini-game has to reach a high standard of gameplay feel. He uses weightlifting as an example: "You're not just pressing a button; it replicates the feeling of lifting weights." A legacy of Red Dead Redemption 2, where mini-games became activities, the outlet says. On darts, he admits: "It takes a lot of work to get the settings right. Darts is something we simply couldn't get to." He adds: "Better not to do it than to do it and have it fall short."

He also concedes even Rockstar has neither unlimited time nor budget: "Sometimes you simply have to admit we can't reach the standard we think is needed." The same attention to detail shows elsewhere: we reported that Rockstar spent months on Lucia opening a door with her elbow, and that fuel is now mandatory.

Why it matters

Nothing says darts or bowling will return: Numerama reports no such promise and we do not invent one. It mostly confirms the studio's method: fewer activities, but polished. Launch information is on the release page.
