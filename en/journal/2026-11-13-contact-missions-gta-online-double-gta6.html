GTA Online: 2X GTA$ and RP on Contact Missions, 4X with GTA+

Rockstar announced on X a 2X GTA$ and RP bonus on GTA Online Contact Missions, reaching 4X for GTA+ Members. The post does not give the duration.

Sources x.com

J-5 GTA 6 releases in 5 days →

Rockstar is running a week of bonuses on GTA Online Contact Missions: the studio announced in a post on X 2X GTA$ and RP, stacking to 4X for GTA+ Members. The post, published at 2 p.m. Paris time, passed 237,000 views.

What does Rockstar say about Contact Missions?

The message is one sentence: "Celebrate GTA Online's colorful cast of supporting characters with 2X GTA$ and RP on Contact Missions (stacking to 4X for GTA+ Members)". The studio spotlights the supporting characters who hand out these missions. The bonus is 2X for everyone and reaches 4X with a GTA+ subscription.

What we don't know yet

The post links to a Rockstar Newswire page whose full content could not be read at the time of writing. The exact length of the event and the list of eligible missions are not in the post, so we do not state them. This article will be updated once the Newswire gives details.

What about GTA 6?

This concerns GTA Online, not GTA 6. It lands six days before the November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, as Rockstar keeps its current online game active. Nothing about GTA 6's online mode comes with this bonus. For the rest of the schedule, see the release page.

Why it matters

For GTA Online players, it is a good week to chain Contact Missions, especially with GTA+. For GTA 6 fans, it mainly shows the studio keeping its weekly event rhythm up to launch.
