GTA mobile ports hit 2.3 million downloads after GTA 6, per AppMagic

The older GTA games on mobile logged 2.3 million downloads in a week, up from 590,000 before GTA 6 launched, according to AppMagic cited by GamesIndustry.biz.

Sources gamesindustry.biz

GTA 6's launch is lifting the older entries too. According to AppMagic data relayed by GamesIndustry.biz, the series' mobile ports totaled 2.3 million downloads between November 16 and 22, up from 590,000 the prior period.

Which mobile game gained the most?

GTA: Vice City – Definitive Edition leads with over 1.1 million installs. The series' mobile record still dates to December 2023, when the trilogy's arrival on Netflix reached eight million downloads between December 11 and 17. The GTA 6 effect is clear but does not match that peak.

What about Steam?

AppMagic also tracks older GTA games on PC. Daily active users of GTA 5 Enhanced Edition rose from 407,000 to 718,000 between November 16 and 21. GTA Legacy went from 293,000 to 494,000, and GTA 4 The Complete Edition from 68,000 to 115,000, the biggest rise for a GTA title on Steam since tracking began in 2024.

Other apps gained too: the PlayStation App reached 1.3 million downloads, more than double the previous week, and Twitch doubled to 1.3 million installs. PS Remote Play and the Xbox app also rose.

What do the console figures say?

GamesIndustry.biz recalls that GTA 6 is the biggest launch in PlayStation history, with over 39 million unique players and 683 million hours played, or 17.4 hours per player. It was reportedly the first game played on 70% of new PS5 consoles. Rockstar had announced 32.5 million pre-orders and $3.79 billion in day-one sales. Those launch figures come from Sony and Rockstar, not AppMagic.

Why it matters

GTA 6 wakes up the whole catalog: players discovering Leonida go back to Vice City, GTA 4 and GTA 5. For Rockstar it is a halo effect at no extra cost. These numbers are a research firm's estimates, not data published by the publisher.
