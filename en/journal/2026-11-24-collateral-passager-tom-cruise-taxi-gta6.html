GTA 6: the taxi encounter that echoes Collateral and Tom Cruise

While driving a taxi, an armed passenger who evokes Collateral can demand a ride to Harborside Park, according to Polygon.

Sources polygon.com

One of GTA 6's strangest random encounters happens while you offer taxi services. Polygon describes it: a bearded man in a dark suit and sunglasses climbs into the back seat with a gun. It plays out the same whether you are Jason or Lucia.

What does the passenger want?

Per Polygon, he wants you to run a red light, ignore phone calls and drive him to Harborside Park in Vice Beach. His calm but forceful voice sounds a lot like Tom Cruise, almost sociopathic. Several clips are already online and many see an obvious nod to Collateral, Michael Mann's 2004 thriller about a hitman who takes a cab driver hostage.

What do you get at the end?

Polygon says the player gets a better deal than the film's Max: if you take the stranger to his destination and follow him in after he takes out some of the criminals, you can find plenty of loot. Most players seem keen on eliminating him rather than keep driving.

Where to watch Collateral?

According to Polygon, the film streams for free on Pluto TV; availability by country is not specified, so check your region. The nod adds to the cultural references players keep spotting, like in our Red Dead universe clues. More on our journal page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for progression, but the scene shows Rockstar tucks many film tributes into its random encounters. As far as we know, Rockstar has said nothing about the reference: it remains a reading by players and Polygon.
