GTA 6: a pink crystal-covered “Vice City” Birkin bag is going viral

Moneybags x MB and Privé Porter customised an Hermès Birkin in Vice City colours, according to GQ France. One-off piece, no official link to Rockstar or Hermès.

Sources gqmagazine.fr

The game is a few days old and it already has its luxury bag. GQ France reports that Moneybags x MB, with reseller Privé Porter, customised an Hermès Birkin named “Vice City”, which the magazine says looks straight out of the new game.

What does the “Vice City” Birkin look like?

It starts from the classic pink Birkin and keeps its shape. Everything else changes: every outer panel is covered in hand-set crystals that sparkle with the light. It is the same pink-and-neon palette we have seen in the goodies.

The contrast is the joke: a decades-old symbol of exclusivity next to a series that satirises wealth, consumption and status.

Is it an official product?

No. GQ France states it is not a collaboration between Hermès and Rockstar Games. Moneybags x MB and Privé Porter customised an existing Hermès model. It is presented as a one-off; no price or release date has been given for further pieces, and the magazine doubts there will be any since Hermès does not offer this kind of customisation.
