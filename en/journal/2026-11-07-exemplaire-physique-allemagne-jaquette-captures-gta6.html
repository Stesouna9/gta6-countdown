GTA 6: first physical copy reportedly spotted in Germany, new screenshots on the back

According to the X account GTAVI_Countdown, a GTA 6 box was photographed in Germany, with new screenshots on the back cover. Rockstar has published nothing.

Sources x.com · x.com · x.com

J-11 GTA 6 releases in 11 days →

A GTA 6 box has surfaced online. According to a post by GTAVI_Countdown published at 2:01 p.m. UK time, the first official physical copy was spotted in Germany, with new screenshots on the back of the cover. The post, seen over 168,000 times, credits a Reddit user (IseeIceTea); we could not independently verify the photo or the location.

The account gtasix_ posted a "first look" at the back of the box within seconds, then mentioned new screenshots on that side. Two separate X accounts are relaying the same find, but no outlet and no Rockstar statement backs it yet. Treat it as community information, not official.

What does the back cover show?

According to the posts, previously unseen screenshots appear there, as is usual on a game box. We could not compare them with visuals Rockstar has already released, so we do not describe them. In another post, gtasix_ claims a "PS5 Pro Optimized" mention is printed on the back; that is one account's reading, which we cannot verify. PlayStation has not detailed any PS5 Pro enhancements for the game so far.

Why is a box showing up now?

Boxed versions are due at retailers before the November 19 launch, with pre-loading opening on November 12. Our piece on Rockstar's clarification on the physical edition noted that the box holds a download code, not a disc. A box seen in Germany a little over a week before launch is unsurprising: stock is going to stores. The same day, retailer MediaMarkt showed the double-sided poster tied to the physical edition.

Why it matters

For buyers nothing changes on substance: price and date stay as on our price page. What changes is that back-cover visuals are starting to circulate before any official release. We are waiting for a clear photo of the box, or an identified outlet, before detailing it; the original X post also carries an Amazon affiliate link flagged #ad, a reminder of its commercial interest.
