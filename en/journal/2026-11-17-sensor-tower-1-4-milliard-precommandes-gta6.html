GTA 6 pre-orders top $1.4 billion, Sensor Tower estimates, 87% Ultimate

Sensor Tower estimates GTA 6 pre-orders at more than $1.4 billion, 87% of them for the Ultimate Edition; Rockstar has confirmed nothing.

Sources gamesindustry.biz · mezha.net

GTA 6 pre-orders have passed $1.4 billion, according to a new Sensor Tower estimate reported by GamesIndustry.biz. In a report on the game's impact on player engagement, the firm says 87% of orders are for the Ultimate Edition. Rockstar has not published any figure.

What does the new Sensor Tower report say?

The firm puts pre-order revenue on PlayStation and Xbox above $1.4 billion. On Friday its tracker showed $1.18 billion for 12.13 million copies, as we covered in our November 13 article. That is more than $200 million added in four days, two days before launch.

According to GamesIndustry.biz, daily revenue ranged from $2.4 million to $3.8 million through most of August. It rose sixfold after Netflix's extended look on August 27, reaching $16.3 million the next day, and keeps climbing. GTA 6 is now the most pre-ordered game on PlayStation and Xbox storefronts.

Why 87% for the Ultimate Edition?

The Ultimate Edition, $99.99 and digital only, therefore makes up most orders against the $79.99 Standard Edition. Sensor Tower does not break down its method: these are estimates, not confirmed sales. See our price page for editions.

Which games could lose players?

The report also looks at exposure. Fortnite, GTA Online and other big single-player games should be hit hardest. On PlayStation, 42% of GTA 5 owners also own Fortnite, against 22% of all players. Roblox should hold up better, its audience being "less likely to be lured away by acclaimed single-player launches".

Why it matters

Nothing changes for players, but real sales will soon replace these estimates. Until Rockstar or Take-Two report, $1.4 billion is an outside projection, not an official tally. Release details are on our release page.
