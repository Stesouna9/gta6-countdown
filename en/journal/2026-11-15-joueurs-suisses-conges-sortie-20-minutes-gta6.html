GTA 6 launch: Swiss players take days off for the 19 November release

Per 20 Minuten, Swiss players have booked up to two weeks off for GTA 6 on 19 November; others plan to call in sick.

Sources 20min.ch

J-4 GTA 6 releases in 4 days →

Swiss players are taking time off for GTA 6, according to a report by 20 Minuten published on 14 November. The paper collected accounts from readers aged 16 to 36 who are building their week around the 19 November launch.

Bahra, 18, booked a full week off. Her employer was "rather shocked and surprised" at the reason. Luca, 29, an IT support specialist, took two weeks; his boss reportedly burst out laughing before agreeing. Valton, 26, booked two days six months ago.

Who managed to get time off for GTA 6?

Not everyone did. Leonie, 16, had her week refused and will leave early each day. Lorenz plans to call in sick. As the game is rated PEGI 18, Leonie says she will have her mother buy it. These are individual accounts we cannot verify.

How are players preparing for the night of 18 to 19 November?

Jennifer, 28, and her partner want to replay GTA 5 on two TVs the night before, then play through the night. Luca plans to sleep after 18 November and wake at 11 pm for a "pre-release sleep". Valton wrote his partner a joke contract: calls for emergencies only, attention once a mission is over.

Rockstar has not announced the exact unlock time. Our release page sums up the calendar; preloading has been open since 12 November for digital buyers. See also our piece on the 1.5 million possible absentees in the US.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game, but the report shows the launch spilling into workplaces and relationships. These are reader accounts from a single outlet, not a measure of absenteeism in Switzerland.
