GTA 6 Xbox preload files point to FSR 4.1, but support remains unconfirmed

Windows Central reports two FidelityFX libraries in GTA 6’s Xbox preload. Their names do not establish whether FSR 4.1 will be active at launch.

Sources windowscentral.com · notebookcheck.net

J-7 GTA 6 releases in 7 days →

Two reported library names have opened another question about GTA 6’s Xbox image quality. According to Windows Central, files attributed to the console preload share names with libraries in Microsoft’s FSR 4.1 development sample. That connection offers something specific to investigate, but Rockstar has not confirmed that this version of the technology will be used in the released game.

Which files were reportedly found?

Windows Central identifies amd_fidelityfx_loader_xbox.dll and amd_fidelityfx_upscaler_xbox.dll. It credits Tex2 with finding the files and describes blue nugroho and Y1ng connecting their names to Microsoft’s examples. This article reports that account; we have not independently examined the Xbox package or established how the libraries are called during gameplay.

The distinction is important because a file included in a download does not, by its name alone, demonstrate an enabled feature. Windows Central explicitly warns that the discovery does not guarantee FSR 4.1 at launch. It also supplies no measured resolution increase, frame-rate improvement or player-facing setting established through testing of the released game.

Has Rockstar announced a later update?

No update schedule is established by this reporting. Windows Central considers a post-launch integration possible, while presenting that timing as the writer’s assessment. Notebookcheck also discusses the Xbox preload as a potential clue. A second publication examining the same discovery is further coverage, rather than independent confirmation that the feature works in the game.

It would therefore be premature to turn the file names into a promise of 60 frames per second or a settled comparison between platforms. Our report on announced console performance targets covers the separate baseline. The earlier PS5 Pro preload clues concern another platform and should not be treated as proof of an Xbox implementation.

Why it matters

Xbox Series X and Series S players now have a concrete question to watch in future developer explanations and image-quality testing. They do not yet have a confirmed upgrade, a release date for one or a measured benefit from these two libraries. Until Rockstar explains the implementation or testing establishes it, conditional language remains appropriate. Our GTA 6 release page tracks launch information without turning a promising file discovery into a guaranteed feature.
