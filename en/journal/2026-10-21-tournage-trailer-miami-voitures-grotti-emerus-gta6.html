GTA 6: crews film GTA-style Grotti and Emerus cars in Miami, a live-action trailer?

Fans filmed GTA-themed car convoys and camera crews in Miami. Rockstar has said nothing, so a live-action trailer remains speculation.

Sources ign.com

Miami looks more like Vice City every week, and someone is filming. IGN reports fans posting videos of sports car convoys, sometimes followed by a camera crew. The cars are modified to look like the series' vehicles, with Grotti (a Ferrari nod) and Emerus (a McLaren nod) branding visible.

What do the clips show?

Posts quoted by IGN mention filming of a separate video or trailer and a Rockstar promo with West Coast Customs in Key Biscayne. Fans link it to "Vice City" graffiti and a "Welcome to Vice City" sign on a stadium, and theorise Rockstar is shooting in real settings with real ads and lookalike plates. IGN frames this as a widespread belief online, not a confirmed fact.

What is actually established?

Very little. Rockstar has not commented. What is certain is the scale of the campaign: the Vice City blimp, the fake ads and a Vice City-themed NBA game with the Miami Heat in November, per IGN. Fans guess the latest trailer came early to leave room for a live-action video, perhaps on pre-load day. Nothing proves it.
