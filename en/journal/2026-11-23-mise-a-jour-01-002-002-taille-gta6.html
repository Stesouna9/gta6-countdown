GTA 6 update 01.002.002: first patch is 600 MB on PS5, 1.6 GB on Xbox

Rockstar is rolling out the first GTA 6 update in waves: 603.8 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox Series, with no official patch notes yet.

Sources big-n.fr · insider-gaming.com · x.com

Rockstar has rolled out the first GTA 6 update, version 01.002.002 on PS5, four days after launch. According to Insider Gaming and big-n.fr, it arrives in waves and focuses on fixes, with no new content.

How big is GTA 6 update 01.002.002?

On PS5 it weighs a little over 600 MB: 603.8 MB according to the alert account gtasix_ on X, 603 MB per PlayStation Size. On Xbox Series X|S, version 1.0.2.2 is 1.6 GB according to the same accounts, more than double. Insider Gaming confirms the gap between platforms. Nothing here should force you to clear space.

Who can download it, and when?

The rollout is gradual. PlayStation Size said 33% of players had access on Monday morning, 66% three hours later and everyone after about six hours. If the patch is not showing up for you yet, wait a few hours. Rockstar staggers releases to protect its servers and catch any problem before it reaches everyone.

What does it fix?

That is not known yet. Neither Insider Gaming nor big-n.fr has official patch notes, and Rockstar had not published any at the time of writing. Insider Gaming notes that the studio does not always detail small updates. Players mostly hope for an end to the crashes and progression blockers reported since November 19, including PS5 error code CE-108255-1 cited by big-n.fr. Nothing says this patch addresses them: it is a community expectation, not a Rockstar promise.

This first fix follows the most lucrative launch in games, as we reported in our piece on the $3.79 billion figure announced by Rockstar. A game this size is bound to be patched in its first weeks. Release details are on our release page.

Why it matters

For you, very little: start the download, wait a few minutes, relaunch. The real signal is elsewhere. A first update four days in, with different version numbers per console, shows Rockstar is fixing continuously. If patch notes land, they will say whether the reported blockers are handled. We will relay them as soon as they appear.
