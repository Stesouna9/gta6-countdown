GTA 6: Manni L. Perez, the voice of Lucia, nominated for Best Performance at The Game Awards

The Game Awards officially confirm Manni L. Perez is in GTA 6: the actress linked to Lucia for three years is nominated for Best Performance.

Sources thegameawards.com · thegameawards.com

J-1 GTA 6 releases in 1 days →

It is the first official confirmation: Manni L. Perez is part of the GTA 6 cast. The Game Awards nominated her on November 17 for Best Performance, credited for "Grand Theft Auto VI". Rockstar had never named the actors behind Lucia and Jason.

Does she really play Lucia?

The official listing names the game, not the character. But Manni L. Perez has been linked to Lucia since the first trailer in December 2023, and GTA 6 has only two leads, Lucia and Jason: a lead performance nomination leaves little doubt. No actor for Jason appears on the list.

Who is Manni L. Perez?

An American actress of Dominican descent, she has appeared in Law and Order: SVU, Chicago P.D. and Jessica Jones. Best Performance rewards voice acting, motion capture or performance capture.

Who is she up against?

Angela Sant'Albano (Resident Evil Requiem), Liam McIntyre (Marvel's Wolverine), Patrick Gibson (007 First Light) and Rahul Kohli (Saros). Winners are revealed on December 10 in Los Angeles. GTA 6 has 8 nominations in total, including Game of the Year: all the nominations.
