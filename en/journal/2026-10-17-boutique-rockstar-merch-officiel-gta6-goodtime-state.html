GTA 6 official merch collection goes live on the Rockstar Store

Rockstar's GTA 6 merch is on sale at the Rockstar Store: tees, a cap, a Zippo, album vinyl and a $400 collector's box.

Sources mashable.com

J-33 GTA 6 releases in 33 days →

The Rockstar Store has opened the official GTA 6 merchandise collection, Mashable reports. Neon pink, ocean blue and palm trees, across clothing, accessories and collectibles.

What is in the collection?

According to Mashable, the white Macca the Gator tee puts Leonida's TV character on heavyweight cotton, inspired by outfits worn by Jason and Lucia. There is also a blue cap with an embroidered pink VI palm logo, a pink and blue canvas tote with a laptop sleeve, and a polished-chrome Vice City Zippo.

Entry prices are low: holographic stickers at $4.99, a cropped logo tee at $29.99. Our own shop tracker lists several Goodtime State tees at $29.99, some already sold out.

What about the music?

The album is here too. Mashable lists Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album on CD ($19.98 preorder), standard vinyl ($49.98) and an exclusive two-disc transparent magenta vinyl with blue splatter. The 34 tracks include Travis Scott, Yung Lean and Morgan Wallen. See our music page.

Is the $400 box worth it?

The top item is The Goodtime State, Vice City Collection. Mashable lists an official Macca the Gator figure, Oakley Frogskins, a New Era snapback, a double-sided souvenir map poster and display pieces. It does not include the game. Details on our goodies page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game itself: price, date and editions stay as announced. But Rockstar is building Leonida's look a month before launch, and limited stock goes fast, as the already sold-out Macca tee shows.
