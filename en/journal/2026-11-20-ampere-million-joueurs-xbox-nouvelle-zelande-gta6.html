GTA 6: over a million Xbox players got in early via New Zealand, Ampere says

Ampere Analysis says more than one million Xbox players in the US and UK launched GTA 6 before the official time by switching their console region to New Zealand.

Sources gamesindustry.biz

All they did was change their console's country. According to Ampere Analysis data reported by GamesIndustry.biz, more than a million Xbox players in the UK and US accessed GTA 6 before its official launch by setting their region to New Zealand.

How many players got GTA 6 early?

Ampere counts 1.5 million players in the US and 600,000 in the UK on Xbox. On PlayStation 5, the same trick was used by 100,000 Americans and 25,000 Britons, per GamesIndustry.biz. The gap comes down to how the two consoles work.

Why New Zealand?

GTA 6 launched at midnight local time on November 19, and New Zealand was the first place to get it. On Xbox, players only had to change the console region, restart and launch the game hours ahead of everyone else, from November 18. On PS5, an account's country is locked at creation: players needed a New Zealand account and had to buy the game on that country's PlayStation Store.

The principle is not new, but the scale is, for a game this size. We already covered Sensor Tower's estimate of 24.58 million copies; these early accesses sit inside that first day, and it is unclear in what proportion.

What Newzoo says about a console-only launch

GamesIndustry.biz also quotes Newzoo. Consoles were expected to account for only 22% of global games revenue in 2026. For Emmanuel Rosier, director of market intelligence, GTA 6 is "undoubtedly one of the most important releases of the decade", but its initial impact "will be concentrated in Western consoles". That market would be worth $36.3 billion in 2026, and "the longer-term global story will depend on what happens next, particularly on PC and online".

On PC, nothing is official: Rockstar has announced no version. The multiplayer timeline is also unknown.

Why it matters

These numbers show that a notable share of Xbox players did not wait for midnight at home. They say nothing about any risk to the game or accounts: Ampere describes use of the region setting, not a flaw. If Rockstar or Microsoft react, it will come through their official channels.
