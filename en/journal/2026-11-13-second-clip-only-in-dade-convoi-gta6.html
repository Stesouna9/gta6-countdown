GTA 6: second Only in Dade clip shows a police-escorted luxury convoy

A second Only in Dade clip shot like phone footage shows a luxury car convoy near the Sahara Arena, per Notebookcheck; Rockstar has not confirmed it.

Sources notebookcheck.net

J-5 GTA 6 releases in 5 days →

A second clip that looks like smartphone footage has been circulating since this morning, and many players first took it for a real scene from Miami. According to Notebookcheck, it shows a convoy of sports cars and customised classics rounding a corner outside the Sahara Arena, led and followed by black police cars. Rockstar has not confirmed the footage comes from GTA 6.

What does the second clip show?

Per Notebookcheck, the video appeared on the Instagram account Only in Dade. It shows several sports cars and custom classics escorted by black police vehicles at the front and rear. The police sports car from the first video is among them, along with the blue classic and the colourful cars seen before.

Why does it look like real footage?

Notebookcheck says many Reddit users thought it was real or at least AI-generated. It points to a slightly shaky camera, lighting and framing that give it an amateur feel. In the first clip, passers-by also sold the illusion: some stop to film the cars, others walk, jog or record their own social videos. Notebookcheck ties this to the animations described in the preview of The Guardian's feature, covered in our piece on Rockstar's Miami scouting.

Has Rockstar confirmed it?

Not for this one. Notebookcheck notes the studio has not officially confirmed the footage is from GTA 6, but it shared the first video in its Instagram Story, as we reported on 11 November. That has not happened for the second clip so far. The outlet adds that few community members doubt its origin, so for now it is a strong presumption, not a fact.

Why it matters

Nothing changes about the game's content, but the campaign continues six days before the 19 November launch: Rockstar lets clips circulate that blur the line between capture and reality. Watch for official accounts sharing the second clip. More in the journal.
