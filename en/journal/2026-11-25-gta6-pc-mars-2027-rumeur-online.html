GTA 6 PC release in March 2027: an unproven rumor, nothing official

A leak relayed by HardwareCooking and GameGPU claims GTA 6 on PC and GTA 6 Online in March 2027. Rockstar and Take-Two confirmed nothing, and the method cannot be verified.

Sources hardwarecooking.fr · shattered.io

The question everyone is typing: is GTA 6 coming to PC in March 2027? Short answer: no announcement says so. HardwareCooking relays a leak from an X account claiming that date for both a PC version and a future GTA 6 Online, while stressing that neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed anything.

Where does the March 2027 date come from?

According to HardwareCooking, the poster says they analysed Take-Two’s fiscal 2027 financial documents with an analysis tool, without providing proof. Shattered covers the same trail: GameGPU, a graphics-hardware outlet, published the claim, which starts from an alleged anomaly in the $8 billion revenue forecast tied to online revenue before March 31, 2027, the end of the fiscal year. That is an inference, not an inside leak.

HardwareCooking notes the figures cited prove little: the $8 billion matches the $8.0 to $8.2 billion range Take-Two already published.

What do we actually know about GTA 6 on PC?

The confirmed part is short: the game launched on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the official page does not mention PC. No date, no price, no requirements. Our Release page keeps track.

The content attributed to the online mode (a permanent character, co-op heists, roleplay servers built with the FiveM team) has never been announced by Rockstar either. On the unofficial-port front, see the Xbox build starting on PC and the first reported 3D renders.

Why it matters

Nothing, until Rockstar speaks. PC players have no reason to rush a hardware purchase on a date deduced from accounting tables. We will update as soon as an official announcement lands.
