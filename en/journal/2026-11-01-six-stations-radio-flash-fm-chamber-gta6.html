GTA 6 radio stations: six teased, from Flash FM to The Chamber 106.6

Rockstar posted twelve minutes of previews for six GTA 6 radio stations: Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBANK FM, The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM and Dirty South Classics.

Sources pcgamer.com · big-n.fr

Six GTA 6 radio stations can already be heard. Rockstar presented them on its website with short audio previews, and PC Gamer listened to all twelve minutes of hosts and songs: Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBANK FM, The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM and Dirty South Classics. The total number of stations is not known.

Which six GTA 6 radio stations are confirmed?

According to PC Gamer, Cocoteo FM is the Latin station, with Bad Bunny on the list. Back Country Radio plays classic country and AfroBANK FM African music. The Chamber 106.6 is the metal station, where the magazine names Black Sabbath, Pantera and Slayer. Dirty South Classics covers Southern hip-hop, and Flash FM pop. French site big-n.fr describes the hip-hop as classics from the late 90s and early 2000s.

The Flash FM name will ring a bell for Vice City players: it was the 80s pop station. It returns with a modern sound. big-n.fr cites Charli XCX and PinkPantheress, along with MGMT's "Electric Feel" (2007), which PC Gamer counts among its favorites in the preview.

Who hosts Flash FM?

The station is hosted by Alex Consani, a model and social media figure, and by Swedish singer Robyn, known for "Bulletproof". PC Gamer notes that Rockstar seems to be pairing hosts in duos on every station, which sets up constant banter, and regrets that the previews show very little interaction between them. The series' usual satire is still to be confirmed at launch.

Another first, noted by PC Gamer: GTA 6 will be the first entry with both music stations and podcasts. Rockstar has not detailed their content, and nothing says how many there will be.

Why it matters

For the first time, GTA 6 radio can be judged on actual material, seventeen days before the November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The range is wide, from metal to country, pop and Afrobeat, as in earlier GTA games. What remains unknown is how much of the soundtrack is licensed versus Justice's original score, due December 11, or GTA VI: The Album with its 34 tracks, out on launch day.
