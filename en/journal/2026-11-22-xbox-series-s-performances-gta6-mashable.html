GTA 6 on Xbox Series S: smooth with no slowdown, says Mashable

After a few hours, Mashable calls GTA 6 smooth on Xbox Series S, with no stutter or lag, but has not yet compared it with the PS5 Pro.

Sources mashable.com

Does GTA 6 run well on Xbox Series S? Yes, says Mashable after a few hours: "smooth as butter", with no stutter or lag. Writer Eric Miller had read Reddit worries about crowd density and graphical cuts before buying it on Microsoft's entry console.

What did Mashable test?

The Jason and Lucia prologue by day, night and rain, then free roaming: driving through busy streets, a sunset, swimming among fish, tasks around Jason's home. Mashable saw nothing that broke the AAA feel, but cannot say how it compares with PS5 or stronger hardware. A comparison with the PS5 Pro is under way.

What has Rockstar said about performance?

Known facts: Rockstar says the game runs best on PlayStation, with faster loads and tech that may improve resolution, and the PS5 Pro adds ray tracing. Both are locked at 30 fps. The studio has not said what other trade-offs the Series S needed.

On Xbox, two players had reported a 60 fps frequency bug; editions are on our price page.

Why it matters

One tester, no measurements: reassuring, but not a benchmark. Side-by-side console comparisons will show what the Series S gives up.
