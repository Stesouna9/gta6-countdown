GTA 6 Kitty Litter: VGC maps 20 labs and explains the entry clues

VGC’s guide lists 20 Kitty Litter laboratories and the clues around their entrances. The activity opens during chapter 3.

Sources videogameschronicle.com

A new VGC guide maps 20 Kitty Litter laboratories in GTA 6 and describes how to recognise their entrances. Its useful contribution is a search method: nearby objects and building details can point players towards an activity that is easy to overlook while driving past. The following details reveal the setup of a chapter-three side activity without discussing its story outcome.

When does the activity become available?

VGC says players first need to complete Here, Kitty Kitty in chapter three. The activity involves Jason, Tyrone and Dook. That requirement helps distinguish noticing a suspicious building from having the relevant objective available. Seeing a possible location earlier does not by itself establish that the activity can already be completed.

The total of 20 is the count mapped by VGC’s guide. It is being attributed to that publication, rather than described as a new Rockstar announcement. For anyone organising a route, the guide provides a documented starting point. It does not justify adding rewards, completion conditions or other details that the consulted material does not establish.

Which clues help identify the buildings?

The guide highlights purple smoke, Spin On This laundry vans and purple cat graffiti. Those details encourage a closer look at the surroundings of a building. A parked van can be a local clue, while visible smoke can help distinguish one structure from several nearby properties. The useful habit is to inspect the setting before assuming the entrance is directly ahead.

VGC describes a mixture of access routes, including open windows, apartment stairs, individual doors and garage shutters. A recognisable clue therefore does not necessarily lead to a convenient street-level entrance. Looking around the side of a property or checking an upper floor fits the guide’s descriptions better than repeatedly approaching the same closed doorway.

Why it matters

Kitty Litter offers another reason to read Vice City’s street furniture and building fronts carefully. Our recent coverage of Knife Flights and NoFlusher Tags concerns other activities, each with its own search logic. The news journal brings those references together. Here, the practical sequence is to check the mission requirement first, then use the visual clues to identify the access route. Finding a promising building and recording completed progress remain separate steps.
