GTA 6: full Leonida map from the collector box leaks on Instagram

A since-deleted Instagram reel showed the collector box poster unfolded, per Notebookcheck: Goose Key, Pelican Valley and toll roads appear.

Sources notebookcheck.biz

J-5 GTA 6 releases in 5 days →

The Leonida map is out of its folds. According to Notebookcheck, an Instagram reel, since deleted, showed the poster from the "The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection" collector box fully unfolded. It is the most complete look at the GTA 6 map so far, six days before release.

The account behind the leak, Tex2 according to the outlet, unboxed the €399.99 set and filmed the poster. It is not entirely new: the box displayed at Selfridges in Manchester had already revealed part of it, but folded, as we reported a day ago.

What does the map reveal?

Notebookcheck notes that most regions and place names are now readable, apart from a few edge areas such as Leonida Keys. Lummox County sits in the north; Moreau further on; in the south, Goose Key, a destination never announced before, and Pelican Valley in Grassrivers.

The map also shows toll roads. The Netflix "Extended Look" trailer hinted at tolls, and the printed document appears to confirm it. Its cartoon style also includes a blimp icon, an aircraft seen in American skies.

Why is the map limited to the collector edition?

Players hoped for a map in standard boxes, as with GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2. That is not the case: retail copies contain a double-sided poster with the cover art and the Bikini Girl already seen. According to Notebookcheck, a downloadable digital map will be offered on release day or shortly after. Rockstar has not given an exact date in the material we have.

The context matters: the Discord of the GTA 6 Mapping Project, which rebuilt the map from screenshots and trailers, disappeared this week. Notebookcheck mentions a possible DMCA notice from Take-Two or Rockstar, without the cause being confirmed.

Why it matters

For collector buyers, nothing changes: they have the map. For everyone else, the leak eases some curiosity before November 19, but it is only a view of a deleted reel, and the finished game is what counts. Edition and price details are on the price page, and merchandise on goodies.
