GTA 6 PS5 box codes: reported theft risk remains disputed for sealed copies

Push Square describes a potential code-visibility problem affecting some PEGI boxes, with important uncertainties about sealed retail copies.

Sources pushsquare.com

Some retail PS5 boxes for GTA 6 may leave their download codes insufficiently protected, according to Push Square. The outlet reports claims that strong light can make a code visible through its case. Crucially, it also describes disagreement about whether that is possible with a sealed copy and the included poster still in position.

Are sealed retail copies demonstrably vulnerable?

The available report does not settle that question. Push Square says some people claim to have reproduced the issue, while others say the poster prevents a code from being read. Those conflicting accounts matter. They prevent a potential packaging problem from becoming proof that every copy on a shop shelf is exposed.

The article also describes different printing treatments. US copies reportedly carry a camouflage pattern around the code, whereas some PEGI units apparently lack that protection. That is a reported distinction between examples, not a survey of all European stock. The material available does not establish which batches are affected or provide a reliable count of vulnerable sealed boxes.

Why does the code-in-box format matter?

Push Square points out that the retail package in question contains a download code rather than a Blu-ray disc. The code is consequently the item granting access to the game download. It also says these codes do not require checkout activation in the way some digital vouchers do. If a code could be read before purchase, that would make the alleged weakness consequential.

The packaging itself has already featured in our report on copies reaching shops. The new development concerns protection of the insert, rather than a newly announced edition. Push Square does not describe a large, established wave of customers finding their codes already redeemed. Its report cannot support a numerical claim about victims or a declaration that retail stock has been broadly compromised.

Why it matters

Buyers choosing a boxed copy have a reason to check its condition and retain their purchase documentation. If a code fails, recording the specific problem and contacting the seller is more useful than assuming this report explains every failed redemption. Our pricing and editions page provides context for the different purchase formats. For now, the distinction is clear: a possible packaging weakness has been reported, but the evidence described remains contested for sealed copies. The story is not an official recall, and it provides no basis for treating every PEGI box as unsafe or every existing code as stolen.
