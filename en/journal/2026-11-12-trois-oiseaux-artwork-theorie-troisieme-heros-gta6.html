GTA 6 three birds in the artwork: what the fan theory says

Fans spotted three birds in several GTA 6 visuals and see a nod to GTA 5 or a third hero; Rockstar has said nothing, according to VICE.

Sources vice.com

J-5 GTA 6 releases in 5 days →

What do the three birds in GTA 6's artwork mean? Possibly nothing: VICE describes a fan theory that surfaced in September on Reddit, which Rockstar has neither confirmed nor commented on.

Where does the theory come from?

Per VICE, a viral post on the GTA subreddit says "Rockstar keeps slipping exactly three birds into GTA 6 artwork", a detail said to be there since the 2023 reveal. The outlet adds that it appears in only three pieces: the vinyl artwork, the Game Informer cover and the first trailer's key art. The Extended Look artwork shows six birds.

What explanations are going around?

The most popular one reads it as a nod to GTA 5's three protagonists, Michael, Trevor and Franklin. Another imagines a third, unrevealed playable character next to Jason and Lucia, tied to an epilogue. Others settle for the obvious: birds in a tropical setting. VICE also cites a darker reading, two birds for Jason and Lucia and a third for whoever betrays them, and calls it "a pretty big leap" given how little is known about the story.

For now this is a player observation, not a fact. For what is established, see the release page, and our piece on the music leads going around for another unconfirmed clue.

Why it matters

Nothing in the game: Rockstar has not addressed the birds. The answer, if any, will come from November 19. Until then it is a treasure hunt for players, and nothing more.
