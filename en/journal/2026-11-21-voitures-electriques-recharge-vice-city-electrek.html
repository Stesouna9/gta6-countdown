GTA 6 electric cars feel ordinary in Vice City, Electrek reports

Electrek describes common electric traffic and straightforward charging in its play sessions, while its observations remain vehicle-specific.

Sources pcgamer.com

Electric vehicles have become ordinary traffic in GTA 6’s Vice City, according to PC Gamer. In its account of driving through Leonida, the outlet describes older vehicles around the Keys and increasing numbers of electric models nearer the city. We read the collected article text; the original page could not be accessed through our web tool, so these findings remain attributed observations.

Does charging make electric driving harder?

Electrek says the vehicles it tested offered broadly comparable range to combustion cars, with charging taking about as long as refuelling. That describes the writer’s experience, rather than an established rule for the entire vehicle roster. The collected text does not supply a repeatable test covering different routes, driving styles and every available model. It would be premature to turn that experience into a universal recommendation.

The outlet also describes the fictional charging brand ReVolt America and charging spaces obstructed by combustion vehicles. That detail gives the story an everyday dimension: the infrastructure is present, but other road users can complicate how it works. It is a report about the streets and their behaviour as much as a report about cars themselves.

The Keys and Vice City show different patterns

The geographical contrast is central to Electrek’s account. Its writer encountered fewer electric vehicles in the Keys and more on approaching Vice City, interpreting that as a reflection of adoption concentrated in larger urban areas. This is a reading of the environment experienced during play. It is not a published Rockstar explanation of how traffic distribution is calculated.

Electrek also repeats secondary figures about range testing and charging locations from other outlets. We are not presenting those numbers as independently established measurements because we have not examined the underlying tests. The qualitative observation is sufficient for this report: electric cars appear integrated into the city, rather than reserved for the occasional unusual vehicle sighting.

Why it matters

For players planning journeys, a reported play session is useful context, but its limits matter. Electrek found electric driving relatively straightforward with the models it used; another route or car may produce a different experience. Our community page and GTA 6 journal provide places to follow further observations, alongside our coverage of fast travel. The broader development is environmental storytelling: electric cars and the practical annoyances around their charging are described as part of urban life. That is a more specific and better supported conclusion than claiming an exact advantage for a particular powertrain across the whole game.
