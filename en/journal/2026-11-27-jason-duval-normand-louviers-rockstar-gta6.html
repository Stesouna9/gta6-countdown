GTA 6: a man named Jason Duval from Normandy writes to Rockstar

A resident of Louviers, France, shares his name with GTA 6's hero and has written to Rockstar asking for "a gesture or a message", Presse-citron reports. He has had no reply.

Sources presse-citron.net

There is a real Jason Duval, he lives in Louviers in the Eure department of Normandy, and he has written to Rockstar Games. The story comes from Presse-citron, which cites remarks he reportedly made to Paris Normandie, picked up by Le Parisien. We only read the Presse-citron version.

Who is the real Jason Duval?

A Norman with no link to the game. According to Presse-citron, he does not play GTA and has little interest in it. He learned about the coincidence from colleagues. "At first I thought it was only the first name, but no, it really is Jason Duval," he said.

Since the November 19 release the character is everywhere, and the game passed 32.5 million copies sold on day one, the outlet recalls. For a namesake, that is an odd situation.

What does he ask Rockstar for?

He wrote to the studio after finding out. His request is modest: "a gesture or a message" from the publisher. Per the site, he has received no answer so far. To our knowledge Rockstar has made no public comment.

We do not know whether the studio will reply. A private letter to a publisher this size rarely moves fast, especially a week after a launch.

A name that is already out there

It is not the first time a GTA 6 character spills into real life. We covered Manni Perez, Lucia's voice, thanking her followers. For release details, see the release page.

Why it matters

Nothing for the game, a lot for one man in the Eure whose name now appears on screens worldwide. What happens next is up to Rockstar. If the studio replies, we will say so.
