GTA 6 early access: none, and reviews reportedly land November 18

Mashable says there is no early access to GTA 6 before November 19, and that the review embargo lifts November 18. Rockstar has not confirmed it.

Sources sea.mashable.com

J-12 GTA 6 releases in 12 days →

There is no early access to GTA 6, Mashable writes in answer to players who want in before November 19, 2026. The outlet also reports, without any Rockstar confirmation, that the review embargo lifts the day before launch, on November 18.

Can you play GTA 6 before November 19?

No, according to Mashable: the public gets no early access. Pre-ordering the digital version only lets you start the download on November 10. The game stays locked until the 19th, and its reported 212 GB size is a reason to start early. Our release page has the full calendar.

What do we know about the review embargo?

The same article says some reviewers are already playing, having reportedly received codes in late October, with more going out to gaming and tech outlets over the next two weeks. Reviews are due on November 18, one day before launch. In the sources we checked Rockstar has published no embargo date: the claim comes from Mashable alone and awaits confirmation.

If it holds, first scores and detailed verdicts should land the day before release, not earlier. Footage circulating until then would not come from authorized reviews.

Does the time zone change anything?

Mashable mentions the New Zealand trick, where launch lands roughly 18 hours earlier: switching a console's region and using a VPN would allow play on November 18. We covered it in our piece on staggered launch time zones. Rockstar has not endorsed the workaround, and the exact unlock time remains unknown.

Why it matters

For players: pre-order, free up 212 GB and wait for the 19th. For news: watch November 18 for reviews, with the usual caution until Rockstar or the outlets involved confirm it.
