GTA 6 hype: Kai Cenat to stream five GTA games on November 5

Kai Cenat will play GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, GTA IV and GTA V in one stream on Thursday, November 5 at 3 p.m. ET on Twitch and YouTube, Mashable reports.

Sources mashable.com

J-11 GTA 6 releases in 11 days →

Streamer Kai Cenat will play five GTA games in a single sitting on Thursday, November 5, according to a post on X relayed by Mashable. It starts at 3 p.m. Eastern, which is 9 p.m. in Paris, on Twitch and YouTube.

Which GTA games will Kai Cenat play?

Mashable lists the full 3D era: GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, GTA IV and GTA V. The outlet puts his Twitch following at just under 22 million. His post gives no length for the stream and does not say whether he plans to finish each story, which could take a very long time. Even just jacking cars for a few hours, Mashable notes, would make for a good broadcast.

Why this stream lands now

The timing is no accident: GTA VI launches on November 19, two weeks later, and pre-loading opens on the 10th, as we covered in our piece on the 212 GB download. Creators are filling the gap before release and revisiting the series works as a collective warm-up. What we do not know is whether Rockstar or Take-Two are involved. Mashable mentions no partnership and we found none.

For European viewers the hour is workable. The key dates before launch are on the Release page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game itself. But a streamer with 22 million followers replaying GTA III through GTA V puts the series back in front of an audience that may not have played the older entries. The viewership will be one more gauge of interest ahead of November 19.
