GTA 6: Rockstar drops the launch trailer, featuring Justice, Tame Impala and SZA

Rockstar Games just released the GTA 6 launch trailer, set to "Flower in a Hurricane", a new track by Justice feat. Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. Out November 19.

Sources youtube.com · x.com · rockstargames.com

Trailer 3 special: the video, the music, the dates →

It's official and it's now: on October 15 at 3 pm (Paris time), Rockstar Games released the Grand Theft Auto VI launch trailer, announced on its official X account. It is the game's third trailer: ninety seconds, five weeks before release.

1:30

An original track by Justice

The trailer marks the debut of "Flower in a Hurricane", a new song from the game's original score composers, French duo Justice, joined by Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. A top-tier musical lineup to follow Lucia and Jason through Leonida.

Release confirmed for November 19

Rockstar repeats it in its post: Grand Theft Auto VI launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Pre-orders and all official info are on the Rockstar Games website.
