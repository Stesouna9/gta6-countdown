GTA 6 radio joins a fan website collecting stations across the series

Frandroid profiles GTAMusic.fun, an unofficial browser service with complete radio recordings and shared playback.

Sources frandroid.com

Radio in GTA is more than a collection of songs. Frandroid profiles GTAMusic.fun, a free browser project by developer Shrikar that reportedly assembles 159 stations from twelve games in the series, including GTA 6. Its complete recordings preserve presenters, fictional advertisements and music together. The service is a fan initiative, and the report does not describe it as a Rockstar-supported platform or an official archive.

How does listening work?

According to Frandroid, visitors choose a game and hold Q to open a radio wheel on a computer, or use a long press on a phone. Loading-screen imagery accompanies playback. That design puts the station, rather than an individual track, at the centre of the experience. A presenter’s interruption or an advertisement becomes part of what listeners are choosing to hear.

The outlet says listeners on the same station hear the same point in its recording. There is no rewind to recover a missed song. In practice, that makes the service closer to a shared radio stream than an on-demand music library. The transitions between records and the fictional voices remain part of the programme instead of being stripped away for a playlist.

What is reported about mobile playback?

Frandroid describes continued playback with the screen locked on Android and iPhone, with lock-screen controls used to switch stations. Those are the functions reported by the outlet. We have not independently tested the site across devices or browsers, so the description should not be taken as a compatibility guarantee for every phone configuration.

The report also relays the website’s claim that it does not collect personal data and uses anonymous Cloudflare Web Analytics visitor counting. That is a stated policy rather than a privacy audit by this publication. Frandroid’s text gives inconsistent totals for GTA 6 stations specifically, so we are not repeating a precise number for that individual game.

Why it matters

The project offers a way to revisit the series through its sound, but its unofficial status is central to the story. Frandroid notes that the site disclaims affiliation with Rockstar and Take-Two and provides a removal process for rights holders. Continued availability is therefore not assured.
