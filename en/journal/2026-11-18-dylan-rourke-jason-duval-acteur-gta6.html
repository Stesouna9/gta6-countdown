GTA 6: Dylan Rourke reportedly plays Jason Duval, per two X accounts

According to @gtasix_ and @GTAVI_Countdown, citing IGN Netherlands, Dylan Rourke plays Jason Duval in GTA 6. Rockstar has confirmed nothing.

Sources x.com · x.com · nl.ign.com

A name is spreading tonight: Dylan Rourke is said to be the actor playing Jason Duval in GTA 6. The claim comes from two X accounts, @gtasix_ (over 108,000 views) and @GTAVI_Countdown, which say they took it from IGN Netherlands' GTA VI review. Rockstar has announced nothing so far.

What do we actually know about Dylan Rourke?

For now the name rests on those two posts. We read the IGN NL review: it does discuss Jason Duval, an ex-soldier who teams up with Lucia Caminos, and praises characters "convincingly played by exceptionally good actors", naming Stephen Root. In the text we could read, it does not name Dylan Rourke. The link between the article and the cast claim is therefore unverified, and nothing comes from a Rockstar statement.

The name is not new. On November 17, @GTAVI_Countdown posted an updated voice cast list that already had Dylan Rourke for Jason, without a "confirmed" tag, unlike Manni Perez (Lucia) and Stephen Root (Brian). Earlier, fans had put him forward by comparing the character's voice with a résumé listing motion capture skills: community speculation at the time.

Why is this surfacing now?

Press reviews landed before the November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and actors are free to announce their own roles, according to the same list. If Dylan Rourke confirms or Rockstar publishes the credits, the matter is settled. Until then we write "according to", not "confirmed".

Why it matters

Nothing changes in the game itself, but it puts a face and a voice on the lead hours before launch. We are waiting for official confirmation from Rockstar or the actor.

Update, November 19: both actors change their profile pictures

This afternoon, @gtasix_ (over 100,000 views) reported that the actors playing Jason and Lucia shared a GTA 6 post together on Instagram, and that Dylan Rourke and Manni L. Perez switched their profile pictures to their characters. @GTAVI_Countdown quotes a message from Dylan Rourke about the honor of working with the game's teams. We have not verified these posts and Rockstar has issued no statement on the cast, so Dylan Rourke's role still awaits official confirmation, even if the actors' move points that way.
