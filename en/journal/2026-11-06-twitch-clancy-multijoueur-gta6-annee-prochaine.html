GTA 6 Online: Twitch CEO says multiplayer arrives "next year"

Twitch chief Dan Clancy told Bloomberg that GTA 6 multiplayer would launch next year; Rockstar has confirmed nothing and presents the game as a solo experience.

Sources lacremedugaming.fr

J-11 GTA 6 releases in 11 days →

One sentence from Dan Clancy is enough to restart the speculation. According to La Crème Du Gaming, the Twitch chief told Bloomberg in September that the platform expects "a big spike" in viewership when GTA 6 releases, then "an even bigger spike next year, when they launch multiplayer". He adds that Twitch has spent "a lot of time" discussing Rockstar's plans with the studio.

Is a GTA 6 multiplayer mode confirmed?

No. The same report notes that official communication presents GTA 6 as "a solo experience" for 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Neither "GTA 6 Online" nor "GTA Online 2" appears in Rockstar's releases or on the PlayStation and Xbox listings. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick told shareholders the current GTA Online would be supported "after GTA 6 and beyond".

How much weight does "next year" carry?

It is a hint, not a date. Clancy speaks as an executive at a streaming partner, not as a Rockstar spokesperson, and gave neither a month nor a product name. The common reading, a 2027 window, remains an interpretation. We noted in the Piper Sandler survey how much the online question weighs on the launch, while GTA Online content keeps landing in parallel.

The article also mentions a PC release in Q1 2027 reported by GamesBeat and relayed by IGN. Rockstar and Take-Two have not commented. No PC version has been announced.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for 19 November: the game ships as solo and the unlock time is still unknown. Going forward, only Rockstar can announce an online mode, and it has not. The full timeline is on our release page.
