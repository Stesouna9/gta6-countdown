GTA 6 Fast & Furious car: Polygon locates the green Penumbra FF

Polygon’s guide places a Brian O’Conner-inspired car on Vespucci Island and lists a $6,750 in-game registration cost.

Sources polygon.com

A green car in GTA 6 pays tribute to Brian O’Conner’s Mitsubishi Eclipse from the first Fast & Furious film, according to Polygon.com. Its guide identifies the in-game vehicle as the 1997 Maibatsu Penumbra FF and provides a route to its garage. The following details reveal a vehicle location and its retrieval steps, rather than a major story twist. Readers who prefer finding cars unaided may want to save the directions for later.

Where does Polygon say the car is hidden?

The guide places the house on Vespucci Island in Vice-Dale County, on the east coast. It describes the island as part of the Rialto Islands in the Vice Beach region, northeast of Vice City. When approaching from the west, its directions say to turn left onto the street running north.

The landmark is a large house with a white gate, on a corner opposite a construction site. According to Polygon, the gate opens as the player approaches and no guards are stationed outside. We have not independently driven this route. The official illustration on this page depicts a different vehicle, the Grotti Cheetah, and should not be used as a location map or a photograph of the hidden car.

What happens after reaching the house?

Polygon.com says the door beside the garage is locked and breaking it triggers an alarm. An apparently unguarded entrance therefore does not make the entire visit consequence-free. The green car is inside the garage. The guide also notes that an opening tool available after meeting Moses in Chapter 2 allows entry to the vehicle without smashing its window.

Keeping it comes with a separate cost. Polygon lists registration at $6,750 in game, while selling the car brings $1,100. These are fictional currency amounts, not real-money charges or a purchase tied to an edition of the game. The outlet recommends keeping the vehicle, but that is its preference rather than a requirement for advancing the story.

Why it matters

The guide gives collectors a specific destination for a film reference instead of leaving them to wait for a random roadside encounter. It also highlights the distinction between taking a vehicle and making it part of a lasting collection. Check the reported registration cost before planning the detour.
