🚨 ALERT · Bloomberg's big feature on how GTA 6 was made

Bloomberg Businessweek published "The Making of Grand Theft Auto VI" on October 14, a long feature by Jason Schreier, who visited Rockstar North in Edinburgh in late September. The piece is paywalled, so this summary relies on Bloomberg and detailed reports from VGTimes and LibertyCity.

Is GTA 6 still coming on November 19, 2026?

Schreier calls the date likely after visiting the studio, while noting nobody can be 100% sure until the game is in players' hands. The feature mentions no new delay. Preloading is planned for November 12; see our release page.

How many people work on GTA 6?

Rockstar had 2,441 employees when Red Dead Redemption 2 launched in 2018 and has 4,711 today across 12 studios, per VGTimes. A Los Angeles studio was set up in a hurry to build the non-player characters, with writers, producers and directors.

How much did GTA 6 cost?

Rockstar gives no figure. Publishing head Jennifer Kolbe denies a budget above $1.5 billion. Schreier suggests more than $1 billion, which would make it the most expensive game ever, according to LibertyCity. These are journalist estimates, not official numbers.

Who leads development after Dan Houser left?

Rob Nelson, Phil Hooker and Aaron Garbut share the lead. The feature describes a "dark period" without a clear overall vision after most of the studio moved to RDR2, which pushed GTA 6 back by years. The project was then called Project Americas, with a first research trip to Miami in 2015. The Jason and Lucia story was locked in late 2019. Some staff complain of contradictory decisions, LibertyCity reports.

What is work like at Rockstar?

Since 2024, staff must be in the office five days a week, with remote tool access blocked. Rockstar says crunch is reduced but not eliminated, and overtime earns paid leave. Of the 34 employees fired in 2025, mostly involved in union efforts, the dispute was still before a labor tribunal at publication, per VGTimes. Rockstar cites leaks.

Anecdotes from the feature

During a test, a car collision pulled Phil Hooker away from his mission for two hours of chases and side events. The team also debated whether a beer should arrive instantly or after a purchase sequence. Jason and Lucia's interactions required more advanced companion AI than past games.

What is Rockstar's next game?

No title is named. Hooker and Garbut say the new tools will let the team produce content faster without starting over, aiming for a much shorter wait than the eight years between RDR2 and GTA 6. Analysts cited expect 40 million copies on PlayStation and Xbox, and the online mode is expected in 2027.

What this changes

For the launch, nothing: GTA 6 is still due November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no PC version announced. The feature mainly sheds light on the human and financial cost of the project. See every official image in our official GTA 6 photos gallery and our journal for more.
