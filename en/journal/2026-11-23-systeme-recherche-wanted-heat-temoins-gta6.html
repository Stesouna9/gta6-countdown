GTA 6 wanted system: six stars, Heat and witnesses explained

GTA 6 brings back six stars and adds a Heat meter and witness identification, which Man of Many calls the game's best feature.

Sources manofmany.com

Six stars are back. In an opinion piece, Man of Many argues that the reworked wanted system is GTA 6's best new feature, ahead of the map and the graphics. It is an opinion, but it rests on mechanics described in the game.

What changes in the wanted system?

According to the outlet, GTA 6 restores the maximum wanted level to six stars, where GTA 5 had cut it to five. The star count is only a small part: the game adds a Heat mechanic, witness identification and consequences beyond simply losing the police.

Why do witnesses matter?

In earlier games a crime could draw police even with nobody watching. The writer says GTA 6 takes a more refined approach where witnesses and security systems play a key role. His enjoyment comes from thinking before acting and pulling off a robbery unnoticed.

That is a personal view, and some players will always prefer a six-star mess on the highway. The text does not say how the meter fills or how long consequences last, and we will not fill those gaps. For the rest of the game, see our release page.

Why it matters

The series is trying to make crime less automatic and more deliberate. For players, that means spotting cameras and bystanders before acting. Early community feedback will show whether the balance holds over time.
