GTA 6 pre-load date: starts November 12 on PS5 and Xbox, what we know

GTA 6 pre-loading starts November 12, a week before the November 19 launch; the 210 GB size is still unconfirmed.

Sources clubic.com · thmmagazine.fr · rockstarintel.com

J-11 GTA 6 releases in 11 days →

GTA 6 pre-loading starts on November 12, 2026, seven days before the November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not detailed the process yet, but several outlets already cover it, including Clubic and THM Magazine.

When can you pre-load GTA 6?

On November 12 for the digital version. Files download ahead of time, but the game cannot be launched before the unlock time. Clubic sums up the benefit: you get a week to start the download and be ready on November 19. The exact hour pre-loading opens that day has not been announced.

What about the boxed copy?

The retail version has no disc, only a download code, as we explained in our article on the discless box. According to THM Magazine, citing official documentation, boxes should be available from November 12 to allow pre-loading, with a likely stock shortage. RockstarINTEL gives the same date for Walmart pre-order code pickup in the US, where a "surprise" gift would also be handed out.

How much space do you need?

One figure is circulating: 210 GB on PS5. It comes from a leak and Rockstar has not confirmed it, as covered in our piece on the unconfirmed size. If true, download time depends mostly on your connection: from 30 minutes to over 46 hours. Clubic estimates about 4 h 40 at 100 Mb/s. Clearing space before the 12th is the only safe precaution.

Why it matters

Pre-loading avoids the launch-day server crush, but it does not answer the question many players ask: when does the game unlock? That time is still unknown, and staggered time-zone release remains a hypothesis. All dates are on the release page.
