GTA Online Halloween challenge: Slasher Clown Outfit and GTA$250,000 up for grabs

Rockstar offers the Slasher Clown Outfit and GTA$250,000 for clearing 10 Survival waves without dying, according to its October 29 post on X.

Sources x.com

J-19 GTA 6 releases in 19 days →

Rockstar has launched the Halloween weekly challenge in GTA Online, the studio announced on X: complete 10 waves of a Survival without dying to earn the Slasher Clown Outfit and GTA$250,000. The post passed 118,000 views within hours.

How do you get the Slasher Clown Outfit?

Easy to state, harder to do: clear 10 Survival waves without a single death. The reward, per Rockstar Games sur X: the Slasher Clown Outfit and GTA$250,000. The post does not say how many players are recommended or how hard the Survival is.

What else is in the Halloween update?

Rockstar adds the Brown Vintage Skull Mask, discounts on otherworldly vehicles and more bonuses it does not list in the post. The full details sit behind the link to Rockstar's Newswire. We have no source beyond this official post at this point, so check prices in-game before buying.

What does it mean for GTA 6?

Nothing in the announcement concerns GTA VI, still set for November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (see the release page). It does show Rockstar keeping GTA Online busy with seasonal events weeks before launch, on a day when it also opened pre-orders for Justice's original score.

Why it matters

No upheaval: a standard seasonal event with a generous reward for Online players. For GTA 6 followers, it confirms Rockstar is running GTA Online alongside its launch campaign.
