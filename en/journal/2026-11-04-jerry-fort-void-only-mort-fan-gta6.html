GTA 6: Jerry Fort, the fan who wanted to play it, dies at 25

Jerry Fort, the creator known as Void Only, died on October 29 aged 25, three weeks before the GTA 6 launch he hoped to see, GTA BOOM reports.

Sources gtaboom.com

The GTA community has lost one of its own. Jerry Fort, 25, known online as Void Only, died on October 29, according to GTA BOOM. His family announced his death in a video on his TikTok account around November 2. He will not see the game, due on November 19.

Who was Jerry Fort?

He had lived with interstitial lung disease since he was 17. In August he posted a video from his ICU bed. "I just want to play GTA VI," he said, with the usual joke about Rockstar delays. The clip spread fast and turned a private fight into a community cause.

His mother, Paris Fort, set up a GoFundMe for his lung transplant. GTA BOOM reports that the community pushed it well past its goal, with more than $96,000 from thousands of donors. Jerry hoped to stream the game with the people who had backed him.

His final weeks

He received the transplant in September. Recovery was hard, with serious complications. By his family's account, on October 28 Jerry decided he was ready to stop fighting, and he died the next day. "He was at peace," his sister said in the video, thanking everyone who made his last months beautiful. Their mother is planning a balloon release in his honor.

These details come from the family and have not been cross-checked beyond Jerry's own account. We report them as published.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game, but a lot for those waiting for it. Tributes are pouring in from players who never met him. With the launch tracked daily on our release page, his story is a reminder of what the wait means to some. The whole newsroom is thinking of his family.
