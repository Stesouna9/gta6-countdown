GTA VI pre-orders open: $79.99 standard, $99.99 Ultimate, single-player at launch

Retrospective: written in October 2026 from the sources of the time

Rockstar opened GTA VI pre-orders on June 25 and revealed prices and the contents of the Ultimate Edition. At launch, the game is pitched as a single-player experience.

Sources rockstargames.com · variety.com · polygon.com

Pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto VI open on Thursday, June 25, Rockstar says on its Newswire. Just before they opened, the studio settled the last two commercial unknowns, price and Ultimate Edition contents, according to Variety and Polygon.

The price, and a single-player launch

Per Polygon, the standard edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate $99.99. Variety flags a point fewer people picked up: when the game ships in November, GTA VI will be pitched as a "single-player experience". Neither outlet spells out what that means for online play, and we won't put words in their mouths. Euro prices don't appear in either article; we will add them once Rockstar publishes them, and our price page tracks the file.

What the Ultimate Edition adds

Rockstar's pitch, as Polygon relays it: the bonuses don't land in one block at the start, they are "threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story", with new items uncovered behind each chapter. The list is long: a '95 Grotti Cheetah, Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers inspired by the Vercetti Estate, engraved variants of Jason's and Lucia's sidearms, a Crest kayak, a Dinka Enduro motorbike, a '67 Vapid Dominator buggy and a Shitzu Squalo boat.

More telling, Polygon notes that the Ultimate Edition unlocks places: the Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's mod shops, Sara's Unisex Salon, the Stock 305 clothing store, the Electric Fang tattoo bar (over 50 designs by the artist collective FAILE) and a classic-car collection to restore for a local fixer named Wyman. A Goodtime Gear apparel line draws on Macca the Gator, a character from a TV show inside the game. Pre-orders, for their part, come with the Vintage Vice City cosmetics pack.

What we know since

Euro pricing is set at €79.99 for the standard edition and €99.99 for Ultimate. GTA VI launches on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preloading on November 12 and a PEGI 18 rating. No PC version has been announced. The Goodtime State collector's edition, at €399.99 without the game, and GTA VI: The Album, on November 19, have joined the line-up since.

Why it matters

On June 25, GTA VI stops being a date and becomes a shopping basket: we know what we are buying, at what price, and which bonuses are locked to the priciest edition. Whether Ultimate content spread across the whole story is worth $20 more is a matter of taste, not fact. Our advice: be wary of "what you're missing" bonus lists and wait for the reviews.
