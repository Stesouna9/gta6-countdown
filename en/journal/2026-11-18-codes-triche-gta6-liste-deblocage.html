GTA 6 cheats: every cheat code and how to unlock it

GTA 6 has 61 cheats, unlocked through play then entered on the D-pad. 17 codes are already confirmed by two guides.

Sources powerpyx.com · gtabase.com

Cheats are back: GTA 6 has 61 of them in five groups (player, combat, vehicles, environment, screen filters). Unlike GTA 5, each cheat must first be unlocked through play (finishing the story, shooting range medals, challenges…), then entered on the D-pad. List from PowerPyx, GTABase, story spoiler-free.

Which GTA 6 cheat codes are confirmed?

These 17 combos match in both guides:

  • $250,000: instantly gives you $250,000. Code: UP > RIGHT > DOWN > LEFT > UP > LEFT > DOWN > RIGHT. Unlock: Finish the story.
  • Raise Heat Level: Raises your level of heat across the map. Code: LEFT > DOWN > RIGHT > DOWN > UP > DOWN > UP. Unlock: Lose a 3-star wanted level 25 times..
  • Unlimited Focus: Focus never runs out. Code: DOWN > UP > UP > DOWN > UP > DOWN > UP > DOWN > UP > RIGHT. Unlock: Finish the story.
  • Body Armor: Adds body armor to your loadout. Code: DOWN > UP > LEFT > UP > UP > UP > UP > UP > DOWN > DOWN. Unlock: Complete 100 workouts..
  • Max Ammo: Fills all ammo to maximum. Code: DOWN > UP > RIGHT > DOWN > LEFT > LEFT > UP > UP > DOWN > LEFT. Unlock: Win 25 bronze medals or higher at shooting ranges..
  • Infinite Ammo: Ammo never runs out. Code: DOWN > UP > RIGHT > DOWN > LEFT > LEFT > UP > DOWN > DOWN > DOWN. Unlock: Win 25 silver medals or higher at shooting ranges..
  • No Reload: Ammo never runs out with no need to reload. Code: DOWN > UP > RIGHT > DOWN > LEFT > LEFT > UP > DOWN > DOWN > UP. Unlock: Win 25 gold medals at shooting ranges..
  • Attack Force: Bullets and melee attacks launch targets on impact. Code: DOWN > UP > LEFT > LEFT > RIGHT > DOWN > DOWN > UP > DOWN. Unlock: Finish the story.
  • Exploding Balls: Sports balls explode on impact. Code: DOWN > UP > LEFT > LEFT > RIGHT > DOWN > DOWN > UP > RIGHT. Unlock: Hit 20 enemies with explosive damage.
  • Vehicle Force: Vehicle collisions launch targets. Code: DOWN > UP > LEFT > LEFT > RIGHT > DOWN > LEFT > UP > DOWN. Unlock: Kill 25 enemies with a vehicle (driving over civilians counts too).
  • Maibatsu Boomazoom: Spawns a Maibatsu Boomazoom. Code: LEFT > UP > RIGHT > DOWN > LEFT > LEFT > UP > LEFT. Unlock: Complete 5 shopping challenges..
  • MTL Pumper: Spawns an MTL Pumper. Code: LEFT > UP > RIGHT > DOWN > LEFT > RIGHT > RIGHT > UP. Unlock: Unknown.
  • HVY Bulldozer: Spawns an HVY Bulldozer. Code: LEFT > UP > RIGHT > DOWN > LEFT > RIGHT > RIGHT > DOWN. Unlock: Discover the HVY Bulldozer in the Progress menu..
  • Cycle Weather: Changes the weather, cycling through the different weather types. Code: DOWN > RIGHT > UP > DOWN > RIGHT > UP > RIGHT. Unlock: Finish the story.
  • Cataclysm: Fills the world with murky smoke. Code: DOWN > DOWN > UP > RIGHT > RIGHT > UP > LEFT > LEFT > RIGHT > RIGHT. Unlock: Discover Martha's Vamos in the Progress menu.
  • Dollhouse: Applies a vivid filter with a narrow aspect ratio. Code: DOWN > DOWN > UP > RIGHT > RIGHT > UP > LEFT > LEFT > DOWN > RIGHT. Unlock: Spend 8 hours with your partner.
  • Faded Glory: Applies a color CRT TV filter. Code: DOWN > DOWN > UP > RIGHT > RIGHT > UP > LEFT > LEFT > LEFT > RIGHT. Unlock: Rob 50 stores with your partner.

What about the other cheats?

For the other 44, the effect and unlock condition are known but the combo isn't cross-checked yet: they're flagged "to be confirmed" on our GTA 6 cheats page, updated hourly.
