GTA 6 PC release: what we know, and when it could come

GTA 6 has no announced PC version: Rockstar names only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the 2027 windows from analysts are unconfirmed.

Sources lacremedugaming.fr

J-12 GTA 6 releases in 12 days →

No, GTA 6 is not coming to PC on November 19, and Rockstar has announced no PC version. The game launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. The question everyone searches is when PC might follow. Here is what is established, what is estimation, and what is unknown.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not for now. According to La Crème du Gaming, there is no listing on Steam, Epic or the Rockstar launcher, and the studio's messaging covers the two console families only. Our release page says the same: no PC version announced.

When could a PC version arrive?

Two views circulate, neither confirmed by Rockstar. An earlier GamesBeat report pointed to the first quarter of 2027. Wedbush analyst Michael Pachter, speaking to the BBC, says "6 or 7 months" after the console launch, roughly May or June 2027. These are outside estimates, not a schedule.

Rockstar's track record gives a yardstick: per the same outlet, GTA IV took about seven months to reach PC, Red Dead Redemption 2 a year, and GTA 5 nearly nineteen months. Our piece on mods and Rockstar's rules covers what console players can expect.

What does Take-Two say?

Strauss Zelnick keeps repeating that the strategy is to start with console, while noting PC now weighs heavily: close to 50% of sales for some titles, the outlet reports. That commits to no date.

Should you trust "GTA 6 PC" offers?

No. Until Rockstar says something on its official channels, a PC key, beta or pre-order is a likely scam. We covered the issue in our article on fake pre-order sites.

Why it matters

To play on November 19, a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S remains the only safe route. For those who would rather wait, the next clue will come from a Rockstar announcement or Take-Two's earnings. Until then, any PC date is a hypothesis.
