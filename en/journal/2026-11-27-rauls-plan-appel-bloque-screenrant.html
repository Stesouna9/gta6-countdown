GTA 6 story progression can stall at Raul’s call, ScreenRant reports

ScreenRant describes stalled campaigns around Raul’s Plan, while warning that unfinished Yasmani jobs can look like the same problem.

Sources screenrant.com

An incoming call may be holding up some GTA 6 campaigns, according to ScreenRant, which relays player reports covered by PlayerOne. The reported issue centres on “Raul’s Plan” at roughly 72% story completion. Affected players can apparently keep exploring Leonida and doing side activities while being unable to advance the main narrative.

What happens when Raul does not call?

ScreenRant describes an objective that asks players to wait for Raul’s phone call while controlling Lucia. Some players reportedly continue spending time in the open world without receiving it. The distinction matters: the report concerns story progression, rather than a game that universally fails to launch or prevents all further play.

The completion figure should not become a prediction for every save. ScreenRant provides an approximate point reported by affected players, not a measured failure threshold. The article supplies no incidence rate or platform breakdown. It therefore cannot establish how common the problem is, which console is more exposed or whether any particular reader will encounter it.

Check the story requirements before assuming a broken save

The report first points to Yasmani’s three jewellery jobs and selling the jewellery back to him. According to ScreenRant, these can look optional despite being required for progression. A missing phone call alone consequently does not demonstrate that a save is corrupted or that the reported bug is responsible.

ScreenRant also relays accounts from players who received Valentina’s call before Raul’s. Completing a subsequent mission and settling Abe’s legal fee reportedly allowed progress to resume. That is a player-reported sequence, not an official repair procedure or a guaranteed solution. Keeping those categories separate avoids turning a useful lead into a promise the available evidence cannot support.

Why it matters

Restarting a long campaign is a substantial decision. ScreenRant recommends checking outstanding Story menu objectives and maintaining manual saves in separate slots. Those suggestions offer a way to review progress while preserving options. Our GTA 6 journal follows launch developments without treating every reported experience as a universal defect.

The article says reports continued after the November 23 update. That is not a direct Rockstar acknowledgement of this specific case, nor proof that every absent call has the same cause. Without an identified official fix in the source, this report cannot promise that the issue has disappeared or that the player workaround will restore every stalled campaign. The present evidence supports a clearly attributed warning, with those limits kept visible.
