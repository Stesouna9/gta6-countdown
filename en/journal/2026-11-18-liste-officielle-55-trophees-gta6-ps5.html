GTA 6: the official list of 55 PS5 trophies and every collectible

GTA 6's trophy list is live on PSN: 55 trophies, 100 vehicles, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 5 bank vaults. Spoiler-free guide.

Sources psnprofiles.com

It's official: GTA 6's PS5 trophy list went live on the PlayStation Network, picked up by PSNProfiles on November 18, hours after the New Zealand launch. 55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze (1,350 points). The Xbox list is worth 1,000 G.

The platinum: "Vice Got a Price!"

Unlock every other trophy. The longest will be "A Long Road": reach 100% completion.

Collectibles and completion

  • 100 vehicles to discover, 100 WAiNK locations, 250 stunts, 50 challenges
  • 52 areas to explore, 18 meditation spots, 42 shooting range challenges (gold), 14 Top Fun drone races
  • 20 races to win, 9 activity types, 3 collectible card sets, 8 garages, 10 vehicles on the Scooter Bros app, 500 choices

Heists and money

  • Rob 5 bank vaults, an armored truck and a jewelry store undetected
  • Rob 3 shops back-to-back with your partner, lose a 4-star wanted level together
  • Rob an illegal operation from each of the 9 gangs; launder $250,000 in marked bills; spend $100,000 in shops

Side characters

Trophies mention Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret and Cal, plus 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, an arcade game and Combat Focus.

Spoiler warning

About a dozen story trophies reveal chapters and mission names; we don't list them here. Full list on PSNProfiles.

Sources: PSNProfiles (trophy list); TrueAchievements (Xbox list, same achievement names).

Verified by two sources

  • Platinum: about 200 hours of play (PowerPyx, GTABase).
  • Cheats: using one disables trophies and saving until you quit the game. Avoid them during your trophy run (PowerPyx, GTABase).
  • "A Long Road": 100% completion. The 100% checklist covers about 60% of the game's content (PowerPyx, GTABase).
  • "Positive Mental Attitude": a Chapter 1 meditation with Lucia, possibly missable (PowerPyx, GTABase).
  • "Vice City, Baby!" (Complete Chapter 3): unlocks after the first two Chapter 4 missions, Ocean Beach and Another Life (GTABase; PowerPyx places Another Life in Chapter 4).

Sources: PSNProfiles, TrueAchievements, PowerPyx, GTABase.
