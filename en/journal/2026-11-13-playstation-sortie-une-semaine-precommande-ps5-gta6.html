GTA 6: PlayStation says it launches on PS5 in one week, pre-orders open

PlayStation's official X account posted on November 12 that GTA 6 launches on PS5 in one week with pre-orders available now; release is set for November 19.

Sources x.com

J-5 GTA 6 releases in 5 days →

PlayStation posted on Thursday, November 12, around 11:30 pm Paris time, on its official X account: "Grand Theft Auto VI launches on PS5 in one week", followed by "Pre-orders available now" and a store link. The post already had over 118,000 views, 4,000 likes and 468 reposts.

What does PlayStation confirm?

Nothing new on substance: release stays set for November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The message is a reminder that pre-orders are still open and that the game lands seven days later. It gives no unlock time, no download size and no new edition.

Context: a week of cascading messages

Hours earlier, Rockstar wrote "See you in Leonida next week" on its own account, covered in our Thursday article. In stores, boxes reached Leclerc and Auchan in France, as detailed in this piece. PlayStation relaying the date shows the console partner joining the countdown.

What is still unknown

The exact unlock time on November 19 has not been communicated. No PC version is announced. Prices have not moved: €79.99 for Standard and €99.99 for Ultimate (digital), see the price page.

Why it matters

For anyone who has not ordered yet, the post is mostly a calendar reminder: seven days remain to pre-order and clear disk space on the console. What comes next hinges on the unlock time, which Rockstar has not yet given. The release page will be updated as soon as it lands.
