GTA 6 and PS5 Pro prices: French resellers ask over €1,500 as Sony sells out

Entrevue reports the PS5 Pro is out of stock at PlayStation Direct in France and listed by third-party sellers at up to nearly €2,000, against an official price of €899.99.

Sources entrevue.fr

J-3 GTA 6 releases in 3 days →

Four days before GTA 6 launches, Sony's top console is hard to find at its official price. According to Entrevue, the PS5 Pro shows as out of stock on PlayStation Direct, Sony's own store in France, and the offers still online mostly come from independent sellers.

How much is the PS5 Pro in France right now?

The recommended price has not moved: €899.99. But in early November third-party sellers listed it at €1,579.99 on Amazon, up from €1,469.99 a few days earlier, and between €1,699 and over €1,900 on Boulanger's marketplace, Entrevue says. The site stresses these figures are not a new Sony increase but prices set freely by merchants taking advantage of scarcity.

For context, Sony announced a worldwide console price rise on 27 March 2026, effective 2 April. In Europe the PS5 Pro went from €799.99 to €899.99, the disc PS5 to €649.99 and the digital edition to €599.99.

Is GTA 6 behind the shortage?

Partly, according to Entrevue: the 19 November launch pushes players towards the most powerful machine, and restocks sell out fast. The outlet adds a second cause, tight component supply, especially memory, as AI data centres drive demand. The PS5 Pro, with its 2 TB SSD, would be particularly exposed. Sony has given no timeline for a return to normal.

Do you need a PS5 Pro to play?

No, and the article makes the point itself: what GTA 6 gains on PS5 Pro does not yet allow anyone to say it will always run smoother than on a standard PS5. We covered the files in GTA 6 on PS5 Pro: is a 40 fps mode possible? and the press test in a 60 fps trial with heavy compromises. All editions and prices are on the price page.

Why it matters

If you don't own a console yet, paying double out of fear of missing launch day makes little sense: a standard PS5 is enough to play on 19 November, and nothing suggests Rockstar reserves content for the Pro. Check the seller is not a third party before paying €1,500.
