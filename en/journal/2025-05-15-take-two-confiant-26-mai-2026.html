GTA 6: Take-Two Says It Is Confident in May 26, 2026, as the Delay Costs Dearly

Retrospective: written in October 2026 from the sources of the time

Thirteen days after the delay, Take-Two's CEO says he is confident in the May 26, 2026 date. Analysts put the shift at $2.7 billion for the industry.

Sources ign.com · nordic.ign.com

On May 15, 2025, thirteen days after the delay was announced, Take-Two's line is clear. According to IGN, CEO Strauss Zelnick says he is confident: Rockstar Games can hit May 26, 2026. Nothing in the source says the calendar is locked, only that the publisher's boss believes in it, which is not the same thing. For players coming off a second disappointment after the first delay, the message matters as much as the date.

What the shift costs

IGN also relays an estimate from Ampere analysts: the delay would cost the games industry around $2.7 billion. The show suggests retailers have even more reason to be disappointed than players, which is the host's opinion rather than a figure. The money is not gone, it says, merely moved to next year, when GTA 6 launches on May 26. The amount remains an analyst projection, not an accounting result, but it gives the order of magnitude of what one game weighs on a release calendar.

A cinema calendar

IGN Nordic points out a scheduling detail: The Mandalorian and Grogu hits theaters on May 22, 2026, four days before GTA VI. The site sees the two big cultural events of the year, a 2026 “Barbenheimer”, and bets without hesitation that the game wins. That is a columnist's bet, not data. It does record one fact: GTA VI will be the first new GTA in twelve and a half years. For other publishers, that neighbor is mostly a scheduling problem to steer around.

What we know since

The May date did not hold. On November 6, 2025, Rockstar pushed the release to November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preload on November 12, a PEGI 18 rating, €79.99 for the standard edition and €99.99 for the Ultimate. The Goodtime State collector is €399.99 without the game and GTA VI: The Album arrives on November 19. No PC version has been announced.

Why it matters

Confidence in May, a new date in November: a good lesson in how to read publisher statements, which commit less than Rockstar's own announcements. Prices and editions are tracked on the prices page.
