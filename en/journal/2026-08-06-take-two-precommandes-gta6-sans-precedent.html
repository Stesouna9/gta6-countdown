Take-Two: 'unprecedented' GTA VI pre-orders and the November date holds

Retrospective: written in October 2026 from the sources of the time

For its first fiscal quarter of 2027, Take-Two reports $1.39 billion in sales and confirms November 19. Its CEO calls pre-orders 'unprecedented and astonishing'.

Sources variety.com · nordic.ign.com · reuters.com

Numbers first. According to Variety, Take-Two Interactive drew $1.39 billion in sales between April 1 and June 30, the quarter in which GTA VI pre-orders opened, even as its losses widened. CEO Strauss Zelnick calls the level of pre-orders "unprecedented and astonishing", Variety also reports.

A date that doesn't move

IGN Nordic reports that the publisher still holds November 19 and is counting on "GTA 6: An Extended Look", the upcoming showcase, to grow "consumers' passion and anticipation". Reuters runs the same line: Take-Two also sticks to its annual bookings outlook and says it is on track for November. I note, unsurprisingly, that all three articles say nothing about a PC version.

What the numbers say, and what they don't

Take-Two calls its results "excellent" and says the series "once again exceeded expectations", writes IGN Nordic. GTA V has passed 230 million copies worldwide, and the series grew 3% on microtransactions. GTA V remains the second best-selling game of all time, behind Minecraft and its 400 million copies.

What no source gives is the pre-order count, in copies or in money. "Unprecedented" is an adjective, not a data point, and you can't measure a phenomenon with an adjective. We will need published figures to know what we are talking about.

What we know since

The date hasn't moved. Rockstar presented GTA 6: An Extended Look on August 27, first on Netflix. GTA VI launches on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preloading on November 12, a PEGI 18 rating, €79.99 for the standard edition and €99.99 for Ultimate. No PC version has been announced. The Goodtime State collector's edition, at €399.99 without the game, and GTA VI: The Album, on November 19, have been added.

Why it matters

After a quarter of pre-orders, Take-Two's message is consistent: no delay in sight, and a publisher whose calendar hangs on this one game. For readers, the rule is simple: "record" stays the publisher's judgment until figures are published. Our release page tracks the dates, and the counting will come in due course.
