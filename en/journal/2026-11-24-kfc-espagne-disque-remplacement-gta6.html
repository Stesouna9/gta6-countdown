GTA 6: KFC Spain offers a fried chicken “replacement disc” for physical copies

KFC Spain has launched “The Replacement Disc”, a piece of fried chicken meant to fill the empty disc slot in GTA 6 physical editions, Hypebeast reports.

Sources hypebeast.com

KFC Spain is using the GTA 6 launch for a stunt aimed at a sore point: the missing disc in the physical edition. Hypebeast reports that the chain unveiled “The Replacement Disc”, a limited-edition piece of fried chicken presented as an edible stand-in for the absent disc.

What is KFC Spain offering?

According to Hypebeast, fans can claim it by bringing their game case to selected restaurants. KFC also designed exclusive packaging with Colonel Sanders on the cover so the meal looks like a video game. The text we have does not list the restaurants or the duration of the promotion, so those details remain unconfirmed.

Why a chicken disc?

The joke rests on a real fact: per Hypebeast, Rockstar Games is moving away from physical media, with the box holding only a digital download code. Several shops had already voiced surprise, as in our report on British retailers. KFC turns a collector's frustration into a marketing play.

For the record, the game launched on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, at 79.99 € for the standard edition. Pricing details are on our price page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game itself, but the stunt shows the missing-disc topic has gone well beyond collectors: brands are picking it up. We will wait for KFC to confirm whether the offer extends beyond Spain.
