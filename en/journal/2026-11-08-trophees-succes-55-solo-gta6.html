GTA 6 trophy leak points to 55 single-player goals on PS5 and Xbox

A report from Notebookcheck points to 55 single-player trophies and achievements at launch. Rockstar has not confirmed the list.

Sources notebookcheck.net · x.com

J-10 GTA 6 releases in 10 days →

GTA 6 may launch with 55 PS5 trophies and 55 Xbox Series X|S achievements, according to Notebookcheck. Its report traces the figures to TexFunz2 and PlayStation Game Size, with GameRoll sharing the information. All of those objectives are described as single-player goals. Rockstar has not confirmed the list, so this remains a reported leak rather than an official completion checklist.

What does the GTA 6 trophy leak actually reveal?

Notebookcheck says TexFunz2 first reported 55 objectives for Microsoft's consoles, followed by PlayStation Game Size giving the same count for PS5. The figure also appears in a gtasix_ post on X, which credits both accounts. Those references follow the same trail of information; they should not be mistaken for separate official announcements.

The report does not disclose objective names, citing the risk of story spoilers. That leaves a substantial gap between knowing a possible count and knowing what completing the game will involve. There is no mission checklist here, no verified collection requirement and no confirmed route to 100 percent completion. Readers can follow the news without treating speculative challenges as instructions.

How does that compare with earlier Rockstar games?

Notebookcheck puts the proposed total alongside GTA 5's original 49 Xbox 360 achievements and 50 PS3 trophies. Some of those were tied to GTA Online. Its comparison also includes Red Dead Redemption 2, which arrived with 51 Xbox One achievements and 52 PS4 trophies. A purely single-player list would therefore matter beyond the modest difference in numbers.

It would not, however, tell us how demanding GTA 6 will be to finish. Several rewards might unlock during ordinary story progress, while a single optional objective could require extensive exploration. Until the conditions are available, neither a longer completion time nor a harder platinum follows automatically from having more entries on the list.

Is a special Xbox completion reward confirmed?

The report mentions an objective carrying a value of 1,000 and suggests it could indicate a special achievement comparable to a PlayStation platinum. That is an interpretation of the reported information. It does not establish a complete points breakdown or confirm how Microsoft will classify the reward. A definitive Gamerscore guide would need stronger evidence than this fragment.

Why it matters

Completion hunters have a new detail to watch before the November 19 release, but no reliable shopping or scheduling decision depends on this count. The useful next step is an authenticated list with the requirements attached. Until then, the distinction between a reported number and a verified set of challenges matters more than predictions about how many hours a platinum will take.
