GTA 6: Cyberleek claims to have released a 209 GB Xbox build

Cyberleek claims to have put a 209 GB GTA 6 Xbox build online, minus the executable, on launch day. Nothing is verified and Rockstar has not responded.

Sources videogameschronicle.com · wolfsgamingblog.com · big-n.fr

Cyberleek file: everything we know about the GTA 6 leak →

The Cyberleek group claims to have released a 209 GB Xbox build of GTA 6 on launch day. According to Video Games Chronicle, the group's site now shows a link titled "XBOX BUILD 1.0.2.1 (NOV 2026)". Rockstar has not responded so far.

What does Cyberleek say it has released?

The group describes the "full November 2026 build", going out by torrent minus one file: the main executable, held on a countdown. VGC says screenshots shared on social media appear to show files from a development build of the Xbox version. Without the executable, the game cannot be run.

The statement attributed to Cyberleek claims the community will have it emulated "inside a week" and mentions a PS5 build as a fallback if the Xbox one does not work.

Are the files genuine?

Nothing is established. Wolfs Gaming Blog reports that people who already downloaded the archive believe it is real game data, but no independent check has been published. The outlet also notes the files could come from the retail Xbox release, on sale today, and that nobody has shown the build running on PC. We publish no link and have not downloaded these files.

The executable is tied to a crypto token: per Wolfs Gaming Blog, it would be released early if the token reaches a $15 million market cap, down from the $30 million target set in October. The group had announced "the end of the road" on 14 October. The site advises against paying anything.

How has Take-Two responded so far?

VGC recalls that Take-Two said its efforts to track down the leaker were "rapidly evolving", with several subpoenas sent to Discord. The 2022 hacker's case also moved this week: see our article on Arion Kurtaj.

Why it matters

For players, nothing changes: the game is on sale and the claimed files do not run. For Rockstar, a leak of this size, if confirmed, would be the heaviest since 2022. We will say no more until authenticity is checked; release details stay on the release page.
