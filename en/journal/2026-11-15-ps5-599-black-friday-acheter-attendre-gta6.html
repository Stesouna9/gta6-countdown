GTA 6 PS5 deal: buy at 599 € now or wait for Amazon Black Friday?

On November 15 PS5 consoles sit near Sony list prices at Amazon France; Amazon Black Friday runs November 19 to 30, per Kingofgeek.

Sources kingofgeek.com

J-3 GTA 6 releases in 3 days →

Four days before GTA 6, buy a PS5 now or wait for Black Friday? Kingofgeek notes on November 15 that PS5 consoles are in stock at Amazon France, but at prices close to Sony's official ones, with no notable discount.

Reference prices cited: 599.99 € for the PS5 Slim Digital, 649.99 € for the Slim with disc drive and 899.99 € for the PS5 Pro. On Amazon the digital model sits around 599 € and the disc model around 649 to 650 €.

When does Black Friday really start?

According to Kingofgeek, Amazon France set its Black Friday week for November 19 to 30. First deals therefore land on GTA 6 launch day, while official Black Friday is November 27. The site notes no discount is guaranteed on the console, nor delivery before the weekend.

Buy now or wait?

To play on November 19, the outlet finds a console at official price, from an identifiable seller and deliverable in time, reasonable. Paying above Sony's price out of fear of shortage would be "hard to justify". We flagged that risk in our piece on the PS5 Pro above 1,500 € at resellers.

Another option: Sony-certified refurbished PS5 units, with a Slim disc model at 549.99 € in the official program, subject to availability. The difference between the two Slims is mostly the drive: the digital one costs about 50 € less but forces digital purchases.

On PS5 Pro, Kingofgeek reminds readers that no 60 FPS GTA 6 mode is guaranteed. What the files show is in our piece on a possible 40 fps mode on PS5 Pro.

Why it matters

The calendar leaves no day of margin: any promotion arrives on the 19th, launch day. Rockstar confirms PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, no PC version announced. The game costs 79.99 € standard (prices), details on release. Prices and stock change by the visit: compare final price and seller.
