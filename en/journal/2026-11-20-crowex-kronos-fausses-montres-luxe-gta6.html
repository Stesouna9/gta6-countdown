GTA 6: Crowex, Kronos and Pendulus, the fake luxury watches of Vice City

Le Figaro reports GTA 6 shows no real brands: Crowex, Pendulus, Covgari and Vangelico watches echo Rolex or Patek designs without naming them.

Sources lefigaro.fr

Look at the wrists in Vice City: they say a lot about American society. Le Figaro looked into the watches of GTA 6 and a series rule that still holds: no product placement, invented brands instead.

Which watch brands exist in GTA 6?

According to Le Figaro, you can spot famous models from houses like Rolex, Audemars Piguet, Patek Philippe, Omega, Richard Mille and even G-Shock at first glance. But the game shows Crowex, Pendulus, Covgari or Vangelico, with enough details changed to avoid trademark counterfeiting. The case shape, the bezel and the bracelet links recall the original without being mistaken for it.

Why doesn't Rockstar use real brands?

The paper recalls that product placement is "doubly impossible" in GTA: violence, a critical view of society, satire. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has always ruled it out, writes Le Figaro, especially since real brands could limit freedom of action in the open world. The paper adds that the revenue of the game and its spin-offs makes product placement pointless.

This parody-brand logic runs through the game: we saw it with the RydeMe fast travel, a wink at a very real service.

A Miami ad with a recognisable Omega

Le Figaro notes one detail: in the ad campaign Rockstar ran in the streets of Miami Beach, the fake ad for Kronos Celestial watches showed an Omega Speedmaster, Omega logo included, with the slogan "always comme early". The paper says US law broadly protects cultural or commercial references in a work of fiction, and there is no intent to deceive since the product is virtual. So far, no brand is reported to have sued the game's publishers for trademark infringement, per the paper.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for players: the satire stays intact and the game keeps making up its own brands, according to Le Figaro. For more curiosities from the campaign, see our goodies page.
