GTA VI The Album: Cardi B and Fuerza Regida release the two newest singles

Rockstar has put out Cardi B's "Don't Chart" and Fuerza Regida's "Suzuki" on streaming services. Eight tracks from the album can now be heard.

Sources xboxygen.com · rockstargames.com

When I drive at night, the radio decides the route. Rockstar has just added two songs to the playlist: according to Xboxygen, which relays a post the studio made on X, "Suzuki" by Fuerza Regida and "Don't Chart" by Cardi B are two new singles from Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, available now on the major streaming platforms.

What Rockstar announced

The studio's message is one line: "Check out two brand-new singles from Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album – 'Suzuki' by Fuerza Regida and 'Don't Chart' by Cardi B." It also invites fans to pre-order the full album on vinyl or CD. Xboxygen reports that the final tracklist will have 34 original songs, including Travis Scott, PinkPantheress and Keith Richards, and that the first eight are already out.

Vice City on the airwaves

The singles land hours after the reveal of the six radio stations, which Rockstar details on its Newswire. A useful reminder: the album is separate from the radio. It collects original tracks, while the stations will mix songs, hosts and ads inside the game. Xboxygen also notes that Flash FM, one of the series' most iconic stations, is coming back, which fits Rockstar's habit of winking at Vice City.

The two names are not random. A New York rapper and a Mexican corridos tumbados band: the pairing says a lot about the sound of Rockstar's Florida, a state where Spanish is heard as often as English. Where these songs will sit in the stations is unclear. Rockstar has not said, and we do not know whether the singles will play in-game as released.

What is still unknown

The full list of 34 tracks has not been published, nor the running order. The album arrives on November 19, the same day as the game on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. We keep the details of revealed songs on our music page.

Why it matters

Until now Rockstar has handed out artists drop by drop. Eight playable tracks is nearly a quarter of the album: you can start judging the mood, and guessing who else will make the credits. Personally, I am saving "Don't Chart" for the ring road at night, windows down.
