GTA 6 leaker handed an indefinite hospital order by a London court

Retrospective: written in October 2026 from the sources of the time

On December 21, 2023 a London court ordered the indefinite hospitalisation of Arion Kurtaj, 18, who broke into Rockstar in 2022.

Sources bbc.com · eurogamer.net

A London court sentenced Arion Kurtaj, 18, on Thursday, December 21, 2023 to an indefinite hospital order for the September 2022 intrusion at Rockstar. According to the BBC, he will stay in a secure hospital for life unless doctors judge he is no longer a danger.

A break-in with a hotel TV

For a physicist, the story is almost comic. The BBC says Kurtaj was on bail for hacks against Nvidia and BT/EE, housed under police protection in a Travelodge. His laptop had been seized; he still attacked Rockstar using an Amazon Firestick, the room's TV and a phone. He took 90 clips of the unreleased GTA 6, posted in the internal Slack to demand contact on Telegram within 24 hours or he would release the source code, then put everything online as TeaPotUberHacker. Eurogamer repeats those details and dates the intrusion to September 2022.

A trial without a guilty verdict

Kurtaj has severe autism and was judged unfit to stand a normal trial: the jury only had to decide whether he did the acts, not whether he meant to commit crimes. Eurogamer says a court found in August that he and a younger accomplice took part in the Lapsus$ attacks, which included blackmail and fraud. That 17-year-old accomplice was found guilty at the end of the same six-week trial at Southwark Crown Court. The judge stressed there were real victims; Rockstar told the court the attack cost it $5 million plus thousands of staff hours, and the BBC puts the group's total damage at nearly $10 million. The defence argued the trailer's success showed no serious harm. A psychiatric assessment quoted by the BBC says he kept expressing the intent to return to cyber-crime.

What we know since

Rockstar showed the first Grand Theft Auto VI trailer on December 4, and the BBC notes it had 128 million views in four days. The game is set for November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, preload November 12, PEGI 18, €79.99 standard and €99.99 Ultimate. No PC version is announced. See our release page.

Why it matters

The ruling closes the main legal chapter of the 2022 leak. It tells us nothing about the game, but it recalls that the stolen videos were work in progress, now replaced by Rockstar's own footage. There is also an engineering lesson: security that gives way to a Firestick and a hotel TV deserves a second look. For gtavifrance readers a chapter ends; what counts now comes from the studio.
