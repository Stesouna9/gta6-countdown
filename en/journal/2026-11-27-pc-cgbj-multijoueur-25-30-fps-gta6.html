GTA 6 PC: pirate CGBJ port shows multiplayer at 25-30 FPS, unverified

A private build of the pirate CGBJ port reportedly runs multiplayer with shared cars and proximity voice chat at 25-30 FPS, according to Frandroid. Nothing is public or verified.

Sources frandroid.com

A pirate GTA 6 PC port now reportedly shows multiplayer, a mode Rockstar has not released yet. Frandroid reports it from a video the modder TeK posted on X on Thursday evening.

What does the CGBJ video show?

Two cars pass in a Leonida street while their drivers talk over a microphone. The caption claims "working" multiplayer with characters, vehicles and proximity voice chat at 25-30 FPS. Frandroid says the build is a private version of CGBJ, the project that combines two other ports, VIctory and XCL, and runs at about 25 frames per second in single player.

The creators give neither the hardware nor the player count. The fan site PortWatch VI says the video is authentic without explaining how, and has not rated the build yet. No multiplayer build is public: the code stays with the group.

Why multiplayer is a different problem

Translating Xbox code and shaders is one thing. Making several copies of the game agree is another: they must constantly settle the position of every character and car. With any lag, one player still sees a car the other has already sent off the road. Frandroid recalls FiveM, whose Cfx.re team Rockstar absorbed in 2023.

What we know, what we don't

We already noted that the CGBJ GitHub repository returns a 404 with no confirmed cause. Frandroid says no takedown request against it has been published. The earlier video already ran at about 20 FPS, with drops in town. The ports rely on a leaked copy of the game, and Take-Two had 3,007 GitHub repositories removed on Tuesday.

Be careful with files too: Frandroid cites Kaspersky, which says it detected more than 27,000 malicious programs disguised as GTA 6. Anything sold as a "GTA 6 PC multiplayer" is at best something else, at worst malware. Rockstar still has announced no PC version, as our release page sums up.

Why it matters

Nothing for players: no build is accessible and Rockstar has not mentioned multiplayer. The video mostly shows that port groups aim beyond single player. The performance figures remain self-reported, with no independent check.
