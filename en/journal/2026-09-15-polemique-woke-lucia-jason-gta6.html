GTA 6: the "woke" backlash over Lucia and Jason spreads online

Retrospective: written in October 2026 from the sources of the time

Since the gameplay footage shown on Netflix, a group of players has called GTA 6 "too woke". BFM lays out what they object to, and why this is not the first time.

Sources bfmtv.com

It is the kind of noise that grows when a game gets too big to avoid politics. On September 15, French outlet BFM reported on a fringe of players who find GTA 6 "too woke", building on a piece by Wired. The starting point is familiar: the gameplay footage aired on Netflix on August 27.

What they object to

According to BFM, the criticism targets the diversity of the characters in the trailers and above all Lucia, the first female protagonist in the series. A scene where she asks Jason to pick her outfit is described by one X user as a "humiliation ritual for male players". Others fixate on words like "sequins" and "cocktails", or on a shot of a shirtless Jason, which they read as a game designed to please women and gay men.

The couple's dynamic is also under fire: Lucia is said to be "too dominant", she is always first through the door, and some regret that they cannot break up with her. BFM describes this as rhetoric with a masculinist tone, picked up by a few French players and video makers who fear it will bleed into the writing. These are reactions from users and creators, not data: nothing measures how many players actually think this way.

A familiar pattern

The outlet points out that none of this is new. In 2018, Assassin's Creed Odyssey drew sexist criticism for letting players choose between Alexios and Kassandra. And Grand Theft Auto has always been a satire of the modern world, often attacked for its violence, vulgarity or nudity. What changes is the angle: this time it is accused of politics, not cynicism.

To our knowledge Rockstar has not responded. The debate over the couple and the tone of the game continues on our community page.

What we know since

The controversy did not move the schedule: GTA VI launches on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preload on November 12, a PEGI 18 rating, and prices of 79.99 € for the standard edition and 99.99 € for Ultimate. No PC version has been announced.

Why it matters

It changes nothing about the game itself and a lot about the mood around launch. With two protagonists, one of them a woman, Rockstar is making a choice the series never made before, and the argument will follow the release. Judging the writing from three clips is risky: on November 19 we will see what Lucia and Jason really have to say.
