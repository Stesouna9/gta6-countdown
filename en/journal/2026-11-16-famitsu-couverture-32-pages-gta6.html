GTA 6 on Famitsu cover: a 32-page feature on sale November 19

Japanese magazine Famitsu puts GTA 6 on its cover on November 19, with a 32-page feature and a Rockstar retrospective, according to two X accounts.

Sources x.com · x.com

Japanese magazine Famitsu puts GTA 6 on the cover of its next issue, on sale November 19 in Japan, according to the GTAVI_Countdown account on X, echoed by GTA VI Hub FR. The special feature reportedly runs 32 pages. Rockstar has said nothing on the subject: the information comes from these two accounts, not from an official statement.

What is inside the Famitsu issue?

Per both accounts, the magazine plans a detailed look at the game and a retrospective on the history of Rockstar Games. The exact content is unknown: no image of the table of contents has been published yet, so we cannot say whether new details or screenshots are included.

Not Famitsu's first time

GTAVI_Countdown notes Famitsu already ran a preview of the game in August. The Japanese gaming reference keeps close track of the title as the worldwide launch nears. The game releases November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; preloading opened November 12, and the exact unlock time is still unknown.
