GTA VI: The Album listening parties on November 18, including Fnac Paris

Rockstar has opened a site listing listening parties for GTA VI: The Album on November 18, with early listening, giveaways and a pink and blue vinyl up for pre-order.

Sources x.com · x.com

J-6 GTA 6 releases in 6 days →

The night before release, the album gets listening events. According to the account gtasix_ on X, Rockstar Games has launched a new website for GTA VI: The Album, with an interactive map of listening parties on November 18 worldwide. The account RockstarNewsFRA says the French event will be at Fnac Paris.

What is planned at these events?

Per gtasix_, events include early access to the album, giveaways and pre-orders for a pink and blue vinyl. These details come from that account, which summarises the site: our monitoring holds no Rockstar release nor media pickup yet. Exact address, times, registration or capacity: nothing precise in our sources, check the official album site.

What do we know about the album?

GTA VI: The Album has 34 tracks and comes out on November 19, like the game. We already covered the artists with the Justice, Pusher and Gesaffelstein track schedule, and the whole section is on the music page.

Why listen the day before?

November 18 puts these events ahead of the game's launch. Rockstar has not explained the choice in our sources: this is a calendar observation, not a stated intention. The album push does follow the game's logic, with physical venues from Miami to Paris, after boxes in stores and shop operations.

Why it matters

For fans in France, it is a first concrete chance to live the launch in a public place before the 19th. Access conditions remain unknown: until Rockstar details them in a source we have read, do not plan travel on the strength of a single post.
