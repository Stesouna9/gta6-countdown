GTA 6: Miami adds a Sanguich sandwich and a Leonida coffee collaboration

Miami New Times reports two more local partnerships. Sanguich has yet to reveal its sandwich recipe, while Vice City Bean lists its Leonida blend online.

Sources miaminewtimes.com

J-23 GTA 6 releases in 23 days →

Miami's GTA 6 promotion is spreading beyond the Burger Shot signs. According to Miami New Times, Cuban sandwich shop Sanguich is preparing a “Vice City Special,” while local coffee business Vice City Bean has a “Leonida” blend available through its online store. Both partnerships connect Rockstar's fictional setting with businesses in the city that inspired it.

Sanguich announces the project, with details still missing

The Sanguich collaboration was announced on Instagram, Miami New Times reports. Alongside the limited edition sandwich, the business is working with Miami graffiti collective MSG Crew on original artwork inspired by Vice City. The newspaper says the sandwich will be offered at selected Sanguich locations rather than promising availability across the entire chain.

There is no recipe to examine yet. The report does not provide the ingredients, a launch date, a price or a complete list of participating shops. That makes this an announced collaboration rather than a reason to assume the sandwich can already be ordered. Anyone planning a visit still needs those practical details from the business.

The coffee has a clearer offer

Vice City Bean's Leonida blend is further along. According to Miami New Times, the limited edition coffee is listed online for $23, with shipping charged separately and availability dependent on remaining stock. The medium roast combines Colombian and Ethiopian beans, and the shop presents it as suitable for both drip coffee and espresso.

The cacao, apricot and Brazil nut tasting notes reproduced in the report describe the coffee. They do not reveal anything about GTA 6's map, restaurants or gameplay. A branded product sold in Miami is evidence of a marketing partnership; it does not by itself establish that the same shop or item will appear inside Rockstar's game.

Why it matters

These partnerships give the campaign a different scale from a giant billboard or an arena takeover. They attach Vice City to a lunch order and a bag of coffee, using the identities of existing local businesses. Following the Burger Shot restaurant promotions, they also show that the real world campaign can take several forms without every partner adopting a fictional chain's name.

Our GTA 6 merchandise and collaborations page follows that wider rollout. For now, the useful distinction in the Miami New Times report is straightforward: Sanguich has a product to announce, while Vice City Bean has an online offer to check. The report does not establish shipping availability to France or a shared end date for these two promotions.
