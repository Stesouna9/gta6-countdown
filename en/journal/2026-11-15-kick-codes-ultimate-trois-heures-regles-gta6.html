GTA 6 free codes on Kick: three hours of viewing enters a daily draw

GeeksLands details Kick’s Ultimate Edition giveaway: watching earns a daily chance, with no purchase required and no guaranteed game.

Sources geekslands.fr

Watching a stream for three hours can earn a chance at GTA 6 on Kick, rather than a guaranteed copy. GeeksLands has examined the platform’s Sunset Reward Case rules, describing a pool of 10,000 Ultimate Edition codes for PS5 and Xbox Series. Participation opened on November 12, including for viewers in France.

How does the daily case work?

According to GeeksLands, viewers must watch any livestream for three hours. It does not need to be a GTA 6 broadcast. A case then appears beside the viewer’s avatar. Opening it offers a chance to win a game code; cases can also contain chat emotes and badges. Another case becomes available each day, with no opening guaranteeing a game.

The rules described by the outlet require neither a purchase nor a subscription. Paying does not improve the odds. That matters when comparing this promotion with subscription rewards elsewhere: the viewing requirement grants an entry, rather than reserving an Ultimate Edition copy for everyone who completes it.

Who can receive the console codes?

GeeksLands reports that Xbox codes are available worldwide. PS5 eligibility depends on the country registered to the PlayStation Network account, with France among the accepted countries. Winners are notified through the email address associated with their Kick account, and the code is placed in the account’s Drops & Rewards section. Winners have at least fourteen days to respond.

The report lists no announced closing date and says codes will not be delivered before launch. It also notes that the promotion is not associated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two. Our edition comparison explains the versions being sold, separately from the possibility of winning one through a platform promotion.

Why it matters

Our earlier coverage described Kick’s giveaway and Twitch’s planned game codes. These details add a practical route into Kick’s draw. They do not establish equivalent rules for Twitch: according to GeeksLands, Twitch has yet to specify its total allocation, eligible countries or exact delivery process.

The useful distinction is between access to a draw and ownership of the game. Watching for three hours can unlock the former without providing the latter. Anyone participating should use the relevant platform account and keep the random outcome in mind, rather than giving console credentials to an outside page promising an automatic reward.
