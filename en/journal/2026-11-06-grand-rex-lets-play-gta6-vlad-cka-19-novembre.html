GTA 6 at Paris's Grand Rex: a giant-screen let's play on 19 November

Paris cinema Le Grand Rex hosts a 'Let's Play – GTA VI' night with streamer Vlad_CKa on 19 November at 8 pm, tickets from €15.40 to €22.

Sources sortiraparis.com

J-9 GTA 6 releases in 9 days →

GTA 6 launch day will get its own cinema screening in Paris. According to Sortir à Paris, the Grand Rex will host a "Let's Play – GTA VI" evening on Thursday 19 November 2026 in its Infinite room, starting at 8 pm.

Who is playing and how does it work?

Streamer Vlad_CKa will be on stage with a controller while the audience follows the session on a giant screen. It is a live let's play: you watch someone discover the game and react, with a full room amplifying every moment. The event is organised by Iconic Production.

Times, prices and programme

The session is billed at four hours, from 8 pm to midnight. Tickets cost between €15.40 and €22, and booking is compulsory. The Grand Rex also mentions a show, entertainment and a prize draw, without detailing the content or the prizes. Nothing in the source suggests Rockstar is involved: this is a venue and streamer event.

What we still don't know

The game launches on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, but Rockstar has not communicated the exact unlock time. It is unclear when the game will actually start on stage; the session begins at 8 pm and pre-loading will let the streamer be ready in advance. The detailed programme of the show has not been published.

Why it matters

For fans without a console, or who prefer to share the launch, Paris gets a collective evening on the very same night. It also shows that GTA 6 is becoming a public event, closer to a film premiere than a download started from the sofa. Tickets at these prices may go quickly in one of the capital's largest venues.
