GTA 6: IGN is the official guide, with a 2,532-point interactive map

Rockstar picked IGN as GTA 6's official guide: walkthrough, checklists and a free interactive map with 2,532 markers across 81 categories.

Sources ign.com · ign.com

Rockstar Games has picked its guide. On Thursday the studio reposted IGN's announcement on X: the US site is now the official GTA 6 strategy guide, built with Rockstar. Everything is free, from the full walkthrough to the interactive map and progress checklists.

What is in IGN's official GTA 6 guide?

The guide hub gathers a chapter-by-chapter walkthrough with choices and consequences, pages on the new six-star wanted system, the best early ways to make money, things to do first and a full breakdown of collections and collectibles.

An interactive map with 2,532 points

The centerpiece is the Leonida interactive map, built with IGN-owned MapGenie. We went through it: it holds 2,532 markers across 81 categories in three groups. Locations first: 193 ATMs, 24 Ammu-Nations, 57 gas stations, 276 bus stops, 85 standout vehicles and robbery targets (banks, jewelry stores, convenience stores, safes). Then collections (31 WAiNK landmarks, 35 curiosities, 23 sculptures, 29 magazines, 90 collectible cards) and activities: 130 stunt jumps, 202 under-the-bridge runs, 35 fishing hotspots, 42 drone races and missions from the various contacts. The map has a day view and a night view.
