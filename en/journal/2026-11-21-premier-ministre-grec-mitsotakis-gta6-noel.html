GTA 6: Greek Prime Minister criticised for suggesting it as a Christmas gift

Kyriakos Mitsotakis suggested young people ask their grandparents for GTA 6 for Christmas in a TikTok video, drawing criticism as the game is for adults only.

Sources 20minutes.fr

Greek Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis is under fire for suggesting young people ask their grandparents for GTA 6 as a Christmas gift, 20 Minutes reports. The game is rated PEGI 18, meaning adults only.

What did he say?

In a TikTok video posted Friday, the head of government asked: "Which of you is going to ask for GTA 6 as a gift from grandpa and grandma?" The game's logo flashes on screen. He added: "We all know grandfathers and grandmothers rarely say no to their grandchildren," per 20 Minutes.

The video was meant to promote a 400-euro pension bonus introduced by the conservative government ahead of elections planned next year. The nod to the game was the hook aimed at young viewers.

Who is criticising him, and why?

The opposition socialist party Pasok answered: "The Prime Minister is promoting a video game banned for minors just to be trendy." The left-leaning daily Efsyn called it a "blunder", the conservative paper Dimokratia a "tragic" mistake, according to 20 Minutes. The paper also points to a paradox: Mitsotakis's Greece is among the countries restricting young people's use of social media.

One precision from us: the article describes a game "for people aged at least 17", but in Europe the PEGI rating for GTA 6 is 18, as we noted in the story about US lawmaker Tim Burchett. Either way, the message is the same: this is not a children's gift.

Why is GTA 6 now a communication tool?

The game made 3.79 billion dollars in a day, according to Rockstar (see our piece on the official sales). It does not get more popular than that, and public figures have noticed. Police forces were already using it for prevention messages, see our article. Using GTA 6 to talk about pensions is riskier: the reaction ends up being about the game as much as the message.

It is unknown whether the Prime Minister will answer the criticism or take the video down: 20 Minutes says nothing on that. Follow all the launch news in the journal.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game, which stays PEGI 18. But the episode shows GTA 6 has become a political subject. Greek parents already have what they need to answer their kids: the game is not made for them.
