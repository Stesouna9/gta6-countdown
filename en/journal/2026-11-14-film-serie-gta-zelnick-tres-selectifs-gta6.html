GTA movie or TV show? Take-Two's Zelnick stays "very, very selective"

Asked by David Rubenstein, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick did not rule out a GTA film or series, but set the bar very high.

Sources screenrant.com

J-5 GTA 6 releases in 5 days →

Will there be a GTA movie one day? Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick neither says yes nor no. According to ScreenRant, he was asked on The David Rubenstein Show, five days before GTA 6 launches.

What did Strauss Zelnick say about a GTA movie or TV series?

The quote reported by ScreenRant: "We would make a movie or a television show based on our IP if our creative teams were passionate about it. If they thought that would somehow engage and delight our consumers. It's a high bar to do that, and of course there's always the risk of failure. So we don't rule it out, but we're very, very selective."

Zelnick says Take-Two is "very careful about line extensions" because it sees itself as a creative company aiming at "the best entertainment on Earth". No adaptation has been announced or is known to be in the works: this is a statement of principle, not a project.

What was already known about a GTA adaptation

ScreenRant recalls that Rockstar co-founder Dan Houser has confirmed talks happened at various points. In his words the risk was too big, not least because cinema was in a troubled state. One pitch brought to the Houser brothers was reportedly built around a GTA movie, the outlet says.

The commercial backdrop is very favourable: the site cites pre-orders put above 9.69 million units, a record, with a visible lift for PS5 sales. We covered those figures in our piece on digital pre-orders. For the launch-day schedule, see the release page.

Why it matters

Nothing in the short term: no film, no series, no schedule. But Zelnick's wording is more open than a flat refusal. Everything hinges on the appetite of Rockstar's teams, and Rockstar, not Hollywood, keeps control. The subject will probably return after November 19 if the game's success holds.
