GTA 6: Rockstar posts "See you in Leonida next week" a week before launch

Rockstar Games posted "See you in Leonida next week!" on X on 12 November; the game launches 19 November, with no new announcement or unlock time.

Sources x.com · x.com

J-5 GTA 6 releases in 5 days →

Rockstar Games posted a short message on its X account on Thursday 12 November at 5:00 pm Paris time: "See you in Leonida next week!" The post passed 100,000 views within an hour and is on the official @RockstarGames account.

What does Rockstar actually say?

A single sentence, with no image or video. According to @gtasix_, which relayed it minutes later, Rockstar also thanks the community for its support, but only the original post text can be checked here. No new content, date or time is announced.

Which week is meant?

GTA 6 is set to release on 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, next Thursday, so "next week" most likely points to that date. Preloading opened on 12 November. Rockstar has not shared the unlock time and this message does not either. Full details on the release page.

Why it matters

Nothing concrete changes on the schedule: the message mainly confirms Rockstar is holding its date a week from launch and keeping the hype going. The exact unlock time still needs an official announcement.
