GTA 6 interiors: SlashGear reports hundreds of explorable indoor spaces

SlashGear describes more indoor locations and everyday interactions, although its reported total is not an official Rockstar inventory.

Sources slashgear.com

The interesting question about a larger open world is often what happens when you step off the pavement. SlashGear reports that GTA 6 contains hundreds of distinct interiors, ranging from homes and shops to hotels, clubs and an art museum. That is a reported description of the game at launch, rather than a building-by-building inventory released by Rockstar.

Which indoor locations does the report describe?

SlashGear points to places furnished with pool tables, arcade cabinets and jukeboxes, as well as screens showing sports. It also mentions live music in some venues. These examples suggest more activity beyond street level, but the article does not provide a complete address list or claim that every location offers the same set of interactions.

For comparison, the publication puts GTA 5 at around 80 interiors. Its account does not define a consistent counting method for a room, a building or a separate indoor environment. The comparison is useful as a description of perceived expansion; it is not enough to calculate a precise increase in accessible space between the two games or a reliable ratio for the whole map.

What happens inside those spaces?

According to SlashGear, the protagonists’ home includes drinks in the fridge and household tasks such as repairs and painting. Elsewhere, it describes residents chatting, reading and looking at their phones. Those details matter because a believable indoor location needs more than a doorway: it also needs signs that people occupy it when the player arrives.

The article additionally draws on gameranx’s review to discuss multiple ways into certain locations, with small differences in how missions unfold. That observation should remain limited to the examples described. It does not establish that every building has alternate entrances, that every facade opens, or that players can use every object they see. No overall percentage of enterable buildings is established by this source.

Why it matters

For players beginning in Leonida, the useful takeaway is to pay attention to the places beside the route as well as the distance ahead. A shop or venue may offer an encounter that a map-size comparison cannot capture. Our community page follows those discoveries. A dependable account of the full interior network will still require documented locations and a clear method for counting them.
