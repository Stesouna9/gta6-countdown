GTA 6 Grotti Cheetah: a real one is for sale at Selfridges for £1 million

Rockstar and West Coast Customs built a real Grotti Cheetah, on display at Selfridges in London for around £1 million.

Sources fr.ign.com · manofmany.com

The Grotti Cheetah from GTA 6 now exists in real life, with a price tag. According to IGN France and Man of Many, the car built by Rockstar Games and West Coast Customs is on display at Selfridges in London for £1 million.

What is the Grotti Cheetah sold at Selfridges?

It is a real version of the in-game supercar, inspired by the Ferrari Testarossa Sonny Crockett drove in Miami Vice, says IGN France. Man of Many reports a blue-to-pink paint job called the "Vice City fade", lowered suspension, track tyres and a custom-stitched Italian leather interior.

Man of Many mentions a 4.9-litre naturally aspirated V8 but adds that the donor car has not been confirmed. No performance figures are given.

How do you buy it?

There is no online checkout: IGN France says buyers must contact Selfridges through its personal shopping page. The Cheetah is the only one of West Coast Customs' six vehicles currently confirmed for sale, according to Man of Many.

What about the other five cars?

The six real builds are the Cheetah, Albany Buccaneer lowrider, Progen Emerus, Invetero Coquette, Albany Manana and Pegassi Tempesta police car, per Man of Many. We covered the six West Coast Customs cars earlier; the Cheetah's price is the new detail. It is part of the Selfridges takeover we described in the £2,500 jacket article. More merchandise is on the goodies page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game, out on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S: the car is a collector's item for very deep pockets. It does show how far Rockstar's marketing goes nine days before launch. Still unknown: how many will be built and when it would be delivered; neither Selfridges nor Rockstar gave that in the sources we read.
