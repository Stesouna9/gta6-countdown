GTA 6: Miami accommodation searches jump 55%, says Expedia

Expedia France data cited by TF1 Info shows Miami accommodation searches up 55% since 15 October, and 65% for Panama City, Florida.

Sources tf1info.fr

Vice City is making people book trips. According to TF1 Info, citing Expedia France, accommodation searches for Miami are up 55% on the platform since 15 October 2026 compared with the same period in 2025.

It is not only Miami. TF1 Info reports a 65% rise for Panama City, also in Florida, said to have inspired Port Gellhorn, a coastal town in the game. That link is the outlet's reading; we have not seen Rockstar spell it out.

Why Miami?

Miami inspired Vice City, already the setting of GTA Vice City in 2002. The new map is much larger: TF1 Info cites 125 to 160 km² depending on community estimates, roughly double GTA 5. Those are player estimates, not an official Rockstar figure.

The outlet adds that several places in the Florida city were dressed in Vice City colours for the 19 November launch.

From set-jetting to video games

Travelling to the filming locations of favourite films is called set-jetting, and games now follow. Expedia speaks of going "from the controller to the passport" and ties it to a trend it calls "return to childhood" from its 2027 travel report.

Expedia spokesperson Xavier Rousselou said: "With the release of such a cult title, you can see how strong the need to disconnect from daily life and rediscover a form of carefreeness is among adults." Our release page and journal keep following the real places players spot.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game itself, but the figure shows the scale of the launch. One caveat: Expedia measures searches, not bookings, and gives a percentage without volumes. We will see whether the trend outlasts the novelty.
