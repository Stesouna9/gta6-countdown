GTA 6 Mandy actress: Lexie Shoaibi confirms voice and motion capture

Shane the Gamer reports an Instagram confirmation from Shoaibi and points to the published cast list for corroboration.

Sources shanethegamer.com

Mandy's performance has a reported name attached to it. According to Shane the Gamer, Lexie Shoaibi confirmed on Instagram that she plays the GTA 6 character and supplied both the voice and motion capture. The outlet dates the announcement to launch day, November 19, and points to reporting about Rockstar's published cast list as additional support. That is a clearer basis than comparisons between a character model and someone's photographs.

Who plays Mandy?

The account from Shane the Gamer credits Shoaibi with voice work and captured movement, rather than treating her participation as a visual resemblance alone. It describes the performer acknowledging the part herself. This article relies on that published account; we have not directly inspected the Instagram announcement or the complete official cast list for this report.

The distinction matters because the same outlet describes competing claims circulating online. A familiar face in a screenshot does not establish who delivered a performance. A performer's own announcement, supported by a cast attribution as the article reports, gives readers something more useful to assess. Our news journal keeps that distinction between casting information and fan theories.

Where does Mandy appear?

The following details concern an early side mission. According to Shane the Gamer, Jason encounters Mandy during Pipework, a visit involving rent collection and a blocked sink. The report places the tenant in the Keys and describes a repair sequence paired with flirtatious dialogue. Those are specific story details from the outlet's account, rather than information inferred from promotional artwork.
