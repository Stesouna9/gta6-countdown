GTA 6 closed casinos: why Rockstar left Leonida's gambling shut

Rockstar kept both GTA 6 casinos closed because gambling did not fit Jason and Lucia's story, design VP Stephen Donkin told Kotaku.

Sources geekslands.fr

In GTA 6 you can rob a bank but never place a bet: the map's two big casinos are closed. GeeksLands, relaying Kotaku's report, says Zack Zwiezen never got access in more than 130 hours in Leonida, in the story or in the side missions he played.

Why did Rockstar close the casinos?

Before launch, the journalist asked the studio. Design VP Stephen Donkin answered: "Jason and Lucia's story didn't feel like the right place to introduce gambling, especially with the delicate balance we were aiming for across the whole economy."

Zwiezen notes that money genuinely matters, sometimes making it hard to afford a weapon or car repairs. The game also adds BuckMe family savings, where deposited money can never be withdrawn, with a last tier at 2 million dollars. In his view, reloading saves until a jackpot would have yielded tens of thousands of dollars and broken the narrative. The amount saved partly decides the ending, so he concludes the studio was right.

Are the zoo and cemetery unused too?

Not quite. In his count, the story never took Kotaku to an "extremely detailed" zoo, a small northern town or a large cemetery. But the zoo can be visited outside missions: an IGN preview from late August showed players studying animals, each new species ticking a list.

Donkin adds: "The map was built for the long term, with many parameters in mind. So there are a few areas that leave us options for the future, if we needed them for gameplay or story." He names neither the areas nor the uses. For a sense of how dense the map is, see our basketball hoop count.

What is known about an expansion or online mode?

Nothing announced: Rockstar has confirmed no solo expansion, no online mode and no date. Our GTA Online piece sums up what has been said. GTA 5's GTA Online remains the only one running.

GTA 5 offers a reference point, recalled by GeeksLands: a sign announcing an opening soon stood for years outside the Los Santos casino, which only opened in GTA Online on 23 July 2019, almost six years after launch. Even then its games were blocked in more than fifty countries from day one, which the press of the time put down to local law, without confirmation from Rockstar. That precedent signals nothing for GTA 6.

Why it matters

The answer for players is clear: no playable casino today, by design choice rather than oversight. The closed areas are possibilities, not promises. Follow the latest on the journal.
