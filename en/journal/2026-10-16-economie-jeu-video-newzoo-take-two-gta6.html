GTA 6: Newzoo forecasts up to $5.2 billion in first-week sales

Newzoo estimates GTA 6 could make $3.3 to $5.2 billion in its first week, Sport Stratégies reports, detailing what is at stake for Take-Two and the industry.

Sources sportstrategies.com

The projections for GTA 6 are dizzying. According to Sport Stratégies, Newzoo estimates the game could generate $3.3 to $5.2 billion in sales in its first week. These are analyst forecasts, not Rockstar or Take-Two figures.

What do pre-orders show?

Also per Sport Stratégies, about $180 million in digital pre-orders were counted in the US and the five main European markets. A month out, global estimates point to more than 6 million copies reserved on PS5 and Xbox Series, close to $600 million. The outlet adds that Bloomberg reports analysts expecting 40 million console copies and over $3 billion in revenue over the first four months. Even gift cards feel it, as seen in G2A's record sales.

Why does Take-Two lean so heavily on this launch?

The group expects $8 to $8.2 billion in net bookings for fiscal 2027, around 20% growth, with Rockstar about 37% of it. One label becomes a pillar of a listed company's valuation, a dependence Sport Stratégies calls unusual.

Price is central. In the US the standard edition is $80 against the usual $70 for a triple A, and the Ultimate is $100. CEO Strauss Zelnick justifies it with a 300% rise in manufacturing costs, but says the positioning will not automatically apply to the group's other games. In France prices are €79.99 and €99.99, detailed on our price page.

Rivals are leaving November

According to the same outlet, publishers are avoiding the window and moving big titles to September, October or 2027. A few defy it, such as studio Fairy Mount, whose Flame, Forest and Flood launches on November 19 to "make a statement for the small video game scene".

Why it matters

If the game sells at this price, other publishers get a precedent. If it disappoints, the signal reverses. Unknowns: an online component expected in 2027 has not been officially detailed, and no PC version is dated.
