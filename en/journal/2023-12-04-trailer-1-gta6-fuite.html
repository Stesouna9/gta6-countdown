Rockstar releases GTA 6 trailer 1 a day early after it leaked online

Retrospective: written in October 2026 from the sources of the time

On December 4, 2023 Rockstar rushed out the first Grand Theft Auto VI trailer after it surfaced online: Lucia, Vice City and a release window of 2025.

Sources ign.com · eurogamer.net · variety.com · polygon.com

Rockstar put out the first Grand Theft Auto VI trailer on Monday, December 4, 2023, a day ahead of schedule. According to IGN, it appeared minutes after it leaked on social media; Eurogamer quotes the studio's post on X: "Our trailer has leaked so please watch the real thing on YouTube."

What 90 seconds show

The clip runs 90 seconds, Eurogamer says. It opens on the outskirts of Vice City, then introduces Lucia, the series' first female lead, who begins the story in prison. "Bad luck, I guess," she answers when asked whether she understands why she is there. IGN notes the soundtrack is Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road." We also see a couple holding up a store, alligators where they shouldn't be, and scenes shot like TikToks, a hint that social media will matter in the game. The logo ends the video with a single scheduling detail: 2025, no exact date.

One more leak, then a record

Rockstar had promised "early December" on November 8, then named December 5 on December 1. The early release follows, Eurogamer recalls, another video leak over the weekend. The next day Variety relayed YouTube's announcement: more than 93 million views in 24 hours, a record for a non-music video. Polygon adds, citing Guinness World Records, that it passed MrBeast's record in 13 hours and drew 8.9 million likes in a day, a first for a game trailer. Only two BTS videos, "Butter" (108 million) and "Dynamite" (101 million), did better over 24 hours. Polygon keeps its distance from Guinness, a source it calls uneven.

What we know since

The 2025 window did not hold: the game is now set for November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preload on November 12, a PEGI 18 rating, €79.99 for the standard edition and €99.99 for Ultimate. No PC version is announced. Everything is on our release page.

Why it matters

Since the 2022 leak, Rockstar had shown no official footage. This trailer sets the stage (Vice City), the cast (Lucia and her partner) and a tone somewhere between black comedy and social-media chronicle. It leaves the essentials open: price, platforms, exact date. One thing was settled that night: the first image of GTA 6 finally belonged to Rockstar, not to a hacker.
