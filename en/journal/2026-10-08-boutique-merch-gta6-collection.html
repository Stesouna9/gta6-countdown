GTA 6: Rockstar opens its official merchandise store

The Grand Theft Auto VI Collection lands on the Rockstar Store with tees, caps, a Zippo and keychains, and more items to come.

Photo: Rockstar Games

Sources rockstarmag.fr · insider-gaming.com

Hours after revealing its radio stations, Rockstar opened the Grand Theft Auto VI Collection on its online store. According to Rockstar Mag, around twenty products are available, mostly clothing. Insider Gaming confirms the opening and says Rockstar detailed it in a new Newswire post.

What is on the shelves

Per Rockstar Mag, ten t-shirts sell for €29.99, three of them (blue, pink, black) with the Grand Theft Auto logo on the front and the VI on the back, in men's or women's cuts. Then come six caps, two designs in blue, pink or black at the same price, plus two tote bags at €39.99 and two sticker packs at €4.99 (holographic) and €9.99.

The smaller items nod to Vice City: a money clip at €24.99, a Zippo at €39.99 and a carabiner keychain at €14.99. Insider Gaming gives the build details: a laser-etched chrome metal keyring with the Rockstar logo on one side and the VI with palm trees on the other; a money clip with an embossed Vice City logo; a refillable polished chrome Zippo; heavy canvas tote bags.

A collection still unfinished

Rockstar stresses, Insider Gaming reports, that this is not the full collection: more items will be added over time. If something sells out, a wishlist function lets you wait for it to return. Rockstar Mag warns that stock may go fast, which is the outlet's own judgment, not a Rockstar figure.

The contrast with The Goodtime State box is sharp. That collector costs €399.99 and does not include the game; Insider Gaming notes that some users find these items more appealing than the box's contents. We track it all on our goodies page.

Why it matters

For the game, nothing: date, price and editions stay as announced, with launch on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. But merchandise finally has an entry point, from €4.99 to €39.99, where the collector targeted a very different wallet. Rockstar has not given a schedule for further additions, at least not in the sources we read.
