GTA 6 album tracklist reportedly leaks through early vinyl copies

Reports describe a 34-track list photographed on vinyl packaging; the circulating credits remain unconfirmed.

Sources rom-game.fr · dlgaming.fr · thebiglead.com

J-5 GTA 6 releases in 5 days →

Early vinyl copies may have revealed the full GTA VI: The Album tracklist. Rom-Game.fr reports that records displayed by shops exposed a list of 34 tracks, while DL GAMING and The Big Lead also cover the alleged leak. Multiple reports make this a substantial music story, although they do not amount to Rockstar authenticating every credit on the circulating image.

Where did the tracklist come from?

Rom-Game credits video game music website NOWPLAYING with spotting the early disclosure. DL GAMING describes a photograph of the back of the vinyl release, with the songs arranged across four sides. That makes the reported source physical packaging photographed before release, rather than a complete tracklist published directly by Rockstar.

For record collectors, that difference matters. An image can offer an early look at what a release might contain, but buyers should still check the final product listing and official credits. The names and collaborations described here remain reported details wherever the consulted sources do not establish an official confirmation.

Which artists appear in the reports?

The lists discussed by Rom-Game and DL GAMING include Travis Scott, Dua Lipa, Jennie, Lana Del Rey and Playboi Carti. They also describe two Justice collaborations, one of which would bring together Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. We are leaving out the complete sequence so readers can decide how much of the musical reveal they want before release.

These reports concern the companion album. They should not be read as an exhaustive inventory of the game's radio stations or licensed music. An album credit also does not establish that a musician hosts a station or appears as a character. Our music page follows the broader soundtrack, while the radio page covers that separate part of the experience.

Why it matters

The album is due on November 19, alongside the game. Today's development is the circulation of a detailed proposed running order, now covered by several outlets, rather than a fresh announcement by Rockstar. Listeners have something more specific to discuss, but the final credits still deserve to be checked against an official release.

The different musical events around launch can easily blur together. A festival line-up, a listening party and the album itself answer different questions about where and how to hear the music. Our news journal keeps those developments in context. For now, treat the photographed tracklist as a reported early disclosure and preserve the distinction between a widely repeated image and an authenticated set of credits.
