GTA 6 garages: VICE counts 18 vehicles across standard storage

VICE’s guide separates 16 garage slots from two personal vehicles and places garage purchases behind a Chapter 2 mission.

Sources vice.com

Planning a GTA 6 vehicle collection starts with a storage question. VICE puts the standard story and purchasable-garage total at 18 vehicles: 16 garage spaces plus two personal vehicles. Its guide also notes that motorcycles use the same spaces as cars. A mixed collection therefore needs the same attention to capacity as a lineup made entirely of cars.

When do garage purchases become available?

According to VICE, Chapter 2's Daily Driver mission is the relevant milestone. It grants the Port Gellhorn Station Garage and opens the Vice City Real Estate phone app. The purchase system is presented as a progression unlock, rather than an option every new player should expect to find immediately.

The outlet divides the 16 spaces between eight purchasable garages and four story garages. It separately mentions bonus storage associated with rewards or particular offers. That makes the headline total a baseline for the route described, rather than a universal ceiling covering every edition, reward and account circumstance.

How much storage do you actually need?

VICE lists Mitchell Auto Center as the cheapest purchase at 4,000 in-game dollars and gives 160,000 dollars as the combined full price for all eight paid garages. Those are game-currency amounts. They describe very different goals: obtaining one useful extra space and buying the entire set.

The more helpful decision is which vehicles deserve a permanent place. Keeping a favourite motorcycle has the same capacity cost as keeping another car. Buying every available property may suit a collection project, but the reported total should not become an assumed requirement for enjoying the story. Capacity is a planning tool, not a measure of how correctly someone is playing.

Readers comparing experiences can also visit our community page. The details here are attributed to the published guide; they are not the result of an independent check of every garage, purchase or unlock condition. That distinction matters whenever a route depends on story progress.

Why it matters

Having somewhere to park, unlocking property purchases and being able to keep a particular vehicle are separate questions. Treating them as one can make a missing option look like a problem when the player has simply not reached the relevant mission. Separating them makes the guide easier to use without promising that every desirable car can immediately join the collection.

For an early player, the useful reference is the Chapter 2 mission identified by VICE. For a collector comparing saves, the important qualification is bonus access. Two different totals do not necessarily contradict one another if the underlying rewards and offers differ. The baseline needs to travel with those qualifications.
