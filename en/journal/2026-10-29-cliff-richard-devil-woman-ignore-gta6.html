GTA 6: Cliff Richard, 86, thinks the game with Devil Woman is a TV show

Cliff Richard, whose Devil Woman is on the GTA 6 soundtrack, told The Times he thinks the game is a TV game show, GamesRadar reports.

Sources gamesradar.com

He sings on one of the year's biggest releases and does not know what it is. According to GamesRadar, citing an interview Cliff Richard gave The Times (relayed by IGN), the 86-year-old British singer knows his song Devil Woman is in "GTA 6", but files it under television.

What did Cliff Richard say about GTA 6?

He says family told him: "I was told this happened! My nephew, who has two boys, said they came screaming to him, 'Dad, it's Uncle Cliff!' They didn't realise I could sing like that." Then the line GamesRadar highlights: "The show is a game show, isn't it, really? One of the biggest on TV?"

The mix-up is charming, and it says something about the game: its name reaches far beyond players. According to GamesRadar's writer, the song can be heard in the "Extended Look" trailer, and he admits he has had it on repeat since.

Why is a 1970s rock singer on the GTA 6 soundtrack?

Rockstar has always mixed new and heritage tracks on its radio stations. For GTA VI the studio announced 34 songs for the November 19 album and Justice's instrumental score, 50 tracks on December 11. Devil Woman, from 1976, is one of Cliff Richard's best-known UK hits. GamesRadar notes his popularity never reached the same international heights.

The singer takes it in stride: "People will no longer just say, 'Oh, Cliff Richard is from the past.' Because I am, but that past is what makes everything what it is today."

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game: Devil Woman was already audible in the trailer, and Rockstar has not published a full Vice City station list. The story does show the soundtrack's pull on people who never play. More artist anecdotes are collected in the site journal.
