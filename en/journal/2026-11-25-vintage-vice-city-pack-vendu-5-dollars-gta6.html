GTA 6 Vintage Vice City Pack: the preorder bonus now sells for $5

The Vintage Vice City Pack given to GTA 6 preorders is now sold separately for $5, according to Notebookcheck citing Insider Gaming.

Sources notebookcheck.biz

The GTA 6 preorder bonus was not exclusive. According to Notebookcheck, citing Insider Gaming, Rockstar now sells the Vintage Vice City Pack on its own for about $5.

What is in the Vintage Vice City Pack?

Per Notebookcheck, the pack includes extra outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, a weapon skin, a classic sedan, the 1955 Vapid Stanier, and a garage near Ocean Beach to store loot. Preorders opened in June 2026, and Rockstar said at the time that anyone preordering before November 20 would get the pack at no extra cost.

Did Rockstar promise exclusivity?

No, and that is the nuance. The outlet stresses that Rockstar never guaranteed the content would be preorder-only. Some players assumed so anyway: a Reddit thread from about three months ago, cited by Notebookcheck, says "Only with preorder. They said so", and another user gave it as the reason for preordering. Since exclusive content as a preorder incentive is common in the industry, the disappointment is understandable, though no promise was broken.

In practice, the preorder advantage boils down to a $5 saving, per the outlet. The game's price is unchanged: €79.99 for the standard edition, details on prix.html.

Why it matters

Nothing disappears for those who preordered, but the "reserved bonus" argument is gone. Everyone else can get the pack cheaply. We have no Rockstar statement on the change: the information rests on Insider Gaming's findings relayed by Notebookcheck.
