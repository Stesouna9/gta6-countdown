GTA 6: the Hyde Island easter egg, a Saw-style hunt

Lummox County: Hyde Island, a kidnapping and a hunt. PowerPyx and Dexerto describe the sequence and the FF-50 rifle reward.

Sources powerpyx.com · dexerto.com

Spoiler warning: the hidden encounter itself. Direct answer: the Hyde Island easter egg starts on that island in Lummox County (PowerPyx), reached by boat along the Moreau River. According to PowerPyx (23/11/2026) and Dexerto, you get kidnapped, wake up without weapons in a container and must survive an armed hunter. Dexerto adds an explosive collar, PowerPyx a dog sent after you.

Steps

  1. Take a boat from the docks just south of the island and go up the Moreau. After a short time on the island you are kidnapped (PowerPyx). Random alternative in Vice City, per PowerPyx: help the woman trying to load a couch into a truck.
  2. Your weapons are confiscated in the container. After the countdown, the container opens.
  3. Grab weapons from the lit areas: a pistol with 2 bullets first, then a shotgun on top of a stack of containers to the northwest. More ammo and an adrenaline shot are further south (PowerPyx). Dexerto also advises grabbing the shotgun first.
  4. Kill the dog, one pistol shot is enough according to PowerPyx (the only source mentioning the dog), then avoid the sniper hunter.
  5. The hunter is heavily armored and wears night-vision goggles (PowerPyx). Aim for the head, or knock him down with the shotgun, a tip given by both sources.
  6. After defeating him, loot his body for the Island Poacher FF-50 sniper rifle then head to the boathouse to retrieve your gear: the cash is paid when you leave the island (Dexerto).

Confirmed vs. unconfirmed

Location, outline and weapon reward match between PowerPyx and Dexerto. To confirm: the cash amount ($30,000 at PowerPyx, $20,000 to $30,000 at Dexerto), plus the dog and the exact weapon stash details, described by PowerPyx alone. If you die during the encounter, you are sent back to the mainland and can restart it by returning to the island (PowerPyx).

This is not an official Rockstar announcement: status "reported".
