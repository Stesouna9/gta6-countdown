GTA 6 on Steam: a suspicious listing, not the PC version

A GTA 6 listing has appeared on Steam, but SteamDB flags it as suspicious and advises against downloading it; Rockstar has announced no PC version, per Notebookcheck.

Sources notebookcheck.net

No, GTA 6 is not on Steam. According to Notebookcheck, a listing with the game's name did surface on the platform, but it is not official and SteamDB flags it as suspicious.

What does the Steam listing show?

The outlet describes a page with no screenshots, developer or publisher, tagged free-to-play, which clearly does not match Rockstar's release. According to SteamDB, the application was added to Steam nearly three years ago and stayed mostly inactive until it recently received several updates. The site flagged it as potentially malicious or impersonating another product and advises against buying or downloading it if it ever becomes available.

Is there a PC version of GTA 6?

Not officially: the game launched on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar has announced no PC version. Notebookcheck notes PC players are still waiting for a date. The context fuels confusion: leaker Cyberleek released a roughly 209 GB Xbox build without its executable, which he promises for November 23, and community developers are already trying to run it on Windows. Nothing guarantees they will succeed.

We already covered the Leonida and Xbox2Windows projects; they do not boot the game at this stage.

Why it matters

Nothing changes on availability, but there is a security risk: any "GTA 6 PC" listing or file outside official channels should be treated as a trap. When Rockstar announces something, it will be on its Newswire and official pages.
