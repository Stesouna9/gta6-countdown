GTA 6 Online: Bloomberg Documentary Hints at a Release a Year After the Game

A Bloomberg Originals narrator says GTA 6 multiplayer arrives a year after the game, around late 2027; Rockstar and Take-Two have confirmed nothing.

Sources notebookcheck.net · notebookcheck.biz

J-5 GTA 6 releases in 5 days →

Is a documentary line a release window? Notebookcheck picked it up: in a Bloomberg Originals video, the narrator says "the game that ships in 2026 is not even its final form" and that "the real long-term engine arrives a year later with multiplayer".

The video is titled "Why GTA 6 Took 13 Years and $1 Billion to Make" and features Jason Schreier and other Bloomberg reporters. Read literally, the line puts GTA 6 Online around late 2027.

Does GTA 6 Online launch with the game on November 19?

No. As Notebookcheck recalls, the single-player campaign is playable at launch but the game ships without multiplayer. Our earlier piece on Strauss Zelnick and GTA Online support points the same way.

What did we know before?

In August, Jason Schreier reported that Take-Two and Rockstar did not plan to detail the new GTA Online anytime soon. He later clarified that Zelnick had not discussed a multiplayer launch date. The narrator's line is the first timeframe on record, but it comes from neither Rockstar nor Take-Two.

Read also GTA 6 Twitch giveaway: Ultimate Edition codes planned for November 19–30

What remains unknown?

Nearly everything. Notebookcheck stresses it is unclear whether the narrator slipped out an unannounced detail or simply assumed. No date, price or format (free expansion, update, separate game) is confirmed. We treat this as a lead, not a fact.

Why it matters

For anyone waiting on multiplayer, the cautious takeaway is to plan on the story first. No date is official and Rockstar has said nothing. Everything confirmed about launch is on the release page, and unconfirmed claims stay in Buzz.
