GTA 6 radio: Daft Punk’s Around the World reported on Flash FM at launch

Eklecty-City places Daft Punk on Flash FM and describes a broader station lineup, distinct from the game’s original album and score.

Sources eklecty-city.fr

A familiar track can give a new city an immediate sense of place. Eklecty-City reports that Daft Punk’s Around the World appears on Flash FM in GTA 6. The French outlet describes the selection as part of a broader presence for French electronic music at launch. This is its account of the radio lineup, rather than a fresh Rockstar announcement that we have independently inspected.

Where does the report place Daft Punk?

Eklecty-City identifies Flash FM, hosted by Robyn and Alex Consani, as the station carrying the track. Its description also names Kylie Minogue, Dua Lipa, Charli XCX, The Weeknd and Gorillaz among the surrounding artists. The development concerns the music selection on an already discussed station, rather than a first announcement that Flash FM is returning.

The publication also places Justice and Tame Impala’s One Night / All Night on Stockyard FM, alongside references to Fischerspooner and Róisín Murphy. Those names offer a starting point for listeners seeking electronic music. They do not establish a broadcast order or tell players exactly when a particular song will play during a drive through Leonida.

How is radio different from the original album?

A licensed song on an in-game station is a different proposition from a new recording made for GTA VI: The Album or a cue in the original score. Eklecty-City discusses all three strands when tracing the French contribution. Daft Punk appearing on Flash FM does not by itself mean the duo recorded a new album track or composed music for the game’s missions.

The outlet additionally reports 22 stations and another secret frequency. That broadens the picture beyond the six stations covered in our earlier radio article, which drew on Mashable. Those six previously described names were not a complete verified inventory. The larger total should still be attributed to Eklecty-City rather than presented as a count we have independently confirmed in the game.

Why it matters

For a first drive, the report gives listeners two useful places to begin: Flash FM for Around the World and Stockyard FM for the Justice collaboration. Our music page brings together the separate projects. Keeping radio, album and score distinct prevents a familiar licensed song from becoming an unsupported claim about a newly recorded collaboration, and leaves room to verify the wider lineup as launch discoveries accumulate.
