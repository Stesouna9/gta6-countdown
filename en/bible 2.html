Characters

ConfirmedJason DuvalJason Duval is one of the two playable protagonists of GTA 6. A former soldier, he works for drug runners in the Leonida Keys when he meets Lucia Caminos.ConfirmedLucia CaminosLucia Caminos is the first playable female protagonist of a modern-era GTA. Fresh out of the Leonida Penitentiary, she wants the good life her mother dreams of, and Jason could be her way out.ConfirmedCal HamptonCal Hampton is Jason's friend and an associate of Brian Heder. A laid-back paranoid, he prefers staying home, snooping on Coast Guard comms with a few beers within reach.ConfirmedBoobie IkeBoobie Ike is a Vice City legend: a former street hustler turned businessman, he owns real estate, a strip club and a recording studio, and funds the Only Raw Records label with Dre'Quan Priest.ConfirmedDre'Quan PriestDre'Quan Priest is a young, ambitious Vice City producer and former dealer who co-runs the Only Raw Records label with Boobie Ike and has just signed the Real Dimez duo.ConfirmedReal Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy)Real Dimez is a Vice City rap duo, Bae-Luxe and Roxy, friends since high school, who went from social media to the Only Raw Records label after an early hit with rapper DWNPLY.ConfirmedRaul BautistaRaul Bautista is a seasoned, charming and confident bank robber, always hunting for recruits ready to take the risks that bring the biggest rewards. His recklessness raises the stakes with every score.ConfirmedBrian HederBrian Heder is an old-school drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys. He still moves product through his boat yard with his third wife Lori, and lets Jason live rent-free in exchange for favors.
02

Regions of Leonida

ConfirmedVice CityVice City is the big city of Leonida and the heart of GTA 6: beaches, neon, skyscrapers, clubs and yachts. It is the return of the city from GTA Vice City (2002), fully rebuilt for the present day.ConfirmedLeonida KeysThe Leonida Keys are the chain of islands in the south of the state, home turf of Jason Duval, Brian Heder and Cal Hampton. This is where Jason's story begins, between a boat yard and smuggling runs.ConfirmedGrassriversGrassrivers is the wetlands region of Leonida: slow waters, airboats and alligators, a fictional take on the Everglades presented among the game's six official regions.ConfirmedPort GellhornPort Gellhorn is a port town on Leonida's Gulf coast, smaller and more worn than Vice City. It is one of the six official regions presented by Rockstar.ConfirmedAmbrosiaAmbrosia is an inland town of Leonida, dominated by its sugar refinery and its fields. One of the game's six official regions.ConfirmedMount KalagaMount Kalaga is the great natural park in the north of Leonida: forests, rivers, hunting and wide open spaces. One of the six official regions presented by Rockstar.

Buying the game

ConfirmedGTA 6 editions and pricesGTA 6 comes in two editions: Standard at $79.99 / €79.99 (boxed or digital) and Ultimate at $99.99 / €99.99 (digital only). Same story in both; the Ultimate adds vehicles, weapons and shops unlocked through the campaign.
