How do you open a safe in GTA 6?

Mission and gang operation safes have a fixed code, the same in every playthrough: it is in the list below. Store safes in free roam change every time: threaten an employee or the owner, or look for a note in the back room (Focus mode helps). Without a code, a sticky bomb from Ammu-Nation opens most safes, but it is loud and can damage the loot. Bank vaults cannot be opened this way.

Always type all 4 digits, zero included: 0300 works, 300 is rejected.
