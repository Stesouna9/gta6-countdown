Release FAQ

When does GTA 6 come out?

GTA 6 launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The date is confirmed by Rockstar Games.

What time does GTA 6 release?

Midnight local time, in the night of November 18 to 19. The PlayStation Store listing shows November 18 at 23:00 UTC for France, i.e. midnight in Paris, and regional stores follow each country's midnight. Rockstar has not yet announced an official time for every platform: our countdown follows local midnight and will be corrected if the announcement differs.

When does GTA 6 preload start?

Preload for digital versions opens on November 12, 2026, seven days before release. You can download the game in advance but cannot play before it unlocks.

How big is GTA 6?

The download size has not been published yet. The 200 GB figure going around does not come from Rockstar: keep plenty of space free and check when preload opens.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not at launch. Rockstar has not announced a PC version or date so far.

Is GTA 6 coming to PS4 or Xbox One?

No. GTA 6 is only coming to PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

In France, €79.99 for the Standard Edition and €99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. Editions and stores are detailed on our Prices page.
