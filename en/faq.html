GTA 6: every answer

Short, verified answers to the questions everyone is asking.

When does GTA 6 come out?

November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar confirmed the date on November 6, 2025, after two delays.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

$79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. Pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026, with the Vintage Vice City Pack bonus.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not at launch. Rockstar releases on consoles first; the PC version will come later, as with GTA V.

Where is GTA 6 set?

In the fictional state of Leonida, inspired by Florida, and in Vice City, its Miami-inspired metropolis.

Who are the GTA 6 protagonists?

Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, the series' first playable duo. Lucia is the first female lead of a modern GTA.

Will GTA 6 be in Arabic?

Arabic is not among the 13 languages announced for launch. Players across the Arab world are asking for it with #GTA6Arabic.
