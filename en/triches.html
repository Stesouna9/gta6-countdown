How do you enter cheat codes in GTA 6?

Cheats only exist after the story ends: the list appears under Progress > Cheats, and each code must first be unlocked through a challenge. Then pull out the phone (D-pad up), hold L1 (LB on Xbox), press R1 (RB) and enter the combo on the D-pad. Combos are the same on PS5 and Xbox.

Do cheats disable trophies?

Yes: an active cheat disables trophies, achievements and saving. No code makes you invincible.
