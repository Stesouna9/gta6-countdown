GTA 6 missions: story and side mission list

Every GTA 6 mission: a prologue and 5 chapters, 148 missions (119 main, 29 optional), Strangers & Freaks and story length (60-80 h).

How many missions are in GTA 6?

GTA 6's story has a prologue and 5 chapters: 148 missions in total, 119 main (1 in the prologue, then 25, 18, 31, 34 and 10 per chapter) and 29 optional (sources: PowerPyx and GTABase; 15 titles not yet cross-checked are flagged "to be confirmed"). Expect 60-80 hours. There are no mission medals and no mission replay: keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter.

Strangers & Freaks: 8 strands

You must finish 8 specific Strangers & Freaks questlines (silver trophy): Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count; Shirley, Billy, Andy, Yasmani, Tyrone and Eddie Acosta do not. All remain available after the story.
