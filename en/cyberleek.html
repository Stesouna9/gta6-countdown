Who is Cyberleek?

Cyberleek is the alias of an anonymous leaker who claims to hold a development build of GTA 6. Since October 2026 they have posted clips presented as gameplay, according to several gaming outlets. Their identity is unknown.

Are the Cyberleek leaks real?

Rockstar Games and Take-Two have confirmed nothing. Some outlets find the clips credible, others urge caution. GTAVIFRANCE shares no leaked image or video and reports everything as unconfirmed.

What is the Cyberleek token?

According to IGN and Kotaku, Cyberleek launched a cryptocurrency and tied new leaks to market-cap milestones, up to 30 million dollars. The token then crashed. Do not buy it: it is a speculative asset tied to a leak.

Does the leak change the GTA 6 release date?

No. GTA 6 still launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preload on November 12. No delay has been announced.
