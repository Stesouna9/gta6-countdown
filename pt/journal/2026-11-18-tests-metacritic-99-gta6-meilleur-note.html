GTA 6 reviews are out: Metacritic score of 99 and perfect marks

The review embargo has lifted and GTA 6 sits at a 99 Metascore on Metacritic, according to Frandroid, with glowing reviews and one note on framerate.

Fontes frandroid.com

J-1 GTA 6 sai em 1 dias →

GTA 6 reviews went live on Wednesday 18 November, a day before release. According to Frandroid, the game stood at a Metascore of 99 on Metacritic late in the morning, based on about a hundred reviews. The figure is provisional and may move during the day. Rockstar has not commented on the scores.

What scores did GTA 6 get?

Frandroid reports a "masterpiece" from Bloomberg and a game that "meets every expectation" from Video Games Chronicle, which gives 5/5. Inven Global goes to 100/100 after more than 80 hours. At 99, GTA 6 tops GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 (97 each) and GTA 4, the saga's best so far at 98. We flagged the embargo in our earlier piece, and noted that Metacritic removed fake reviews the day before.

What do reviewers say about the map and story?

The open world comes up in every review. For VGC's Jordan Middler, Leonida is the most immersive world ever built, with NPCs you can interact with and many enterable interiors. Vice, after more than 130 hours to reach 100%, says it may be the best open world ever. TechRadar, at nearly 100 hours, praises the sound work. VGC counts about sixty hours for the main story alone, above the 50 hours reported on Sunday.

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are a criminal couple dreaming of a quiet life. Per VGC, a relationship meter shapes the story and leads to several endings. In most missions you can pick either character or switch mid-action.

Any complaints?

One notable technical reserve, from VGC: the framerate dips in the densest areas. The game targets 30 frames per second on consoles, and the drops are rare according to the reviewer. Digital Foundry's John Linneman calls it the most technically impressive game he has played.

Why it matters

Preorderers have little doubt left on quality hours before unlock. The exact French launch time remains unknown, see our release page. Final Metacritic numbers can still move as more reviews land.
