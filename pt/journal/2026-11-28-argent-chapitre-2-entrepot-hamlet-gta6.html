GTA 6 money: $15,000-$20,000 early in chapter 2 with no fighting, Mashable says

Mashable says two combined methods earn $15,000 to $20,000 at the start of GTA 6 chapter 2, including an abandoned warehouse near Hamlet (light spoilers).

Fontes mashable.com

Money runs short fast in GTA 6, especially at the start of chapter 2. Mashable lays out two methods that, combined, would earn between $15,000 and $20,000 with almost no fighting. The piece contains light spoilers for early chapter 2, and we keep ours to a minimum.

Where do you find $10,000 to $14,000 quickly?

Mashable describes an abandoned warehouse outside the town of Hamlet, reachable from the start of the game. Landmark: an Apollo sign out front, with vehicles and construction equipment outside. Inside, the place is empty apart from corpses and "more than $10,000" of contraband. Grab it all, stash it in a car trunk and sell it to a fence: at least $10,000, up to $14,000 according to Mashable, which tested the method after seeing it at GamingBible.

Mashable advises waiting for chapter 2, since players lose all their money at the end of chapter 1. A fence unlocks early in that chapter, so it suggests playing a few missions first. Hauling is also easier with Jason and Lucia together, given the volume.

What about the second method?

The outlet says this one comes from IGN and that it verified it too. The text we could read stops before the details, so we cannot describe it and will not guess. On X, one account claims a trick worth over $600,000 in five minutes, with no press pickup so far: see our Buzz section.

Why it matters

In a game where car and property prices climb quickly, a few thousand dollars of head start counts. The warehouse method needs no weapon and no police chase, which suits players who dislike shootouts. Nothing suggests Rockstar will fix it, but money tricks can disappear with a patch, so test before relying on it. For the wider news, see the journal.
