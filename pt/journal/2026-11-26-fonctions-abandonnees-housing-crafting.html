GTA 6 cut features reportedly included home decoration and crafting

Xboxygen relays an interview about discarded activities and the room Rockstar wanted to leave for quieter scenes.

Foto: Rockstar Games

Fontes xboxygen.com

Decorating a home and crafting objects were among the ideas considered for GTA 6, according to Xboxygen. The outlet relays a PC Games interview with Rockstar North development director Rob Nelson and design producer Scott Mitchell about features discarded during production. Their account concerns the selection of activities for the released game. It does not announce a schedule for bringing those ideas back through later updates.

Which possibilities were left behind?

Xboxygen lists home decoration, a crafting system and the ability for Jason and Lucia to crawl. Its account says some proposals eventually failed to fit the intended story or gameplay. That explanation matters because an idea explored during development is not automatically a public promise. The report does not provide a full chronology of when each possibility was tested or removed.

Tennis was also dropped, reportedly because Rockstar was not entirely satisfied with the result. Minigolf is mentioned as an activity that survived. The contrast suggests decisions about particular implementations, rather than a broad rejection of leisure activities. It would be difficult to judge the quality of the discarded tennis from its absence alone, without seeing the work that informed the decision.

What about cinema and theatre?

According to Xboxygen, cinema screenings and theatrical performances were removed because the studio considered them insufficiently interactive. That supplies a design reason, but does not establish how finished those sequences were. The account cannot tell us what their final form might have been, how long they would have lasted or whether they could ever reappear.

The report also describes Nelson discussing Red Dead Redemption 2 as an influence on allowing quieter stretches within a story. Those pauses can help develop Jason and Lucia’s relationship. That adds context to our coverage of the decision against mission replays and the discussion around the romantic emphasis. Here the focus is the selection of actions surrounding the central characters.

Why it matters

A list of cut features is most useful when it explains priorities rather than becoming an imaginary update roadmap. These remarks describe developers weighing activities against the experience they wanted to build. They do not establish a future release for decoration, crafting or tennis. Readers exploring the existing game can find further discussion through our community page. The immediate news is about how Rockstar describes its decisions, with the boundaries of that account kept intact.
