GTA 6: Billboard and Rolling Stone confirm the 34-track album list

Billboard and Rolling Stone say sources confirmed the 34-song tracklist of GTA VI: The Album, first seen in photos of the vinyl; Rockstar has not published it.

Fontes billboard.com · rollingstone.com · ign.com

The 34-song tracklist of GTA VI: The Album, circulating since photos of the vinyl, has changed status: Billboard and Rolling Stone say sources confirmed it, and IGN relays it. Rockstar has published nothing so far. We covered it yesterday as an unconfirmed leak.

Who is on the album?

Billboard names Playboi Carti, Dua Lipa, Cardi B, Lana Del Rey, Gunna, Yung Lean, Future, Metro Boomin, Travis Scott, Fred again.., PinkPantheress, Morgan Wallen, Tame Impala and Fuerza Regida. Also on it, per Rolling Stone: Jennie, Zara Larsson, Rina Sawayama, Young Thug, Arca, Tove Lo and a Justice duet with Michael McDonald. Several songs are already on streaming services.

Billboard publishes the full list, opening with Justice and Michael McDonald's "Starting From Zero", and including Lana Del Rey's "Crazy for You" and Keith Richards' "Bright Lights, Big City".

How did the list get out?

Per IGN, a video from a Barnes & Noble-like retailer shows the front, back and spines of the double LP. Rolling Stone calls the record "prematurely sold". Billboard adds that vinyl is sold at several retailers, each with an exclusive pressing and poster.

What is the album release date?

Billboard and IGN say November 19, the game's launch day. Rolling Stone writes Friday, November 20. We keep November 19, the announced date, until Rockstar or Atlantic Records clarifies. Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival takes place November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami, with Cardi B, Travis Scott and Rauw Alejandro among others, per Billboard.

Why it matters

The list is no longer an isolated rumor: two major US music outlets say they had it confirmed. Still, confirmation by sources is not an announcement, and the album is distinct from the game's score and radio stations. Our music page follows the story.
