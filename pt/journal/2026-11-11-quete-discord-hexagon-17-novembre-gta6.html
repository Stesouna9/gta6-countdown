GTA 6 Discord "Hexagon" quest leak points to a November 17 start

A Discord "Hexagon" campaign tied to GTA 6 was spotted in the app's code and would start November 17, per a dataminer. Not confirmed by Rockstar or Discord.

A Discord campaign tied to GTA 6 and named "Hexagon" has turned up in the code of a test build of the app, reports La Crème Du Gaming. Dataminer Wumpus Central found a GTA 6 app icon, a Vice City app theme and a username font inspired by the game's typography. Neither Rockstar nor Discord has confirmed anything.

What is in the Hexagon campaign?

The code mentions a "quest with unknown reward" that "may be coming soon during the campaign too", per Wumpus Central as quoted by La Crème Du Gaming. Discord Quests are tasks where you earn a bonus by playing, watching a video or joining an event. Some outlets already call it a "free DLC", a label nothing supports yet: the reward could stay inside Discord (profile decoration, theme) rather than reach the game.

When would it start?

According to the datamine relayed by RockstarINTEL, as La Crème Du Gaming writes, Hexagon would begin on November 17, 2026 at 18:00 UTC, two days before the November 19 release. Free users could take part until December 8, Nitro subscribers until February 15, 2027. These dates come from a test file and may change or vanish.

The pattern is familiar. The last days before a launch attract leaks, like the 55 trophies or the multiplayer functions spotted in the game files. Each one holds up or not within days, and this one is no exception.

What should you do meanwhile?

Nothing, except check Discord's Quests tab around November 17. The source flags a real risk: fake "GTA 6 Discord Hexagon" links may circulate by direct message to phish accounts. Accept no quest outside the app's official interface.

Why it matters

If confirmed, GTA 6 would get a first promotional reward before release, but its nature is unknown. For now it is a datamine lead, to be handled with care. Verified information is on the community page.
