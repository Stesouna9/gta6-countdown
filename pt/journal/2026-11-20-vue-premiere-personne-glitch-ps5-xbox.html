GTA 6 first-person glitch tested on PS5 and Xbox: how it works

Two outlets report a playable first-person camera reached through a controller setting and phone trick, with visible limitations.

Foto: Rockstar Games

Fontes pcgamer.com · purexbox.com · x.com

A first-person camera in GTA 6 is now being described through hands-on reporting rather than a single circulating clip. PC Gamer says its own Morgan Park tested a player-discovered method that keeps the viewpoint active while moving around. Pure Xbox also describes trying the glitch and says the approach works on both Xbox and PS5. Neither report establishes an officially supported first-person mode.

How do players reach the camera?

The sequence set out by PC Gamer begins in the controller settings. Under Controller Features, the touchpad or select button is assigned to “Camera 2 / 2”. The player then enters a car, opens the phone with the D-pad, closes it with Circle or B and quickly presses Select. The resulting perspective goes beyond the temporary close-up used when looking at the phone.

For Xbox players, Pure Xbox identifies the relevant control as the View button, with the PS5 touchpad filling the equivalent role. Its writer reports being able to run around and drive after triggering the effect. These descriptions explain what the outlets observed; they are not a guarantee that every control layout or attempt will produce the same result.

What are the visible drawbacks?

According to PC Gamer, raising a long gun can bring the character's shoulders through the camera. Breaking into a car also looks unfinished in the footage it describes. Pure Xbox similarly notes that some items do not behave properly from this angle. A usable viewpoint and a fully finished set of first-person animations are different technical achievements.

Getting out of the perspective is another limitation. PC Gamer reports that loading a save restores third-person, even if that save was made while using the glitch. Pure Xbox mentions some situations that can also reset the camera. These are reported observations, rather than Rockstar support instructions. Our release information remains separate from experiments with unintended behaviour.

Why it matters

The trick had already appeared in our Buzz coverage. Two outlets describing their own tests give it stronger footing than an isolated social post. They do not establish when, or whether, Rockstar will release an official version. Suggestions about future updates or paid content remain speculation, and the existence of this glitch cannot settle those questions.
