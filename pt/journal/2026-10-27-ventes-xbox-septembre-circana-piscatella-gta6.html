GTA 6: Xbox Series sales fell 27% in September, Circana analyst says

Circana analyst Mat Piscatella says the GTA 6 showcase did not lift US Xbox hardware sales, down 27% year on year in September.

Fontes blog-nouvelles-technologies.fr

The prospect of GTA 6 is not yet selling Xbox consoles. According to Blog Nouvelles Technologies, citing an interview given by Circana analyst Mat Piscatella to GamesBeat, unit sales of Xbox Series X|S fell 27% year on year in September 2026, the worst September for the line since launch.

What did the Circana analyst actually say?

Piscatella believes the "GTA VI Extended Look" showcase, streamed on Netflix in August, had no clearly visible effect on Xbox hardware sales in the US. He cautions that it is hard to isolate the influence of one game on console purchases: his view comes from trends in the data, not from an exact measurement.

The PS5 tells a different story. In the same interview, the showcase helped Sony's console, but the analyst credits most of the lift to the recently released exclusive Marvel's Wolverine: roughly 70% of the effect for Wolverine, 30% for GTA 6. He stresses the split is approximate.

How does this square with the record Xbox pre-orders?

Blog Nouvelles Technologies recalls remarks by Matthew Ball, Xbox's strategy chief, saying GTA 6 pre-orders on Microsoft consoles would break records. The two claims measure different things: strong pre-orders for a game do not necessarily mean console purchases. Plenty of players can pre-order digitally on an Xbox they already own.

What is known about the marketing around the console?

The launch trailer carries the line "Plays Best on PS5", which Blog Nouvelles Technologies cites as something that may steer buyers toward Sony. The site notes it does not by itself explain the sales gap. Rockstar has announced no difference between the two versions of the November 19 release, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for Xbox owners already equipped: the game launches the same day at the same price, €79.99 standard, with preloading on November 12. The September figures do not prove GTA 6 will have no effect on Xbox sales; they only show that the August showcase was not followed by a notable rise. October and November data will show whether the approach of launch changes the picture.
