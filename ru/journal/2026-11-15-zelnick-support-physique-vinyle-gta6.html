GTA 6 physical release: Zelnick compares discs to vinyl, 'a very small part'

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick says physical games will stay a niche like vinyl, while the GTA 6 box only holds a download code.

Источники darkhorizons.com

J-4 GTA 6 выйдет через 4 дн. →

Take-Two's chief compares physical games to vinyl: a business that survives, but a "very small" one. Strauss Zelnick told Christopher Dring of The Game Business, and Dark Horizons picked up the remarks four days before GTA 6 launches on November 19.

"I predicted that digital distribution would be well over 90% of the business more than 10 years ago. I don't always get it right, but this one I got right," Zelnick says, as quoted by Dark Horizons. He allows there will "always be some physical business", "in the same way that there's still a vinyl business in recorded music", but it will be "a very small part of the business".

How much does physical weigh at Take-Two?

Almost nothing, going by the publisher's latest quarterly report, which Dark Horizons sets beside the quote: physical retail and other brought in 2%, or $38.4 million, of $1.6 billion in net bookings for the quarter. Back in August, Zelnick had already said discs "don't really make sense for the consumer" for big games, the site recalls.

What about the GTA 6 box?

It exists, without a disc. Dark Horizons notes the boxed edition ships with only a download code. That is the logic we covered in our piece on late physical pre-orders: you buy packaging, then download the 212 GB like everyone else. Prices are on our price page.

Zelnick was equally measured on other topics this week, as in his answer on a GTA film or series. His line is consistent: few promises, plenty of numbers.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for November 19: the box stays a code box. But GTA 6 is the ideal real-world test of Zelnick's argument: if nearly all buyers go digital, vinyl will prove the right comparison. The exact physical share for this launch is unknown: neither Take-Two nor Rockstar has published a figure.
