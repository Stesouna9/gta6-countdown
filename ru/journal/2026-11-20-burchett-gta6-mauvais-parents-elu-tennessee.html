GTA 6: US lawmaker Tim Burchett says parents who allow it are bad parents

Gamekult reports Tennessee Republican Rep. Tim Burchett believes letting children play GTA 6 makes someone a bad parent.

Источники gamekult.com

Every GTA launch revives the same debate. Gamekult reports that Tennessee Republican Rep. Tim Burchett attacked GTA 6 in front of journalists, calling it a "practical video game" for committing crimes.

What did the lawmaker say?

According to Gamekult, Burchett claims game violence "desensitizes kids" and that "they've done study after study", without naming one. Asked whether letting kids play GTA VI makes someone a "bad parent", he answered: "I believe so". The clip circulated on X, the site says.

Gamekult points out that it is unclear which age he means: a child under 10 or a teenager close to 18? The answer changes everything. It links the episode to the moral panic over early GTA games and to lawyer Jack Thompson's campaign.

What does the rating say?

The age question already has an official answer: GTA 6 is rated PEGI 18 in Europe, meaning adults only. See also The Guardian's advice to parents.
