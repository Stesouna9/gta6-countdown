GTA 6 PS5 crashes: IGN reports CE-108255-1 error complaints

IGN France describes PS5 crash reports affecting story progression, without establishing their scale or a specific cause in the available text.

Фото: Rockstar Games

Источники fr.ign.com

Some PS5 players are reporting GTA 6 crashes accompanied by error CE-108255-1, according to IGN France. The outlet says the interruptions can leave affected players unable to progress through the story. Its available text does not establish how widespread the issue is or identify a technical cause. These are reports of disrupted play, with a diagnosis still missing from the material we could examine.

How much do the reports establish?

IGN France describes repeated crashes for affected users in the days following release. That does not provide a measured failure rate across the PS5 audience. The collected excerpt gives no documented case count, no percentage of players experiencing trouble and no breakdown by console model. Calling this a universal issue would therefore go beyond the available account.

CE-108255-1 is the shared error identifier mentioned in the report. It is not, by itself, an explanation of what went wrong. We have not reproduced the crashes or inspected technical logs that could attribute the behaviour to game software, an update or hardware. Similar messages appearing on several machines cannot alone resolve that question.

Is there a confirmed patch?

The IGN France excerpt describes players waiting for a fix from Sony and Rockstar. It does not supply a dated official announcement, an update number or a resolution schedule. There is consequently no basis here for promising that a particular download fixes these incidents. The original page was inaccessible during our web check; our account relies on the excerpt preserved by the press collection.

For someone affected, the immediate issue is an interrupted session and potentially blocked story progress, as described by the outlet. The consulted passage does not say that saves are destroyed. It would be misleading to add that consequence, or merge these crash reports with separate discussions about saved-game problems following launch.

Why it matters

The reports warrant specific technical follow-up, with care over what is still unknown. Without an established cause, we cannot present a workaround as a guaranteed repair. Our GTA 6 release page provides the wider launch context. For this PS5 issue, useful new evidence would include an official acknowledgement, a clearer account of affected configurations and patch notes that explicitly identify the behaviour being addressed. Until then, the reported error is a reason to track the issue, not a complete diagnosis.
