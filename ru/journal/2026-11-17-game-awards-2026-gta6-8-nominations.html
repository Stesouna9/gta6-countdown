The Game Awards 2026: GTA 6 earns 8 nominations, including Game of the Year

GTA 6 earns 8 nominations at The Game Awards 2026, including Game of the Year and Best Performance for Manni L. Perez. Official list published November 17.

Источники thegameawards.com · thegameawards.com

GTA 6 is nominated in 8 categories at The Game Awards 2026, including Game of the Year. The official list went live on November 17 on The Game Awards website, two days before the game's release.

Which categories is GTA 6 nominated in?

CategoryUp against
Game of the Year007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time
Best Game DirectionControl Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Pragmata, Resident Evil Requiem
Best Narrative007 First Light, Control Resonant, Resident Evil Requiem, The Blood of Dawnwalker
Best Art DirectionControl Resonant, Mouse: P.I. for Hire, Phantom Blade Zero, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time
Best Score and MusicAce Combat 8: Wings of Theve, Mixtape, Phantom Blade Zero, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time
Best Audio DesignAce Combat 8: Wings of Theve, Control Resonant, Resident Evil Requiem, Star Wars: Galactic Racer
Best Action / Adventure007 First Light, Control Resonant, Pragmata, Resident Evil Requiem, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time
Best PerformanceManni L. Perez (GTA 6) against Angela Sant'Albano (Resident Evil Requiem), Liam McIntyre (Marvel's Wolverine), Patrick Gibson (007 First Light) and Rahul Kohli (Saros)

Why does it matter?

GTA 6 appears in 8 of this year's 30 categories, covering nearly every craft award: direction, story, art, music, sound and acting. And the game is not even out yet: it launches on November 19 at midnight local time on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It is absent from the indie categories and from Most Anticipated Game, since it releases before the show.

Manni L. Perez's Best Performance nomination is the first official confirmation that she is part of the GTA 6 cast. We cover it in a separate article.

When will the winners be announced?

The ceremony takes place on December 10 at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, streamed on YouTube, Twitch and, for the first time, Disney+. Fan voting is open on thegameawards.com until December 9 at 9 p.m. ET. The public vote counts for part of the result; the rest comes from the media jury.
