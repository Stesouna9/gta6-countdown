GTA 6 Ultimate Edition codes are dropping free on Twitch until November 30

During the GTAVI Launch Celebration campaign, Twitch hands out GTA 6 Ultimate Edition codes by daily random draw until November 30, according to La Crème du Gaming.

Источники lacremedugaming.fr

A free copy of GTA 6 is possible, but only as a lottery. According to French outlet La Crème du Gaming, Twitch's "GTAVI Launch Celebration" Drops campaign slips GTA 6 Ultimate Edition codes into some viewers' inboxes, drawn at random, every day.

How do the GTA 6 Twitch Drops work?

The campaign runs from November 19 to 30, 2026 in US time, ending December 1 at 8:59 a.m. in France, per the site. It applies automatically to your Twitch account. Under "Drops and rewards" sit 13 GTA 6 badges and emotes (Bling, Heist and Golden Iguana collections), unlocked by watching at least one hour of live per day on a participating channel in the GTA VI category.

Badges are guaranteed for anyone who watches long enough. The game is not. Ultimate Edition codes are dropped "as a surprise" each day among eligible viewers, La Crème du Gaming reports, citing a Reddit user who received one unannounced. There is no way to force the draw: watching more simply gives more chances to be in the right pool.

Where do you claim the code, and by when?

A won code shows up in the "Drops and rewards" tab as "GTA VI Ultimate Edition – game code", with a button to claim it and pick PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. The claim deadline is December 7, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific, or December 8 at 8:59 a.m. in France. After that the code expires, and your PSN or Xbox account must match the code's region.

The edition is the Ultimate, sold at €99.99 digitally, as detailed on our price page. The same article mentions a similar offer on Kick with its own rules, which we have not checked.

Watch out for scams

The campaign runs only through Twitch's official site and app, with no payment or outside form. A private message or website promising a "free GTA 6 giveaway" has nothing to do with it. ICI warned the day before about fake guides and trapped software around the game: same reflex here.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for those who already own the game, but there is a badge consolation prize and a small real chance for everyone else. We have no figure on how many codes are given out, and the source does not report one: treat it as a lottery, not a buying plan.
