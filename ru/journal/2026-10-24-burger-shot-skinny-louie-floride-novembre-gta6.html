GTA 6: Burger Shot is taking over every Skinny Louie in Florida in November

Rockstar is partnering with Skinny Louie to turn all of its Florida restaurants into Burger Shot from November to the end of the year, per Secret Miami. It has already started at Kaseya Center.

Источники secretmiami.com

Will Vice City's Burger Shot actually serve cheeseburgers? In Florida, yes, for a few weeks. According to Secret Miami, Rockstar Games is teaming up with the Skinny Louie chain to turn all of its Florida restaurants into Burger Shots, announced on social media.

When and where can you find Burger Shot in Florida?

The pop-ups begin in November and run through the end of the year, walk-in and delivery, Secret Miami says. It has already kicked off at the Skinny Louie inside Kaseya Center, home of the Miami Heat, in sections 105, 120, 308 and 324. The outlet saw staff in GTA VI uniforms, with a cap carrying the "VI" from the logo. We mentioned the Kaseya Center Burger Shot on Wednesday.

What about Cluckin' Bell?

The same arena has a Cluckin' Bell stand with fried chicken combos and a mural of Macca the Gator, the game's mascot. Secret Miami says it is not yet known whether Cluckin' Bell locations will be available statewide like the Burger Shots.

The rest of the Miami campaign

The outlet notes the push goes beyond food: a "Welcome to Vice City" sign on the arena, street art in Wynwood, a Goodyear blimp in Vice City livery, and a Heat game against the Bucks on 18 November with never-before-worn jerseys and a special court. Digital billboards in Wynwood also show ads for fictional in-game spots such as Pfister, Zenith Gym and Kowalski. We already walked through those Miami ads.

Why it matters

Nothing is announced for France: the operation covers Florida, and Secret Miami mentions nothing elsewhere. But it shows Burger Shot working as life-size merchandise well before official goodies. The game launches on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
