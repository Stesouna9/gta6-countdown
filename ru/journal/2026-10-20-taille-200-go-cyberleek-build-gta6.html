GTA 6 file size: Cyberleek claims its build is 200GB

Cyberleek says its GTA 6 build is 200GB, over 20% of a base console's storage. It is not the final size of the game, which has not been announced.

How big will GTA 6 be on PS5 and Xbox Series? Rockstar has published nothing. The only figure out there comes from Cyberleek, whose site lists a 200GB build, according to Mashable, which cites PCMag.

Where does the 200GB figure come from?

Per Mashable, an update on Cyberleek's site threatened to release a full build if the leaker's memecoin hit a $30 million market cap. The coin has since crashed. The same message gave the file size of the build the clips come from: 200GB. We followed that episode in our piece on the new videos and the demand for a disc.

I only treat the number as a claim by Cyberleek. Nothing confirms it is true or what the file actually contains.

Is that the size players will see?

Probably not. Mashable says so itself: the build could be older and unoptimized, and the final game could be smaller. A day-one patch could also add to it. The November 12 preload on digital stores will be a full download in any case.

For scale, the outlet notes that 200GB is more than a fifth of the usable space on a base PS5 or Xbox Series X, but several recent Call of Duty titles are larger. High, then, but not unprecedented.

What should players do for now?

No need to clear space yet. Preloading opens on November 12, and the PlayStation or Xbox store page, not a leaker, will show the real size. Known dates are on the release page.

Why it matters

Not much in practical terms: it is a ballpark, not a spec. It does hint at the SSD space to plan for, and it is the first figure, however shaky, to be put forward. Until Rockstar confirms one, GTA 6's file size remains unknown.
