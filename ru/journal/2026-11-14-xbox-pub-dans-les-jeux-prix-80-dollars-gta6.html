GTA 6 at $80: Xbox executive floats in-game ads to hold prices down

Xbox's Jonathan Stringfield told GamesBeat games either get much pricier or find other revenue such as ads, GAMINGbible reports, tying it to GTA 6's $80 price.

Источники gamingbible.com

GTA 6's $79.99 standard price keeps fuelling debate across the industry. GAMINGbible reports that Xbox executive Jonathan Stringfield told GamesBeat that in-game advertising could help contain price rises. He does not mention GTA 6 in the quotes cited; the site draws that link itself.

What exactly did the Xbox executive say?

GAMINGbible quotes him: "We either need to dramatically increase the price of games, which I don't think would be terribly popular with folks, or we need to find other mechanisms to allow access." He adds that advertising, and adjacent recurring revenue such as subscriptions, "allows for an alternative". He wants integrations where they make sense, even at a smaller reach.

Ads in every Xbox game?

Not according to him: "There is zero world where there is intention, written, explicit, implicit, or otherwise, that ads will be an option in all games." He adds it might not apply to every console feature. GAMINGbible notes Xbox has weighed ads since 2022, first for free-to-play games, and now targets ad-supported cloud tiers.

What does this have to do with GTA 6?

The site argues the jump to $80 raised the bar for other publishers, with big projects ahead such as The Elder Scrolls 6 and The Witcher 4. That is GAMINGbible's reading, not Rockstar's position. An Alinea analyst already called the precedent risky, as we reported in today's earlier article. The game's exact prices stay on our price page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for GTA 6: no in-game ads are announced for Rockstar's game, which launches November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. But the remark shows how a platform holder sees life after $80. Whether players prefer higher prices or targeted ads remains to be seen.
