GTA 6: Michel Drucker, 84, says he has played ten hours without sleep

French TV host Michel Drucker told Canal+ he spent the night on GTA 6 and is already ten hours in, according to 20 Minutes. He also praised Justice’s music.

Источники 20minutes.fr

At 84, French TV veteran Michel Drucker is playing GTA 6 and says so openly. According to 20 Minutes, the host of Vivement dimanche told Mouloud Achour on Canal+’s Clique: “I love GTA! I love it.” He says he is “already ten hours in” and adds: “I haven’t slept.”

What did Michel Drucker say about the game?

He explained he was eager to “come back to Vice City” after waiting thirteen years, per 20 Minutes. He says he got “flashed with five stars” on his return behind the wheel, meaning he hit the maximum police wanted level. He also said he is happy with “the kit with the wigs.”

The game launched on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the anecdote shows how far it has travelled beyond regular players. The same 20 Minutes piece mentions retirement homes picking it up too.

What music does he play to?

Drucker is a fan of the soundtrack and wanted to “salute the performance of the group Justice,” whose track with The Weeknd drew attention at launch: see our article on Justice. Asked what he listens to while driving, he named Huntrill and two favourite lines, including “Je vis pas pour l’argent, je vis avec” from the track Anticapitaliste. The full tracklist is on the Music page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game itself, but it says a lot about its place in general culture. When an 84-year-old presenter discusses it on prime-time television, GTA 6 is no longer only a forum topic. Player figures still come from Sony and Rockstar, not from a talk show.
