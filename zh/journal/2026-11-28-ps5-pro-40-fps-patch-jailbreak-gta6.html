GTA 6 on PS5 Pro: a pirate patch pushes past 40 FPS, Rockstar silent

A patch for jailbroken PS5s lifts GTA 6 from 30 to 40-48 fps on PS5 Pro, N-Gamz reports. Rockstar has announced no 40 FPS mode.

来源 n-gamz.com

GTA 6's 30 fps cap is not a hard wall. N-Gamz reports that a Canadian video creator posted more than three minutes of PS5 Pro footage on X on Thursday, November 26, with a counter hovering between 40 and 48 frames per second. Rockstar has announced nothing, and the trick only works on modified consoles.

How does the unlock work?

The footage relies on a patch posted the same day on a PS5 hacking forum, followed by a fuller version on GitHub. According to N-Gamz, it only runs on jailbroken consoles on firmware 13.60 or lower, with an extracted and decrypted copy of the game. The 30 fps limit is replaced by half the display refresh rate, so a 120 Hz TV is needed to aim for 60.

The technical point is elsewhere. On Xbox Series, a Quick Resume bug had already broken the cap, but the game ran too fast, with Jason's animations playing at double speed. The patch author says the engine stops frame time from dropping below one thirtieth of a second, so every extra frame speeds up the world. Lowering that floor would keep game speed normal. See also our earlier PS5 Pro piece.

What do the numbers look like?

N-Gamz says analysis of the clips shows about 45 fps on average on PS5 Pro, with peaks above 50: roughly 22 milliseconds per frame against 33 at 30 fps. The GitHub version reportedly adds a locked 40 fps mode for 120 Hz screens, and lowers internal resolution to 1080p before PSSR upscaling, against about 1260p by default. Which settings the video used is unknown.

The clips mostly show country roads, a bridge to the Keys and quiet districts, where the CPU has headroom. That is the bottleneck: N-Gamz quotes Rockstar's Marc Chambers, who said the PS5 Pro CPU, barely faster than the standard model's, could not run the simulation faster. Panicking crowds and vehicle explosions already drag performance down, as covered in our console graphics settings piece. On a standard PS5, patch users report 30 to 38 fps, dropping to about 25.

Why it matters

The demo shows a 40 FPS mode looks feasible on PS5 Pro at the cost of resolution. Stable 60 fps still looks out of reach, and Rockstar has said nothing. Jailbreaking breaches Sony's terms and requires running an extracted copy, so this is not an option for ordinary players.
