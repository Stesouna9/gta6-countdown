GTA 6 infinite money glitch reported, with early progression limits

TweakTown describes repeatable warehouse loot, but says some players avoid the shortcut and some funds can become restricted.

图片：Rockstar Games

来源 tweaktown.com

Repeating a valuable loot pickup is different from finding a one-off stash. According to TweakTown, GTA 6 players have identified a warehouse loop near Hamlet that reportedly produces around $8,000 to $11,000 per run. The outlet describes an exploit rather than an officially documented earning method. Neither that reported return nor the continued availability of the behaviour should be treated as a guarantee for every save.

What is being reported?

TweakTown places the warehouse northeast of Hamlet in South Vice-Dale County. Its account describes loot returning after leaving the area or manually reloading, with the collected goods then sold to a fence. The significant claim is repeatability: a location that pays once is a discovery, while a stock that replenishes in this way can become a shortcut around the intended pace of earning money.

We previously covered a valuable warehouse find near Hamlet. The amounts from the two reports should not be combined or assumed to measure the same thing. Their sources, scope and described routines differ. The range in this article belongs to TweakTown’s account of the repeatable loop, and has not been independently reproduced by our newsroom.

Why are some players avoiding it?

The outlet describes divided reactions. Some players welcome an easy source of cash; others believe bypassing financial pressure diminishes the early story. That is a selection of responses presented by the report, rather than a representative survey. It offers a useful discussion about pacing without establishing how most GTA 6 owners feel about exploiting their way past the initial shortage of money.

There is also a practical reservation. TweakTown says that access to some accumulated funds may become restricted depending on story progress. The collected text does not provide a complete set of conditions or a chapter-by-chapter breakdown. It would therefore be misleading to promise that every dollar obtained through the loop remains immediately available, or to invent a precise restriction applying to all players.

Why it matters

The decision is partly about the experience players want: a quick balance increase can remove some of the pressure that gives early objectives their purpose. Our community page is a place to compare those preferences. The report provides no confirmed patch schedule and no assurance the behaviour will persist. Its earnings and limitations remain attributed claims, not a universal route to unlimited spending throughout the campaign.
