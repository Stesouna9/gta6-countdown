GTA 6: PS5 Pro sold out at Sony, resold for up to €1,579.99

Sony's store lists the PS5 Pro as out of stock with a one-per-order limit, while a third-party Amazon seller asks €1,579.99, according to GeeksLands.

来源 geekslands.fr

Two weeks before GTA 6 launches, Sony's priciest console is getting hard to find. According to GeeksLands, which checked prices on the afternoon of 6 November, Sony sells the PS5 Pro for €899.99, but its online shop shows it out of stock, with a limit of one console per order "due to high demand".

How much is the PS5 Pro from resellers?

On Amazon only one third-party seller is left, at €1,579.99, promising delivery the week GTA 6 comes out. The same seller listed it at €1,469.99 on 31 October, so the price rose €110 in a week. Another listing from that seller reaches €1,599.99. These are resale prices, not Sony's, and can change hourly.

Is GTA 6 behind it?

GeeksLands notes that Sony does not link the shortage to the game. The timing is suggestive though: GTA 6's PlayStation Store page carries a "PS5 Pro Enhanced" label, while neither Sony nor Rockstar has detailed the improvements. We covered the removed PlayStation France page and console stock worries earlier this month. Still unknown: the actual gain on Pro, since no resolution or frame-rate figure has been published.

Do you need a PS5 Pro for GTA 6?

No. The game launches on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S and also runs on a standard PS5. GeeksLands says Amazon sells the disc-drive PS5 at €649, in stock and shipped by Amazon itself. For the Pro, it suggests waiting for PlayStation Direct restocks, then Black Friday on 27 November.

Why it matters

Paying €600 to €700 extra for a console whose benefit in GTA 6 is not quantified is a hard sell. The game itself stays at $79.99 in the US, see our price page, and preloading opens before launch: freeing 212 GB is a better use of the next two weeks than overpaying for hardware.
