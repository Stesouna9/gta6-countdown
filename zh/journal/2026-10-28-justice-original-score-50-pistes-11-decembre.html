GTA 6: Justice score listing points to 50 tracks and a December 11 release

Press reports identify a separate Original Score listing on Apple Music, with two tracks available to preview.

来源 insider-gaming.com · jeux.ca · gamingbible.com

A New Zealand Apple Music listing puts Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score on December 11 with 50 tracks composed by Justice, according to Insider Gaming. That points to a separate musical release after players arrive in Leonida. It does not change the game's November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it should not be confused with the artist album scheduled for launch day.

One game, two distinct albums

As Insider Gaming explains, Original Score is separate from Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album. The latter brings together 34 original songs and is due alongside the game on November 19. The December listing describes a different collection of 50 pieces. Treating those figures as a single radio playlist would erase the distinction between the projects and suggest a track selection that has not been published.

Jeux.ca also reports December 11 and the Justice credit, while stressing that Rockstar has yet to officially confirm the score's release date and track count. That qualification matters. A store listing is something readers can identify, but its details may still change. The reports do not establish a French retail price or a physical edition for this particular release.

A preview rather than a full track list

Two tracks are already available to preview, Insider Gaming reports: Pusher, credited to Justice and Gesaffelstein, and Flower In A Hurricane. Jeux.ca lists Justice, Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard on the latter. Those names provide a first indication of the collaborators involved without telling us how the music will function during a particular mission.

The complete names of the 50 tracks have not been revealed in the material reviewed here. There is no basis for matching these songs to story events, assigning them to characters or reading an ending into the listing. Nor does the presence of a score tell us which licensed songs will play on each station. Our GTA VI music guide keeps those different parts of the soundtrack in view.

Why it matters

Listeners now have two release windows to keep apart: November 19 for The Album, and December 11 for Original Score according to the listing described by the press. The second would arrive after players have had several weeks with the game. For now, the useful information is the reported schedule, the Justice credit and the available previews. A dedicated Rockstar announcement would settle the remaining uncertainty around the full collection and its release arrangements.
