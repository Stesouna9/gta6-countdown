GTA 6 scams: fake preorder sites and booby-trapped downloads, a warning

Three days before launch, franceinfo warns about fake preorder shops and free GTA 6 downloads: there is no PC version and no official early access.

来源 franceinfo.fr

Scammers are cashing in on the wait. In its "Vrai ou faux" segment on November 16, franceinfo notes that cybersecurity firms have flagged for months a surge of fake preorder shops, free downloads and early-access offers for GTA 6. Three days before the November 19 release, it concerns anyone searching for the game online.

What do security researchers say?

According to franceinfo, Avast spotted more than 200 GTA 6-related domain names in just 24 hours in October, about 10% of them carrying a medium or high risk. It found a fake Ultimate Edition shop modelled on the PlayStation Store. Test bank cards entered on suspicious sites faced attempted fraudulent charges, says Luis Corrons, quoted by the radio station.

Another trap is the "free download". In an investigation published on November 3, Netcraft describes a site offering a supposed full copy of the game. The file GTAVI.exe was scanned by 68 engines on VirusTotal: 34 flagged it as malicious, mostly as a Trojan. As early as August, Malwarebytes had found fake demos that stole passwords saved in browsers. Other sites ask for crypto payments or forms in exchange for "free codes", methods also documented by Kaspersky.

How can you spot a GTA 6 scam?

The confirmed facts rule out most traps. GTA 6 launches on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only: no PC or mobile version has been announced. A site offering to install it on a computer or phone is suspect. There is no official early access either, even with the Ultimate edition: preloading lets preorder buyers install the game, not play it before launch. The exact unlock time is still unknown.

Why it matters

Nothing changes in the game, but there is a habit to adopt before November 19: buy only from the official PlayStation and Xbox stores or a known retailer, never enter console credentials elsewhere, and distrust any .exe file presented as GTA 6. The closer the launch, the more of these traps will appear.
