GTA 6 launch: Notebookcheck reports GTA V Steam activity above 238,000

Notebookcheck highlights renewed GTA V activity on Steam, a separate issue from any official GTA 6 PC release announcement.

图片：Rockstar Games

来源 notebookcheck.net

Notebookcheck reports that GTA V passed 238,000 concurrent Steam players on November 22, citing GTATracker. The publication places that increase alongside the launch of GTA 6. The number describes activity in the older game; it does not announce a computer version of its successor or establish a release window.

What is actually being counted?

The figure relayed by Notebookcheck concerns the GTA V versions monitored on Steam. It is not a census of everyone playing on a computer. The report explicitly excludes the Epic and Rockstar storefronts. Keeping that scope attached to the number matters, because a platform-specific measurement can easily acquire a much broader meaning as it is repeated.

Concurrency is also different from a daily total of individual players. One measures how many people are present together, while the other asks how many distinct people appeared across a period. A headline should not quietly exchange one for the other. The same principle applies when comparing future figures: the time window and coverage need to remain consistent.

Interest does not establish a PC schedule

Notebookcheck connects the increased activity with attention surrounding GTA 6. That is the publication’s interpretation of the timing, rather than a survey of every participant. The data alone cannot tell us whether someone returned out of curiosity, continued an established routine or logged in for an unrelated reason. A large number does not reveal a single shared motivation.

Nor can Steam activity substitute for a Rockstar announcement. Demand, speculation and an official availability date are separate kinds of information. Readers looking for practical release information can use our release page, while the news journal brings together developments and their sources. Those distinctions are particularly useful when discussion of a future version spreads faster than confirmed details.

Why it matters

The report offers a useful snapshot of continued interest in GTA V during a busy moment for the series. Its strongest value is as a reference for later measurements taken on the same basis. It would be a mistake to turn that snapshot into proof that all PC players have made the same choice, or that a new product is about to arrive. For now, the game being measured is GTA V. Any official GTA 6 PC availability still requires evidence of its own, independent of the size of an older game’s audience.
