GTA 6: Travis Scott posts a copy of the game on Instagram before launch

Travis Scott posted on Nov. 11 on Instagram what looks like a physical PS5 copy of GTA 6, eight days before release, according to Billboard.

来源 billboard.com

Does Travis Scott already have GTA 6? On Wednesday, Nov. 11 he posted an Instagram carousel showing what appears to be an unopened physical PlayStation copy, reports Billboard, captioned: "What y'all on? Tryna lock in. Beats, pizza, bring ya own controller. Who down?"

What does the post show?

Per Billboard, the box shows in the first and third slides, which prompted "Travis Scott got GTA 6 before GTA 6" jokes in the comments. Nothing says he is playing: the game is out Nov. 19, and the outlet only notes that preorderers have been able to start the 212 GB preload. Boxes ship this week, as covered in our piece on what the boxes contain.

How is Travis Scott tied to GTA 6?

Directly: also per Billboard, he is on the 34-track GTA VI: The Album with a song titled "Rhyno" and co-headlines the GTA VI festival in Miami with Cardi B on Nov. 21, alongside Don Toliver, Kodak Black and Rauw Alejandro among others. See our Wynwood article for the festival and the music page for the album.

Why it matters

Nothing about the game: it is an artist's post, with no Rockstar statement. It mostly shows that the game's music push, album and festival, runs through the people taking part.
