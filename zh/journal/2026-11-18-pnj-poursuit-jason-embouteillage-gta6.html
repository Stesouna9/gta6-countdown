GTA 6: an NPC chases Jason after he cuts her off, per a viral clip

A driving clip has been spreading since late afternoon: according to a post from the @gtasix_ account on X, seen more than 119,000 times, a driver whom Jason has just cut off in traffic chases after him. The post reads "An NPC chased Jason after he cut her off in traffic in GTA 6" and comes with a video.

What does the clip show?

Per the post, a non-player character takes offence at being cut off and goes after Jason instead of simply taking it. The account gives no location, time or platform. Neither the press nor Rockstar has picked it up as of now, so we treat it as an unverified player clip, not a confirmed feature.

Why is it spreading?

The post has about 3,000 likes and 147 reposts. With release set for November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, players are watching how bystanders react, a detail the series has always cared about. Nothing says whether this is common behaviour or a rare case triggered by the situation. Such clips also land in our Buzz section.

Why it matters

Nothing confirmed yet, but worth watching: if more players film bystanders chasing them after a collision, it would show traffic in GTA 6 reacts to how you drive. We will wait for a second source before calling it a rule. Release details are on our release page.
