GTA 6: Pocketpair gives staff five days off to play at launch in November

The Palworld studio is setting aside November 19 to 23 for employees planning to play, according to IGN and Push Square.

来源 fr.ign.com · pushsquare.com

Palworld developer Pocketpair is giving employees five days off to play GTA 6, according to IGN France. The Japanese studio describes the break as a business trip to Vice City. Behind that joke is a specific workplace announcement: staff who intend to play Rockstar's game will have time set aside for its arrival on November 19.

A break beginning on launch day

Push Square reports that the leave runs from November 19 through November 23. Its account specifies that the special holiday is for employees planning to play Grand Theft Auto VI. That qualification matters. This is an arrangement announced by one developer, rather than a national holiday or an industry rule that applies to everyone working in games.

IGN France also reports that Pocketpair shared a letter apparently addressed to its own staff. The available reports establish the announcement and its purpose, but they do not explain every administrative detail. They do not describe how individual teams will arrange their work during the break or provide a complete set of internal leave rules.

Making room to play

For a company that makes video games, openly reserving time to enjoy another developer's release is a notable gesture. It recognises that the people building games also want an opportunity to experience them. Instead of leaving that enthusiasm entirely to evenings and weekends, the announcement gives it a place in the working calendar.

Five consecutive days should not be read as a statement about GTA 6's length. Neither report provides evidence that this is the time required to finish the story, explore the map or see everything Rockstar has built. A company's leave schedule cannot establish any of those details. The same caution applies to access: the announcement does not establish that Pocketpair has an advance copy or a partnership with Rockstar.

The interesting point is therefore the decision itself. Pocketpair has publicly made space for staff who want to join the launch. The reports describe a concrete response to anticipation for the game, without providing a basis for broader claims about how every studio or employer will react.

Why it matters

Players outside Pocketpair still face the same GTA 6 release schedule: November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. This leave announcement adds no platform, release-hour detail or early-access promise. What it does offer is an example of a games company treating a major release as a shared occasion worth planning around. Its significance lies in those five days of announced leave, rather than in any unreported consequence for the game's development or launch.
