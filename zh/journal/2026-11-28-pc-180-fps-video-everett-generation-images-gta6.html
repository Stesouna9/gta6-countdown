GTA 6 PC: the 180 FPS video looks like frame generation, not a real port

Frandroid notes the 180 FPS clip from D Everett Hinton always shows exactly six times the real 30 fps, a frame generation signature; no code has been published.

来源 frandroid.com

A video has circulated on X since Thursday afternoon: GTA 6 supposedly runs six times better on a pirate PC port than on PS5 Pro. Frandroid examined it closely and concludes the numbers do not hold up. There is no official PC version: Rockstar has announced none.

What does the video claim?

Its author, D Everett Hinton, claims 30 native frames per second boosted to 180 with frame generation, in HDR10+, with a "completely rewritten" graphics engine and Nvidia Reflex. He adds that most other clips online are fake, unlike his. Frandroid notes his name appears in none of the projects listed by sites tracking the port race, and no code has been published.

Why is the figure suspect?

Per Frandroid, the clip only covers the indoor prison prologue, far from open-world load or a car chase. It is sold as 32:9 ultrawide but sits in a 1080p file with black bars. The author himself concedes the 30 fps is measured at 16:9, not at 5,120 by 1,440. The counter is homemade and names no GPU or CPU.

A more telling detail: the displayed number is always exactly six times the real rate (30 gives 180, 27.6 gives 165.6). Frandroid sees DLSS 4.5's 6X mode, where RTX 50 cards invent five frames for each rendered one. The game still runs at 30 fps and controller response does not improve. The outlet finds it plausible that this is a console capture run through frame generation, a trick already shown in mid-November with a PS5 Pro feeding an RTX 5090 PC. That is a hypothesis, not proof.

Where are the real projects?

Frandroid says no project is near 30 fps in open world, and GitHub has posted no new Take-Two takedown since Tuesday's wave (3,007 repositories removed). We covered the more modest demos in our CGBJ 20 FPS piece and the 60 FPS cutscene. Fake ports are a risk too: see Bitdefender's warning.

Why it matters

Nothing changes officially: no PC version is announced. The lesson for players is to distrust a homemade frame counter and to download no "port". See also our release page.
