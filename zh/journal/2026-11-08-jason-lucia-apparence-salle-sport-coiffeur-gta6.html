GTA 6 Jason and Lucia: weight, muscle, haircuts and how they change

Per GTA BOOM, which draws on press previews, food, exercise and sleep change Jason and Lucia's bodies in GTA 6, and barbers change their look.

来源 gtaboom.com

In GTA 6, Jason and Lucia do not keep the same look from start to finish: their habits leave marks on their bodies and outfits. That is what GTA BOOM lays out, drawing on previews already published by the press. The site itself notes that those previews give no numbers and no duration for the changes.

Do Jason and Lucia's bodies really change?

Yes, according to GTA BOOM. Overeating adds weight, the gym builds muscle and strength, and eating restores health. In the demonstrated apartment, coffee boosts stamina and a protein shake restores health. Both characters can work out alone or together.

Lifestyle cuts the other way too: long police pursuits, time away from bed and multi-day benders can leave visible effects on faces and bodies. The site adds that the inspected previews say nothing about exact gains, limits, timing or reversibility.

Barbers and clothing stores: what is confirmed?

At home, Jason can shave. A shorter cut is possible at home, but a new style means visiting a salon or barber. Clothing stores and hair salons offer different options from place to place: some styles are standard, others specific to one shop, so visiting several businesses can reveal choices missing from the first.

Appearance plays a part in the wanted system. Per GTA BOOM, changing your hair answers the appearance clue police look for while leaving the recognised outfit unchanged; a hat or glasses do not replace a full change of clothes.

Alone or together, what about the relationship?

Players can exercise, shop and visit salons alone or with their partner. Shared outings can strengthen the bond between Jason and Lucia. In the demonstrated build, however, a stronger relationship grants no specific gameplay benefit, the site says.

The material comes from preview write-ups: Dazed (September), IGN (August, "69 New GTA 6 Gameplay Details") and Edge (October 29), which GTA BOOM lists in its notes. We have not re-read each of those texts. For the notoriety and profile mechanics, see our piece on the Criminal Profile described by Edge.

Why it matters

The system makes daily life count for the leads, but how heavily it weighs over a full playthrough is still unknown. Rockstar has not published a dedicated page; everything remains to be checked at launch on November 19. Dates and conditions are on the release page.
