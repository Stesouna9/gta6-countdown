GTA 6 casinos and Hot Skidz kart track stay shut, PC Gamer reports

Finishing the story did not open these venues in PC Gamer’s playthrough. Their presence is no announcement of a future update.

图片：Rockstar Games

来源 pcgamer.com

Finishing GTA 6 did not turn its casino into a playable attraction for Christopher Livingston. Writing at PC Gamer, he describes returning after completing the main story and finding the doors still shut. His report also covers the nearby Hot Skidz karting venue. These are observations from his playthrough, rather than a Rockstar statement about future content or an exhaustive account of every possible interaction.

What did PC Gamer actually find?

Livingston says he first noticed a large purple casino while flying over the city. After reaching it on foot, he tried its entrances and found no access to gambling activities. A utility room was the exception, according to his account, but an accessible service space is clearly different from an operational casino floor. He also reports encountering a smaller casino that remained closed.

The same article describes Hot Skidz, a kart track displaying a sign advertising a future opening. Returning later did not reveal a working karting activity. Livingston managed to ride a motorcycle onto the circuit, but that improvised lap did not provide an actual racing event. A physical track and a functioning minigame are separate things, even when the scenery makes the missing activity look inviting.

Does the scenery promise an update?

PC Gamer’s writer hopes the venues might eventually become additional singleplayer content. That is his interpretation of the unused spaces, not a confirmed plan. The collected report contains no opening date, no commitment from Rockstar and no settled answer about whether these locations might serve solo play or an online component. The signs alone cannot settle any of those questions.

Why it matters

Anyone racing through the campaign specifically to unlock casino games or karting now has a useful account to consider. In PC Gamer’s completed playthrough, those venues stayed unavailable. That does not establish their permanent fate, but it does weaken the assumption that story completion automatically opens them. A future claim about their arrival will need fresh evidence, rather than another reading of the same signs.
