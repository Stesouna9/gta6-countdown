GTA 6 money guide: stolen cars, gang hideouts and the BuckMe account

To earn money early in GTA 6, ActuGaming recommends selling cars to Moses from chapter 2, raiding gang hideouts and depositing cash at BuckMe.

来源 actugaming.net

Money matters more in GTA 6 than in earlier games, with cars, weapons and clothes pushing Jason and Lucia to fill their accounts fast. ActuGaming lists its most profitable early-game methods.

How do you make money fast in GTA 6?

First, steal vehicles and sell them to Moses, met in chapter 2. Pick cars without trackers, as he does not take every model yet. A car pays a little over $1,000, with a short wait between sales. From chapter 3, new resale options appear, including for tracker-equipped vehicles.

Second, gang hideouts hold illegal goods, valuables and sometimes cash, but you must first deal with several criminals. Drugs, jewellery and marked bills then sell to fences. Third, random events: stealing the loot of a robbery by others, or trafficker deals on some airport strips.

What should you do with cash?

Each protagonist can carry only $20,000 in cash, a limit quickly reached by exploring lockboxes, stashes, homes or small cannabis plantations. It can be lost in part or in full if you die or get arrested. ActuGaming advises depositing it regularly into your BuckMe account at Leonida ATMs: it is safer and covers most purchases, even some big required spends.
