GTA 6 main menu theme on PS5: alleged first listen goes viral on X

A video from @gtasix_, viewed over 360,000 times, claims to show the GTA 6 main menu theme on PS5; nothing is confirmed.

The X account @gtasix_ posted a video on Monday morning billed as the "first listen" to the GTA 6 main menu theme on PS5. The post has passed 360,000 views. Nothing is official yet: neither Rockstar, Sony nor any outlet has confirmed the clip is genuine.

Where does this clip come from, and can it be trusted?

All we have is the post itself, a video with a one-line caption. No source for the recording is given and no other account or reputable outlet has picked it up at this hour. We are keeping it conditional: it could be a real clip from an early console, or an edit.

Why the menu theme matters

The main menu is the first thing players hear at every launch. Fans want to know whether Rockstar chose an original piece or a licensed track from the Vice City era. Rockstar has never officially presented the menu music.

Confirmed facts: the game launches on 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preloading from 12 November, and no PC version has been announced. We will update this piece as soon as it is confirmed or denied. See also our preload guide and the release page.
