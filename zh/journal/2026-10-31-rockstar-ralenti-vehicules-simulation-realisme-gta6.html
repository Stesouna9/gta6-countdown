GTA 6 slowed down: Rockstar reined in vehicles and simulation for realism

HotHardware, citing Edge's cover story, says Rockstar slowed GTA 6 vehicles and the wider simulation because the game felt too fast for its level of detail.

GTA 6 runs slower than its engine allowed. That is what HotHardware reports from Edge's cover story: Rockstar development head Aaron Garbut says the game had to be slowed to reduce an uncanny valley effect and improve realism. The detail comes from a preview, not a Rockstar press release.

Why did Rockstar slow GTA 6 down?

According to HotHardware, the world became so detailed that it no longer held up at full speed. Vehicles and the overall simulation were slowed, and traffic was narrowed, not just slowed, for realism. The logic is simple: the finer the scenery, the more a car crossing it too fast breaks the illusion.

The outlet notes the full Edge story can be read online at GamesRadar. Rockstar shared it on X, but that post adds nothing new.

What do weather and pedestrians change?

Per HotHardware, dynamic weather travels with clouds across the map, so players can drive through it, around it, or brave rough seas. Pedestrians take cover from rain and thunder. They grow wary if you carry a weapon on your back, and one released his dog because the player stared too long. Some fight back when you hijack their car, others give police tracking information on newer models.

What is not known

No speed values are given, nor any figure compared with GTA 5. Whether a setting will let players speed things up is also unknown.
