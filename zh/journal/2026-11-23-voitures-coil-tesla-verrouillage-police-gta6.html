GTA 6: stealing a Coil car, the Tesla parody, locks you in and drives you to the police

IGN France reports that GTA 6's Coil cars, a parody of Tesla, lock when a player tries to steal them and drive to the nearest police station.

来源 fr.ign.com

Stealing a Coil in GTA 6 is a bad idea. IGN France reports that the game's vehicles inspired by Elon Musk's cars all carry advanced security systems that stop them from being stolen.

What happens when you try to steal a Coil?

According to IGN France, a mechanism kicks in and locks the player inside. The car then drives itself to the nearest police station, or stays put until officers arrive and make the arrest. The outlet adds that owners of these vehicles also get preferential treatment from the police.

A parody going back to GTA V

Rockstar has parodied Tesla since GTA V through its fictional "Coil" range, IGN France recalls. In GTA 6 the joke goes further: the connected car becomes a trap. Our piece on the new wanted system already details how law enforcement reacts to crime.

Nothing suggests Rockstar has commented on this detail. Players found it and IGN France relayed it, with a spoiler warning. We have not seen a full list of affected models.
