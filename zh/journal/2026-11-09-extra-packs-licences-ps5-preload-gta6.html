GTA 6 PS5 preload: four unnamed "Extra" packs appear in the menu

GTA 6's PS5 preload menu now lists four unnamed extra packs of 1 MB each, reportedly licenses, and Rockstar has not said what they are, per Sportskeeda.

GTA 6's PS5 preload menu now shows four extra items alongside the game itself. Sportskeeda reports it, based on a post by X user iGrandTheftAuto. They carry placeholder names, Extra2 to Extra5, with no description.

What are the four packs?

Nobody knows, and Rockstar has said nothing. The one solid detail comes from PlayStationSize, an account that reads PlayStation's database: according to Sportskeeda, each pack is about 1 MB and they appear to be licenses, not downloadable content. The same account says the current PS5 build is 01.001.004.

A 1 MB file cannot hold game content. It looks more like a key that unlocks something already inside the 209 GB, or switched on later. That reading is ours, not a confirmed fact.

What are players guessing?

Replying to the original post, user PLTytus suggested the Vintage Vice City Pack, Ultimate Edition bonuses, language packs or GTA Online. These are guesses, Sportskeeda stresses. The first two at least have a basis: Rockstar already confirmed the Vintage Vice City Pack as a pre-order bonus and the €99.99 Ultimate Edition as a separate version.

On Online, caution is needed. PlayStationSize also found multiplayer functions (joinSession, launchMultiplayerActivity) in the PS5 data, as we wrote in our piece on the multiplayer shortcuts, but Sportskeeda notes nothing links them to the four packs.

Why it matters

Players have nothing to do: preloading has started and these licenses weigh nothing. Four of them does suggest several separate unlocks are planned. Rockstar has announced nothing, so we should know what they do by November 19 at the latest. Editions are detailed on the price page.
