GTA 6 launch: Pornhub traffic dropped 15.5% after release, Mashable reports

Pornhub's US traffic fell 15.5% an hour after GTA 6 launched, according to the site's own figures relayed by Mashable.

来源 mashable.com

Pornhub's US traffic dropped 15.5% an hour after GTA 6 launched, Mashable reports. The site had actually peaked just before: at 10 p.m. ET on Thursday, November 19, traffic was 16.5% above an average day.

What do the Pornhub numbers show?

According to the data Mashable cites, the decline began an hour later: traffic fell to 9.9% above average, then to 7.5% below average by midnight. The night and morning stayed uneven. At 8 a.m. Thursday it sat 6.4% below average, before drifting back slightly above average later in the day. Mashable suggests a time-zone effect or players taking a break, without claiming certainty.

GTA-related searches exploded

The same site says searches containing "GTA" rose 16,196% on November 19, with 15 million searches since November 18. Top terms were "gta 6", then "gta 6 lucia" after the female lead, and searches about the game's strip clubs. Mashable takes it as proof that "Rule 34" is alive and well.

These are figures published by Pornhub itself and relayed by a single outlet: they show a timing correlation, not a proven cause. No other site or regulator has confirmed the US figure so far, and nothing is known for France.

A launch that shifts habits

The detail adds to other signs of an outsized launch: PlayStation reports more than 39 million PS5 players, and mobile game spending is already falling, as the AppsFlyer study shows. Release details are on the release page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game itself, but the number shows the scale of the event: a title able to divert part of the traffic of one of the world's most visited sites. Whether the dip lasts beyond launch weekend is not covered by the article.
