GTA 6 song "Welcome to Vice City" credited to Bad Bunny and Diplo?

Two X accounts say a music licensing database lists a "Welcome to Vice City" track credited to Bad Bunny and Diplo, unconfirmed by Rockstar.

The first track of GTA 6 may be called "Welcome to Vice City". That is what the X accounts @GTAVI_Countdown and @gtasix_ say, after spotting it in a music licensing database (MLC). Rockstar has confirmed nothing.

Who is credited on "Welcome to Vice City"?

According to these two accounts, the MLC database lists Bad Bunny and Diplo as writers. The information first appeared in a GTAForums post and was relayed on X. No outlet in our monitoring has cross-checked it yet: this is a lead, not an established fact. Nothing is known about the track itself: performer, length, place in the game.

Why the title echoes GTA V

Both accounts point out that GTA V opened with "Welcome to Los Santos" by Oh No. A mirror title for Vice City would fit the series tradition, but nothing indicates the song will actually open the game: that is the accounts' inference, not Rockstar information.

On the official side, Rockstar already released the Tove Lo single, see our article on the "Pre-Load Now" trailer. The album GTA VI: The Album, 34 tracks, arrives November 19; the music page follows announcements.

Why it matters

For now, nothing verifiable: a licensing database entry can change, be a working title or not appear in the game. We wait for confirmation from Rockstar or a serious outlet before treating it as settled. On November 19, the game's 34-track list will settle it.
