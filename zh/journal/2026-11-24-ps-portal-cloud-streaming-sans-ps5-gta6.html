GTA 6 PS Portal cloud streaming: play without a PS5 with PS Plus Premium

GTA 6 now streams from the cloud to the PlayStation Portal with no console, provided you own the digital game and subscribe to PS Plus Premium.

来源 journaldugeek.com · pushsquare.com · notebookcheck.net

GTA 6 can now be streamed from the cloud to a PlayStation Portal with no PS5 at home. Push Square reports that the game has been updated to support PS Plus Premium cloud streaming on PS5 and Portal, a feature missing at launch.

Do you need a PS5 to play GTA 6 on the PS Portal?

No. Until now the Portal was a remote screen: it needed a PS5 at home running the game through Remote Play. With the cloud, everything runs on Sony's servers. Push Square tested it on its own PS5 in the UK and says it works well. Journal du Geek dates the arrival to 23 November, four days after release, while Xbox Cloud Gaming had the game from 19 November.

What you still have to pay for

The game is not free. Push Square is clear: GTA 6 has not been added to the PS Plus Premium catalogue and you must own it. Streaming only works with digital games in your library, so you need the PlayStation Store version or a disc-less box bought in a shop, according to Journal du Geek. Notebookcheck adds that the purchase can be made from the mobile app or the website.

On cost, Journal du Geek puts the Portal at 249.99 euros in France, plus 18.99 euros a month or 151.99 euros a year for PS Plus Premium: roughly 460 euros for the first year with the game, less than a PS5. For the game's own price, see our price page.

How good is the image?

Journal du Geek says streaming tops out at 1080p and 60 frames per second on the Portal, recommends at least 15 Mbit/s and quotes 80 to 90 ms of latency on home Wi-Fi. Fine for a third-person action game, less so on tethered or hotel connections. Saves go through the PlayStation cloud and carry over between PS5 and Portal.

Notebookcheck points out one limit: Sony has discontinued its Windows app, which closes the door on PCs. Before launch a report claimed Xbox would hold exclusive cloud rights; Push Square says this update confirms that was not the case. We covered the Microsoft side in our piece on Xbox Cloud Gaming and Remote Play.

Why it matters

Anyone who already owns a Portal and a Premium subscription can play without buying a console. For everyone else the bill stays high, and there is still no official PC version.
