GTA 6 Burger Shot expands to Skinny Louie stores across Florida

IGN reports that Skinny Louie stores in Florida will feature Burger Shot branding and themed food from November 13 through December 31.

Burger Shot is moving beyond its basketball-arena appearance and into Skinny Louie restaurants across Florida, IGN reports. The collaboration is scheduled to run from November 13 through December 31, bringing themed signage, staff clothing and food to the partner’s stores. The real-world promotion therefore starts before GTA 6 arrives on November 19.

Where is the restaurant promotion taking place?

IGN describes an expansion from the Kaseya Center, where a Skinny Louie partnership previously brought Burger Shot to Miami Heat games, to the chain’s standalone Florida locations. That changes the setting: the experience is no longer described solely as something attached to visiting the arena during a basketball game.

There are practical limits to the information available. The collected article does not provide a complete address list, individual opening hours or a store-by-store availability schedule. Anyone planning a visit should check the actual location. Nothing in the report establishes a French rollout or international delivery of the themed meals.

What food is being offered?

One item detailed by IGN is a triple smash burger using Black Angus beef, American cheese, bacon, pickles and sauces. The publication also describes a double burger with lettuce, onions and Burger Shot sauce. Together with the uniforms and signs, the food turns the fictional brand into a temporary restaurant experience.

The source text does not supply usable prices, so there is no basis for presenting the collaboration as a particular bargain. Promotional pictures describe the concept, but cannot guarantee stock at an individual branch. Nor does the stated campaign window establish that every recipe will be available without interruption throughout that period.

Why it matters

The launch campaign is giving people a way to encounter a piece of Vice City away from a console. An established restaurant adopts the identity of a fictional business, allowing customers to visit before they can explore the game. That is a different kind of promotion from the merchandise covered on our GTA 6 collectibles page.

Its scope should remain clear: this is a temporary Florida restaurant collaboration. It does not reveal new gameplay mechanics, establish an interactive in-game menu or tell us what Jason and Lucia can order. The concrete development in IGN’s reporting is the move from the arena partnership to Skinny Louie’s standalone stores, with a campaign lasting through launch and the end-of-year holiday period.
