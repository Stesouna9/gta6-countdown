GTA 6: PS5 consoles crash or die mid-game, no proven link yet

Players report PS5s shutting down, freezing or dying after hours of GTA 6, but no cause is proven and Sony has not responded.

Kaynaklar tweaktown.com · gamingbible.com

Less than a week after the November 19 launch, PS5 owners describe consoles that shut off, crash or won't reboot during GTA 6. According to TweakTown, two Twitch streamers lost their machines; GAMINGbible talks of a PS5 "blackout", a phrase borrowed from a post on X.

What exactly happened?

The most dramatic case is streamer shivjam: the game freezes, green artifacts streak across the screen, a symptom often tied to failing graphics hardware, then the console won't boot. He wrote that GTA 6 "fried" his PS5 and posted photos of the fans to rule out dust. Another streamer, JoJoJosiah, says his console started smoking while the game was paused.

Is GTA 6 really killing PS5s?

There is no proof. Repair technician modyfikatorcasper measured 274.2W at the wall in a max-load test, calling it "the toughest real-world PS5 stress test" he has seen. TweakTown says a typical game peaks at 180 to 220W and Sony designed the console to draw up to 340 to 350W: GTA 6 sits high in that range but inside it. The technician suspects the load exposes weak cooling on older models.

The outlet adds that far more players report crashes than dead hardware, error code CE-108255-1 being the most common, and that Rockstar's first patch reportedly did not fix it. We covered that code in our PS5 crash story and the first patch contents.

GAMINGbible, whose writer played over a hundred hours on a launch PS5 with a single crash, also urges not to blame the game too fast: dust in the vents, alleged manufacturing flaws or upkeep may play a part. It has asked Rockstar and Sony for comment, with no reply so far.

What to do meanwhile?

Caution rather than panic: clear dust from the intakes, give the console room to breathe and watch its temperature. Sony and Rockstar have said nothing. The shortage described in our stock story makes a replacement harder.

Why it matters

GTA 6 is becoming the reference stress test for the PS5, and older units may pay for it. If Sony or Rockstar respond, we will update this piece. Official dates and platforms are on sortie.html.
