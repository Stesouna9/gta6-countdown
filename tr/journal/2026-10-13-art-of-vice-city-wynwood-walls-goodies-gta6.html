GTA 6 merch: Rockstar and Wynwood Walls launch The Art of Vice City

Rockstar partners with Miami's Wynwood Walls museum on The Art of Vice City, a clothing and accessories line now in pre-order on the museum's online shop.

Sources dlgaming.fr

Miami street art on a T-shirt. According to DL Gaming, Rockstar Games is partnering with the Wynwood Walls museum in Miami to launch The Art of Vice City, a range of clothing and accessories inspired by works from artists who appear in GTA 6.

What is in the collection?

Per DL Gaming, it includes T-shirts, water bottles, mouse pads, notebooks and keychains, all in pre-order. Other pieces are named: a "We Love Vice City" beach towel by Queen Andrea, an "Eye Got You" hoodie by AHOL and a "Hit the Road" laptop sleeve by D*Face. The artwork comes from artists the project was developed with.

Where can you buy it?

Only from Wynwood Walls through its online shop, not the Rockstar Store. DL Gaming notes prices may change and that shipping and taxes should be checked before paying. We do not repeat prices here, as we could not verify them at the source.

What about Miami residents?

The partnership includes free entry to the museum for Miami-Dade County residents, plus new murals by artists tied to the game, again per DL Gaming. It fits the campaign Rockstar is running in the city, of which the Brooklyn mural was another chapter.

For collectors, it is a new entry point outside the Rockstar store. For the game, nothing: this is merchandise. More picks are on our goodies page.
