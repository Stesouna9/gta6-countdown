GTA 6: Rockstar joins Douyin to promote preloading in China

Rockstar Games opened a verified Douyin account, TechNode reports; its first Chinese-language video promotes preloading ahead of the November 19 launch.

Kaynaklar technode.com

Rockstar Games has opened a verified account on Douyin, ByteDance's short-video platform, TechNode reported Thursday, citing GamerSky. The first post is a Chinese-language clip telling players they can preload GTA 6 ahead of the November 19 release on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

What did Rockstar post on Douyin?

According to TechNode, the clip is the same one Rockstar already posted on its Bilibili account, where it has shared the game's trailers. The message is simple: preload is open, the game is seven days away. The article mentions no new trailer or footage.

Preloading started on November 10, as we covered with the "Pre-Load Now" trailer. In China the campaign follows the same calendar as everywhere else.

Why Douyin matters for the launch

Douyin is the Chinese counterpart of TikTok, with its own audience and rules. Rockstar adds a second foothold after Bilibili, which leans toward gaming communities. What the studio plans to post there before November 19 is unknown: TechNode quotes no Rockstar statement, only GamerSky's finding.

No conclusion should be drawn about availability in China: the report covers promotion, not distribution. Platforms and date stay PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, with no PC version announced. Full launch details are on our release page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for players in France, beyond one more sign: a week from launch, Rockstar is opening another channel rather than closing one. What is established is a verified account and a preload video. Posting frequency or exclusive content is not documented.
