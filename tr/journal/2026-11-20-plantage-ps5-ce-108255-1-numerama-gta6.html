GTA 6 crashes on PS5: error code CE-108255-1 hits several players

Numerama reports PS5 players seeing GTA 6 crash with error CE-108255-1; Rockstar points to Sony and has announced no patch or date.

Kaynaklar numerama.com

Some players waited over a decade only to hit an error screen. Numerama details a GTA 6 crash on PS5 that returns error code CE-108255-1, based on a reader's email and matching reports on Reddit and English-language forums.

What is happening on PS5?

The reader owns a PS5 Slim bought in August 2026 and the €79.99 digital edition. The game crashes during the opening cutscene, even when skipped, or after a few minutes in Leonida. His other games run fine. Numerama says cases appear on standard and Slim models, in France and abroad, and usual troubleshooting changed nothing.

What do Rockstar and Sony say?

A Rockstar Support ticket was closed without a technical fix, and the help page points to PlayStation, as GamesRadar+ also noted according to Numerama. Sony advises updating the game, though Rockstar has released no patch for this crash, then sends players to its repair service. A player quoted by GamesRadar+ says he was offered a £300 estimate, around €350.

Is it a console problem?

Unknown. Numerama stresses most consoles are not affected and reports a suspicion that the game is so demanding it exposes faults in some PS5 units, an unproven theory. This is separate from the save-loss crashes on PS5 and Xbox we covered earlier.

On performance, Rockstar targets 30 fps on every platform, as we explained in our piece on the 60 fps mode.

Why it matters

For affected players there is no official fix yet, and Rockstar has announced no patch or date. Think twice before paying for a repair until there is a clear answer, and follow our release page for any patch.
