GTA Online: Grotti Turismo Omaggio free via Twitch until 1 November

Rockstar is giving away the Grotti Turismo Omaggio and Rockstar Motorsport livery in GTA Online to viewers of NoPixel V streamers from 30 October to 1 November.

Rockstar has launched a Twitch campaign for GTA Online. In a post on X on 30 October, the studio says players can unlock the Grotti Turismo Omaggio and the Rockstar Motorsport livery by watching selected streamers on the NoPixel V roleplay server between 30 October and 1 November.

The details are on the GTA Online "Twitch Drops" page on Rockstar's site. We did not find the list of streamers or broadcast times there, so check it before you start watching.

What do you need to do to get the Turismo Omaggio?

According to Rockstar's post, you watch selected streamers playing on NoPixel V during the 30 October to 1 November window. Twitch Drops usually require a Twitch account linked to the Rockstar Social Club, but Rockstar does not say so in its post: confirm on the official page.

Does this involve GTA 6?

No, it concerns GTA Online, the online mode of GTA V. It shows Rockstar still supporting its current game three weeks before GTA 6 launches on 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The post passed 125,000 views in under two hours.

Why it matters

For GTA Online players, a vehicle and livery to grab in three days. For GTA 6, nothing concrete: the post contains no new announcement. Exact conditions are on Rockstar's page.
