GTA 6 PC release: Michael Pachter says six to seven months after console

Analyst Michael Pachter told the BBC GTA 6 will reach PC about six to seven months after launch; Rockstar has announced no PC version.

Wedbush Securities analyst Michael Pachter expects GTA 6 to reach PC six to seven months after the console launch, around May or June 2027, according to GTA BOOM. He said so on the BBC. Rockstar has announced no PC version.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC, and when?

Officially it is unannounced: Rockstar and Take-Two have only confirmed the November 19 release on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Pachter, on BBC Business Today in a segment about the game's commercial impact, said GTA 6 "will come to PC", per GTA BOOM. This is an analyst's estimate, not information from Rockstar.

How does it compare with other reports?

GTA BOOM sets it against GamesBeat's report, citing sources familiar with Rockstar's plans, of a first-quarter 2027 window, two to four months after console. Pachter is a little later but in the same half-year. We covered that lead in GTA 6 on PC: what we know.

The outlet recalls the history: about 19 months' wait for GTA V and 13 for Red Dead Redemption 2. Every past GTA eventually reached PC, but the delay is unknown, and a timeline from an analyst binds only the analyst. Pachter has commented on the game's price before and his calls have not always been right, the same source notes.

Why it matters

Nothing concrete for PC players: no date, no confirmed platform, no specs. It adds to a list of unofficial estimates placing a port in early 2027. Meanwhile the next news is Edge's October 29 feature. For official dates, see the release page.
