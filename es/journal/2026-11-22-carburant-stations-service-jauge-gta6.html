GTA 6 fuel gauge: cars run dry in 18 to 29 minutes, per Auto Moto

Every car in GTA 6 has a fuel gauge: a sports car runs out after 18 minutes, a pickup after 29, according to Auto Moto.

Fuentes auto-moto.com

Stealing a car no longer guarantees a clean getaway: every vehicle in GTA 6 has a fuel gauge, reports Auto Moto. It is a first for the series, and electric models also need a charging point.

How long before a car runs dry?

When the tank nears reserve, the game warns the player and points to nearby stations. Auto Moto relies on early Kotaku tests: a Banshee sports car can run out after a little over 18 minutes of fast driving, against more than 29 minutes for a large pickup. Range therefore varies a lot with the model and driving style, and these figures come from testers, not from Rockstar.

Is refuelling slow?

No. According to Auto Moto, filling up takes about ten seconds in a short animated sequence. Stations also repair damaged vehicles. Electric cars recharge at dedicated points spread across the fictional state of Leonida, and you cannot drive forever without watching the gauge.

We already covered Electrek's take on charging: the topic matters because it touches everyday driving, far from the chases. Release details are on the release page.

Why it matters

Escaping in a stolen car becomes a matter of planning: schedule a stop, pick an enduring vehicle, accept ten seconds at the pump mid-chase. Whether stations are frequent enough for the gauge to stay a constraint rather than decoration will only show with longer play.
