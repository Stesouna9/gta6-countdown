GTA 6 Online: nothing announced, but the map keeps unused areas

Officially, GTA 6 Online does not exist: per Gamekult, Rockstar has teased nothing, yet the map holds areas the story never visits.

Fuentes gamekult.com

Does GTA 6 Online exist? Not officially, Gamekult reminds readers in a long piece published Tuesday: Rockstar has neither announced nor teased anything. That has not stopped the question from dividing the community since November 19.

Why is everyone talking about GTA Online?

Because GTA V sold 235 million copies and its online mode is a big reason why. Gamekult relies on data publicly exposed in April 2026 after an intrusion claimed by the ShinyHunters collective: players allegedly spent over $5 billion in GTA Online between 2014 and 2024, peaking at $744 million in 2020. According to that leak, annual revenue never fell below $400 million since 2017, and from September 2025 to April 2026 roughly 74% of spending came from Shark Cards against 26% for the GTA+ subscription. These figures come from a leak and are not confirmed by Rockstar.

Does the GTA 6 map point to an online mode?

Gamekult notes that parts of the map are never visited by Jason and Lucia, in the main story or optional missions: a zoo, a cemetery, two casinos and a small town in the north. Design vice president Stephen Donkin told Kotaku that Rockstar kept some room: "The map was designed for the long term and with many factors in mind. So there are a few areas on the map that give us options for the future, in case we need them for gameplay or storytelling."

The outlet sees several readings: future solo content, or a future online game. Nothing settles it. On the related topic of progression, we already addressed the rumor that 100% completion unlocks Online: no such rule has been announced.

Why it matters

Nothing is official yet: no date, no name, no confirmed content. What is established is the financial stake described by the leak and the existence of areas unused in the solo game. Until Rockstar says something, treat any circulating date as a rumor. Confirmed dates stay on our release page.
