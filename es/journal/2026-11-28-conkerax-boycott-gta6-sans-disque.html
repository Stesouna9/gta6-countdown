GTA 6: French YouTuber Conkerax boycotts the game over the missing disc

Orange Actualités reports that Marseille creator Conkerax, with nearly 500,000 subscribers, will not play GTA 6: with no disc, he says players cannot truly own it.

Fuentes actu.orange.fr

A fan of the series, absent from the game: Orange Actualités runs a video report on Conkerax, a Marseille content creator followed by nearly 500,000 people on YouTube, who has decided not to play GTA 6. His main reason, according to the outlet: no disc in the box.

What is his complaint?

Per Orange, Conkerax has more than thirty videos on Rockstar games. He says that without physical media, players cannot own the game as they see fit: no resale, no lending, access tied to an online store. Orange calls it "one of the reasons"; the report we could read does not detail the others, and we will not invent them.

What is actually in the box?

The November 19 release is the first GTA sold only digitally, which Orange notes in its related reports. Boxes in shops hold a code, not a disc; we covered the pricing side in our Black Friday piece on the €69.99 boxes. List prices remain €79.99 for the standard edition and €99.99 for Ultimate, digital only: see our price page.

An isolated boycott or a trend?

Nothing in our sources says this boycott affects sales. The available figures point the other way: Alinea estimates 53 million copies sold, as we detailed in our piece on that estimate, and it is an estimate, not a Rockstar figure. Conkerax's case illustrates a criticism shared by part of the player base: the feeling of renting rather than buying.
