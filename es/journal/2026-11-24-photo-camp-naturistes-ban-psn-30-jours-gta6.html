GTA 6: player banned from PSN social features for 30 days over a nudist camp photo

A player says Sony suspended him for 30 days after he sent an in-game photo of GTA 6's nudist camp through PlayStation messaging, according to Le Parisien.

Fuentes leparisien.fr

Snapping an in-game photo and sending it to a friend is routine. It reportedly cost one GTA 6 player 30 days of PSN, according to French daily Le Parisien, which picked up a post on X by @DerrickGott007 first spotted by Numerama.

What happened?

By the player's account, he found a nudist camp south of Grassrivers in Leonida, took a photo of it with his character's phone and sent it to a friend through PS5 messaging. A Sony notice followed: PlayStation's security team had determined that content uploaded from his account to Message on PlayStation violated the code of conduct in the terms of service.

He warned others on X, in capital letters, not to send nude in-game camera photos over PSN messaging. The GTAlphaintel account repeated the story overnight, again citing a 30-day suspension.

What does Sony say?

Per Le Parisien, PlayStation expects players to act with decency and respect and points to a code of conduct that bans obscene, pornographic or illegal content. The notice makes no distinction between real nudity and virtual characters. A repeat offence could mean a second suspension or a permanent removal from online services.

What we don't know

This rests on a single player's account. Neither Sony nor Rockstar has commented publicly, and we have not seen the original notice. The ban targets PSN social features, so the single-player game stays playable. Le Parisien also dates the ban to "Saturday 21 October", which cannot be right given the November 19 release, so we leave it out. Nudity has been part of the game from the start, as we covered in our piece on Jason and Lucia's nude scenes.

Why it matters

If you play on PS5, photos shared through PlayStation messaging follow Sony's network rules, whatever the game's PEGI 18 rating allows. Keeping risky shots local avoids the problem until Sony clarifies. We are waiting for an official answer or more reports showing whether the filter is automatic or report-based.
