GTA 6: more than 39 million players on PS5, PlayStation says

PlayStation says more than 39 million unique players have played GTA 6 on PS5 since November 19, calling it its biggest launch ever.

Fuentes x.com

PlayStation says on its official X account, overnight between November 23 and 24, that more than 39 million unique players have jumped into GTA 6 on PS5 since the November 19 launch. The company calls it "the biggest launch in PlayStation history" and links to a page of further highlights.

What does PlayStation actually say?

The post is three lines: "More than 39 million unique players jumped into Grand Theft Auto VI on PS5 since launch," a thank-you to players and a link to more "launch highlights." A unique player is an account that started the game at least once, not a copy sold. The figure says nothing about physical or digital sales, nor about Xbox Series X|S players.

The post passed 236,000 views shortly after going up. Several news accounts picked it up, including GTAVI_Countdown and gtasix_. The latter adds figures said to come from the PlayStation page: 683 million hours played, 17.4 hours per player on average, a record for concurrent players on the console. We could not check those in the official post itself, so treat them with caution until we have read the page.

How does it compare with sales?

On November 21 we covered Rockstar's official numbers in our piece on the $3.79 billion launch, taken from an SEC filing. The two figures do not compare directly: one measures revenue, the other active players on a single platform. Still, 39 million players in four days on PS5 shows how central the console is to this launch, with Xbox and cloud play counted separately.

GTAVI_Countdown sets the figure against the 100 million PS5 consoles sold worldwide: more than one console in three would already have run the game. That math is theirs, not PlayStation's, and it assumes one player per console, which shared accounts blur. The same account mentions 45 million copies sold across all platforms, for which we have no official source.

What is still unknown

PlayStation gives no split between digital pre-orders, or between Standard and Ultimate editions (see our prices page). There is no Xbox figure either, and Microsoft has published nothing so far. Rockstar has not commented on the PlayStation post.

Why it matters

Nothing changes in the game itself. For the market, it is the first audience figure a platform holder has published since launch, and it backs up what Rockstar's numbers suggested: GTA 6 is off to a stronger start than any game released on PlayStation. We will update this piece if the launch highlights page adds more official data.
