GTA 6: 90% of surveyed teens want to play GTA Online, Piper Sandler says

A Piper Sandler survey of 9,000 US teenagers puts GTA Online at the top of their wish list, according to big-n.fr, while Rockstar has announced no online mode for GTA 6.

Nine out of ten teenagers want to play GTA Online. The figure comes from a Piper Sandler study of 9,000 young people, reported by big-n.fr on November 5, two weeks before GTA 6 launches on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

What does the survey actually measure?

According to big-n.fr, 90% of respondents say they want to play GTA Online. That is stated interest, not purchases, and it concerns GTA V's multiplayer, already part of a whole generation's habits. Nothing in the report says these teens are talking about an online mode in GTA 6.

Has Rockstar announced an online mode for GTA 6?

No. The game is presented as a single-player experience, including on retailer listings, big-n.fr points out. Rockstar and Take-Two have confirmed no name, date or multiplayer feature. Talk of a future "GTA Online 2" remains speculation.

The money explains the interest: GTA Online's Shark Card model has earned Take-Two vast sums. The outlet also cites a Circana study saying nearly one in three US players considered buying GTA 6, and analyst James Callahan's $290 price target for Take-Two stock.

Why it matters

Nothing concrete changes for players: a survey adds or removes no feature. It does show the November 19 launch will also be judged on what comes next, but Rockstar has said nothing on the subject. Follow announcements in the journal.
