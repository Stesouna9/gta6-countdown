GTA 6: Amazon's game and vinyl bundle with The Album is still on sale

Amazon's bundle of GTA 6 and the pink double vinyl of The Album (34 tracks) is still available at £109.98 in the UK, GAMINGbible reports.

Fuentes gamingbible.com

Vinyl fans still have a shot. GAMINGbible reports that Amazon's exclusive bundle of the GTA 6 Standard Edition and Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album on vinyl remains on sale at £109.98 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, £5 under the £114.98 list price.

What is in the vinyl bundle?

Per GAMINGbible, the album comes on two pink records with purple splatter in a gatefold sleeve, plus an exclusive 12x24-inch poster. The vinyl alone is £44.99 in the Amazon exclusive version and £34.99 in standard black. In the US, the exclusive is $49.98 as a separate pre-order.

What is on the album?

Rockstar says it holds 34 original tracks capturing Vice City and the state of Leonida. Singles named by the site include Travis Scott's "RHYNO", Cardi B's "Don't Chart", Yung Lean's "That's It" with Future and Metro Boomin, and Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. The album arrives November 19 with the game; details are on our music page.

What about CD?

The double CD, same 34 tracks, costs £15.99 at Amazon UK and $15.97 in the US, where the listed date is November 20. Game-plus-CD bundles are £80.98: GAMINGbible says they lost their discount and are back to the price of buying both separately. The Standard Edition keeps its £64.99 price instead of £69.99. The article gives no euro prices; see our price page.

Why it matters

Nothing new about the game, but a signal: Rockstar treats its soundtrack as a product in its own right, from vinyl to CD. The prices are Amazon UK on one day and can change. Our piece on the official store covers the rest of the merchandise.
