GTA 6 Travis Scott Nike Air Force 1: first photos of the black pair

Unofficial photos show the black Travis Scott x Rockstar x Nike Air Force 1 Low, reportedly due November 19, per Hypebeast and GTA BOOM.

Fonti hypebeast.com · gtaboom.com

The Travis Scott, Nike and Rockstar Air Force 1 now have a first look: photos shared by @prm.stocks.01 reportedly show a black pair with GTA-inspired details. Hypebeast and GTA BOOM are passing them on. Nothing is officially announced.

What does the pair look like?

Per Hypebeast, the black Air Force 1 Low has a money band around the toe box and a graphic of the six-star wanted level. GTA BOOM adds a reverse Swoosh, pink trim around the toe and pink stars across the sole. The shots also spread through Sneakerologue and Sneaker Files on X, credited to prm.stocks.01.

What do we know about the release and price?

GTA BOOM, citing an October 31 report from Sneaker Market RO, says Cactus Jack is working on three Air Force 1 Low '07 colourways at US$130 a pair for November 19, the game's launch day. We covered that lead in our piece on the three Air Force 1 due November 19. What changes today: there are finally images, but they come from a leaker, not a brand.

Nike, Rockstar Games and Cactus Jack have announced nothing, so prices, dates and colours remain unconfirmed.

Why it matters

Scott is already on the soundtrack: "RHYNO" came out on September 17 as "from GTAVI: The Album", per GTA BOOM, and the full album lands November 19 (see the music page). Sneakers on the same day would extend the rollout. Collectors should stay careful: without an official announcement, nobody knows where or how the pair would be sold.
