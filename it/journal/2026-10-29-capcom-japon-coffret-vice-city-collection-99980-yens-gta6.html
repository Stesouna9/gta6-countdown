GTA 6: Capcom opens pre-orders for a 99,980 yen Vice City collector's box in Japan

Capcom will release "The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection" in Japan on November 19 at 99,980 yen, with pre-orders opening October 30 at noon on e-Capcom; the game is not included.

Fonti saiganak.com

Capcom has announced an 11-item collector's box for GTA 6 in Japan, reports Saiga NAK. It is called "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection", releases on Thursday, November 19, 2026, the same day as the game, and costs 99,980 yen including tax. Pre-orders open Friday, October 30 at 12:00 (Japan time) on the e-Capcom online store, which has the exclusive in the country.

What is in the Japanese box?

According to Saiga NAK, the set includes a McCa figure with a cigarette and a Flamingo Blood Martini whose base twists open to hide valuables, matte black Oakley Frogskins with Prizm Sapphire lenses, a New Era 9FORTY snapback "Vice City Leonida" cap and a ripstop crossbody bag. It also lists a laser-engraved Manatee Chunkie shot glass, a razor-style keychain, a decorative spoon, nine enamel pins in a tin, a pink glass tray, nine vinyl stickers and a Leonida topographic map with an illustrated poster on the back.

The outlet says quantities are limited and the game itself is not in the box.

Is this the €399.99 box?

Nothing in the source says so. The Goodtime State box sold at €399.99 without the game is a separate offer, covered on our goodies page. This one is a Vice City collection distributed by Capcom in Japan, with its own price and contents. No source in our data says whether it will be sold elsewhere. The game's price is unchanged at €79.99 for the standard edition, see prices.

Why it matters

Japanese collectors have a short window: sales open tomorrow at noon, stock is limited, delivery at launch. For the rest of the world nothing is announced, so a European version is not confirmed. The countdown to the release runs on to November 19.
