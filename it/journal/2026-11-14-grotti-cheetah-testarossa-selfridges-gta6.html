GTA 6: a real Grotti Cheetah is on sale for £1 million at Selfridges

A Ferrari Testarossa turned into a Grotti Cheetah, built for Rockstar, is on offer in London for £1 million.

Foto: Rockstar Games

A real-life Grotti Cheetah from GTA 6 is for sale in London at one million pounds, about $1.3 million, Notebookcheck reports. It is a customised Ferrari Testarossa on show in the Corner Shop at Selfridges on Oxford Street, part of the department store's GTA 6 pop-up.

West Coast Customs, a Los Angeles workshop, built it for Rockstar Games. According to Notebookcheck it wears a pink-to-blue Vice City fade, keeps the Testarossa's pop-up headlights and side strakes, and Grotti's rearing rabbit sits where Ferrari's prancing horse used to be. It was flown in from Miami for the London display.

How do you buy the Selfridges Cheetah?

There is no online checkout. Notebookcheck says sales go through an appointment with Selfridges Personal Shopping, to be booked before 28 November. The car is on display from 9 to 28 November 2026. "Few cars epitomise GTA quite like the Grotti Cheetah," the retailer says in its listing. Mechanical specifications have not been published, so the engine and the car's real condition are unknown.

One of six, and the only one for sale

West Coast Customs built six replicas for Rockstar. The Cheetah is the only one on sale. The other five, which recently toured Miami, are GTA takes on the Lamborghini Huracán, McLaren Senna, Chevrolet Corvette C8, Cadillac Eldorado and Buick Riviera.

Grotti is the series' answer to Ferrari, and the Cheetah has played Testarossa lookalike since GTA III. In GTA 6 the Cheetah '95 is a bonus vehicle in the Ultimate Edition, priced at $99.99 and sold digitally only. Players who get it in the game will not need a million to drive it, at least on screen.

The pop-up also stocks Vice City merchandise, from £25 for a Vice City Narcos snapback to £1,750 for a GTA 5 varsity-style jacket. We already covered the paper map of Leonida sold at Selfridges; the Oxford Street address is becoming a launch meeting point. For other official items, the goodies page keeps the list up to date.

Why it matters

For players, nothing changes: the in-game Cheetah stays an Ultimate Edition bonus. For Rockstar, it shows how the game is leaving the screen before 19 November: cars, pop-up shops, merchandise. The operation is small, one car for sale, but it gives the launch a concrete pink-and-blue image that is already circulating in the press.
