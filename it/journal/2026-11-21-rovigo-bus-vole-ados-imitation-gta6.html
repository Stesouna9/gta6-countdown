GTA 6: in Rovigo, two teens steal a bus and police call it imitation

Two minors wrecked a bus depot in Veneto and drove a vehicle eight kilometres, an act local police tie to the launch of GTA VI, according to Le Dauphiné Libéré.

Foto: Rockstar Games

Fonti ledauphine.com

The launch of GTA 6 already has its news item. In the night from Thursday to Friday in Rovigo, Veneto (north-east Italy), two teenagers are suspected of imitating the game by stealing a bus, Le Dauphiné Libéré reports, citing a local police statement.

What happened in Rovigo?

The two minors broke into a bus depot and trashed it: ticket validators smashed, windows broken, vehicles rammed on purpose. Of about thirty buses present, around ten were damaged. They then drove one for eight kilometres until it ended up in a ditch outside town, and fled on foot.

They were spotted before dawn at Rovigo station and arrested. Local police speak of "vandalism by imitation" and say the act has no political overtone, according to Le Dauphiné Libéré.

Can we call it a GTA 6 effect?

Caution. The link to the game comes from the police's characterisation, not from a detailed confession relayed by the press, and nothing proves the teenagers had played it: the game only came out on Thursday 19 November, though its footage is already everywhere online. Since launch, "just like GTA" news items keep multiplying, and the debate about young players has also reached US politicians, as we covered in the journal.

Leggi anche GTA 6 on screen? Take-Two's boss does not rule it out but stays very selective

Remember too that GTA 6 is rated PEGI 18: it is not meant for minors, and the game itself does not ask anyone to steal a bus. What is certain is the wrecking of a real depot, at a transport company's expense.

Why it matters

For the game, nothing. For the public debate, it is one more example that some will cite and others will downplay. Every major release brings back the same question: the game's responsibility against that of parents and the teenagers themselves.
