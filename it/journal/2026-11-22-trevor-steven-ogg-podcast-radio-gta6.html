GTA 6: Trevor Philips reportedly appears on radio, voiced by Steven Ogg

Journal du Geek reports that GTA 5's Trevor is interviewed on a GTA 6 radio station, and a podcast mentions Michael and Franklin through Wade.

Fonti journaldugeek.com

Is Trevor in GTA 6? Not on screen, but on air. Journal du Geek says GTA 5's hero is interviewed on an in-game radio station about drug and cocaine trafficking, voiced by Steven Ogg, and spots two nods to Michael and Franklin. Rockstar has not confirmed any of it.

Where do Wade, Michael and Franklin come up?

In the on-demand podcasts, says Journal du Geek: the episode "Un alibi en tétons" on the channel L'Acte Final hands the mic to Wade, Trevor's friend from Sandy Shores. Wade recounts the murder of his cousin Floyd and Floyd's fiancée Deborah, suspecting Michael and Franklin. Trevor goes unnamed, referred to as his "best friend".

What about Trevor on the radio?

The voice cameo is said to be on another station. The site reads this as proof the third GTA 5 ending, where all three survive, is canon. That is the outlet's interpretation, not a studio statement.

We have followed the game's radio since launch, see the D'Angelo exclusive on Worldwide FM and our radio page.
