GTA 6: Take-Two doesn't rule out a GTA film or TV series, says Zelnick

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick says a GTA film or series isn't ruled out, but nothing is planned yet, Première reports.

Fonti premiere.fr

Will there ever be a GTA film or TV series? Strauss Zelnick, CEO of Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar Games' parent company, says it is not in the plans for now but he is not closing the door, Première reports.

What did Strauss Zelnick actually say?

On The David Rubenstein Show, he says the company is very careful when adapting its franchises to other media. He adds: "We could make a GTA film or TV series, from our own intellectual property, if our creative teams were passionate about the project." He warns that the bar is very high and failure is always a risk, so the teams are extremely selective.

Première notes that Take-Two expects about $3 billion in revenue by the end of the year from GTA VI, a figure that tempts Hollywood. For now, no adaptation has been announced and no studio is named.

Why has Rockstar always resisted?

The franchise has never had an official adaptation produced with Rockstar Games, while The Witcher, The Last of Us, Super Mario Bros., Sonic and Minecraft found their audience. In 2024, co-founder Dan Houser told The Ankler that past discussions had not convinced him. He feared handing control of the games to outside filmmakers.
