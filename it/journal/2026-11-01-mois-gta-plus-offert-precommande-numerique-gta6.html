GTA 6 free GTA+ month: how to redeem it with a digital pre-order

Every digital GTA 6 pre-order includes a free month of GTA+, redeemable until 31 March 2027 on PlayStation, per Cultured Vultures. The boxed code does not qualify.

Every digital GTA 6 pre-order includes one month of GTA+ at no extra cost, and it can be redeemed right away, before launch. That is what Cultured Vultures lays out. One catch: the code in the boxed version does not grant the free month.

How do you get the free GTA+ month?

After an eligible purchase, go to the GTA+ page on the PlayStation Store to redeem the offer. Buying the game and redeeming the membership are separate steps. On PlayStation the deadline is 31 March 2027. On Xbox the offer must be redeemed within 180 days of the eligible digital purchase, so that deadline depends on your purchase date rather than a fixed day.

Should you redeem it now?

You can use it today for GTA Online in GTA 5, where GTA+ gives GTA$500,000 a month, access to the GTA+ games library and bonuses. Cultured Vultures suggests waiting to learn how GTA+ will feature in GTA 6 before starting the clock. Rockstar has not detailed what the subscription will bring to the new game.

Does it renew on its own?

Yes. Once redeemed, it renews monthly at the regular price until cancelled. Rockstar says you can cancel at any time on its website. The outlet advises checking renewal settings right after redeeming, because "free" does not mean it stops by itself. The price quoted is £6.99 a month in the UK and $7.99 in the US, with no cheaper option; a euro price is not given in this source.

Why it matters

The free month is a small perk, but it turns into a paid subscription if forgotten. For digital pre-orders at €79.99 or €99.99, note the deadline and how to cancel. The game launches on 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
