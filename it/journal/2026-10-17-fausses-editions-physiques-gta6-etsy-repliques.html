GTA 6: Chinese sellers list fake physical PS5 boxes before launch

Replica PS5 boxes of GTA 6, Standard and Ultimate, are circulating ahead of the November 19 release. The disc is empty and the game unplayable.

Fonti numerama.com

There is no GTA 6 disc, and yet someone is already selling one. According to Numerama, citing a tweet from the GTA 6 Info account on Saturday, October 17, Chinese manufacturers are offering "physical" PS5 boxes of the game, in Standard and Ultimate versions.

What is inside these fake boxes?

Numerama reports that each box holds a disc with different artwork per edition but no data on it. The Ultimate version adds a card with a code that is probably useless. The listings state plainly that you cannot play with these items: they are collectible replicas. Only the PS5 version seems to be offered.

One user claims to have bought a copy on Etsy, which Numerama says it cannot verify. The listing credits the visuals to Lionheart, apparently a branding agency based in Shanghai and London, per the same outlet.

Why do these replicas exist?

Because the official retail box has no disc. Rockstar and Take-Two chose to ship only a download code, a decision heavily criticised since June. Sellers are feeding the demand for a real object to put on a shelf. One giveaway: the Ultimate edition only exists digitally at Rockstar, so no official box can carry it.

It fits a wider pattern: the collector set at €399.99 sold without the game already drew complaints, and the Leclerc listing shows even the code-in-a-box format raises questions. For genuine merchandise, see our goodies page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for your preorder, but take it as a warning: before the November 19 release, distrust any "disc edition" of GTA 6 sold online. None is official. Numerama notes that Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick did not rule out a real disc version later, in comments to IGN in August. Nothing is confirmed. Rockstar has not reacted to the replicas.
