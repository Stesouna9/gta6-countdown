GTA 6: Cyberleek posts Xbox build executable, could aid PC port

Cyberleek has posted the executable of GTA 6's Xbox build, per Wccftech and IGN. An unofficial PC port is now conceivable, but the game does not boot yet.

Fonti wccftech.com · news.google.com

File Cyberleek: tutto ciò che sappiamo sul leak di GTA 6 →

Cyberleek has posted the executable of the Xbox Series build of GTA 6, Wccftech reports, and IGN, IGN Pakistan, PCMag and Notebookcheck picked it up on Monday. The file was the missing piece needed to make the build data circulating since November 19 readable. Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has responded so far.

What did the leaker publish?

Cyberleek calls the executable an "early Christmas gift", according to Wccftech. A Pastebin note says the leaker is fighting subscriptions bolted onto single-player games and 20-dollar "ultimate editions" that unlock content already in the files, and claims the ending and story were never leaked. Those are the leaker's own statements; we have not verified them or examined the file.

GameGPU describes it as version 1.0.2.1, released three hours ahead of the deadline Cyberleek had set, which was tied to a crypto funding goal covered in our November 21 article.

Does an unofficial PC port become possible?

That is the concern raised by IGN Pakistan and Notebookcheck. The Xbox Series CPU is x86-64 like a PC. Per Wccftech, modder NotRexed, behind the Hookshot native port of Ocarina of Time, was waiting for this very executable for Leonida, a static recompilation of the Xbox version. It translates the RDNA2 GPU programs to Vulkan and reimplements the console's system software. The author says it is early and the game does not boot yet.

We covered these efforts in GTA 6 on PC: Leonida and Xbox2Windows progress. The executable removes one obstacle, but nothing shows a playable version will follow soon.

What are the risks?

Wccftech notes NotRexed's Tears of the Kingdom repository has vanished, likely after Nintendo action, and Leonida could face the same. Downloading these files also exposes players to malware, as a fake PDF guide already showed. Rockstar has announced no official PC version.

Why it matters

Nothing is playable on PC today. For Rockstar, the legal and technical pressure grows for a game released November 19 only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. We are following Leonida's progress and the publisher's response. Latest updates are in the journal.
