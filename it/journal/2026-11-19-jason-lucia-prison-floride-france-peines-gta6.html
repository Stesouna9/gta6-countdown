GTA 6: how many years in prison would Jason and Lucia get in Florida?

A lawyer consulted by 20 Minutes reviewed the trailer: in Florida Jason and Lucia would face centuries in prison, life in France.

Fonti 20minutes.fr

How much time would Jason and Lucia face if tried for real? French outlet 20 Minutes asked lawyer Dylan Slama to review the latest GTA 6 trailer against the penal code, first French, then Florida, the state Leonida parodies.

What does a robbery cost?

It is the core of the game and the harshest charge. In France, armed aggravated theft is 20 years and 150,000 euros, 30 years as an organised gang, five more for hostage-taking. In Florida, armed robbery with a firearm carries life. If someone dies, even unintentionally, "felony murder" applies: murder is charged to everyone involved, with death or life without parole possible.

And for car chases and explosives?

In France, refusing to stop costs two years and 15,000 euros, five if it endangers others. Florida grades fleeing: five to fifteen years for simple flight, up to thirty if it seriously injures or kills. Blowing up a car is ten years and 150,000 euros in France, one to ten years for possessing an explosive device in Florida, life if someone dies. The man standing on a taking-off plane risks six months and 7,500 euros for airside trespass in France, twenty years for hijacking.

Even Jason's snake matters: keeping a venomous or protected snake without permit is three years in France, five in Florida. The game reminds us Lucia has just left prison, which changes the picture on carrying weapons.

Why it matters

The verdict hinges on cumulative sentences. In France, same-kind sentences cannot exceed the highest legal maximum: life. In Florida they add up, hence centuries.
