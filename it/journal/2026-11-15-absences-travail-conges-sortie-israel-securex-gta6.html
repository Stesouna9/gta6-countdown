GTA 6 launch day: up to 95,000 Israeli workers may stay away, IsraJ says

IsraJ estimates 42,000 to 95,000 Israeli employees could be absent on 19 November; in Belgium, Securex says you can't play on the clock without your employer's consent.

Foto: Rockstar Games

Fonti israj.media-j.com · dhnet.be

Thursday 19 November could be a slow day at the office. According to IsraJ, between 42,000 and 95,000 Israeli employees might stay away on GTA 6 launch day, a potential loss of 75 to 170 million shekels in a single day. It is an estimate, not a measurement.

Why Israel is a special case

IsraJ starts from 4.45 million employees, of whom 1% to 2.1% could be absent, mostly young workers and people in tech and services. The key detail: 19 November is a Thursday, the last working day of the week for many. One day off turns the weekend into a three-day stay in Vice City.

The outlet cites figures from elsewhere: about 1.5 million US workers might miss a day, for nearly a billion dollars in lost productivity, and up to 2.56 million absences in the UK according to an Uswitch study, or £403.5 million. I could not check these projections at the source; they come from private studies.

Can you play during work hours?

DHnet, relying on Belgian HR firm Securex, is clear: no, not without your employer's agreement, even when working from home. Securex suggests two options: a temporary break, as long as you stay reachable and make up the time the same day, or a half-day of leave, with the rules agreed with your manager.

Some companies are getting ahead of it. Per IsraJ, Logitech gives its 7,000 employees worldwide a paid day off, called "Logi Game Day". In Switzerland, players were already booking leave for the launch.

Why it matters

Nothing changes on Rockstar's side: release stays 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the unlock time is still unknown, which complicates planning. See the release page for what we know. These numbers mostly show the launch has become an HR issue. Telling your employer before Thursday is the safest move.
