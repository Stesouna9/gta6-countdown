GTA 6: Sony requires the PS5 update before midnight, players grumble

Sony says system update 26.07-14.23.00 must be installed before midnight on November 19 or GTA 6 won't launch on PS5; it is the fourth update in under two months.

Fonti numerama.com · autoevolution.com

Sony's message is now explicit. On X, the PlayStation Support account says the system software update "is required to play Grand Theft Auto VI" and that players must download it "before midnight on November 19", reports Numerama. The file, version 26.07-14.23.00, released November 17, is 1.3 GB according to the site. We covered its arrival in yesterday's article.

What does Sony say about the update?

Release notes only mention "system software security fixes", Numerama writes. Sony doesn't say which flaws are fixed or whether the patch targets piracy. The site notes it is the fourth update in under two months and that the PS5 has been heavily targeted by piracy lately.

Why are players angry?

autoevolution collected reactions under Sony's post. One player jokes about spending launch night staring at "Downloading update… 7 hours remaining", another mocks a company "scared of jailbreakers". According to the site, some users think the update is mandatory to push as many consoles as possible onto the latest version, harder to compromise than older ones. That is players' reading: Sony hasn't confirmed it. The site also notes Microsoft hasn't released a similar Xbox update.

Another claim neither Sony nor the press has established: some say the patch blocks the New Zealand region switch used to play early. autoevolution says there is no confirmation.

How do I install it now?

Numerama gives the path: Settings, System, System Software, System Software Update and Settings, then "Update via Internet". The console reboots by itself. You can also tick automatic download and install, then leave the PS5 in rest mode with "Stay Connected to the Internet" on. The core advice is simple: do it before the unlock, because latecomers will wait at launch while Sony's servers push the file. The game's own preload has been open since November 10 for digital and November 12 for code-in-box, see the release page.

Why it matters

Nothing for anyone already updated: the PS5 will be ready at midnight. For the rest, the risk isn't the gigabyte of the update but the first-night queue. Still unknown: what exactly the patch fixes, and whether it really affects region switching.
