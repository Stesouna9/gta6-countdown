tin đồn 2026-10-28GTA 6 on PS5: the 210 GB size is still unconfirmed by Rockstar

According to PlayStation Game Size, GTA 6 would take 210 GB on PS5 in its standard edition. Insider Gaming says it corroborated the figure, but Rockstar has published nothing.

Đã báo cáo 2026-10-28GTA 6 purchase intent hits 29% in the US, a Circana record

Circana says 29% of US console and PC gamers intend to buy GTA 6, the highest purchase intent the firm has recorded for a video game. It measures intention, not orders.
