GTA 6 trophies: 55 in total, 28 hidden, platinum takes about 200 hours

GTA 6 has 55 trophies on PS5 and 55 achievements on Xbox Series, 28 of them hidden, all solo, and the platinum needs roughly 200 hours, according to Sortir à Paris.

Nguồn sortiraparis.com

GTA 6's list holds 55 trophies on PS5 and 55 achievements on Xbox Series, 28 of them hidden behind question marks, according to the guide by Sortir à Paris. Here is what it details, and what remains open.

How many trophies does GTA 6 have?

On PS5: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. On Xbox Series, the 55 achievements are worth 1,000 Gamerscore, and the platinum equivalent is a "Mythic" achievement. Good news for completionists: everything is solo, with no online trophy, unlike the previous entry according to the site.

Which trophies are hidden?

28 are masked: 12 tied to the story, 6 to side missions and 10 secret ones. The 12 story trophies unlock on their own and cannot be missed. Six more reward finishing secondary characters' storylines, so finish each mission chain when it appears. For the secret ones Rockstar gives only a hint. Sortir à Paris says the conditions for four of them are not confirmed yet.

What is the platinum and how long does it take?

The platinum is called "Vice Got a Price!" and requires every other trophy. First estimates point to about 200 hours.

Which trophies are hardest?

None depends on difficulty, but some take time or skill. The toughest skill one: gold medals in all 42 shooting range events. Those medals unlock three ammo cheat codes, but cheat codes disable trophies, the guide warns. Vaults only open at the end of chapter 4 and each heist must end in a successful escape.

Why it matters

The list is readable and free of online traps, but long: plan the platinum from chapter 1. The four remaining secret trophies are worth watching. For the rest, see the release page.
