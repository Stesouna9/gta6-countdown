GTA 6 at 120 FPS on PS6 and Xbox Helix: unconfirmed insider claims

Leaker Moore's Law Is Dead says GTA 6 could hit 60 FPS or 120 FPS on PS6 and Xbox Helix, per La Crème du Gaming. Nothing is confirmed.

Nguồn lacremedugaming.fr

GTA 6 could run at 60 FPS, or even offer a 120 FPS mode, on the future PS6 and Xbox Project Helix, claims leaker Moore's Law Is Dead, reports La Crème du Gaming. This is an insider leak, with no confirmation from Rockstar or the platform holders.

What frame rate does GTA 6 run at today?

On PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S the game is locked to 30 FPS: no 40 or 60 FPS mode is offered. According to La Crème du Gaming, Rockstar stands by that choice to preserve open-world density and ray tracing. Digital Foundry's analyses point to around 1440p at 30 FPS on PS5 and Series X, with an Xbox edge thanks to 120 Hz and VRR. Our PS5 versus Xbox Series X comparison covers this.

What does the insider claim for next-gen consoles?

Moore's Law Is Dead says PS6 and Xbox Helix would have a GPU "the equivalent of a 5080", a quote relayed by Notebookcheck. In his view a stable 60 FPS is realistic and 120 FPS an optional performance mode. The PS6 would target up to 40 RDNA 5 Compute Units, Xbox Helix up to 56, with a CPU going from 8 Zen 2 cores to up to 11 Zen 6 cores above 4 GHz. Insider Kepler_L2 is clearly more cautious about the PS6.

When would these machines arrive?

Per the same leak: PS6 production in Q2 2027, release between late 2027 and early 2028, Xbox Helix in the same window but around $1,000 to $1,200, roughly 950 to 1,150 euros in Europe. None of these figures is official.

No "Enhanced" version of GTA 6 has been announced for consoles that do not exist yet. Our piece on graphics settings per console recalls what is really known today.

Why it matters

Nothing for current players, who stay at 30 FPS on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. These estimates mostly show the smoothness debate continues after launch. Until Rockstar says something, they remain targets attributed to unannounced machines, not a promise. The game's date and price are on the price page.
