GTA 6 revenue: analysts expect $3-4 billion, says Lombard Odier

Private bank Lombard Odier cites analysts who expect $3 to $4 billion in revenue in the months after the November 19 launch.

Nguồn lombardodier.com

GTA 6 is an economic case study: in a note published on November 17, private bank Lombard Odier reads the launch as a sign of the rise of intangible assets (software, data, brands and intellectual property). The game releases on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

What does the bank say about GTA 6?

The note, titled "From gaming to gigantism", starts from the fact that the financial press has taken up the subject. Lombard Odier recalls that the Financial Times analysed the project's financial ramifications, with production said to have cost more than a billion dollars, and that Bloomberg visited the publisher's headquarters to tell the story of almost eight years of work by Rockstar's teams. Those cost figures are not confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two.

For the bank, value rests more and more on knowledge and experience rather than physical assets. GTA 6 illustrates that: a digital product, a brand established for almost thirty years, a catalogue that keeps earning.

Which GTA 5 numbers are the benchmark?

Lombard Odier cites GTA 5, sold at nearly 230 million copies since 2013. It earned about $800 million on day one and passed $1 billion in three days. More than ten years on, it still feeds the publisher's revenue through its digital ecosystem, online content and community.

On GTA 6, the bank notes that several analysts estimate the game could generate between $3 billion and $4 billion in the months after launch. That is an analyst estimate, not official data. On pre-orders, we already covered Sensor Tower's $1.4 billion and the Newzoo pre-order profile.

The public price is known: €79.99 for the standard edition and €99.99 for the digital-only Ultimate, detailed on the price page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for players, but outside observers now read a game launch as an economic event, with the financial press and banks in the front row. After November 19 we will see whether real figures match the estimates. Rockstar and Take-Two have published no sales figures so far.
