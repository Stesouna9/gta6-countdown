GTA 6: Rockstar sends Miami Heat players a custom GTA 6 PS5 Pro

Miami Heat players received a custom GTA 6 PS5 Pro, but neither Sony nor Rockstar has announced a retail version, according to Frandroid.

Ảnh: Rockstar Games

Nguồn frandroid.com

Miami's basketball players unboxed their package this Wednesday, November 18. Frandroid, citing Notebookcheck, says Davion Mitchell, Nikola Jović, Myron Gardner and Keshad Johnson showed a hard case sent by Rockstar in their Instagram stories.

What is in the case?

A custom PS5 Pro, two matching DualSense controllers and the physical game. The shells are black or grey, with palm trees and the GTA 6 logo, plus three backlit gradient strips from teal to magenta lifted from the poster. The controllers are blue, with a VI on the touchpad.

Can you buy it?

Not for now: Frandroid says neither Sony nor Rockstar announced a retail version of the console, shells or controllers. Sony's Prism Collection shells arrive February 4, 2027 at 74.99 euros, but only for the standard PS5 and Slim.

On Reddit it went down badly, as NBA players are among the few who can buy a PS5 Pro without thinking. In France it costs 899.99 euros since the April 2, 2026 hike, up from 799.99 at launch. See our November 15 article.
