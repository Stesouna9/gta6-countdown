GTA 6 user score hits 5.6 on Metacritic in Zelda review-bombing feud

Polygon reports GTA 6's Metacritic user score fell to 5.6 by 22 November amid a review-bombing feud with Zelda: Ocarina of Time fans.

Nguồn polygon.com

The 99 Metacritic score has a flip side: Polygon reports that GTA 6 and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time are trading user-score attacks. As of Sunday, 22 November, GTA 6's user score had dropped to 5.6 out of 10, labelled "mixed".

Why is Zelda involved?

According to Polygon, GTA 6 debuted with a Metascore of 99 from more than 150 critics, pushing Ocarina of Time, the previous number one, to second place. Some Zelda fans then gave GTA 6 zeros, and some GTA fans did the same to Ocarina of Time and its Switch 2 remake, whose user scores remain in the mid-8s, Polygon says.

The outlet adds that Soulcalibur, released on Dreamcast in 1999 and ranked third by Metacritic's algorithm, is also catching hostile reviews from GTA 6 supporters.

What do the negative reviews say?

Polygon quotes reviews aimed at the 30 fps cap, console exclusivity, restricted freedom or uninteresting characters. Others attack the female co-lead and mechanics that ask players to be considerate toward her, which the outlet files under culture-war criticism. It notes that many reviews are the bare minimum, typos included.

What we already knew

This is not new: on 21 November we covered the user score falling to 5.1. What is new is the feud between fan communities, which widens the story to other games.

Why it matters

A user score that drops within days reflects a wave of reviews, not a considered verdict. The critics' 99 and the players' 5.6 do not measure the same thing. Our journal keeps track of the numbers over time.
