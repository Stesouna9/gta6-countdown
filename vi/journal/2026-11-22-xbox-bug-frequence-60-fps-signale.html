GTA 6 Xbox frame-rate glitch reported, with double-speed warning

Two accounts describe an Xbox frame-rate glitch. One warns that gameplay also runs at twice its normal speed; Rockstar has not confirmed it.

Ảnh: Rockstar Games

Nguồn x.com · x.com

Two Xbox reports point to a possible GTA 6 frame-rate glitch, but the most significant detail is a warning. @GTAVI_Countdown describes a display-settings sequence intended to unlock the frame rate. @GTAlphaintel reports a Series X issue associated with Quick Resume and says the game then runs at double speed. Neither post amounts to confirmation from Rockstar of a supported performance option.

Does this establish a working 60 fps mode?

No. The accounts describe a glitch rather than an official setting. More frames can look appealing, yet the result would be fundamentally different if movement, animation and the rest of the simulation also accelerated. A usable performance mode needs to preserve the intended pace of play while improving how frequently the picture is updated.

The method described by @GTAVI_Countdown involves moving between the console’s 60 Hz and 120 Hz output settings around the game’s startup sequence. That account names both Series X and Series S. @GTAlphaintel refers specifically to Series X and Quick Resume. Those differences matter: we cannot turn two related reports into a single verified method that works across both machines.

What evidence is still missing?

We have not reproduced the behaviour or reviewed an independent frame-time measurement alongside these reports. The original posts were inaccessible during our web check, so this account relies on the messages preserved by our monitoring feed. The claims remain those of the named accounts, including the warning about doubled gameplay speed.

A meaningful test would need to establish the normal passage of time as well as the number of rendered frames. It would also have to identify the console model and show whether the behaviour holds in comparable scenes. Changing a television output setting alone cannot demonstrate that the game engine is producing a fresh frame at every screen refresh.

Why it matters

This is a technical lead worth documenting for Xbox players, rather than evidence of a newly released patch. It does not establish an official 60 fps option, and there is no verified promise here for either Series model. Our release information page covers the broader launch context. For this specific issue, reproducible testing or a Rockstar statement would move the discussion beyond two social-media reports and clarify whether the apparent improvement can preserve normal gameplay.
