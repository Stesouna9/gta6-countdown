GTA 6 : Kai Cenat beats four GTA games in under 100 hours on stream

Streamer Kai Cenat finished GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas and GTA IV in under 100 hours and has started GTA V ahead of GTA 6 on November 19.

Streamer Kai Cenat, who has 22 million followers on Twitch, has finished four Grand Theft Auto games in under 100 hours and has started the fifth, GTA V, according to Mashable. It is a warm-up ahead of November 19.

Where is Kai Cenat's marathon at?

Per Mashable, he started the challenge on November 5 with a plan to beat five games live. He has cleared GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas and GTA IV. On Monday night he told fans on X he had reached the last stop: "PLAYING GTA 5 NOW GET IN HERE".

The games are spilling into real life too. In a YouTube video with more than 500,000 views, he dressed as GTA IV hero Niko Bellic and took a performer dressed as Big Smoke through a McDonald's drive-thru, where Big Smoke tried to place his famously huge San Andreas order.

What about GTA 6?

The marathon is a warm-up. One viewer joked he was "warming up like it's a preseason game". For GTA 6, Mashable says Cenat plans to take his time, exploring between story missions and urging fans to play too. He wrote that he will not come off stream "until I feel like it's the right time", with no end date given.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game itself, but the signal counts: top Twitch names are replaying the whole saga before launch and will fill streams from day one. How long his GTA 6 stream will last is unknown.
