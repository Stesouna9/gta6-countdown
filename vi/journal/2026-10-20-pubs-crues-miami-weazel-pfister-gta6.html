GTA 6: crude Pfister, Kowalski and Kronos ads appear across Miami

Insider Gaming reports new in-game brand ads spotted in Miami, with crude taglines and a Weazel News van.

Nguồn insider-gaming.com

Miami keeps turning into Vice City. According to Insider Gaming, a new batch of advertisements has been spotted in the city: a Weazel News van and kiosk ads for the Vice City Manatees, Pfister, Kowalski and Kronos.

The photos were posted on X by OfficialJoelF, the outlet says. To our knowledge neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has commented as we write; Insider Gaming presents the ads as part of the launch campaign.

What do the Miami ads say?

Insider Gaming reports the taglines: "Go Topless" for Pfister, "Suddenly You Are Big Enough" for Kowalski, "Always Come Early" for Kronos. Typical double entendres for a series known for them.

These are fictional brands: the GTA versions of famous names, including Porsche and Swarovski according to the outlet. None is a real product.

A busy street campaign

The ads follow real-life GTA 6 cars seen on Miami streets and a collaboration that turned Miami Heat into Vice City. We also covered the Goodyear blimp announcing "Vice City, Leonida, USA" and the Miami-Dade County split over Vice City, which voted on October 20.

The full schedule is on our release page. The game launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Why it matters

A month before launch, Rockstar is staying in the street rather than in trailers, and Miami is the real-world backdrop for Leonida's satire. Insider Gaming expects more ads to surface. Nothing new about gameplay: the slogans tell us the tone, not the mechanics.
