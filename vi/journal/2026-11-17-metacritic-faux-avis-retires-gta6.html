GTA 6 user reviews removed by Metacritic before the game launches

VGC reports that Metacritic removed premature user reviews and closed submissions. Those scores cannot establish how the game plays.

Ảnh: Rockstar Games

Nguồn videogameschronicle.com

Video Games Chronicle reports that Metacritic briefly accepted GTA 6 user reviews before launch, then removed the premature entries and blocked further submissions. Some negative contributions had already pulled down the displayed user score. The incident concerns the public review section ahead of the November 19 release, rather than an established verdict from people who have completed the game.

What did the early score actually show?

A number on a review page needs context. Posting before release does not demonstrate access to the game, and VGC describes these particular entries as fake. Their presence therefore tells readers about a problem with submissions becoming available. It does not establish the quality of Rockstar’s new story or how the finished experience performs on a console.

The distinction between user reviews and professional criticism also matters. VGC’s report addresses user contributions. It does not provide a full count of deleted posts or an account-by-account explanation. Without that information, there is no sound basis for estimating how large or coordinated the episode was. The response can be reported without inventing the scale of the activity that prompted it.

Why is there normally a waiting period?

According to VGC, Metacritic generally keeps user reviews closed for at least 36 hours after a game releases. That delay is intended to discourage ratings from people who have not played. A general policy should not be mistaken for a confirmed submission timetable for this particular game: the report does not specify an exact GTA 6 opening time.

Removing the entries addresses the immediate problem described by the outlet. It cannot prove that future attempts will all be stopped. Early averages are also especially sensitive to a small pool of contributors. Readers looking for practical launch information can use our GTA 6 release page, where platform and release details serve a different purpose from a changing community score.

Why it matters

Screenshots can outlive the review entries they show. An image of an early score may keep circulating after moderation has removed the posts behind it, losing the context that makes it meaningful. Checking when a rating was recorded and whether the associated reviews remain available is more useful than treating the number as settled. VGC’s account supports caution about these premature scores. It supports neither a conclusion that the game has failed nor a claim that every player will agree about it once the public can play.
