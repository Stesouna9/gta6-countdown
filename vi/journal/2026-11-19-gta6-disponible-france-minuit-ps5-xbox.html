GTA 6 release in France: out since midnight on PS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has been playable in France since midnight on the night of 18 to 19 November, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Nguồn actugaming.net · x.com · franceinfo.fr

It is done: at midnight, GTA 6 became playable in France. ActuGaming says players who preordered and installed the game can now "finally see what Rockstar has been cooking for all these years". Thirteen years after GTA 5, Jason and Lucia are waiting in Leonida.

What time did GTA 6 release in France?

At midnight local time on the night of 18 to 19 November, as Rockstar's support page indicated. On X, RockstarNewsFRA posted at 00:00: "GTA 6 is now available, welcome to Leonida". GTAVI_Countdown reports the same midnight launch across most of continental Europe, followed an hour later by the UK and Portugal.

What you need to play

The game is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, at €79.99 for the standard edition (see our price page). No PC version has been announced. Players who started the preload on 12 November can jump straight in; everyone else has to download first.

A launch that moved France

On the France 2 evening news, franceinfo described a company where 75 employees took leave on 19 or 20 November, about 10% of its French workforce, and cited close to 15 million preorders. We covered that figure in our Sensor Tower article.
