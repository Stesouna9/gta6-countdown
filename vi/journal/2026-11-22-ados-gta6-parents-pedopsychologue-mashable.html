GTA 6 and teens: what parents need to know, according to Mashable

A child psychologist quoted by Mashable says it is even advisable for parents to say no to GTA 6; the game is rated PEGI 18 in Europe.

Nguồn mashable.com

Can teens play GTA 6? In a guide for parents, Mashable quotes child psychologist Dr. Brett Biller, who says it is "even advisable" to say no. The game, the site recalls, features sex, strip clubs, drugs and graphic violence.

What does the psychologist recommend?

Biller, of Hackensack University Medical Center, has no objection to video games in general. He argues that exposing teens to GTA 6's hyperrealistic violence is "simply not worth it": "the negative consequences can outweigh the positive impact". He adds that research does not prove game violence causes aggression, but notes a link between violence at home and aggression.

How to talk to a teen who wants to play?

Mashable advises acknowledging the teen's frustration and fear of being left out when friends play. Co-playing is called a "double-edged sword": it allows parents to discuss disturbing scenes but can also suggest violence is no big deal. Common Sense Media calls the game an "extremely mature crime game" for adults 18 and older, while noting that only parents know whether an older teen is ready.

In Europe the game is rated PEGI 18, as our release page explains. The debate is not new: a US lawmaker already weighed in, as we reported.

Why it matters

Launch moved the question from theory to the dinner table. Mashable's advice is not a rule, but it gives parents a way to answer: listen first, set the limit second, and keep the conversation open.
