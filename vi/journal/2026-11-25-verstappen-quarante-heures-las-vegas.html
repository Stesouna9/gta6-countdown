GTA 6: Max Verstappen says he played 40 hours in Las Vegas

Anadolu reports the driver’s account of morning and evening gaming sessions, with more than 80 percent of missions completed according to his own estimate.

Ảnh: Rockstar Games

Nguồn aa.com.tr

Anadolu Ajansı reports that Max Verstappen says he spent around 40 hours playing GTA 6 during the Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend. The agency attributes the account to a livestream on the Twitch channel of Verstappen Sim Racing. This is the driver describing his own gaming time, rather than an independently measured total or a connection record released by Rockstar Games.

Was he playing on the flight home?

That was apparently the question, but not the answer. According to Anadolu, Verstappen said he slept on the plane and did most of his gaming in the mornings and evenings while in Las Vegas. It is a small distinction that prevents an anecdote about downtime in the city from becoming a different story about a flight spent in Vice City.

The reported total is approximate. The agency does not supply a session diary or a breakdown for individual days. His account supports the observation that he spent a substantial amount of time with the game. It cannot establish exactly how he fitted every session around his other activities, or how much he played on any particular morning. Those details are absent from the report.

Has he finished all of GTA 6?

Anadolu says Verstappen described himself as being beyond 80 percent of the missions. That is narrower than claiming complete progress through every activity, and different from a verified 100 percent save file. The article does not explain the precise scope of the percentage he mentioned. It should therefore remain his estimate of mission progress, without being upgraded into a comprehensive completion claim.
