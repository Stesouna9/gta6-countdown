GTA 6: Call of Duty loses over 40% of daily players, Alinea estimates

Alinea Analytics data relayed by La Crème du Gaming shows Call of Duty falling from 7.6 to 4.4 million daily active players after GTA 6 launched.

Nguồn lacremedugaming.fr

GTA 6 has left a mark on Activision. According to La Crème du Gaming, citing Alinea Analytics and analyst Rhys Elliott, the Call of Duty HQ app (Modern Warfare 4, Warzone, Black Ops 7) fell from about 7.6 million daily active players to 4.4 million right after November 19.

How far did Call of Duty fall after GTA 6?

The drop reaches 42% across all platforms, peaking at 44% on PS5. Four days later, Call of Duty would still sit about 41% below its usual level, while Fortnite and EA Sports FC 27, also hit, were already recovering according to the same source. These are estimates from an analytics firm, not figures published by Activision.

Context matters: Modern Warfare 4 launched on October 23, 2026 and its live seasons were just starting. Many publishers moved their releases out of Rockstar's window, but Activision did not. La Crème du Gaming recalls that Activision marketing director Tyler Bahl expected the opposite, that GTA 6 would bring more people to gaming in general.

Why does a single-player game weigh so much on a live-service one?

Because it eats time. Sony cites more than 39 million unique PS5 players and about 683 million hours played, close to 17.4 hours per player (we covered those numbers in our PlayStation record story). Alinea separately estimates 44.9 million copies sold in four days for nearly $4 billion. Every two or three hour session in Vice City is an evening not spent in a Warzone lobby. France would be the second biggest PS5 market for GTA 6, with around 3 million copies.

Why it matters

The game has neither an online mode nor a PC version. Rockstar has announced no date for GTA 6 Online, and no PC version is announced: the rumors the outlet mentions, a PC port in early 2027 and multiplayer in autumn 2027, remain rumors. If they proved true, the contest with Call of Duty would only be starting. For now, the picture is a launch month in which one single-player game empties the queues of the others.
