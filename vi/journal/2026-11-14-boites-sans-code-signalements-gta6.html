GTA 6 physical boxes reportedly arrive without PS5 or Xbox codes

Notebookcheck reports missing redemption codes in sealed cases, with affected buyers struggling to obtain help.

Ảnh: Rockstar Games

Nguồn notebookcheck.net

Some GTA 6 buyers have reportedly opened sealed retail cases and found no usable download code. Notebookcheck describes complaints involving both PlayStation and Xbox copies, including customers who say their requests for help have gone unresolved. The report offers individual examples rather than a count of affected units, so the scale of the issue remains unclear.

What are buyers reporting?

One PS5 case described by Notebookcheck contained an insert with a grey strip where the redemption code should have been. Another customer, identified as Reddit user InParties, reportedly ordered directly from Rockstar and received the double-sided poster but no PlayStation Store code. These are accounts relayed by the publication, rather than findings from an official investigation.

The practical distinction matters for a code-in-box release. Receiving the packaging does not itself grant access to the digital purchase. A poster and an otherwise complete case cannot substitute for the missing redemption information. That is the access problem raised by the reported complaints, regardless of how attractive the physical extras might be.

Has support resolved the cases?

According to Notebookcheck, Best Buy declined a refund in one example. The customer who purchased from Rockstar reportedly had two support tickets closed. Those experiences do not establish that every retailer or every support agent is following the same policy. They show that the specific buyers cited had not obtained the outcome they wanted.

The publication also says Rockstar directs customers who bought from third-party retailers back to those stores. It suggests a manufacturing error as a possible explanation for missing inserts. That remains a hypothesis: the collected report supplies no confirmed cause, affected batch numbers or official recall. There is no basis here for calculating a failure rate.

Why it matters

This is a separate concern from the reported risk of codes being exposed through some packaging. A missing code and a stolen code are different situations. The available evidence does not establish one explanation for every complaint, and the reports should not be combined into a claim of widespread theft.

For anyone preparing for the November 19 release, delivery of a case and successful access to the game are two separate checks. Keeping order details and accurately describing the contents gives a seller a clearer account of a problem. The reported cases cannot establish an automatic entitlement to a particular support outcome, or show that all PS5 and Xbox retail copies share a defect.
