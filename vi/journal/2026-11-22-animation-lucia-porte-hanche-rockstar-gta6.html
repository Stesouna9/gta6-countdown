GTA 6: Rockstar spent months on Lucia nudging a door open with her hip

Creative Bloq says Rockstar spent months animating Lucia bumping a door open with her hip when her hands are full, a realism detail by design.

Nguồn creativebloq.com

Why does Lucia open doors with her hip? Because her hands are full, and Rockstar spent months on it. Creative Bloq spotted it in its review, when Lucia buys a shake and ice cream and turns to push through. Mike Jones, Director of Animation: Gameplay, says: "That took months of development, but it matters."

What do the animators say?

Per Creative Bloq, Jones wanted a game "fast and responsive yet maintain a weight which grounds our world in realism". Red Dead Redemption 2 had a slower heartbeat; Jason and Lucia's world "demands a faster pacing". Teams cared about "something as mundane as how the players might use a door when carrying drinks".

What about traversal and the couple?

Jones calls traversal "far more fluid than anything we've achieved before", with momentum-keeping vaults and climbs, "countless variations" and rooftops with "astounding" detail. Mark Tennant, VP of Animation, says the couple's physical side needed "a lot of thought and iteration", letting players interact "in a natural, loving way". He cites close to a thousand ambient animation sets and hundreds of one-off events.

The couple has drawn plenty of commentary, from PCMag's review to Kotaku's two-screen take. Release details are on our release page.
