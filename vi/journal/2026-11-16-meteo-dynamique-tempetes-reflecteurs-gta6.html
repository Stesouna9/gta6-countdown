GTA 6 weather: dynamic storms and road reflectors, per The Weather Channel

Rockstar reportedly detailed GTA 6 weather to The Weather Channel: rain cutting visibility, tropical storms and road reflectors, according to relays on X.

Rockstar sent six new screenshots and a batch of details about GTA 6 weather to The Weather Channel, according to several GTA accounts on X on Monday evening, including GTAVI_Countdown and GTA6 Hub FR. We could not yet read the original piece; this relies on those relays, and nothing has appeared on the Rockstar Newswire at this hour.

What do we know about GTA 6 weather?

According to @gtasix_, the system was fully reworked: dynamic, localized and persistent, meaning conditions don't hit the whole map at once and they linger. Tropical storms stir up the sea and, per Rockstar News FR, leave marks on nature after they pass.

Does rain change driving?

If the details hold, yes. Rain cuts visibility depending on intensity and light. Reflectors were installed on some roads to help drivers in heavy rain. GTAVI_Countdown adds that you can repair your car's headlights at Coastal Auto Care, which matters when driving at night in a storm.

What about the swamps?

One screenshot shows a character walking through the Pelican Valley swamps during a storm. GTA6 Hub FR says animals are harder to spot there. It fits what Rockstar has shown of Leonida's wildlife, see our latest journal articles, but no accessible official source confirms it.

Why it matters

Three days before the November 19 launch, weather becomes a gameplay element and not just scenery: visibility, roads, sea, nature. We still need to see how far the system goes in practice, and to read The Weather Channel's text. We will update as soon as the source is readable. All dates are on the release page.
