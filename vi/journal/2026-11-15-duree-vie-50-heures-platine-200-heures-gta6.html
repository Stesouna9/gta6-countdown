GTA 6: 50-Hour Story and 200 Hours for Platinum, Insider Gaming Says

According to Insider Gaming, GTA 6's campaign runs about 50 hours across nearly 100 missions, and Platinum would take 200 hours. Nothing official from Rockstar.

Ảnh: Rockstar Games

Nguồn insider-gaming.com · x.com

How long does GTA 6 take to finish? According to Insider Gaming, citing people who have played the game, the campaign runs around 50 hours, with nearly 100 missions across five chapters. Rockstar has published nothing of the kind: these figures are unofficial, and the outlet itself admits the length varies from player to player.

How long is the GTA 6 story, according to Insider Gaming?

The site, run by Tom Henderson, gives "around 50 hours" as an average benchmark, based on a consensus among those who have touched the game. The figure is firm on one point: nearly 100 missions, five chapters. Real playtime will depend on your style and on the side activities Rockstar leaves open.

For scale, Insider Gaming recalls a statement by Rob Nelson, Rockstar's head of development, to The New York Times: his own February playthrough lasted 80 hours, main story plus some optional goals with narrative consequences. He also hopes players will "explore, maybe get into some trouble". An 80-hour run is not a 50-hour run, and we don't know how much of it is pure story.

Platinum in 200 hours, 170 species to scan

The heavy part is trophies. Per Insider Gaming, the Platinum would take around 200 hours on PlayStation, more than double GTA V. Red Dead Redemption 2, the other benchmark cited, has been logged at 196 hours. The outlet notes part of it depends on luck, since some trophies rely on random events.

One more detail: the map's 170 wildlife species will need to be scanned, as in RDR2. For anyone who loves Leonida's nature, it is a long hunt, and how the scan works in practice is still unknown. The post from X account @gtasix_ (over 459,000 views) relays this and also mentions "some trophies" that the article does not detail.

What remains unknown

The full trophy and achievement list is not public. Nor do we know where the numbers come from: "people who have played" is an anonymous source. Release is set for November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, four days away. For date and preload details, see our release page.

Why it matters

If you booked time off, 50 hours of story is a week of full evenings. Platinum hunters know what to expect: several weeks of play. Until launch, these numbers remain an estimate to treat with caution.
