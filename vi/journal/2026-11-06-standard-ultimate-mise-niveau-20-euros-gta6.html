GTA 6 Standard to Ultimate upgrade reported at €20 in France

French reporting describes a €20 Ultimate upgrade for Standard owners, while SVG reports a $20 option in the US.

Ảnh: Rockstar Games

Nguồn lacremedugaming.fr · svg.com

La Crème Du Gaming reports that French console players can move from GTA 6 Standard to Ultimate through a €20 digital upgrade. SVG describes a matching $20 option in the United States. That makes the decision about extras something buyers can revisit after choosing the base edition, rather than necessarily settling it with their first order.

Can Standard owners upgrade later?

The French report describes a separate downloadable add-on available to Standard owners, including after release. It lists a €20 price in the euro-area Xbox store and says the PS5 price generally becomes visible once Standard is attached to the account. These are the publication’s observations. We have not independently checked the live product listing for every account, country or console.

SVG says US buyers can preorder the upgrade for $20 through either console storefront. Its account also covers the boxed Standard release: players would first redeem the download voucher, then purchase the additional content. A US dollar price should not be treated as a universal price, and an add-on is not a standalone copy of the game.

What does the extra payment change?

Using the French prices reported by La Crème Du Gaming, the calculation is straightforward: €79.99 for Standard plus €20 for the upgrade equals €99.99, the listed Ultimate price. At those prices, waiting changes when the decision is made rather than reducing the total. Retail discounts are a separate consideration and should be checked against the actual offer before purchase.

La Crème Du Gaming distinguishes the shared main campaign and map from the extra vehicles, equipment and activities included with Ultimate. SVG takes a more critical view of that division. Neither description removes the need to read the store’s own content list. Our edition and price guide provides context for comparing the packages, while the listing attached to a player’s account determines what they are buying.

Why it matters

For an undecided buyer, the useful part is flexibility. Standard can be the initial purchase, with a later decision about whether the extras justify another payment. The French report treats preorder rewards separately from the upgrade, so those should not be assumed to come with it. Neither source establishes early access through this add-on. GTA 6 remains scheduled for November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
