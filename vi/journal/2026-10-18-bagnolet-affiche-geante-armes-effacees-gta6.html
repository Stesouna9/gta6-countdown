GTA 6: two guns vanished from the giant cover banner in Bagnolet, near Paris

<a href="https://www.konbini.com/internet/gta-6-saffiche-en-geant-dans-le-93-mais-il-manque-deux-petits-details-sur-la-jaquette/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Konbini</a> reports that the GTA 6 banner on the Mercuriales tower in Bagnolet no longer shows Lucia's pistol or an assault rifle from the official cover.

Driving around eastern Paris, you may have met Lucia at building size. According to Konbini, the GTA 6 cover has covered one of the Mercuriales towers in Bagnolet since early October, visible from the ring road and the A3. One detail is off.

Which weapons are missing from the banner?

Comparing it with the official artwork, Konbini spots two disappearances. At the top, Lucia has lost her pistol, and the hand holding it. Lower down, an assault rifle is gone. Everything else is unchanged: cars, characters, poses, mood. So this is not a strip hung upside down but a deliberate edit.

Who erased the guns?

Nobody has said. Konbini cites Kotaku to note that neither Rockstar Games nor the Bagnolet town hall has explained the changes. The theories going around (a precaution tied to outdoor advertising rules, a request from the tower's owners) are not backed by any source. We will not pick one either.

Outdoor ads for GTA 6 have already stirred reactions elsewhere, such as in Miami Beach, where the promotion passed by a 4 to 3 vote. The launch campaign has entered the cityscape, and the cityscape has rules.

Why it matters

Nothing for the game, out November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, rated PEGI 18. But the anecdote shows how a franchise full of guns presents itself in the open air. More campaign curiosities are on our community page.
