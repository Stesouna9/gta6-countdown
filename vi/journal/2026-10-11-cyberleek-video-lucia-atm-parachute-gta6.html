GTA 6 leak: Cyberleek posts new Lucia video with ATM robbery and parachute jump

Cyberleek posted five minutes of Lucia gameplay in Vice City, apparently captured with a dev menu per Eurogamer; the $30M full build threat is still hanging.

Sources eurogamer.net · insider-gaming.com · wolfsgamingblog.com

J-38 GTA 6 ra mắt sau 38 ngày →

Cyberleek is back. Eurogamer reports that the leaker posted a five-minute video on ResetEra following Lucia around Vice City, days after the 25-minute Jason footage and one day after threatening to release a full playable build. We are not showing any of it and we are not linking to it: the footage sits outside any official channel, and fake download links keep multiplying.

According to Eurogamer and Insider Gaming, Lucia steps out of an old coupe, heads up to an ATM, beats a passer-by and takes their cash. The machine shows a $15,000 limit and the logo of a fictional bank, Buckme. She then enters a building, takes a call in the elevator and is handed a parachute on the roof. Cyberleek botches both landings, which incidentally shows a checkpoint system. Wolf's Gaming Blog notes a side mission tied to a fictional cryptocurrency and an in-game ad for the banking app.

Does this video show what the final game will contain?

No, and the press says so. Lucia appears undressed and nobody reacts, and Eurogamer notes the player seems to have a dev menu: it would be a debug option, not something you can do in the open world. Insider Gaming adds that people who saw a demo say the final game has no full nudity. These are development images, not a release build.

The outcome had been signposted. Our piece on the Lucia-dominated poll pointed to it, since the votes asked for exactly this. Cyberleek now promises a 4K close-up, per Insider Gaming.

Đọc thêm Cyberleek cuộc thăm dò do Lucia dẫn đầu khi người hâm mộ đặt câu hỏi về video mới nhất của người rò rỉ

What about the full build threat?

It stands. The leaker ties publication to a $30 million market cap for their coin, as covered in the $30M ultimatum. Wolf's Gaming Blog says it is unclear whether that means a playable build or a video of the whole game. Eurogamer recalls that Rockstar called the leaks "heartbreaking" and that Take-Two is hunting the person behind them, even trying to subpoena Discord.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for launch: GTA 6 arrives on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preloading from 12 November (all dates). What changes is your risk: the more Cyberleek posts, the more fake downloads and booby-trapped sites thrive. Leaks are no reliable preview of the game and mostly spoil the discovery. The safest move is to wait for what Rockstar publishes itself.
