GTA 6 PC release: the 209 GB torrent is not a real port, TweakTown says

A 208.62 GB file sold as a GTA 6 PC version is on Rutor, but it runs the leaked Xbox build through VIctory, with no reliable benchmark, TweakTown reports.

सूत्रों का कहना है tweaktown.com

A 208.62 GB "PC version" of GTA 6 is circulating on a Russian torrent site, but it is not a Rockstar port. TweakTown, citing HKEPC, says the archive appeared on Rutor on 26 November, labelled "Recomp", version 1.0.2.1, "portable" with no installation.

Is it a real GTA 6 PC release?

No. Rockstar has announced no PC version, and our release page lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. According to TweakTown, the package uses VIctory, an open-source compatibility layer that runs the leaked Xbox Series X|S build on Windows. It executes the game's x86-64 code directly, swaps out the console's system software and translates shaders for DirectX 12. The size, about 209 GB, matches the leaked Xbox package. The site adds that "recomp" is misleading: most of these projects rely on translation, not true static recompilation.

How does it run?

Nothing solid. TweakTown quotes Reddit users reporting 10 to 30 FPS on 32 GB of RAM and 16 GB of VRAM, very low resolution, dark images and missing or flickering textures. Another claim of 30 FPS on an RTX 4070 is called unverifiable, since that card has 12 GB of VRAM, below the listing's own 16 GB minimum. No reliable benchmark exists. For comparison, the best known forks on the PortWatch VI tracker sit at 3 to 5 FPS, per the same source.

We have covered this scene before: the BBC looks at the AI race to clone the game, and the teen behind Project Leonida vows to continue.

Should you download it?

TweakTown "strongly" advises against it: 200 GB from an anonymous uploader is a real security risk, especially as booby-trapped fake ports already exist. We reported two fake GitHub ports flagged by Bitdefender. Some Reddit users vouch for the tracker's moderation; that proves nothing about this file. And downloading a copy of a leaked game is still piracy.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for players who want to play legitimately: there is still no official PC version and no date. What is new is the packaging, a ready-made 209 GB download with performance claims nobody has measured. Caution and patience.
