GTA 6: Australia's workplace regulator explains how to book launch day off

On Instagram, the Fair Work Ombudsman playfully sets out the unpaid-leave rules for November 19. One more sign of how big the launch has become.

When a labour regulator takes notice of GTA 6, the game has moved beyond ordinary entertainment. According to MP1st, the Fair Work Ombudsman, the Australian body that oversees workplace law, posted a deliberately light-toned message on Instagram to guide players who may want to be off at launch.

What the post says

The post imagines a game coming out tomorrow. It reminds workers they can ask for general unpaid leave, but only with their employer's agreement. Several kinds of unpaid leave do not apply here, such as caring for a family member, volunteering or parental reasons. With no category covering a game launch, workers without paid leave depend on their boss's approval.

For annual leave, MP1st adds, employees do not have to explain why they are asking, but employers can refuse if too many colleagues request the same day for business reasons. The message is mainly meant to prevent sudden, unexplained absences.

A game bigger than its market

The Australian move is not isolated. The same outlet recalls that in Norway a retailer is offering a free copy to parents whose baby is born on launch day. These stories say the same thing: Thursday, November 19, 2026 will be treated as a cultural event, with consequences for work schedules.

A useful caveat: these rules apply to Australia. Elsewhere local law applies, and most employers have planned nothing specific. As for the game, it launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S with preloading from November 12, so you can start right away without waiting on the download. The exact unlock time is still unknown; our release page follows it.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game, but the episode shows institutions are preparing for the launch as much as players are. If you plan to book a day, ask early: employers can refuse when requests pile up, and November 19 is likely to be in demand.
