GTA 6: Arion Kurtaj pleads guilty to the 2022 Rockstar hack

Arion Kurtaj, behind the 2022 leak, pleaded guilty in London on 16 November, three days before GTA 6 launches; sentencing is set for a later date.

Arion Kurtaj, the hacker behind the 2022 GTA 6 leak, pleaded guilty on 16 November at Woolwich Crown Court in London, Rock Paper Shotgun reports, citing the BBC. The hearing comes three days before GTA 6 launches on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. He will be sentenced at a later date.

What did Arion Kurtaj admit?

He admits the Rockstar Games breach and several other attacks carried out with the Lapsus$ group when he was 16 and 17. According to Insider Gaming, he pleaded guilty to every count with no visible emotion. He is now 21.

Rock Paper Shotgun says the Rockstar hack was done from a Travelodge room, where he was housed under police protection, using an Amazon Fire TV Stick plugged into the TV plus a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse. He was on bail for other offences at the time.

What the leak exposed

Ninety clips of GTA 6 in development went online, close to an hour of gameplay, before the first trailer existed. They confirmed a female protagonist, among other things. Rockstar refused his demand to contact him on Telegram within 24 hours. The publisher put the cost of the breach at $5 million, not counting thousands of hours of staff time, according to Rock Paper Shotgun.

Why it took four years

Found unfit for a standard trial because of the severity of his autism, Kurtaj went through a "trial of the facts": jurors decided whether he committed the acts, not whether he had criminal intent. In December 2023 the court ordered him held in a secure hospital indefinitely. A routine assessment later found him fit to stand trial and he moved to a regular prison in January, Insider Gaming writes, citing the BBC. We covered the trial date in our 10 October article.

Not to be confused with Cyberleek

Kurtaj has no link to the 2026 leaks attributed to "Cyberleek", Insider Gaming notes. See the release page for launch details.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for players: the 19 November date is untouched. The court case is closing, but the sentence is unknown and no sentencing date has been announced yet.
