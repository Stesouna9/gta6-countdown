GTA 6 PS5 Pro 40 fps mode: what the preload files show

A PS5 Pro preload datamine reveals a 120 Hz mode and PSSR 13.60, per La Crème du Gaming. Rockstar has confirmed nothing and still targets 30 fps.

Could GTA 6 get a 40 fps mode on PS5 Pro? According to La Crème du Gaming, dataminer Tex2, a GTAForums regular, found a 120 Hz mode and a newer version of Sony's PSSR upscaler in the PS5 Pro build of the preload. Rockstar has confirmed nothing.

What is in the PS5 Pro files?

The outlet says the files, available since 10 November, hold a "High Frame Rate 120 Hz" flag and a "High CPU Frequency Mode", pointing to a profile separate from the standard PS5. There is also an "MFSR / PSSR 13.60" module, while many "PS5 Pro Enhanced" games stay on the 13.00A branch. The base PS5 build does not mention QSSR, the upscaler for non-Pro consoles. RockstarINTEL and the specialist press picked up the find.

Why talk about 40 fps?

The report also notes that no VRR tag appears. A 120 Hz output without VRR suits a 40 fps mode, since 120 divided by 40 gives three refreshes per frame, an even rhythm. Going from 30 to 40 fps means roughly a third more frames and slightly lower latency, provided the TV runs at 120 Hz. This reads what the engine declares it can do. It is not an announcement, and a datamine cannot say what the game will actually offer.

What does Rockstar say?

Officially the target remains 30 fps on consoles. La Crème du Gaming recalls remarks by Rob Nelson, co-director of Rockstar North, quoted by Notebookcheck: the developers "aim for 30 fps for the 19 November release", with an open world like Leonida being very demanding on the CPU. The outlet mentions an internal resolution around 1440p upscaled to 4K, a figure that does not come from Rockstar. We previously covered a 60 fps test with heavy compromises on the same console.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for players yet: a 120 Hz mode in the files makes 40 fps possible, not certain. A mode like this could arrive later through an update, but no source announces it. For the 19 November launch, expect 30 fps, as Rockstar says. Launch details are gathered on the release page. The answer will come with the first console tests once the game unlocks.
