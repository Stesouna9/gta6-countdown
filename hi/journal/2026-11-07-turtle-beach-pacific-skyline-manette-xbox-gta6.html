GTA 6: Turtle Beach Pacific Skyline controller up for pre-order at €89.99

Turtle Beach lists a Vice City-themed Xbox and PC controller on Amazon at €89.99 for 19 November, according to big-n.fr.

फ़ोटो: Rockstar Games

A GTA 6-themed accessory is already up for pre-order: the Turtle Beach Pacific Skyline wireless controller, listed at €89.99 on Amazon, according to big-n.fr. Its release is announced for 19 November 2026, the same day as the game.

It works on Xbox and PC, not on PS5. The site describes a pastel design evoking the Pacific coast, with palm-tree silhouettes, and RGB lighting that is said to "reveal the hidden San Diego skyline", per the maker. The nod to Vice City is obvious, though Rockstar's name does not appear in the description the site relays.

What is inside the controller?

The listing quoted by big-n.fr mentions TMR thumbsticks, a technology meant to limit stick drift, a rechargeable battery and a 3-metre USB-C cable for charging or wired play. Amazon promises delivery "a few days after" release, with its usual 14-day returns.

Official licence or just a wink?

That is unclear. Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has announced a partnership with Turtle Beach so far, and the source quotes no statement from the maker on this. We found only one outlet describing the product, so treat specs and price with caution until retail is live. See our goodies page for the official merchandise.

big-n.fr also notes that Sony has not shown an official GTA 6 controller for PS5, so Xbox and PC owners get accessories first.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game, but third-party makers are clearly targeting 19 November. Xbox players get one more option to weigh against standard pads before pre-ordering. We will update if Turtle Beach or Rockstar confirm an official link.
