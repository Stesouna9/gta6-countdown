GTA 6: Australia's NBN sets a traffic record of 33.19 Tbps

NBN Co recorded 33.19 Tbps on November 12, the day code-in-box copies reached stores, and more than 70 petabytes of extra traffic since GTA 6 preloading opened.

Australia's internet didn't break, but it set a record. TechRadar got figures from NBN Co, the national network operator, on the effect of GTA 6 preloading, open since November 10. On Thursday, November 12, the day code-in-box copies reached stores, the network "hit a new traffic record of 33.19Tbps", according to Rob Bates of NBN Co media relations, quoted by the site.

What exactly is the record?

The peak tops the previous one, 32.91 Tbps, set in 2025. NBN Co calls it "the most data ever transmitted across the network per second". That is about 3 Tbps, or 10% above baseline. NBN Co is careful: a Fortnite patch shipped the same day, so the record can't be credited to GTA 6 alone.

How much data since preloading began?

Since preloading opened, NBN Co counts more than 70 petabytes of additional traffic, which it says is roughly equivalent to 330,000 GTA 6 downloads. The file is 212 GB on PS5. The math gives a scale, not an actual player count: not all that traffic is Rockstar's game.

Over the first four days, Tasmania saw the biggest peak rise above its baseline at 16.3%, ahead of South Australia (14.2%), Victoria (13.9%), New South Wales (13.8%) and Queensland (13.6%). Western Australia was lowest at 6.6%. On average across the four days Tasmania led again with 10.7%, then Victoria (9.1%) and South Australia (8.2%). These percentages are rises over each region's usual traffic, not total volumes.

How does this compare with Europe?

Australia is among the first countries served by the rolling timezone unlock, after New Zealand, as we covered in our piece on the Australian PS5 file. In France we already reported Virgin Media's traffic records and Orange's traffic during preloading. Australia shows the same pattern: the bump comes during preload, before launch.

Why it matters

For players, nothing changes: if your game is preloaded there is nothing left to download before midnight except the PS5 system update. For operators, these readings are a live test ahead of launch day. NBN Co has not published a figure for November 19 yet. Regional timings are on the release page.
