GTA 6: Miami Beach approved the game's promotion by a 4-3 vote

Miami Beach let Rockstar promote GTA 6 by a 4-3 vote, for at least $3 million. Some officials object to the image it gives the city.

In Miami, the GTA 6 campaign is going ahead, but not without friction. According to DualShockers, citing the Miami Herald, the city of Miami Beach let Rockstar advertise there by a 4-3 vote, and some officials still resent the decision.

What does the Rockstar and Miami Beach deal include?

The vote took place on September 10, 2026. Rockstar will pay the city at least $3 million, and game branding will run from October 15 to December 31, reports DualShockers. A smaller contract skipped the vote: Miami-Dade County Mayor Levine Cava can negotiate deals under $1 million alone, hence a $975,000 agreement to put the Vice City logo atop the Kaseya Center.

Why do some officials object?

The fear is about the city's image. During debate, Commissioner René Garcia said "we're depicting Miami like a murder capital," per the Miami Herald as cited by DualShockers. Commissioner Micky Steinberg said "philosophically, I feel we should not be moving forward." Chair Anthony Rodriguez replied: "This is a fictional game. We are all adults in this community. We recognize it's just a game."

What does the campaign look like on the ground?

Rather discreet, notes DualShockers: few GTA logos, mostly "VI", Vice City mentions and the game's color palette. Residents report cars with Vice City plates, a Vice City blimp overhead, and branded beach chairs and umbrellas. The Miami Heat will also play a game with Rockstar on November 18, the day before launch, with advertising inside and outside the arena.

Elsewhere in the US, the same source mentions a wrapped bus in Chicago, a Times Square billboard with the guns left out of the artwork, and a mural on Hollywood Boulevard. The missing weapons echo the Paris banner in our Paris story. See also the game cars spotted in Miami.

Why it matters

The campaign will not stop: the deal is voted and paid. But the 4-3 split shows real Florida is not unanimous about its fictional double, and Rockstar is handling it carefully. Other cities should follow until November 19.
