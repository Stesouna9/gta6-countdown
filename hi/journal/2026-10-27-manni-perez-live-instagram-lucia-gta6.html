GTA 6: Manni Perez, linked to Lucia, goes live without naming the game

The actor fans link to Lucia Caminos held an 11-minute Instagram live without saying GTA 6; Rockstar hasn't confirmed her role.

Actor Manni Perez, whom many fans link to Lucia Caminos, made an Instagram appearance without ever saying the game's name. Polygon describes an 11-minute live session a few weeks before GTA 6 launches. Rockstar has not confirmed her role.

One limit up front: the cast has never been revealed. The link between the 34-year-old actor and Lucia is a community conviction fed by a resemblance. Konbini calls her the "presumed" actress and notes Rockstar has confirmed nothing.

What did Manni Perez say during the live?

Per Polygon, she framed it as a chance for people to know her better and explained her low profile: she calls herself an intense introvert. "I'm not a fan of social media, but for the work that I do, unfortunately, we need it," she said. She added she doesn't feel photogenic.

She also pushed back on an assumption: "I am not rich, people." She says she shops, cooks and does laundry herself and drives a small used sedan. Konbini repeats the quote.

Why does this feed the Lucia rumors?

Because thousands of comments mentioned Lucia and GTA 6 while she never named the game, according to Polygon. The outlet floats two explanations, fatigue or an NDA; that is the writer's guess. Polygon also notes she deleted her accounts in mid-2025 after harassment from some fans, which makes her return notable.

Konbini adds she has appeared in Jessica Jones, The Blacklist and Law & Order: SVU, and in GTA Online as a blackjack dealer, per IGN. None of that proves anything about GTA 6. For the character, see our Lucia Caminos profile.

Why it matters

Nothing about the game: no confirmation, no name in an official credit. It shows how closely the community scrutinizes every clue before November 19, at the risk of harassing a private person. The full cast will surface at launch or in the credits. More on the journal page.
