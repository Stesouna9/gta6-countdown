GTA 6: PlayStation releases limited-edition GTA VI DualSense controllers

PlayStation says on X that the Grand Theft Auto VI limited-edition DualSense controllers are available today, launch day on PS5.

सूत्रों का कहना है x.com

PlayStation announced it on its official X account: the Grand Theft Auto VI Limited-Edition DualSense wireless controllers are available today, launch day for the game on PS5. The post, seen more than 100,000 times in under an hour, links to a PlayStation store page.

What does the GTA VI DualSense look like?

Going by the product visuals, the controller is white, with a VI logo engraved on the touchpad and a blue-to-purple gradient around the sticks. The box carries the game's palm trees and pink-orange gradient, with a "Limited Edition" label and the Grand Theft Auto logo. It works with PS5, PC, Mac and mobile.

Price and stock: what we don't know yet

PlayStation's post gives neither a price nor a quantity, and we have not checked them on the store. Rely on the official page before buying.

It joins the other launch merchandise, such as the game-themed PS5 Pro given to Miami Heat fans. More items are on the goodies page, and game pricing is on the price page.

For PS5 players it is a collectible accessory available from day one. How many units exist is the open question: "limited edition" does not say.
