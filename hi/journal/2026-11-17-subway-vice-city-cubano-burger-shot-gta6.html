GTA 6 Subway Vice City Cubano in Miami, Skinny Louie turns into Burger Shot

Subway renames a sandwich the Vice City Cubano in Miami for GTA 6, while Popeyes launches a Spice City sandwich on November 19.

सूत्रों का कहना है ign.com

Subway is renaming its Cubano Pulled Pork Melt the "Vice City Cubano" for GTA 6's launch, but only in Miami, announces IGN. Other chains follow: Popeyes launches a "Spice City" sandwich, and burger chain Skinny Louie becomes Burger Shot.

What is in Subway's Vice City Cubano?

According to IGN, the recipe stays that of the existing Cubano: hardwood-smoked pulled pork, black forest ham, melted provolone, pickles and mustard on Italian bread. It comes as a six-inch or footlong, in store or delivered. It is available only in the city of Miami.

Which other brands are joining in?

Popeyes offers a "Spice City" chicken sandwich for one week from November 19 at participating locations. IGN also cites Miami restaurants and stores officially teamed up with Rockstar. The most visible is Skinny Louie, a Florida burger chain turned into Burger Shot, with menu, uniforms, packaging and signs refitted until the end of the year. We already covered Miami going Vice City in our Travis Scott and Miami Heat collection article.

Why this wave of partnerships?

IGN describes Rockstar's marketing as reaching "unprecedented levels", prompting brands with no deal to ride the hype. The distinction matters: Skinny Louie is an official partnership, while the Vice City Cubano is a menu reskin, and the source does not state a Rockstar agreement.

No effect on the game. For players outside Miami, these offers are out of reach. Official merchandise is on our merch page.
