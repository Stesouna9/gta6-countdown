GTA 6 reaches 3.31 million Twitch viewers, GeeksLands reports

GeeksLands cites provisional Streams Charts figures for a gaming peak, while Twitch’s overall event record remains separate.

फ़ोटो: Rockstar Games

सूत्रों का कहना है geekslands.fr

GTA 6 reached a peak of 3.31 million simultaneous Twitch viewers around its release, according to GeeksLands, which attributes the figures to Streams Charts. The outlet calls this a record for a game on the platform. Its numbers remain provisional, and we have not independently checked the underlying chart. This is a report of that published assessment rather than an audience audit by our newsroom.

What kind of record is being claimed?

The comparison in GeeksLands puts the game above the 3.11 million viewers recorded for the 2022 League of Legends Worlds final between T1 and DRX. It also lists Fortnite at 3.04 million in December 2023. These are simultaneous audience peaks. They do not describe every viewer who opened a stream over the course of a whole day.

Nor does the claim mean that GTA 6 has overtaken every event ever shown on Twitch. According to the report, La Velada del Año retains the overall record with 9.68 million simultaneous viewers in 2025. Keeping a gaming category separate from a creator boxing event is essential to understanding the headline without inflating what it says.

Different measures tell different stories

GeeksLands also reports a combined peak of 7.9 million across platforms, including 3.2 million on YouTube and 1.5 million on Kick. Those rounded platform figures should not be treated as a verified count of unique people. The collected account does not set out its complete measurement method or explain how viewers moving between services are deduplicated. We cannot establish a unique audience from those totals.

The outlet gives another figure of 36 million hours watched on Twitch in a single day. That is accumulated viewing time rather than another concurrent audience peak. It also reports 1,798 simultaneous channels on Kick. These measures answer separate questions: how many viewers were present at once, how much time they spent watching, and how many creators were broadcasting.

Why it matters

The figures describe the release as a shared viewing event, but they do not establish sales or the number of active players. A final assessment will depend on stable totals and a clear account of their scope. Our GTA 6 journal keeps commercial announcements separate from reported audience measurements. For now, the specific claim is a provisional gaming record on Twitch, with an explicit attribution and an overall event record still held elsewhere.
