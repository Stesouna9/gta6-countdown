GTA 6: shared activities can bring Jason and Lucia closer, Edge reports

Dexerto’s account of Edge describes a four-level relationship meter and activities for the two protagonists. Their everyday freedom would still sit within a criminal story.

Time spent together could matter beyond GTA 6's main missions. Dexerto.fr, reporting on Edge's magazine feature, describes a four-level closeness meter for Jason and Lucia and activities that can strengthen their relationship. These details suggest that the space between major crimes is also intended to develop the protagonists, although the available account does not explain every rule behind the system.

Spending time together

Dexerto lists beach walks, cycling, exercise and drives among the shared activities. They are presented as ways for the pair to grow closer. The report does not specify progression thresholds, the precise value of each activity or any rewards attached to individual levels. There is therefore no basis for turning this information into an optimisation guide ahead of release.

A meter tells us that a relationship can change, rather than explaining every possible outcome. The source does not establish which scenes depend on it or whether different activities have different effects. Those are questions for more detailed information or the finished game, and should remain separate from the features actually described in this account.

Everyday routines have limits

According to Dexerto.fr, the 90-minute PS5 demonstration also included buying cigarettes, drinking coffee for a temporary stamina benefit and consuming a protein shake for a health bonus. Ordinary actions would consequently have a practical role as well as contributing to the atmosphere. The report describes their broad effects without supplying detailed durations or numerical values.

यह भी पढ़ें GTA 6 कहानी कथित तौर पर अपराध छोड़ने की कोशिश कर रहे Jason और Lucia पर केंद्रित है

There is also an important boundary. Dexerto says Jason and Lucia remain criminals and cannot simply abandon that path to take an ordinary job. Smaller robberies, targeted vehicle thefts and shop hold-ups would fill some of the gaps between larger story missions. Comparisons with GTA roleplay servers help describe the flavour of everyday routines, but do not establish that the same range of freedom is available here.

Why it matters

The relationship system offers another way to understand time spent in Leonida. Shared outings would not merely occupy the player before the next mission; they would also contribute to the bond between the protagonists. How flexible or significant that becomes will require a closer look at the actual game rather than assumptions based on the existence of a meter.

Our GTA 6 release guide covers practical launch details separately. Edge's feature, as reported by Dexerto, adds context about the single-player experience. It does not announce an online mode, nor does it promise a life simulator in which every civilian occupation becomes a playable career.
