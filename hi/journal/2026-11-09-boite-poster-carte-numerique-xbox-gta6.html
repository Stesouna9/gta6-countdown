GTA 6 physical edition: A3 poster, no paper map in the box

GTA 6's boxed edition holds a code, a double-sided A3 poster at some retailers and no paper map, which will be a free download, according to Pure Xbox.

What is inside the GTA 6 box on Xbox? A download code, a double-sided A3 poster at some retailers, and no paper map: according to Pure Xbox, the map will be a free download at launch. Big-N reports the same details for the PS5 box.

No disc, but a poster?

The physical edition on Xbox Series X|S and PS5 has no disc: the box holds a code redeemed on the Microsoft Store. Pure Xbox says the boxed copy now comes with a double-sided A3 poster, "at least" at certain retailers, with the main artwork on one side and character art on the other. Big-N notes it is unclear whether the poster is exclusive or standard.

What happened to the paper map?

This is the new part. Rockstar drops the folding map of earlier GTA boxes. Per Pure Xbox, a digital map will be free to download at launch for every buyer. A paper copy will be sold on the Rockstar Store "shortly after launch" for $0.99, according to both outlets; Pure Xbox expects it to sell out fast.

When will the box arrive?

Rockstar gave November 12 as the shipping date, yet several retailers say they ship on the 11th, and a few players reportedly already have a copy. You can also preload from tonight through the Xbox mobile app. See our earlier stories on the box photographed in Germany and pricing.

Why it matters

The box becomes mostly a display or gift item, as Pure Xbox puts it, and the map moves from paper to file. Still unknown: whether every store includes the poster, and when the paper map goes on sale.
