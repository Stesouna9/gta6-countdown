GTA 6 60 FPS on PS5 and Xbox: what we know about 30 FPS

No official 60 FPS mode: per Digital Foundry via SVG, all gameplay shown runs at 30 FPS on console.

Rockstar has not announced a 60 FPS mode for GTA 6 on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. According to SVG, which relays Digital Foundry's analysis, everything shown of the game runs at 30 FPS, and nothing official has changed. Twelve days before the November 19 launch, here is where things stand, and what is still unknown.

Will GTA 6 run at 60 FPS on console?

Not confirmed so far, with a clear lean towards 30 FPS. Rockstar has published no performance spec. SVG notes that Digital Foundry analysed the Extended Look showcase aired on Netflix: all gameplay holds 30 FPS, including the raw file Rockstar uploaded. Digital Foundry says the focus was maximum visual detail at a fixed 30 FPS on every platform, with frame drops described as extremely rare.

It goes further on the future: a 60 FPS mode would be "extremely unlikely" on console, because both CPU and GPU are already saturated to hold 33.3 ms per frame. Reaching 16.7 ms would mean compromising the world, it argues. That is an analysis, not a Rockstar statement.

What did creators at Rockstar North hear?

Per SVG, content creators invited to a preview event in August said co-director Rob Nelson implied 60 FPS would not be there at launch. That is second-hand, never detailed publicly by Rockstar, and months old. La Crème du Gaming adds that the studio reportedly aims first for a stable 30 FPS on all consoles, with any 60 FPS only at the cost of compromises. Again, press reporting, not studio communication.

यह भी पढ़ें GTA 6 PS5 Pro: PSSR और रे ट्रेसिंग कथित तौर पर PlayStation फ़्रांस द्वारा सूचीबद्ध है

Can the PS5 Pro change that?

The PlayStation Store listing shows a "PS5 Pro Enhanced" badge, which proves optimisation, not a 60 FPS mode. We already covered the withdrawn PlayStation France page: it listed PSSR and advanced ray tracing, but contained a line copied from another game and is not reliable. Clubic points out it mentioned neither 60 nor 40 FPS, and that the PS5 Pro mostly gains on the graphics side (up to 45% faster rendering, per Sony) while its CPU improves by roughly 10% in clock speed.

Digital Foundry does not rule out a PS5 Pro-only scenario, and SVG notes Rockstar has not excluded a performance mode after launch. Neither is a promise.

Why it matters

For pre-orders nothing changes: same game, same price, and the release date stays November 19. On day one, expect a smooth game capped at 30 FPS, and be happy if it turns out better. Still unknown: whether a 60 FPS mode ever arrives, and what the PS5 Pro really adds. Reviews, expected before launch, will tell.
