GTA 6 Burger Shot restaurant opens at Miami's Kaseya Center, home of the Heat

IGN reports that the Kaseya Center in Miami has rebranded a restaurant as Burger Shot, the GTA fast-food chain, ahead of the Nov. 18 Vice City Heat game.

The fattest burgers in Los Santos and Vice City now have a real address. According to IGN, Miami's Kaseya Center, home of the Heat, has rebranded one of its restaurants "Burger Shot with Macca & Friends", with Vice City posters hung inside the venue.

What do we actually know about this Burger Shot?

The story started with a fan on X who shared the first photos with GTA accounts, which IGN picked up. Neither Rockstar nor the arena has commented as far as we know. It is unclear whether the menu will copy the game's dishes: the seven-patty "Heartstopper" IGN mentions seems unlikely in real life, and IGN itself thinks it is mostly decoration.

Still, Burger Shot has always been a parody of the big American chains, and seeing it on a stadium menu is a fitting joke.

An arena turned Vice City showcase

The Kaseya Center has already gone all in. IGN recalls the $975,000 "Welcome to Vice City" neon sign on top of the arena and the Nov. 18 regular-season game where the Heat become the "Vice City Heat", with new jerseys and a GTA 6 court, the night before launch. We already covered the fake in-game brand ads around the city; the logic is the same.

IGN adds that leaks point to some in-game beers being sold in Miami bars, with no word on wider availability. See our earlier piece on the beers.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game, out Nov. 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. But the campaign is clearly leaving the screen, with the story's city used as an advertising playground. Whether the menu will really be themed, and whether fans outside Florida can try it, remains unknown. All the dates are on our release page.
