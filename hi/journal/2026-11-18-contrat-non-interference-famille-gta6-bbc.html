GTA 6: Scottish dad makes his family sign a "non-interference" contract

Painter Sean Little, from Newton Stewart, booked a week off to play GTA 6 and had his wife and daughter sign a peace contract, the BBC reports.

A week off to play GTA 6 was not enough for Sean Little. The painter and decorator from Newton Stewart, Dumfries and Galloway, had his wife and 13-year-old daughter sign a "non-interference contract", the BBC reports. He made it as a joke for his TikTok.

What is in the contract?

Sean, 38, pays for peace: £50 to his wife Natalie, £20 to daughter Charlotte, and his weekly contribution to household finances raised to £500. In return: no chores, no comments on how long he has been on the couch, no spoilers. "We're not allowed to make any comments about him being lazy", Natalie told the BBC. He even bought paper plates to avoid washing up.

Why so much effort for GTA 6?

He played the first GTA as a teenager and has waited since GTA 5, thirteen years ago. He compares himself to a Star Wars fan: they do not stop being fans at 60. He worked 13-hour weekend days to save up. And Benidorm? "Vice is probably better. Sorry Benidorm."

He is not alone: Italy leads in time off booked for launch, with the official date still 19 November.
