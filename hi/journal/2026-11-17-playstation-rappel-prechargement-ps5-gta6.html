GTA 6: PlayStation reminds players PS5 pre-loading is open

PlayStation reminds players on X that GTA 6 can be pre-loaded on PS5 before the November 19 launch. The unlock time is still unknown.

PlayStation is reminding players that they can pre-load GTA 6 on PS5 before the November 19 launch. The message comes from the official @PlayStation account on X, posted on November 17: "Reminder: You can pre-load Grand Theft Auto VI on PS5 now before it launches November 19", with a link to the game's PlayStation Store page. The post already has more than 126,000 views.

Can you still pre-load GTA 6 on PS5?

Yes, according to PlayStation. Pre-loading has been open since November 12 for digital versions, and the November 17 reminder confirms it is still possible two days before launch. The game downloads ahead of time but cannot be played before the unlock time.

When does the game unlock?

Neither PlayStation nor Rockstar gave the exact time in this message, so the unlock time remains unknown for now. We will publish it as soon as it is officially announced. For the date and platforms, see our release page.

Can pre-loading handle the load?

Internet providers have already reported traffic peaks, as we noted with Orange's record and then Virgin Media's. Pre-loading early avoids waiting on a download on November 19 while everyone launches the game at once.

Why it matters

Nothing new in substance: this is a reminder, not an announcement. But coming from the official PlayStation account, it nudges latecomers: anyone who bought the game, including as a boxed code, should start the download now. Prices and editions are on the price page.
