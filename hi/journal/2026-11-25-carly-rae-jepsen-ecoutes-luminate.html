GTA 6: Carly Rae Jepsen song streams rise 288%, Luminate figures suggest

Run Away With Me reached 196,059 worldwide on-demand streams on November 21, Le Parisien reports, citing Rolling Stone and Luminate.

फ़ोटो: Rockstar Games

सूत्रों का कहना है leparisien.fr

Run Away With Me is finding fresh listeners around GTA 6’s release, according to Le Parisien. The newspaper cites Rolling Stone and Luminate figures showing 196,059 worldwide on-demand streams on Saturday, November 21, compared with 50,425 the previous Saturday. That is the reported 288% increase. The measurement concerns Carly Rae Jepsen’s song, rather than the game’s entire soundtrack or the singer’s full catalogue.

What does the increase actually measure?

The useful comparison is between two Saturdays a week apart. It does not establish that every service or territory experienced the same percentage change. Le Parisien also reports 170,094 worldwide streams on launch day, again drawing on the American magazine’s reporting. These are listening totals; the supplied figures do not identify which individual listeners arrived through the game.

Originally released in 2015 on Emotion, the track is an older recording receiving new attention. That matters when discussing GTA’s musical impact. A song appearing in a newly released game is not necessarily a newly commissioned song, and rediscovery can be just as visible as a debut when a large audience encounters a familiar catalogue in a different setting.

Where does GTA 6 fit into the story?

Le Parisien describes multiple appearances connected with Lucia’s journey, alongside availability on Flash FM. Without unpacking those story scenes, the combination explains why players may remember the song beyond a passing radio encounter. A track associated with a particular moment can become something a player actively seeks out after putting the controller down.

The newspaper compares the renewed attention with that surrounding Sylvie Vartan. Those are different recordings from different periods, but both illustrate the value of placing established music in a setting that introduces it to fresh ears. Our music page provides a broader route through GTA VI’s soundtrack, rather than treating one weekend’s result as a verdict on every artist featured in the game.

Why it matters

The reported figures put a measurable scale on a revival eleven years after Run Away With Me first appeared. They support a story about renewed interest around launch, while leaving the precise contribution of the game unmeasured. They also cannot tell us how long the rise will last. Further like-for-like listening reports would be needed to establish whether this is a short launch effect or a more lasting change in the song’s audience.
