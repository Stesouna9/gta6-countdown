GTA 6 in a browser: shared PS5 experiment is reportedly paused

La Crème du Gaming reports that Germán Fernández shared his PS5 through a browser queue, but the experiment is paused and is not a PC release.

फ़ोटो: Rockstar Games

सूत्रों का कहना है lacremedugaming.fr

A browser window displaying GTA 6 is not evidence of a Windows release. According to La Crème du Gaming, Spanish engineer Germán Fernández made his own PS5 accessible through a web page, letting visitors queue for a turn at the controls. There is an immediate qualification: the outlet says the community experiment is temporarily paused. Readers should therefore treat this as a report about a shared console, rather than a currently available way to start playing.

What actually runs the game?

The arrangement described by La Crème du Gaming leaves the work with the PS5. That console produces the picture; the browser receives the video and sends player inputs back. The computer is a viewing and control device. It does not execute a native Windows edition, and this setup does not demonstrate that an unofficial PC conversion has become playable.

Fernández, identified by the outlet as germanfndezz, reportedly devised the project to share a console that otherwise sits unused for part of the day. Visitors enter a queue, then take control when their turn arrives. The article describes a purchased copy on the owner's account. The appealing idea is a remotely shared controller, with access divided between strangers rather than a separate game session for every visitor.

Can this replace owning a console?

The limitations reported by La Crème du Gaming suggest a much narrower use. Players face a wait, network delay and no save tied to their own account. Availability depends on the host console. Seeing the action on a computer screen does not remove any of those dependencies. Above all, a service described as paused cannot reasonably be advertised as an immediate option for every reader.

Separate reports explain the incomplete early PC rendering work and PS Portal cloud streaming; neither should be mistaken for this privately hosted experiment.

Why it matters

The story shows how a console can be shared through another screen. It does not establish that GTA 6 has arrived on Windows: no official PC edition has been announced in the material reviewed. The useful questions are where the game actually runs, who controls the session and whether access is currently offered. In this case, La Crème du Gaming describes a private PS5, a queue and a temporary suspension. Those qualifications belong alongside the browser claim, because they determine what a prospective player can realistically expect.
