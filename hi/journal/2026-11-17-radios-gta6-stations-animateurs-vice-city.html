Six radio stations, hosts ranging from Bad Bunny to Slayer: Mashable pulled together on Tuesday what players will hear in Leonida, two days before the November 19 release. Rockstar presented the stations on October 8.

What are the GTA 6 radio stations and who hosts them?

According to Mashable, Bad Bunny runs Cocoteo FM with singer RaiNao, a station of modern Latin music including reggaeton and Caribbean sounds. Flash FM, a name Vice City fans know well, returns with Sweden's Robyn and model Alex Consani, for pop and dance-floor tracks.

Country gets Back Country Radio, hosted by Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey. Her husband Jeremy Dufrene appears as "roving reporter, swamp meteorologist, and airboat captain", the outlet quotes. Burna Boy teams with DJ Palms Trax for amapiano and African classics. Slayer's Kerry King and Tom Araya host The Chamber, a heavy metal station, and Miami rappers Trick Daddy and Trina run Dirty South Classics, on the history of Southern hip-hop.

What about the album and the score?

The same piece recalls that Rockstar releases Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album on November 19, 34 tracks featuring Travis Scott, Cardi B, PinkPantheress, Morgan Wallen and Rauw Alejandro. Three songs are named: "Sexy Magic", Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard, and "Overtown Lights" by Stanley Clarke, Yussef Dayes and Robert Glasper. Per Mashable, Morgan Wallen's track is the most streamed on Spotify, with over 25 million plays.

French duo Justice composed the original score, announced October 15 with the launch trailer. Rockstar also confirmed a score album, with no date or tracklist yet. We covered the album in our 34-track article.

What is still unknown

Mashable does not list the songs played on each station: Rockstar has not detailed those playlists, nor the total number of in-game tracks. Everything will be revealed at launch, and the site's radio page will track what gets confirmed.

Why it matters

Radio is half the atmosphere of the series. With hosts this famous, Rockstar is betting on a musical Vice City built as a living backdrop, from pop to metal. Players can pick a side before they even start: Latin, country, afrobeats or Southern rap.
