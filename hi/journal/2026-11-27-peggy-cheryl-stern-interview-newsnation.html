GTA 6: Peggy voice actor Cheryl Stern reflects on unexpected attention

Cheryl Stern tells NewsNation that Peggy’s popularity caught her by surprise, bringing messages from players and a proud response from her family.

फ़ोटो: Rockstar Games

सूत्रों का कहना है newsnationnow.com

Peggy may be a supporting presence in GTA 6, but her voice actor has found herself receiving attention from far beyond the recording booth. Speaking to NewsNation, Cheryl Stern describes her surprise at the response to the elderly character who uses a mobility scooter. The outlet points to circulating clips and players’ affection for Peggy, offering a cast member’s perspective on what happens once a performance reaches an audience.

What did Stern know during recording?

According to NewsNation, Stern says confidentiality agreements meant she did not know which game she was recording for at the time. That is an account of her own experience, not a complete description of Rockstar’s casting process. It does not tell us what every other performer knew, or whether different actors received the same information during their sessions.

She recalls doing her acting work and then encountering the character’s much wider reception years later. The separation matters: a performer can finish a professional assignment long before seeing how people will relate to it. In Peggy’s case, Stern describes the resulting attention as both welcome and unexpected, rather than something she had anticipated while working on the voice.

How has the response reached her?

Stern tells NewsNation about letters, texts and emails, including contact from Tajikistan. These examples illustrate the reach she has personally experienced. They are not audience statistics, and the interview does not provide a comprehensive count of the clips or messages. It would therefore go beyond the evidence to declare Peggy the game’s most popular supporting character on the strength of this conversation.

The actor also talks about two nephews who play the game and enjoy recognising their aunt’s involvement. That family response brings the story back to the person behind the performance. Our earlier look at the voice cast explored how actors shape the world’s identity; Manni Perez’s message to fans offered another post-release perspective. Stern’s account adds the experience of a performer behind a smaller role.

Why it matters

There is no expansion announcement or promise of more Peggy material in this interview. Its value is hearing a credited performer explain the reception in her own terms, without treating that reaction as a clue about future content. Stories like this belong alongside the discussions in our community section: attachment to Leonida can form around its supporting residents as readily as its central couple. For Stern, that public relationship arrived after the recording work itself had ended.
