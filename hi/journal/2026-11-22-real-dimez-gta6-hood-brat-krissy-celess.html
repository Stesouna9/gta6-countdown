GTA 6: who are Real Dimez, played by Hood Brat and Krissy Celess?

Real Dimez, GTA 6's fictional rap duo of Bae-Luxe and Roxy, is performed by Hood Brat and Krissy Celess, per Rockstar's credits cited by RouteNote.

सूत्रों का कहना है routenote.com

Real Dimez does not exist outside the game, but its songs are real recordings. According to RouteNote, the Vice City rap duo is made of Bae-Luxe and Roxy, two friends who use social media and music to chase success.

Who performs Real Dimez?

Per Rockstar's official credits cited by the outlet, Hood Brat plays Bae-Luxe and Krissy Celess plays Roxy Rashad. Those names identify the performers, rather than theories about possible inspirations. In the game's world the duo is tied to Only Raw Records, with an early collaboration with local rapper DWNPLY and a relentless push to go viral.

Which songs and which station?

The remix of "FTH" appears on the secret Vault Radio playlist, making it the easiest track to tie to a station. For the rest, RouteNote says credits confirm inclusion, not necessarily placement on every station. Production credits include DJ Slugo, Cardo Got Wings, Yung Exclusive, Johnny Juliano and Bornbad! on "Cooties", ATL Jacob, RushDee and Corey Dollaz on "Vibin' With The Dimez", and Take A Daytrip on "Money Don't Lie". Sexyy Red joins the duo on a remix.
