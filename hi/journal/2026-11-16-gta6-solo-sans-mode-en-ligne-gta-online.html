GTA 6 has no online mode at launch: PlayStation calls it single-player

GTA 6 launches November 19 with no online mode: PlayStation's FAQ calls it a single-player experience, and Rockstar has announced no GTA Online for it.

GTA 6 launches on November 19 with no online mode. According to GeeksLands, the FAQ on PlayStation's official game page answers the multiplayer question with: "Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience." The same page lists "1 player" and never mentions GTA Online.

Will GTA 6 have an online mode at launch?

Not according to what is public today. Rockstar has announced no online mode and given no window. Before GTA 5, the studio said GTA Online would follow two weeks later, in October 2013. Nothing similar this time. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier estimated this summer that Rockstar would not talk about its next GTA Online any time soon, the same source reports.

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said much the same to shareholders in September: "We're launching GTA 6 as a single-player experience on two major platforms, so we don't expect any recurrent consumer spending for this launch." Recurrent spending means in-game purchases such as Shark Cards. See also the report of a GTA Online in 2027.

What happens to the GTA 5 version of GTA Online?

It stays live. GeeksLands adds that Take-Two says in its quarterly filings, per IGN, that Rockstar will keep supporting it with content and events. Zelnick said: "We intend to continue to support GTA Online, which has done very well." The last big update, Kortz Center Heist, came out this summer. No server shutdown is announced.

यह भी पढ़ें GTA 6 ऑनलाइन: Bloomberg डॉक्यूमेंट्री गेम के एक साल बाद रिलीज़ होने का संकेत देती है

The only link between the two games so far is GTA+: a digital purchase of GTA 6 on the PlayStation Store before November 20 gives one month of the subscription, to claim before March 31, 2027. Sony's FAQ does not say whether GTA+ will do anything in GTA 6. No character or money transfer from Los Santos to Leonida is announced. More on GTA 5 leaving PS Plus.

What about trophies?

Two dataminers point to 55 PS5 trophies, with as many Xbox achievements, without publishing titles (our take on the leak). Sites relaying it infer an all-solo list, but Rockstar has confirmed no list. Treat that as inference, not fact.

Why it matters

On November 19, expect a single-player campaign only. Any online mode remains unannounced: its date, content and business model are unknown. Release timing is on the release page.
