GTA 6: Travis Scott’s revealed Nike Air Force 1 is for selected fans

Hypebeast describes the revealed sneaker as a friends and family exclusive that Scott plans to gift to supporters, rather than a public retail drop.

Travis Scott's newly shown “GTA 6” Nike Air Force 1 Low is being described as a gift for selected supporters, rather than a pair anyone can order. According to Hypebeast, Scott revealed the custom sneaker on Instagram and intends to give pairs to longtime fans, gaming fan pages and close friends. The report moves the story beyond the leaked black shoe photographs we covered earlier, while leaving an important question about retail availability unanswered.

Can fans actually buy the revealed pair?

Hypebeast calls the shown version a friends and family exclusive. Its account describes gifts chosen by the artist, including pairs for supporters and gaming pages, rather than a public release. The available report supplies no application process, giveaway rules or retailer listing for this specific version. Being a fan does not, on the evidence provided, establish a way to obtain one.

That distinction matters because our earlier report concerned leaked photographs of a black Air Force 1. Those pictures circulated through specialist accounts. Hypebeast now attributes a presentation to Scott himself and describes how he plans to distribute the sneaker.

What does Hypebeast describe?

The publication reports black patent leather, camouflage ripstop panels and pink accents. Scott's reversed Swoosh is oversized and white. According to the same account, “GTA” lettering replaces the usual “AIR” marking on the side of the midsole, while pink stars decorate the inner side and the outsole. Those details establish the visual reference described by the fashion publication. They provide no evidence about the police systems or wanted levels that players will encounter in GTA 6.

Where does the $130 claim fit?

Hypebeast's English summary also lists additional colourways for November 19 at $130. That statement should be kept separate from its description of the revealed friends and family shoe. The collected text offers no Nike product page that connects a purchasable reference to this exact design. Our earlier coverage of the reported three-colourway release addressed the commercial rumours before these new details emerged.

For readers outside the United States, the report does not establish local pricing, participating stores or regional distribution. A dollar figure attached to other colourways cannot answer those questions for the pair shown by Scott.

Why it matters

The useful update is about access: Hypebeast describes this model as something Scott will give to selected people. Collectors still need a clearly identified retail reference before treating it as a purchase to plan around. The broader release claims remain a separate part of the story, rather than proof that this exclusive will reach shops.
