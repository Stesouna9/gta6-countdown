GTA 6: Cal brings a physical disc players cannot, notes Polygon

Polygon notes a GTA 6 character brings a disc of the game Tenebrae Lux, while GTA 6 is digital-only and its code does not share easily.

सूत्रों का कहना है polygon.com

In GTA 6's opening hours, Cal, a friend of Jason, spends an afternoon gaming with him. Polygon points out the irony: Cal brings a physical disc, something players cannot do with GTA 6 itself.

What happens in the scene?

According to Polygon, Cal introduces Jason to Tenebrae Lux, a very artsy game spoofing Jonathan Blow's The Witness. Jason is unimpressed and would rather kill zombies. The night ends in a drunken fight over the bed, then both sharing it in their underwear. The writer sees a tender sequence in which Jason opens up about strains with Lucia on the eve of her prison release.

Why talk about discs?

GTA 6 is digital-only; boxed versions contain a download code. Polygon recalls Sony announced it will stop making PlayStation discs in January 2028, and that the "no disc, no buy" blackout had no visible effect. It judges GTA 6 likely to be 2026's best seller despite the uproar, which remains an estimate. See how to share the game and our release page.

Why it matters

Nothing in the game itself: it is a wink that reads as an accidental comment on the end of physical media. Rockstar has not commented.
