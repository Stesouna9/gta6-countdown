GTA 6 credits: Rockstar posts the full list, about 6,000 names, with spoilers

Rockstar Games has put the full GTA 6 credits online, about 6,000 names according to IGN France, and they reveal major story spoilers.

सूत्रों का कहना है fr.ign.com

Rockstar Games has published the full credits of GTA 6 online, IGN France reports. The outlet counted the names and puts the list at about 6,000. It also warns that the credits reveal major spoilers about the story.

What do the full GTA 6 credits contain?

According to IGN France, thousands of developers are listed, which is no surprise for a production this size, along with the dozens of actors who lent their faces and voices to the characters. The 6,000 figure is the outlet's own estimate, not a total given by Rockstar. We do not have the detailed list in our sources, so we cannot say which roles are confirmed beyond what has already been reported.

Why does IGN France mention spoilers?

The headline is plain: it is great for the teams, but beware of spoilers. A complete credits roll names characters, sometimes in order of appearance, and can give away plot points you are meant to find by playing. IGN France does not detail those spoilers in the passage we could read, and neither will we. If you are mid-game, stay away from the page until you finish the story.

The credits land at a time when names were already circulating. Several cast confirmations have been shared on social media since launch. For release date and platforms, our release page remains the reference: the game came out on 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. We also covered the first reviews in our article on the 99 Metacritic score.

Why it matters

For players, nothing changes right now: credits are a reference, not game content. For the teams, it is public recognition of years of work, and the first chance to check who actually took part. For the curious, it is also a source of official confirmation on the cast, which should be mined over the next few hours. We will check names before repeating them and say what remains unconfirmed.
