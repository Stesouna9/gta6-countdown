GTA 6 on PS5 for 60 euros at E.Leclerc: a code in a box, no disc

According to Le Figaro, E.Leclerc sells GTA 6 on PS5 for 60 euros as a code-in-a-box, with no disc and two-hour pickup in 94 stores.

French retailer E.Leclerc has GTA 6 back in stock on PS5 at 60 euros, reports Le Figaro in its deals section. That is below the 79.99 euro standard edition on the PlayStation Store.

What is in the 60 euro E.Leclerc box?

It is a "Code in a Box" version: despite the name, there is no disc, only a digital activation code, Le Figaro specifies. Pre-orders also get the Vintage Vice City pack, presented as a bonus. The code lets buyers preload to be ready on 19 November.

Where and how can you pick it up?

Click & Collect is free, with pickup possible within two hours in 94 stores carrying the offer. One catch: the code only works with a PlayStation account registered in France or a short list of European countries.

Should you be wary of boxed codes?

The Figaro piece comes from its Shopping team, independent of the newsroom, which may earn an affiliate commission, so the price is indicative. We have already covered boxes reported without a code and fake pre-order sites: buy in store or from a known retailer, and check the code before redeeming it. Official prices are on the price page.

Why it matters

For buyers who have not purchased yet, 60 euros is nearly 20 below the public digital price, if stock can be found. Le Figaro does not say how long the offer runs, and nothing indicates a similar deal on Xbox.
