GTA 6: Lucia writer quits X after a wave of online harassment

Alexa D'Agostino, who discussed writing Lucia, deleted her X account after targeted abuse, per Yahoo Tech and Video Games Chronicle. Neither she nor Rockstar has commented.

सूत्रों का कहना है tech.yahoo.com

Alexa D'Agostino, a writer who talked about creating Lucia for GTA 6, has deleted her X account after a wave of online harassment. According to Yahoo Tech, citing Video Games Chronicle, people unhappy about Lucia being a playable lead targeted her.

What are the accusations against Alexa D'Agostino?

The posts blamed her for perceived faults in the game, mostly Lucia being playable. An X thread also pointed to her support for Pride and her posts critical of Donald Trump. Others claimed she once ran a blog telling women to manipulate men. Per Video Games Chronicle, the post cited was actually advice on meeting a boyfriend's friends.

She also appears in the thank-you credits of GTA 5, GTA Online and Red Dead Online. Rockstar has not presented her as the game's lead writer: what the report establishes is that she discussed writing Lucia in an interview.

Which quote was used against her?

The anger centred on a LOVE Magazine interview given before launch. She said: "When it came to writing Lucia, I thought of the women I know." She described her mother, an immigrant who grew up in Newark, New Jersey, close to Lucia's hometown of Liberty City. She added that a fully realised person ends up "perfectly imperfect, with clichés, but also undeniably real".

Her comparison with Arthur Morgan and Niko Bellic was turned against her. Video Games Chronicle says many of the posts about her are misogynistic and racist.

Did the harassment stop once she left X?

No. The outlet reports that much of the vitriol then went to another woman with the same name. Neither Alexa D'Agostino nor Rockstar has commented so far, according to Video Games Chronicle. So there is no studio statement, and no confirmation of the full scale of the abuse beyond that reporting.

The story follows our pieces on the voice cast of Lucia and Jason and on fans' take on the romantic turn. The character divides opinion, but this concerns real people, not the game.

Why it matters

Nothing changes in the game itself. The case is a reminder that discussion around a hugely anticipated title can slide into personal attacks on people who simply talked about their work. No official Rockstar reaction is known, and we do not invent one. We will follow any statement in the journal.
