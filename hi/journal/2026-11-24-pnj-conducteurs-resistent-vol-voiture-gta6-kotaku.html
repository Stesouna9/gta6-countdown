GTA 6: some NPC drivers fight back over car theft, Kotaku reports

In GTA 6, some drivers do not flee when you steal their car and fight back, in a Kotaku piece calling them among the funniest in the game.

सूत्रों का कहना है kotaku.com

Stealing an occupied car in GTA 6 does not always end with the driver running. Kotaku devoted a piece to those Vice City residents who refuse to hand over their vehicle and hit back. The outlet says it laughs more than it should.

What happens when you carjack a driver?

According to Kotaku, the driver runs away "more often than not". But some, clearly having a bad enough day, fight back. The writer cites players who describe full meltdowns behind the wheel, and even an NPC who appears to sniff something before counter-attacking, in a video shared by a player. The piece is anecdotal: no figure on how often these reactions occur and no explanation from Rockstar about the system that triggers them.

Kotaku keeps a columnist tone. It stresses the idea of a living world where passers-by have their own troubles, chatting with friends or making loud phone calls. It adds that even facing resistance you can still take the car, call the cops or outrun them.

Is it one more detail in how residents behave?

Mostly it is an example of the variety of reactions players have documented since November 19. On the chase side, we already covered the wanted system and witnesses, and cars locked against police.

Why it matters

Nothing mechanical that Rockstar has confirmed: these are player observations relayed by Kotaku. Still, these scenes say something about the pace many wanted. A car theft is no longer a consequence-free gesture, and Vice City does not feel like a set of extras. Whether these reactions vary by neighborhood or driver profile is unknown: no source says.
