GTA 6: Rockstar reshares Only In Dade's Miami video on its Instagram story

Rockstar reshared an Only In Dade clip on its Instagram story that several accounts present as GTA 6 gameplay filmed like Miami phone footage. Nothing is officially announced.

A short video has been circulating since 8 pm on Wednesday that looks like real footage shot in Miami. The Instagram account Only In Dade posted it with the hashtag "OnlyinViceCity" and, according to GTA6 Hub FR, Rockstar then reshared it on its Instagram story. Several accounts see it as in-game GTA 6 footage captured through an in-game phone camera.

What do we know about the clip?

The first relay came from GTAVI_Countdown at 8:23 pm: "potential new GTA 6 gameplay captured from an in-game phone camera", posted by OnlyinDade. About half an hour later the same account wrote that it was "now confirmed to be real after being shared and liked by Rockstar", a post seen nearly 195,000 times according to GTAVI_Countdown. gtasix_ claims Rockstar recreated real Miami footage in GTA 6, and RockstarNewsFRA calls it a new look at the game, "via Rockstar on Instagram".

Is it official?

Not as an announcement. Rockstar has published no statement or Newswire post, and none of the outlets in our press review has covered it yet. What the posts show is a story reshare, which can amount to tacit approval without the studio confirming what the clip contains. We could not check the story ourselves, as it disappears after 24 hours, so whether it is real capture or a render remains unconfirmed.

Why now, on the eve of preloading?

Preloading opens tomorrow, 12 November, and the game launches on 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has tended to let community content circulate rather than release big trailers at this stage. For dates and editions, see our release page.

Why it matters

Nothing concrete changes about the game: no new information on content or date. But an official reshare lends weight to footage that looks like gameplay a week before launch. We will update this article if Rockstar or an outlet confirms it.
