GTA 7 outside the US? Rockstar's Rob Nelson says "never say never"

Asked by The Guardian, Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson did not rule out a GTA outside the United States, but said there is "still so much to do in America".

Will Rockstar ever set a GTA outside the United States? Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson answered in a Guardian interview summed up by Notebookcheck: "Never say never, but there's still so much to do in America." Nothing is announced.

What did Rob Nelson say about a GTA 7 elsewhere?

Per Notebookcheck, the question was about an international setting for a future game. Nelson committed to nothing either way and added: "I think we're all outsiders to this version of America", noting that Rockstar North is based in Scotland.

How did Rockstar research Leonida?

Also per Notebookcheck, research included staying in a Miami nightclub until 4 am, visiting a racetrack hours later, riding along with police and scouting bars in the Florida Keys. See our earlier piece on the Guardian feature.

What about the South America idea?

The outlet recalls a 2018 leak saying the game first included parts of South America, the "Project Americas" report, which Rockstar later verified and said was scaled back because of stretched resources. For Take-Two's side of sequels, see our Zelnick GTA 7 article. Launch details are on the release page.

Why it matters

Nothing in the short term: Leonida is the only confirmed setting and no GTA 7 is announced. Nelson's answer only says Rockstar is not closing the door, without promising anything.
