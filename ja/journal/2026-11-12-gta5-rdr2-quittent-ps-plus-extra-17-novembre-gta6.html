GTA 6: GTA 5 and RDR2 leave PS Plus Extra on November 17

Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2 leave the PlayStation Plus Extra catalog on November 17, two days before GTA 6 launches.

情報源 fr.martincid.com

Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2 leave the PlayStation Plus Extra catalog on November 17, two days before GTA 6, according to Martin Cid Magazine, which relies on Sony's PlayStation Blog announcement. The outlet notes the timing, which Sony does not comment on.

Which games arrive and which leave?

The same day, Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii, Atomfall and Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake join the catalog, along with Trepang2, MotoGP 26, Hell Let Loose and Cricket 26. On the departures side, Push Square counts eleven games removed on November 17, including Football Manager 26 and The Talos Principle 2, while Notebookcheck lists twelve on the US PlayStation Store: the list varies by region.

What does a subscriber replaying GTA 5 lose?

Access, plainly. Once a title is removed, the only way to keep playing is to buy it. A subscriber who relaunched GTA V before the release will lose access 48 hours before its sequel arrives. Sony says GTA 6 is the most preordered game in PlayStation Store history, without giving a figure.

Another detail from the announcement: subscribers in the US, UK and Japan must wait a week longer, until November 24, for Pirate Yakuza, and the first two for other titles. Push Square sees this as another Sony experiment, which the company does not explain.

Why it matters

For GTA 6, nothing changes: the game unlocks at midnight in each country, as we explain in our release time article, and preloading is open, see 212 GB on PS5 and Xbox. For those hoping to finish GTA V or Red Dead first, there is until November 16. See also the release page.
