GTA 6: Leonida's map only truly opens after 30% of the story, SVG says

According to SVG, the first third of GTA 6's story takes place in the Leonida Keys, so much of the rest of the map has little to offer early on.

情報源 svg.com

Leonida is the largest map in Rockstar's history, but it does not unfold at once. According to SVG, which warns its text contains slight spoilers, the first hours play out in the Leonida Keys in the south, and that setting limits the point of going further early on.

Why stay in the Keys early in GTA 6?

After the tutorial, which introduces Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos separately, you play Jason until Lucia gets out of prison. Missions come from a man named Brian Heder, and you also have to keep the home in order. SVG writes that the first 30% of the story takes place in the Keys, and that Lucia does not leave the islands until around 35% completion.

You can leave whenever you like: the bridge north reaches Vice City after roughly 5 to 10 minutes of driving, then Mount Kalaga and Kelly County. But, per SVG, side activities outside the Keys are often locked behind content you have not unlocked. Example given: Vice City stores are far easier to rob with gear, like a mask, that Jason does not have yet.

Does money change anything?

The outlet advises against chasing money early: most of it is lost later because of the story. Robbing Vice City shops before leaving the Keys is therefore not worth it. Exploring the whole map before the story gets you there wastes progression time, SVG says, and it recommends following the Keys thread to reach Vice City sooner.

Past 30%, Jason and Lucia live in the heart of Vice City and travel across the map before the credits. This is the outlet's view of its own playthrough, not a rule announced by Rockstar.

Why it matters

For anyone starting the game today, better to accept the pace: the Keys are the ground of the first part, and the full map arrives with the story. No need to force the way north.
