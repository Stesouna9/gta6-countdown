GTA 6 France boxed launch sales reach 1.046 million, report says

KultureGeek cites Oscar Lemaire for 1.046 million French boxed launch sales, with PS5 accounting for 93%. The figures describe a specific retail channel.

写真：Rockstar Games

情報源 kulturegeek.fr

GTA 6 has reportedly passed one million boxed sales at its French launch. KultureGeek cites Oscar Lemaire for a total of 1,046,000 units, describing it as the country’s second-largest physical game launch behind GTA V. That is a substantial retail result, but its scope matters: it is a boxed-sales figure, rather than a complete count of every French console purchase.

What does the French launch figure include?

KultureGeek’s report concerns physical retail sales. It does not supply a combined French total covering both boxed purchases and direct sales through console storefronts. Nor should the unit figure be treated as a count of individual players. Copies purchased, accounts accessing a game and people actually playing it are different measures.

The report also points out an unusual feature of these boxes: they contain a download code rather than a game disc. In this case, physical describes the packaging and retail channel. It does not mean that buyers receive the game’s data on optical media. Our pricing and editions page provides context for the different purchase options.

How does the comparison with GTA V work?

According to KultureGeek, GTA V’s French physical launch remains ahead by 120,000 units. The outlet also stresses that the earlier game arrived in 2013, when direct digital purchases played a much smaller role in the market. Comparing boxes alone therefore measures a particular retail activity across two different purchasing environments.

That distinction prevents a misleading conclusion about the entire launch. A smaller boxed total cannot, on its own, demonstrate a smaller overall audience. The collected report does not provide all the digital data needed to make that broader comparison. Its ranking needs to remain attached to the physical-sales category.

The platform split is similarly specific. KultureGeek reports that PS5 accounted for 93% of those French boxed purchases, with Xbox representing the remaining 7%. Those percentages cannot automatically be extended to all French owners or players. A different mix of digital purchasing on either platform would change the broader picture, and that information is not included here.

Why it matters

The report illustrates how a box can retain a commercial role even when the actual game must be downloaded. It also explains why launch statistics need labels: worldwide revenue, unique players and French retail units can all describe successful activity without measuring the same thing.

These numbers remain attributed to Oscar Lemaire through KultureGeek’s account. The collected text does not set out the full methodology or define the exact interval covered by launch. Until that detail is available, the defensible takeaway is narrower: this reported French boxed-sales tally exceeds one million units, and PS5 dominates that particular channel.
