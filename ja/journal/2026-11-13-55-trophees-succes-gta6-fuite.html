GTA 6 trophy list leak: 55 trophies, one achievement worth 1,000 points

GTA 6 would have 55 trophies on PS5 and 55 Xbox achievements, one worth 1,000 points, per a leak reported by 01net. Rockstar has not confirmed.

情報源 01net.com · goclecd.fr

GTA 6 would have 55 trophies on PS5 and as many achievements on Xbox Series, according to 01net, which relies on platform files from Sony and Microsoft. Rockstar has confirmed nothing, so treat the list as a leak, not a fact.

How many trophies will GTA 6 have?

The figure is 55, platinum included on PlayStation. Both platforms show the same total independently, which lends weight to the extraction without confirming it, 01net notes. The person who pulled the data withheld the names to avoid spoilers. For comparison, GTA 5 launched with 49 Xbox 360 achievements and 50 PS3 trophies, Red Dead Redemption 2 with 51 and 52.

Why is one achievement worth 1,000 points?

On Xbox the list shows 54 entries worth 1 to 54 points and one worth 1,000, according to Goclecd, citing data miner TexFunz2. Two readings circulate: a "Mythic" achievement, a rare Microsoft distinction for exceptional feats, or a placeholder value used in internal testing. Nothing settles it yet.

The count also says nothing about the game's size or how hard the platinum is: RDR2 and its 52 trophies are still seen as a tough one. A dubious screenshot mentioned 58 trophies last year, so these figures can move before launch.

Why it matters

For trophy hunters it is a first order of magnitude: slightly more than GTA 5 and RDR2. Names and conditions will only appear at launch. We will update this piece if Rockstar or the platforms publish the list.
