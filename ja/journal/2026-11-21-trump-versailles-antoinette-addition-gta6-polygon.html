GTA 6: a nod to Trump and the Versailles bill, according to Polygon

Per Polygon, GTA 6's Antoinette restaurant, modeled on Miami's Versailles, displays a flyer: "all government officials must pay before leaving."

情報源 polygon.com

Rockstar promised to stay away from current politics, yet a flyer suggests otherwise. According to Polygon, GTA 6 slips a discreet reference to Donald Trump into its version of Miami's Cuban restaurant Versailles, renamed Antoinette in the game.

What does the Antoinette flyer say?

A sheet taped to a window reads: "All government officials must pay before leaving. Thank you." The outlet says TikToker Quique spotted it, and Florida viewers tied it to a specific episode in his video's comments. The real Versailles' owners made the connection too, per Polygon.

Which episode is it about?

In 2023, Donald Trump stopped at Versailles after a federal arraignment in Miami, according to the Miami New Times cited by Polygon. The crowd sang "Happy Birthday" as his birthday fell the next day. He said there would be "food for everyone," which some customers took as a round on him. He reportedly left after about ten minutes without paying for anyone. The outlet notes people still debate whether he truly broke a promise, given how short the visit was.

Had Rockstar ruled out this kind of joke?

Before launch, Rockstar North studio head Rob Nelson dismissed the idea of addressing the US president directly, saying it could date the game. The flyer names nobody and avoids politics, which fits that line. Polygon even finds the joke tame for a series known for lampooning American politics.
