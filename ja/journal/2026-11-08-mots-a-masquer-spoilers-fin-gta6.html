GTA 6 spoilers: the keywords to mute on social media before launch

A popular community account shared about thirty keywords to mute on social media to avoid GTA 6 story spoilers before release. The list is unofficial.

情報源 x.com

J-11 GTA 6 発売まであと11日 →

The account @gtasix_ posted a list of keywords to mute on Sunday so fans can avoid GTA 6 story spoilers on social media before launch. The post passed 137,000 views by late afternoon, with over 3,500 likes and 2,500 bookmarks. This is a community initiative: neither Rockstar nor any outlet has endorsed the list.

What is on the list?

The Vice City-colored graphic gathers about thirty English phrases. One group starts with "GTA VI": ending, plot twist, final mission, secret ending, true ending, story leak, final cutscene, death scene, betrayal. The other group targets terms without the game's name: "ending leaked", "ending explained", "who dies", "all endings", "final cutscene".

Several entries pair the names Jason and Lucia with "dies", "death", "killed", "ending" or "betrayal". The wording hints that the feared spoilers concern the fate of the two leads, but nothing is confirmed: the plot has never been revealed and the account cites no source.

How do you mute them?

On X, muted words are set under Settings, "Privacy and safety", "Mute and block", "Muted words". Each phrase can be hidden from the timeline, notifications or both, for a chosen duration or indefinitely. Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Reddit offer comparable word filters, with different menus. Terms in other languages are not on the list and must be added by hand.

Why now?

The topic keeps coming back. On November 4 we reported that Rockstar may remove spoilers even after release, according to Insider Gaming. Meanwhile, the leaks announced by Cyberleek have made part of the community wary of its own feed. Regional release times, covered on the release page, can also leave hours of gap between countries, so some players may see spoilers before others can play.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game itself: no new story information is revealed. The list is simply a starting point for those who want to reach launch day knowing nothing about the ending. It does not replace usual caution: avoid suggestive video titles, comments and searches on platforms. We will not publish leaks or plot excerpts on this site.
