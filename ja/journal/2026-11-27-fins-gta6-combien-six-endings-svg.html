GTA 6 endings: how many are there? Six found so far, per SVG (spoilers)

Players have found at least six different endings in GTA 6, according to SVG, depending on one choice at the roadblock, BuckMe savings and criminal profiles.

情報源 svg.com

How many endings does GTA 6 have? At least six, according to SVG, which says more could turn up. Spoilers ahead for Lucia and Jason's story.

What decides the ending?

Per SVG, everything plays out in the mission "The End of the Road": an assault on Giorgio Gallo's penthouse, then a run down a police-filled highway. The outlet lists several factors: your story choices, the money in the BuckMe savings account, the strength of the couple's relationship and their criminal profiles. See our piece on criminal profiles.

What are the happy endings?

Every ending where both of Tomás' parents survive goes through a police roadblock: you must pick "swim for it" over "no surrender". The best one, says SVG, needs low criminal profiles, at least $2.5 million in BuckMe, a strong "Hot Together" relationship and Captain Aldo Méndez dead before the final mission. Jason takes the fall, Lucia strikes a deal and, two years later, bails him out with the BuckMe money. With low profiles but the other conditions unmet, Jason still goes to prison and Lucia visits with Tomás.

And the tragic ones?

The earliest ending skips the final mission: sparing Méndez and taking his deal in "Protective Custody" gets the pair handcuffed and driven to a swamp. Taking the deal and then trying to flee leaves Jason shot and Lucia choosing between dying with him or surviving alone. "No surrender" at the roadblock is, in SVG's view, the most cinematic ending: they charge holding hands. Even swimming can end badly if the criminal profile is high enough.

Why it matters

Keeping several saves before "Protective Custody" looks wise, as SVG suggests. The count of six is not final and the full list of conditions is unconfirmed. More on the journal.
