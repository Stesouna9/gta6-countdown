GTA 6 trailer 1 rebuilt in LEGO by a fan after nine months in Blender

Per La Crème Du Gaming, a fan remade GTA 6's first trailer shot by shot in 3D LEGO, nine months of work in Blender, posted on Reddit and YouTube.

A fan has rebuilt the first GTA 6 trailer in LEGO bricks, shot for shot. La Crème Du Gaming reports the project took nine months in Blender and was posted on Reddit, then YouTube. It is not a LEGO GTA game, just a fan video, but it is spreading.

Who made the GTA 6 LEGO trailer?

The outlet cites a Reddit post titled "I remade the first trailer in LEGO!" on r/GTA6, with a video hosted on the YouTube channel CatCGIUser. The author says they worked "on and off" for nearly nine months before finishing the edit in October 2025. Per the outlet, citing GamePro, it is their longest project. Everything is CGI in Blender: no physical bricks, no stop-motion.

What does it show?

Vice City, its beaches, its car chases and the pace of the edit, rendered as minifigures and LEGO-style sets. It looks like a real ad for a game that does not exist. Comments quoted by the outlet call the work "incredible"; some write "We got LEGO GTA 6 before GTA 6", others joke Rockstar should hire the author. Those are fan reactions, not a studio position.

Is a real LEGO GTA game coming?

Nothing says so. Neither Rockstar nor LEGO has announced anything, and the video hints at no partnership. The original trailer remains Rockstar's: the official schedule is on the release page, and the fiction spilling into Miami shows how far the universe inspires outside the game.

Why it matters

Nothing for the November 19 release. It is one more sign of how attached the community is to the first trailer, and a patient piece of work worth watching.
