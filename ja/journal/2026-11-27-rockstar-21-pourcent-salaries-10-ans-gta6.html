GTA 6: 21% of Rockstar staff have worked at the studio for over 10 years

According to Bloomberg, relayed by La Crème du Gaming, 21% of Rockstar employees, about 1,000 people out of 4,711, have been at the studio for more than ten years.

情報源 lacremedugaming.fr

About 1,000 Rockstar Games employees, 21% of the workforce, have been at the studio for more than ten years, according to journalist Jason Schreier in Bloomberg, a figure reported by La Crème du Gaming. We have not read the Bloomberg piece.

What does the figure actually say?

It does not describe the GTA 6 team alone but all of Rockstar: 4,711 employees across 12 studios in 2026, versus 2,441 at the time of Red Dead Redemption 2, per the site. It is not a strict retention rate, more a snapshot of tenure. Per PC Gamer, cited by the site, many department heads and directors have over twenty years at the company, some since GTA V.

Why does tenure matter?

The outlet sees "institutional knowledge": teams that know each other, mastered in-house tools, tested processes. PC Gamer compares it to studios like FromSoftware, Larian and Arrowhead, while series such as Halo or Dragon Age suffered heavy turnover. That is an interpretation, not proof: these numbers do not establish a link between tenure and game quality.

For context, the game logged 32.5 million pre-orders and $3.79 billion in day-one sales, per Take-Two results cited by the site, with a 99 out of 100 on Metacritic.

What about labor tensions?

The site notes that 31 UK layoffs are contested before the Glasgow tribunal, with the IWGB union accusing the publisher of obstructing union activity. It mentions a vote on recognizing the Rockstar Games Workers Union; we already reported that the vote failed, per Kotaku. On sales, see also the UK boxed launch.

Why it matters

Nothing for players short term, but a clue about how GTA 6 was built: by teams that have worked together for a long time. Read the number with care, as a group-wide average. More in the Journal.
