GTA 6 external hard drive: can PS5 and Xbox run it from USB storage?

No: GTA 6 must be installed on internal storage to run on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, according to Engadget, though you can park it on an external drive.

情報源 engadget.com

Can you play GTA 6 from an external hard drive to save console space? No, Engadget says. You can move the game to external storage while you are not playing, but to run it locally it has to sit on the console's internal storage.

The question comes up because of the size of the install, which we covered in our 212 GB report. With a nearly full SSD, players look for a way out.

Which storage can run GTA 6 on PS5 and Xbox?

According to Engadget, the game has to run from the PS5's built-in SSD, an SSD installed inside it, or a compatible NVMe expansion card on Xbox. The reason given: like many recent games, GTA 6 relies on the fast loading of current consoles, and a USB drive cannot feed data quickly enough.

Engadget notes that the same rule applies to disc-based PS5 and Xbox Series X games, which install to system storage before play. Only older games are exempt: the PS5 runs PS4 titles from external storage, and Xbox Series X|S runs Xbox One and Xbox 360 games.

Is there a way to play without freeing up space?

Yes, cloud streaming. On Xbox Series X|S the game can be streamed. Engadget flags two limits: Microsoft just introduced cloud gaming time caps, with up to 15 hours a month included for Game Pass subscribers, and GTA 6 is limited to Xbox Series S graphics settings in that mode.

On PlayStation, streaming works on PS5 and PlayStation Portal for PlayStation Plus Premium subscribers, with no time cap. We detailed it in our Portal piece. Either way you must buy the game: it is not in PS Plus or Game Pass, and Engadget doubts it will be any time soon.

Our reading: an external drive is still handy to park the game between sessions, if you can live with the copy time. Rockstar has announced nothing that would change this. See the price page for editions.

Why it matters

For anyone with a full console SSD, buying an external drive solves nothing: you will need to delete other games, expand internal storage or use the cloud. Engadget does say moving the game to external storage is possible, which spares a full re-download.
