GTA 6: Michael and Jimmy De Santa produced a film, poster shows

A film poster found in Vice City credits GTA V's Michael and Jimmy De Santa on 'Righteous Slaughter Overkill', according to GAMINGbible.

情報源 gamingbible.com

A nod for GTA V fans has been circulating since this morning: in Vice City, a film poster credits Michael and Jimmy De Santa. According to GAMINGbible, which relays a find by X user CaptainGlack shared by DiscussingFilm, the two characters did make a film together after the events of GTA V.

What does the poster show?

The film is called "Righteous Slaughter Overkill". Zooming in, CaptainGlack saw the credits name Michael De Santa and Jimmy De Santa, though it isn't clear whether they are producers, directors or something else. GAMINGbible says the poster can be found across Vice City.

Why does that title ring a bell?

"Righteous Slaughter" is the series' Call of Duty parody. In GTA V, Michael watches Jimmy play "Righteous Slaughter 7", and characters in GTA VI wear "Slaughter Hood Ops" shirts. The outlet sees a loop: Jimmy's favorite game turned into a Vinewood blockbuster by his dad.

It extends GTA V's canon ending per GAMINGbible: Michael leaves crime for a producer career in Vinewood, Jimmy survives and moves out.
