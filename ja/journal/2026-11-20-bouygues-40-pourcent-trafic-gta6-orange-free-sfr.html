GTA 6: up to 40% of Bouygues Telecom traffic, more than Netflix and YouTube

GTA 6 downloads made up as much as 40% of Bouygues Telecom's daytime traffic on Thursday, its technical director says; Orange hit a record, Free and SFR report peaks with no impact.

情報源 leparisien.fr · 20minutes.fr

GTA 6 is digital only, and French networks felt it. According to Le Parisien, several French internet providers reported traffic peaks on Thursday, with the game playable from midnight. 20 Minutes reports the same figures.

What do Bouygues, Orange, Free and SFR say?

At Bouygues Telecom, technical director Jérôme Latrille cites "a traffic peak never reached on our network", 10% above its busiest usual days such as Sundays. During the day, game downloads reached up to 40% of total traffic, "more than Netflix and YouTube combined", which normally sit near 20% each. No throughput figures were given.

At Orange, downloads helped set a new traffic record on 12 November, when preloading opened, and again on Wednesday evening with a 10 pm peak. Free saw a higher peak than the week before without notable trouble. SFR mentions a Wednesday spike with no impact.

Why so much traffic?

Every player must download the game, boxed buyers included, as the box holds only a code. Preloading spread part of the load, but day-one updates and new purchases concentrated demand. It is global: see NBN's 3.3 Tbps record in Australia and Virgin Media's records during preload.

Why it matters

Nothing for players already installed. For others, slower downloads at peak hours remain possible, though no operator reports trouble. See our release page for the launch facts.
