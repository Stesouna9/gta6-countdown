GTA 6: Jordan moves toward a ban, regulator cites child safety

Jordan's telecoms regulator says it is working on an official ban of GTA 6, pointing to children's safety and contact with strangers online.

情報源 thenationalnews.com

Is Jordan about to ban GTA 6? The National reports that Lara Al-Khatib, chair of the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission (TRC), told Roya News the body is working with other authorities on an official ban after public calls for tighter controls. No date or legal text has been given.

What is the regulator's argument?

According to The National, Al-Khatib says modern games are interactive digital spaces where players talk to strangers by voice and text, which can expose children to inappropriate contact and security risks. She argues age ratings alone cannot stop minors reaching games through digital platforms, even though GTA 6 is adults-only. The TRC is building a monitoring and blocking system and urges parents to supervise.

What about neighbouring countries?

The paper notes mixed approaches. Saudi Arabia lifted its long ban on the series in 2025, and the game is out there with a 21+ rating. The UAE also uses 21+, while its drug enforcement authority warns about the portrayal of drugs. The report places the story against a record launch: $3.79 billion in 24 hours, per Reuters, and 32.5 million units sold before release, according to Rockstar.

In Europe the game is rated PEGI 18, see our release page. The debate is not new: a US lawmaker weighed in earlier.

Why it matters

This is a stated intention, not a law. How Jordan would enforce it, through platform blocking or store removal, is unknown, and Rockstar had not responded in the sources we read.
