GTA 6: Lucia actress Manni Perez thanks fans after launch surge

Per two X accounts, Lucia's actress posted a thank-you message after gaining over 500,000 followers since November 19; Rockstar has not commented.

情報源 x.com · x.com

Manni L. Perez, the actress who plays Lucia, has posted a new message to her followers, according to the X accounts GTAVI_Countdown and gtasix_, which shared it on Thursday evening. She thanks fans and says she spent almost a decade working with Rockstar on the character.

What does Manni Perez's message say?

Per gtasix_, the actress thanks her followers for their support and says she spent nearly ten years bringing Lucia to life. GTAVI_Countdown adds that she has gained more than half a million followers since GTA 6 launched on November 19. We could not find the full original post: these two accounts describe its content, and none of the outlets we monitor has picked it up yet. Treat the details with caution until the exact wording is checked at the source.

Who is Manni Perez?

According to our cast article, based on the credits reported by Première, Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos opposite Dylan Rourke as Jason. A decade of work fits the game's long development, but the exact duration comes from the actress herself and has not been confirmed by Rockstar.

Why the jump in followers?

The half-million figure comes from GTAVI_Countdown with no screenshot of the account's stats, so we cannot verify it. It does fit a moment of heavy scrutiny on the people behind the game: Lucia's writer Alexa D'Agostino left X after a wave of harassment (our article today). Perez's thank-you message lands on a day when the team behind the character is under the spotlight.

Why it matters

Nothing changes in the game itself: this is a thank-you note, not an announcement. But an actress gaining that many followers within days shows how much Lucia resonates with players.
