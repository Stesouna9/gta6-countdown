GTA 6 backrooms location: a permanent stamina reward near Tallaloko

Journal du Geek describes a hidden container encounter with a stamina upgrade and tattoo reward. Location and encounter spoilers follow.

写真：Rockstar Games

情報源 journaldugeek.com

A yellow shipping container is the starting point for an unusual GTA 6 detour, according to Journal du Geek. Its guide describes a hidden encounter that sends Jason or Lucia through strange spaces before awarding a permanent stamina improvement. A tattoo is another reported reward. This article discusses the encounter and its location, rather than the ending of the main story.

Where does the encounter begin?

Journal du Geek places the entrance near Lake Tallaloko, northwest of Vice City and southeast of Mount Kalaga. The landmark to look for is a workers’ camp with shipping containers. Inside an open yellow container, the guide identifies a box carrying an eye symbol. Opening that object starts the sequence.

That makes the initial search more specific than simply looking for an abandoned structure. The source points to an interaction inside a particular container, rather than a random doorway. Our official Leonida illustration is general artwork and does not show the entrance; it should not be used as a navigation image.

What does the guide say about rewards?

The reported journey passes through several areas and floors. Journal du Geek calls it a reinterpretation of the backrooms idea, rather than a recreation of the familiar yellow room. Completing it reportedly provides a permanent stamina gain and an exclusive tattoo available through tattoo parlours in Vice City and Leonida.

The outlet also advises thinking about which protagonist to use, because some upgrade encounters cannot be repeated. That caution should not be expanded into an unsupported rule. Its text does not quantify the stamina increase or demonstrate that the reward transfers between both protagonists. We therefore cannot promise that completing it once improves Jason and Lucia together.

Why it matters

The practical attraction is a lasting character improvement attached to exploration. A strange roadside discovery can mean more than another location to photograph. For players following the discoveries in our news journal, the useful distinction here is the explicit link between an encounter and a permanent ability reward.

There are still limits to what this guide establishes. It offers an entrance and names two rewards, but does not supply an upgrade percentage, a chapter requirement or an exhaustive catalogue of similar encounters. Keeping those gaps visible helps players decide how to approach the detour without turning one reported secret into a claim about every hidden activity across Leonida.
