GTA 6 PS5 dump reportedly runs on a jailbroken console, per Notebookcheck

Videos from 25 November show a GTA 6 PS5 dump running on a firmware 11.60 jailbreak, says Notebookcheck. Rockstar and Sony have confirmed nothing.

Źródła notebookcheck.net

A dump of the PS5 version of GTA 6 is reportedly running on a jailbroken console, according to Notebookcheck. The site cites videos that surfaced on 25 November and 540 GB of files circulating on piracy networks. Neither Rockstar nor Sony has confirmed anything.

What do the videos show?

Per Notebookcheck, several clips show the game installed on the console dashboard and a debug menu identifying firmware 11.60. One shows a car driving through dark Leonida streets. Redditors have verified, the site says, that the files are being shared on piracy networks: the 540 GB download contains the decrypted eboot.bin, similar to an .exe file, and the sce_sys folder.

Why does it puzzle specialists?

Before release, a dump had been made on firmware 14.22, while the Relapse kernel exploit only works up to 13.60. Some tinkerers had therefore chosen to wait. Notebookcheck notes it is unclear how the game boots on 11.60, when the retail version requires 14.23, and whether it would work on consoles at 13.60. Those gaps matter before reading this as a complete demonstration.

How does this relate to the PC port?

The site recalls that Cyberleek recently distributed a full Xbox build that players are already adapting for Windows, with badly compromised frame rates. Take-Two responded by shutting down Discord servers and issuing DMCA notices against GitHub repositories, as we wrote about the DMCA against Leonida and 3,007 repositories. Notebookcheck also names the KytyPS5 emulator, available on Windows and Linux, which reads folders with eboot.bin files, but stresses that much work remains to stream assets correctly and reach acceptable performance.

Our note on modders booting the Xbox build still applies: a game that launches is not a playable game.

Why it matters

For players, nothing: GTA 6 is still announced only for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no official PC version. For Take-Two and Rockstar, it is one more piracy front. We relay no links or methods. The official release information remains our reference.
