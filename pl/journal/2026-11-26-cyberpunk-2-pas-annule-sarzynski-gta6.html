GTA 6: Cyberpunk 2 is not cancelled, the director's line was a joke

Cyberpunk 2's creative director joked on Bluesky after praising GTA 6; CD Projekt RED's game is still in pre-production, GeeksLands reports.

Źródła geekslands.fr

No, Cyberpunk 2 is not cancelled. According to GeeksLands, the line circulating since the weekend was indeed written by Igor Sarzyński, creative director at CD Projekt RED, but as a joke, two days after GTA 6 launched.

What did the creative director write?

On Bluesky on Saturday evening, Sarzyński first praised Rockstar's game with no irony: "GTA VI open-world developer depression" is real, and the game is "probably the greatest artistic achievement in history". Then the joke: "cyberpunk 2 is cancelled, imma go sweep autumn leaves in my backyard now". That evening he clarified they are making something of their own, "with a different and unique flavor". Several outlets took the joke literally, GeeksLands says.

Where is Cyberpunk 2 really?

The site reports that CD Projekt said in its quarterly results that 199 developers worked on Cyberpunk 2 on October 31, up from 184 on July 31, with a target of about 320 by the end of 2027. The game is still in pre-production and the start of full production is unknown. The Witcher 4 still has 513 developers. Co-CEO Michał Nowakowski acknowledged a dip in console activity since GTA 6, with no effect on PC, where Rockstar's game has not launched.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for Cyberpunk 2: the cancellation rumour comes from a misread joke. Associate director Paweł Sasko also congratulated Rockstar. GTA 6's real impact on rivals' schedules remains to be seen.
