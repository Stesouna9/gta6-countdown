GTA 6 collector box without the game compared with Peak's collector

GAMINGbible contrasts GTA 6's Goodtime State box, sold without the game, with the collector edition of Peak on PS5, which includes two copies.

The GTA 6 collector box keeps people talking. GAMINGbible sets it against the collector edition of Peak, a co-op climbing game coming to PS5, arguing the small indie beats the big releases.

What is in Rockstar's box?

The Goodtime State: Vice City Collection costs 399.99 euros in France and includes neither the game nor a download code. GAMINGbible lists a crocodile figurine, a hat, a bag and sunglasses. We detailed this collector without the game when it was revealed. The game is bought separately: 79.99 euros standard, 99.99 euros Ultimate, digital only.

What does Peak offer?

Per GAMINGbible, Peak's collector edition contains a passport kit, a physical copy and a "buddy pass" that works as a second full copy for a friend. The site also mentions a since-deleted listing at retailer VGP showing $21.09. Treat that price with caution: the listing is gone and nothing confirms it was right.

The outlet also cites the God of War: Laufey collector, with a statue, cards and an art book, again with no copy of the game.

Is the comparison fair?

Mostly it is about tone. A 400 euro box and a roughly 20 dollar indie do not share an audience, and GAMINGbible openly writes a mood piece. One factual point stands: for GTA 6 as for God of War, the collector is a display item, not a way to get the game. See our goodies page for the products.

Why it matters

Nothing changes in Rockstar's offer: the box stays without the game. If you want the collector, budget for the game on top, close to 480 euros at minimum.
