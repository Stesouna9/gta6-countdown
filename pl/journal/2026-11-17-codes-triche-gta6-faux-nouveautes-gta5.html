GTA 6 cheat codes: the lists going around are fake, Rockstar published none

No official GTA 6 cheat code list exists: the screenshots in circulation mostly recycle GTA V codes, according to GinjFo.

Źródła ginjfo.com

There is no official list of GTA 6 cheat codes. That is the verdict of GinjFo, two days before the November 19 release on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S: screenshots of "new codes" are spreading on social media, and the site stresses that Rockstar Games has published no list for GTA VI.

Are these GTA 6 cheat codes real?

Not as presented. According to GinjFo, several entries are old and already appear in the GTA V guide. Two examples: LAWYERUP, which lowers the police wanted level, and TOOLUP, which hands out weapons and ammo. In GTA V these are dialed as phone numbers on the character's cell phone (1-999-5299-3787 and 1-999-866-587).

The trick is in the layout. The screenshots mix controller combos, PC commands and in-game phone numbers, which makes the list look complete and new. Yet no PC version of GTA 6 has been announced, and nothing shows that an in-game phone accepts these numbers.

What do we actually know about cheats in GTA 6?

Nothing confirmed. Rockstar has said nothing about whether cheat codes return. GinjFo says a code should only count once reproduced in story mode, with the console, the input and the on-screen effect documented. See our release page for what is confirmed.

Launch scams are multiplying too, from fake pre-order sites to booby-trapped downloads, as covered in our scams article. A "code list" to download, or a site asking payment for one, deserves suspicion.

Why it matters

For now, nothing in those screenshots applies to GTA 6. Wait for November 19 and documented player reports before believing any code. Until then, any "official" list is fake, since Rockstar published none.
