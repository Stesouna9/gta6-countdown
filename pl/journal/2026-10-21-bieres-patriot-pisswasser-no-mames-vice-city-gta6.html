GTA 6 beer: Patriot, Pißwasser and No Mames Vice City may soon be real

US regulators approved three GTA-related beer labels, including a new No Mames Vice City lager, reportedly brewed by Florida's Tripping Animals.

Źródła ign.com

GTA's fake beers may be leaving the screen. According to IGN, citing iGrandTheftAuto, the US Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau approved three labels: Patriot Beer, Pißwasser and a new brand, No Mames Vice City.

Who is brewing it?

IGN says Florida's Tripping Animals Brewing is behind them. No Mames Vice City is a 6% ABV lager with a small dog in sunglasses and a Vice City chain on the label; the other two sit at 4.2%, the same as Bud Light. Patriot and Pißwasser have been in the series for years, so seeing them on a can is a small homecoming.

Where could you drink it?

Nobody knows. IGN has no date or location and guesses at Miami bars or one-off events. Neither Rockstar nor the brewery has confirmed anything, and a label approval is not a release schedule. It does fit the campaign: after the fake Weazel, Pfister and Kronos ads in Miami, the game is spilling into the street, then into the glass.

IGN adds that Rockstar is also advertising in Paris, New York, Chicago and Los Angeles. For official merchandise, see our goodies page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game, out November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. But a fictional brand sold for real shows Rockstar treating Vice City as a place you can visit. Watch for an official announcement or cans photographed in Miami.
