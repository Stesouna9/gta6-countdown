GTA 6 ads spread to Berlin, Singapore and Melbourne ahead of launch

Notebookcheck lists GTA 6 posters, murals and decorated trams across Europe, Asia and Australia, two weeks before the November 19 release.

Źródła notebookcheck.biz

J-14 GTA 6 wychodzi za 14 dni →

With under two weeks to go, the GTA 6 campaign is no longer an American affair. Notebookcheck lists posters, murals and decorated vehicles across Europe, Asia and Australia, based on photos shared by players on the GTA subreddit.

Where are GTA 6 ads showing up worldwide?

In the US it began with the Brooklyn murals and the billboards on the Kaseya Center roof in Miami. Notebookcheck adds Times Square in New York. In Europe, two large posters greet commuters on the Alexanderplatz platform in Berlin, the Lisbon metro carries the game too, and a large ad at La Défense in Paris still looks unfinished in photos.

In Asia, lit-up ads join the signs of Osaka, Japan. In Singapore a whole metro station has been redecorated, with a moving walkway covered in ads along its full length. A double-decker tram in China is said to carry GTA 6 ads, but the outlet notes no photo has surfaced and there is no other report from the country.

What about Australia?

In Melbourne, a huge mural, apparently painted by two artists, covers the side of a multi-storey building. Decorated trams have been spotted in Melbourne and Sydney.

A count to treat carefully

The X account GTAlphaintel claims the campaign reaches at least 14 countries. That is a fan tally, not confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two. The photos come from private users and Notebookcheck did not verify each one. Rockstar has published no official list of cities.

We covered the Miami tourist bus ads in our Vice City Narcos article. What is new is the scale, a few days before the November 19 release.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the date or the price: release is November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. But the campaign shows Rockstar is finishing in the street, after the Edge, Game Informer and IGN features. Watch for more cities and more photos.
