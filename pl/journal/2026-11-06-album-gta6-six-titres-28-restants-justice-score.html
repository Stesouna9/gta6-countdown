GTA 6 Album: six of 34 tracks out, 28 to come, Justice score on December 11

Six of the 34 tracks on GTA VI: The Album can already be streamed, the other 28 arrive on unannounced dates before November 19, and Justice's score follows on December 11.

Źródła rockstargames.com · actionnetwork.com

Six of the 34 tracks are already out. Rockstar has said Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album lands on November 19, 2026 with 34 original songs, and the first six can be streamed, Action Network writes in a November 5 roundup. The other 28 will arrive over the coming weeks, on dates Rockstar has not given.

Who is on the first six tracks?

According to Action Network, the first batch brings together PinkPantheress, Keith Richards, Yung Lean and Travis Scott. Scott's track, "RHYNO", is produced by Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo of Daft Punk. The mix is deliberately wide: closer to a Vice City radio dial than a conventional soundtrack record.

The six stations Rockstar has already detailed, from Flash FM to The Chamber 106.6, set the frame; see our piece on the stations and the music page.

What is still to be revealed before November 19?

Twenty-eight tracks. Action Network says Rockstar is releasing them in waves with no public schedule, so any track can drop without notice. The same outlet notes that Kalshi prediction markets are betting on the names to come, with Lana Del Rey leading at nearly 90% implied probability. Those are traders' bets, not information: Rockstar has confirmed no artist beyond the first batch, and the full list is unknown.

The same caution applies to the rumoured Drake station: the outlet stresses there is no official confirmation to date.

What about the score by Justice?

Two separate projects. Rockstar presented an Original Score composed by French duo Justice on its Newswire. Action Network reports it is out December 11 on Ed Banger Records, three weeks after the game. It serves as adaptive music for missions and exploration, including "Pusher" with Gesaffelstein.

The November 19 album collects the radio-station tracks. In short: the songs you hear on the car radio on one side, the music that scores the action on the other.

Why it matters

For listeners, the calendar tightens: the full tracklist will be known by the November 19 release at the latest, so expect day-by-day announcements. For players, the soundtrack is being revealed piece by piece like one more teasing campaign. The artist lists going around remain guesses until Rockstar issues a statement. The exact release date of each remaining track is unknown.
