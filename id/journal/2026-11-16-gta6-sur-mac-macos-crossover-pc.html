GTA 6 on Mac: no macOS version, but PC port and CrossOver could open a path

GTA 6 has no Mac or PC version at its November 19 launch; MacGeneration sees a possible route through a future Windows port and CrossOver.

No GTA 6 on Mac on November 19. French outlet MacGeneration notes that Rockstar has planned no PC version at launch, let alone a macOS port, but lists several routes Apple players could try in the coming months.

Will GTA 6 come to Mac?

A native version looks "highly unlikely", writes MacGeneration: GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 never got macOS versions, and Rockstar's few Apple ports are old iOS titles like Bully. A Windows port would have to come first: GTA V waited 19 months for PC, Red Dead Redemption 2 a little over a year. See also what Strauss Zelnick said about PC ports.

What about CrossOver and cloud gaming?

The outlet cites CrossOver, a compatibility layer that already runs GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 on Apple Silicon Macs, along with Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 and Helldivers 2. Cloud gaming is the other option. It suggests the community could tinker GTA 6 onto Mac the same way once a Windows version exists.

It also quotes former Rockstar technical director Obbe Vermeij, who told Ars Technica that AI-assisted reverse engineering could change things. Example: the Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake, released November 5 on Switch 2, ran on Mac less than 48 hours later thanks to hobbyists. MacGeneration adds that this is legally dubious and would be harder for GTA 6.

Why it matters

Nothing before a PC version, which is not announced. Even then, MacGeneration warns that the game's protections, graphics requirements and engine could block compatibility, and that a recent chip will be needed. Confirmed platform facts are on our release page.
