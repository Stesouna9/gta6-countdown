GTA 6: Rockstar reveals six radio stations, a 34-track album and opens the store

On October 8 Rockstar opened the GTA VI music page: six Leonida stations with their hosts, GTA VI: The Album due November 19, six singles already out, and a merch collection on the Rockstar Store.

Forty-two days before launch, Rockstar dropped more about GTA 6's music in one day than in two years of teasing. The page rockstargames.com/VI/music presents six radio stations, an original album and previews to play.

Six stations, six moods

Flash FM is back from Vice City, hosted by Robyn and Alex, still on pop. Cocoteo plays modern Latin with El Martillo and La Gata. Back Country is renegade country from MW and Delta Dawne. Afro Bank FM blends amapiano and African classics with Burna and Palmsy. The Chamber brings metal to Leonida with DJ Kerry and DJ Tom. Dirty South Classics, with Trick and Trina, plays Southern regional rap. Rockstar calls it the biggest and widest-ranging selection of radio and podcasts in the series.

GTA VI: The Album, 34 tracks on November 19

The original album ships on launch day, digital, vinyl and CD. Six singles are out now: That's It (Yung Lean with Future and Metro Boomin), RHYNO (Travis Scott), Sexy Magic (CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.., Etienne de Crécy), Last Thing You Need (Morgan Wallen), Macacoa 2000 (Rauw Alejandro) and Bright Lights, Big City (Keith Richards). All of them play on our music page.

The store is open

The Rockstar Store listed the GTA VI collection: tees and caps at €29.99, totes at €39.99, a Vice City Zippo at €39.99, a money clip, keychain, stickers, and the album on vinyl or CD. The Goodtime State collector's box stays at €399.99, no game included. The full list, prices refreshed every ten minutes, is on the merch page.

Why it matters

The soundtrack was the last big unknown of GTA 6. What is still missing: the exact unlock time and the install size. Rockstar said nothing more about those today.
