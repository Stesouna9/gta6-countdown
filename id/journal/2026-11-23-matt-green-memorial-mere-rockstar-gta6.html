GTA 6 developer honors his late mother with a memorial in Leonida

Matt Green, a QA developer at Rockstar, had his late mother's name added to a memorial players can visit in Leonida, according to IGN Nordic.

Sumber nordic.ign.com

Among Leonida's hidden details, this one is no trick. According to IGN Nordic, Matt Green, a QA developer at Rockstar Games, paid tribute to his late mother with a memorial built into GTA 6 that players can find and visit.

What did Matt Green say?

The outlet says he told the story in an Instagram post. His mother helped launch his career in game development. She died eight months before GTA 6 launched, a week ago, and Rockstar still managed to include her in a wall of names at a memorial in Leonida.

Where is the memorial?

The report gives no exact location and we will not guess one. It only says players can reach the wall in the game. Until Rockstar or the developer say more, we stick to what IGN Nordic reports: a real tribute, visible in the retail game.

Studios have long slipped nods to their teams into open worlds. This one is more personal. The game came out on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and players keep finding new details; what the community logs is on the community page.

Why it matters

Nothing changes in gameplay, but it changes how the game reads: behind a huge world there are teams whose personal stories end up in it. Players who find the wall will know what it stands for. Rockstar has not commented so far.
