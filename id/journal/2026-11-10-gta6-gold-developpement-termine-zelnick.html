GTA 6 has gone gold: Take-Two says Rockstar has finished development

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said on Monday that Rockstar has finished developing GTA 6, due November 19, and ruled out another delay.

Sumber x.com · x.com · ign.com

GTA 6 is finished. According to GTAVI_Countdown and gtasix_, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick confirmed on Monday's earnings call that development is complete: the game has gone gold. Both accounts quote him: "They've done their very best work." Rockstar has not issued a dedicated statement yet.

What does "gold" mean for GTA 6?

It means the final build is locked for manufacturing and distribution. For a digital-only launch, it means the release build is frozen. Whether a day-one patch is planned has not been announced, and we won't guess. The 212 GB preload already showed the files were in place, as we wrote in our preload article.

Will GTA 6 be delayed again?

Per GTAVI_Countdown, Zelnick said for the first time, in an interview with IGN, that the game will not be delayed again. We could not re-read the full interview, so we stick to what that account reports. The date stays November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Context matters: pre-orders are running "well above" Take-Two's expectations, as covered in this article.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for players before November 19: preloading is open and the unlock time is still unknown. But another delay now looks far less likely. All the dates are on our release page.
