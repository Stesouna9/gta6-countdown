GTA 6: Justice on the track with The Weeknd, "almost an easter egg"

Speaking to Fun Radio, French duo Justice says it scored part of GTA 6's story mode, including a track with The Weeknd that some players may never hear.

Sumber funradio.fr · funradio.fr

On L'After Fun Radio, the duo Justice shed light on its work for GTA 6. According to Fun Radio, The Weeknd sings on a story-mode track the pair call "almost an easter egg". The pairing felt obvious: "The minute we wrote the instrumental, we were thinking of Abel."

How did The Weeknd end up on the GTA 6 score?

Justice says it waited several months before reaching out: "You sometimes have to be wary of what's too obvious." A Grand Theft Auto fan, the singer said yes right away: "He told us ok, cool, I'll record something tonight." A day and a half later the duo had a version already close to the final track, per Fun Radio.

A challenge accepted after a week in New York

In a second interview, {L(F2,'Fun Radio')} details the first doubts: "We weren't sure we were the right people to do this." The link with Rockstar dates back to GTA IV, which already featured one of their songs. For GTA 6 the same contact came back, a fan who knew their catalogue "by heart". Justice spent a week at the studio's New York offices, eight hours a day watching the game, before signing on. The work stretched over several years; one member joked: "When I started I had one child. When we finished I had three."

Justice worked on the story-mode music, especially its urban side. That differs from supplying songs to in-game radio: it means scoring action and emotion. "It always had to be justified on screen," they say, with "a lot of back and forth" with Rockstar's music supervisors.

Why it matters

We already knew the band from Flower in a Hurricane climbing the charts. What is new is the method and one in-game detail: the Weeknd track sits behind a path players who skip missions to explore freely may never cross. Rockstar has confirmed nothing about its exact location, and we do not know more. The full soundtrack lives on the music page.
