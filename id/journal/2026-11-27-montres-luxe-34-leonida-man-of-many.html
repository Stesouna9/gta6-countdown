GTA 6 luxury watches: 34 hidden across Leonida, one based on a Rolex Daytona

Rockstar hid 34 luxury watches in GTA 6, per Man of Many; collecting 10 and 20 reportedly unlocks two cheats after the main story.

Sumber manofmany.com

GTA 6 players are now hunting watches. According to Man of Many, Rockstar hid 34 luxury watches across Leonida, out of 79 collectable clothing styles. The outlet notes the game has been out only a week, so mapping is still in progress.

How many watches are there and where?

Man of Many says GTABase's interactive map pins 31 of the 34. Locations are sorted by confirmation: those backed by at least two independent guides, four that Leonipedia describes without a second guide, and community-map pins no one else has backed. Examples include a watch in a VR gamer's house in Key Lento and the Crowex Octane "Kaleidoscope", in a Casanova Island mansion near Vice Beach.

The Takio Digital Sports Watch and Lucia's rose gold Ark Royal Ashore are still unlocated, along with about a dozen unnamed pins.

What does collecting them unlock?

Ten watches unlock the "Move Time Forward" cheat, twenty the "Pause Time of Day" cheat. Both work only after the main story and disable trophies and saving while active. Man of Many stresses that button codes are unconfirmed and Rockstar has published no official list of watches or cheats.

Which real watches inspired them?

The outlet matches the Crowex Octane "Kaleidoscope", a gold chronograph with a rainbow sapphire bezel, to the Rolex Cosmograph Daytona Rainbow, citing watch press such as Wrist Aficionado and Coronet. Another gold Crowex is compared to the Rolex Day-Date 40 with a black dial. These are journalists' comparisons, not Rockstar statements.

It fits the other collectible hunts we covered: Kenton's wrecks and treasures and the 40 Knife Flights. See also release.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for story progress, but there is a new collection hunt with a concrete reward: control of the clock. The exact count and codes remain unconfirmed.
