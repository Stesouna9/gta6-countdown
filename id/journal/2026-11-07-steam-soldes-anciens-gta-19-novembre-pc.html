GTA 6 launch week: older GTA games discounted on Steam until November 19

Steam discounts GTA IV Complete Edition to €6 and the Trilogy Definitive Edition to €19.79 until November 19, GTA 6's launch day, with no PC version announced.

Sumber big-n.fr · gamesradar.com

PC players will not get GTA 6 on November 19, but Steam offers a consolation prize. According to big-n.fr, the Trilogy Definitive Edition drops to €19.79 (-67%) and GTA IV Complete Edition to €6 (-70%). Both sales end on November 19, the console launch day.

Which Steam deals are live?

GamesRadar+ counts four games across two products: the Trilogy (GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas) and GTA IV Complete Edition with its two expansions. Per big-n.fr, citing Isthereanydeal, the Trilogy price is the lowest ever recorded and GTA IV is close to its own low.

GamesRadar+ adds that the Trilogy is also $19.79 on the Nintendo Switch eShop, but that deal was due to end about eleven hours after the writing of its piece. The Steam sale has a fixed end date, which GamesRadar+ reads as a promotion timed to the launch.

Why do these sales mostly concern PC players?

Big-n.fr sees no equivalent discount on Xbox or PlayStation stores, even though GTA 6 launches on those consoles. No PC version of GTA 6 is announced, as our piece on a former Rockstar technical director's predictions recalls. Whether Rockstar is deliberately targeting PC players with these prices, as big-n.fr suggests, is the outlet's reading: the studio has said nothing.

A quality warning: GamesRadar+ notes the Trilogy was heavily criticised at launch, and its GTA III version is still the lowest rated on Steam, with complaints about the Rockstar launcher, bugs and performance dips. Grove Street Games CEO Thomas Williamson said this year that the early criticism was deserved.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for GTA 6's release on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. For PC players it is a cheap way to catch up on the series while waiting for any announcement. More deals are on our price page.
