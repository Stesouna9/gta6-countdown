GTA 6 celebrity cameos: Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey and Bobby Moynihan

Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Morgan Wallen and Bobby Moynihan voice characters in GTA 6, according to Page Six's list.

Sumber pagesix.com

Who voices whom in GTA 6? Page Six lists the celebrities players have recognized since the 19 November release: Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Morgan Wallen, Bobby Moynihan, Denzel Curry, Freddie Gibbs and Janelle James among them. The site notes that the game stars Jason Duval (Dylan Rourke) and Lucia Caminos (Manni L. Perez).

Which celebrities appear in GTA 6?

According to Page Six, Bad Bunny voices two characters: El Martillo, host of Cocoteo FM, and Mitico Mario, a dancer; his songs "EoO" and "Otra Noche en Miami" play on the station. Lana Del Rey is Delta Dawne on Back Country Radio, with Morgan Wallen as co-host MW. Her unreleased "Crazy for You" is among the 34 tracks of the official album.

Bobby Moynihan, a former "Saturday Night Live" cast member, voices and motion-captures Cal Hampton, Jason's friend, a major role seen often. Denzel Curry plays DWNPLY, a rapper and sneaker-brand owner, and his song "FLA Flow" plays on Vice City Rap Radio. Taryn Manning ("Orange Is the New Black") plays Vicki at a club, her first video game voice credit.

What about the other roles?

Freddie Gibbs plays Freddie, hidden in a bunker, and hosts the podcast "The Safe Trap" with producer The Alchemist. Stephen Root plays Brian Heder, a drug smuggler and Jason's landlord, who lets him live rent-free in exchange for work. Janelle James is Tish, host of the talk show "She Got Assets"; she wrote on Instagram that she "pursued, conceptualized, pitched, scripted and voice acted" the role, joking that she "bullied" Rockstar into it.
