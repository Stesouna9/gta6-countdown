GTA 6: Justice and Gesaffelstein release “Pusher”, a seven-minute dark techno cut

Justice and Gesaffelstein have released “Pusher”, the second single from the GTA VI score, Tsugi reports. The score album is out December 11.

來源 tsugi.fr

J-21 GTA 6 還有 21 天發售 →

The two French acts had never made a track together. Tsugi confirms Justice and Gesaffelstein just released “Pusher”, the second single from the GTA VI score, after “Flower In A Hurricane” with SZA, Tame Impala and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard.

What does “Pusher” sound like?

It runs about seven minutes. According to Tsugi it opens with police sirens, engines and metallic noises, setting a car chase before the first bar. The magazine describes a dark mood, fat bass and dissonant strings, with neither artist overpowering the other.

We have not heard it in the game, only read the description: nothing says yet where the track plays.

A loop closes

Tsugi notes Justice was, in 2008, the first French duo with Jean-Michel Jarre and Kavinsky to join a radio in the series: “Waters of Nazareth” aired on Electro-Choc in GTA IV. Gesaffelstein appeared on Soulwax FM in GTA V in 2013 with his remix of The Hacker’s “Shockwave”.

Why it matters

Two singles, two worlds: the first sunny pop, this one nocturnal and brutal. The score looks less uniform than expected. It arrives December 11, 2026, three weeks after the game on November 19.
