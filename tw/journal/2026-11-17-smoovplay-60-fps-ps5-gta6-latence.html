GTA 6 on PS5: SmoovPlay app promises 60 fps, with about 20 ms extra latency

Notebookcheck says SmoovPlay doubles GTA 6 frames from the PS5 via a PC, at the cost of roughly 20 ms of delay.

圖片：Rockstar Games

Rockstar has confirmed GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on PS5 and Xbox at launch. Some players are already hunting for a workaround: according to Notebookcheck, a new free PC app, SmoovPlay, promises to lift the PS5 game from 30 to 60 fps, at the cost of extra delay.

How does SmoovPlay double the frames?

The tool builds on the Chiaki-ng Remote Play client. It streams the game from the console to a Windows PC, then AI frame generation analyses the 1080p video and inserts artificial frames between the originals, says Notebookcheck. You need a Windows PC, a stable connection and preferably an Nvidia 30, 40 or 50 series card. The developer's site shows side-by-side comparisons from GTA 6 trailers.

What is the catch?

Latency. The developer estimates frame generation adds about 20 ms at 60 fps, more at 90 fps, which can make driving feel sluggish and aiming less precise, Notebookcheck notes. An HDMI capture card would do better, but that route is still experimental. The tool resembles Lossless Scaling, aimed at console players, and the developer is using GTA 6 to promote it.

Neither Sony nor Rockstar has endorsed it, and nobody has tested the final game since it launches Thursday. On official smoothness, graphics modes on PS5 Pro remain an open question; our release page sums up what we know.

Why it matters

Nothing official changes: 30 fps at launch, no PC version announced. The app mostly shows how much PC players want a port. Judge it only after trying it on the real game.
