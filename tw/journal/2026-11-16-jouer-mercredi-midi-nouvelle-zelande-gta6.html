GTA 6: play from Wednesday noon with the New Zealand trick, explained

According to Xboxygen and Dexerto, switching region to New Zealand would let players start GTA 6 on Wednesday, November 18 at noon Paris time. Rockstar has confirmed nothing.

Play GTA 6 from Wednesday, November 18 at noon Paris time, nearly twelve hours before the French unlock? That is the trick going around today. French outlets Xboxygen and Dexerto describe the same workaround: switch to New Zealand, where the game opens first. Nothing is guaranteed, and none of it comes from Rockstar.

Why noon on Wednesday?

It is a time-zone offset. According to Dexerto, if GTA 6 unlocks at midnight in New Zealand, that equals Wednesday, November 18 at 12:00 French time (CEST). In France, the PlayStation Store lists an unlock at 00:00 on the night of Wednesday to Thursday, so November 19. See our release time country-by-country piece.

How does it work on Xbox and PS5?

On Xbox, you set your location to New Zealand under Settings, System, Language and region, then restart the console. Per Dexerto, this normally needs no new account and no repurchase.

On PS5 it is heavier: changing the console region is not enough. You need a PlayStation Network account whose country of residence is New Zealand, then buy the game on the New Zealand store. The same outlet warns that creating an account with a false residence "may breach PlayStation's terms of use", with a higher risk than on Xbox. Console sharing with your main account does not guarantee access before your local unlock either.

What are the limits?

French payment methods can be refused by a foreign store, and PlayStation gift cards are region-locked. Dexerto adds that the method only works if Rockstar does not set a single global time, and that an unlock system could block it. Rockstar has not publicly detailed each country's time, and the exact unlock time remains unknown.

Spoilers are another side effect: from November 18, New Zealand accounts will play first, and early scenes may surface on social media before the French release.

Why it matters

For most players, nothing changes: the game still launches November 19, preload open since the 12th. For those who try it, the gain is twelve hours, at the price of a risk to their PlayStation account and a possible block by the publisher. For pricing, see our price page.
