GTA 6 pre-order: Carrefour offers a €10 voucher from Nov 9

Carrefour promises a €10 voucher for any GTA 6 pre-order on carrefour.fr from Monday, November 9, excluding partner sellers.

來源 touteslesbox.fr

J-11 GTA 6 還有 11 天發售 →

Carrefour opens the second week of its Black Friday on Monday, November 9, called "previews", with GTA 6 as the headline item, reports Toutes les Box. A €10 voucher is promised for a pre-order on carrefour.fr, excluding partner sellers and the marketplace.

For official pricing, see our price page: €79.99 for the standard edition, €99.99 for Ultimate.

What exactly is Carrefour offering for GTA 6?

According to Toutes les Box, the retailer's Black Friday events fall every Monday: November 2, 9, 16 and 23. The 9th puts GTA VI up front on a dedicated hub. The outlet notes that Carrefour's November 2 press release gives no figure for the Nintendo console pack listed alongside, so it does not present it as a deal.

The PS5 standard edition listing is explicit: the box contains only a download code to start pre-loading from November 12, with no disc. A pre-order also includes the Vintage Vice City pack, and the PlayStation code only works on an account registered in a list of countries that includes France.

Is the product actually available?

Not always. Again per Toutes les Box, as of November 8 the listing says the product has left the shelves. On the hub, an offer appears from a third-party seller, TOV, not from Carrefour itself, and the €10 voucher does not apply to partner sellers. Check who sells and who ships before you pay.

Why it matters

For anyone yet to pre-order, that is €10 off a fixed-price game, provided you buy from Carrefour itself. The calendar point matters more: the box code only opens pre-loading from November 12, while the official digital pre-load starts November 10 at midnight local time. Boxed buyers therefore start two days later and must free up to 212 GB. Unlock stays set for November 19, at a time that is still unknown in France.
