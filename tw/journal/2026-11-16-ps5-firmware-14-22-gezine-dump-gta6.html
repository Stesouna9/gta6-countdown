GTA 6: hacker says he dumped a PS5 game on firmware 14.22

Per Notebookcheck, hacker Gezine claims to have dumped a game binary on PS5 firmware 14.22, required for GTA 6; it is not a full jailbreak.

來源 notebookcheck.net

A few days before launch, a post from a known name in the homebrew scene reopens the PS5 piracy debate. According to Notebookcheck, developer Gezine claims to have dumped a game binary on the latest update, firmware 14.22. Datamines indicate this version is required to boot GTA 6 on November 19.

What do we actually know?

Little, and Notebookcheck says so itself: it is unclear which title Gezine refers to or whether the method would work on every game, GTA 6 included. Dumping a game does not mean a full PS5 jailbreak on 14.22 is already possible. The outlet considers short-term exposure of Rockstar's game to piracy unlikely.

Why does Sony keep patching?

Also per Notebookcheck, Gezine had said dumping was no longer an option on versions 14.00 and up, hinting that a hypervisor bypass available only on older versions was needed. Version 13.60, distributed in July, is said to be the most recent one that does not block the Relapse kernel exploit and Byepervisor-Reborn, which combined allow homebrew apps and backups. Hackers face a dilemma: the game requires 14.22, which is more locked down. Each time developers publicized progress, PlayStation shipped an update, which is why Gezine is reluctant to upload findings to GitHub.

What about Take-Two?

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick told the Wall Street Journal that GTA 6's file size and online features partly mitigate the piracy threat, while the publisher has prepared technical and legal measures against any breakthrough, per Notebookcheck. On size, see our article on the game's 212 GB; on Zelnick's remarks, his statements to IGN. Schedule: release.

Why it matters

For players, nothing in the short term: a social media claim without public proof does not make GTA 6 pirateable. Worth watching after November 19: any release from Gezine or a Sony response with a new update.
