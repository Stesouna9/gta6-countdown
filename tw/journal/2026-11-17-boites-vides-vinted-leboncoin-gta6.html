GTA 6 empty boxes appear on Vinted and Leboncoin before launch

Frandroid found listings for packaging after the download code had been used. Asking prices do not establish completed sales.

Frandroid has found GTA 6 packaging listed on Vinted and Leboncoin after the included download code was used. The French outlet reports asking prices between €18 and €30 on Vinted. Those listings concern a physical object and its paper contents; they should not be read as offers of a playable copy at a steep discount.

Does an empty case include access to the game?

Frandroid describes retail boxes containing a redemption code rather than a disc. The code is used through the PlayStation Store or Xbox Store, and the resulting game entitlement stays with the account that redeemed it. Passing the case to somebody else does not transfer that entitlement. This is the crucial difference for buyers browsing photos that look like an ordinary second-hand game listing.

The outlet lists an official poster, a download instruction sheet and a leaflet promoting the Ultimate edition among the physical contents. These can have an appeal of their own. Frandroid spoke to a buyer who considered the package a collectible. That is a coherent reason to want it, provided the buyer understands that the object and the right to play are separate purchases.

What do the listing numbers tell us?

Frandroid counted 37 Vinted results and 62 Leboncoin results for its empty-box search. The figures describe what the outlet saw during its checks. They are not a lasting inventory, a count of unique buyers or proof that all those listings will find a customer. Search results can change as sellers add, remove or revise their offers.

Likewise, an asking price does not demonstrate a completed transaction. The reported €18 to €30 range should not be presented as a guaranteed resale value. Someone comparing editions can consult our GTA 6 price and edition guide to establish what a purchase intended for playing should include. A cheap-looking case is not enough information to make that comparison.

Why it matters

A few words in the description can change the entire transaction: empty box, poster only, code already redeemed. Frandroid says listings generally explain that the code has been used. Collectors may reasonably choose the remaining object, while players need to confirm that a usable game entitlement is actually included. There is also no basis here for promising that selling your own packaging will recover a particular part of the purchase price. The report documents sellers making offers, with a collector explaining the appeal; it does not establish a reliable return for everyone.
