GTA 6: mobile game spending drops 36.6% in France after launch, says AppsFlyer

AppsFlyer data cited by The Media Leader shows in-app spending on mobile games fell 36.6% in France in the two days after GTA 6 launched, while sessions fell only 3.7%.

來源 fr.themedialeader.com

GTA 6's November 19 launch also shows up in mobile players' wallets. According to an AppsFlyer study reported by The Media Leader, in-app purchase revenue in mobile games fell 36.6% in France in the two days after release, compared with the average of the two previous equivalent periods.

What does the study measure?

AppsFlyer, a mobile marketing analytics firm, looked at eight markets. France recorded the sharpest drop in spending. Sessions fell only 3.7% and installs 4.8%, so players kept opening mobile games but spent less in them.

Other categories moved too, per The Media Leader: food delivery app installs fell 8.9%, not the rise you might expect from people stuck on a console. In Action & Strategy, the genre closest to GTA on mobile, installs dropped 11.7%.

Is GTA 6 really the cause?

Adam Smart, director of product for gaming at AppsFlyer, offers a budget explanation: at 80 to 100 dollars, it is reasonable to think some players put money into GTA VI that would otherwise have gone to mobile games. He adds that the steeper fall in Casino games points to other factors, and that GTA VI is unlikely to be the only cause.

Caution is needed. The window is two days, the comparison periods are short and correlation is not a spending transfer. We have not seen the raw data, nor the other countries' figures.

Why it matters

The price link is the real story: at €79.99 standard and €99.99 Ultimate, GTA 6 weighs on a household's entertainment budget. On launch numbers, Sony separately announced more than 39 million PS5 players. If mobile spending recovers within weeks, the effect was a blip. If it stays low, mobile publishers have one more reason to watch the big-release calendar.
