GTA 6 armoured van heist: a former robber rates how realistic it is

JeuxVideo.com had ex-robber François Troukens review a GTA 6 van attack: the Heat nod is spot on, the logistics are not.

來源 jeuxvideo.com

Does a GTA 6 armoured-van robbery hold up against reality? JeuxVideo.com put the question to François Troukens, a Belgian former robber turned filmmaker, and the verdict is mixed: the atmosphere is right, the logistics are not.

Who is François Troukens?

According to JeuxVideo.com, he was born in Nivelles in 1969 and was a known figure of 1990s organised crime, with several van attacks on his record. Sentenced in absentia in 2003, he was arrested in Paris in 2004. In prison he followed a literature course at the Sorbonne, then became a screenwriter and director. He also founded Chrysalibre, an association working on rehabilitation through culture.

What rings true

He spots the Heat reference within seconds: the tracking shot and the motorway interchange recall Michael Mann's 1995 van attack. He finds a three-man crew credible, and hockey masks a good way to hide a face, per JeuxVideo.com.

What does not hold up

The first problem comes before the van even stops: no vehicle blocks its path. A motorbike in front would not do it, the van would simply drive over it. The front windows resist .44 rounds and conventional weapons of war; armour-piercing rounds exist but are very hard to get, so killing the driver to stop the van is not credible either.

Three motorbikes for an operation this size also puzzles him. The worst mistake, he says, is one robber wearing neither balaclava nor hood: a hockey mask only covers the front of the face, which makes identification easy on any video.

A game, not a manual

The piece arrives as GTA 6 sits at 99 on Metacritic. It is a reminder that Rockstar borrows from heist cinema, not from case files. See our article on the six-star wanted system, Heat and witnesses for how the game handles the aftermath.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game itself: this is an expert opinion, not an official statement. But the angle is useful, as heist realism becomes a talking point beyond sales figures. More in the news journal.
