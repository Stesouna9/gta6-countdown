GTA 6 cast: who plays Lucia, Jason, Cal and Mia Caminos

Première says Manni L. Perez plays Lucia and Dylan Rourke plays Jason, alongside Stephen Root, Bobby Moynihan, Taryn Manning and Michael Jai White.

來源 premiere.fr

Who gives GTA 6's characters their faces and voices? Première went through the game's official credits and lists the cast, with several faces familiar from TV and film.

Who plays Lucia and Jason?

Lucia Caminos is played by Manni L. Perez, seen in Law & Order: SVU, Blindspot, Chicago P.D. and Marvel's Jessica Jones, Première notes. Jason Duval is played by Dylan Rourke, less known to the general public, spotted in Grey's Anatomy (as Tyler Hemphill), Modern Family, Station 19 and 9-1-1: Lone Star. For their story, see our piece on the possible endings for Jason and Lucia.

Which cast members are best known?

For Première, the most famous is Stephen Root, made known by Office Space (the unforgettable Milton), then Barry, and the voice of Bill Dauterive in King of the Hill. The magazine also recalls his role this year in Widow's Bay, an Apple TV hit noticed at the Emmy Awards.

Bobby Moynihan, a Saturday Night Live alumnus and regular voice actor, plays Cal. Taryn Manning, known for Orange Is the New Black but also 8 Mile and Sons of Anarchy, is in the cast. Roselyn Sánchez (Devious Maids, FBI: Most Wanted, Rush Hour 2) plays Mia Caminos. Actor and martial artist Michael Jai White, seen in Spawn, The Dark Knight and Black Dynamite, is also credited.

One last name: a Miami Dolphins receiver, an NFL star, voices a fictional American football player. The passage of Première we could read does not give his name, so we do not write it.

Why it matters

These names show Rockstar chose established actors rather than headline stars. Nothing in Première's report changes the game itself, but it feeds the conversation: the community can now put a face to each voice heard in missions.
