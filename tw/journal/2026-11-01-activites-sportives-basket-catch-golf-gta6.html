GTA 6 sports activities: basketball, wrestling, golf and gym workouts

Yahoo Tech lists the sports activities GTA 6 could offer, from basketball to mini golf. Some are seen on screen, the rest awaits Rockstar's confirmation.

Basketball, wrestling, gym workouts, pool, mini golf: Yahoo Tech has compiled the sports and leisure activities GTA 6 could offer. The outlet relies on YouTuber TGG, who says he saw a demo at Rockstar North in July 2026. Some items come from official footage, others from reported observations.

Rockstar has not published an official list, so each item below carries its own level of certainty.

What has actually been seen?

According to Yahoo Tech, basketball is played on outdoor courts with shots you aim and time, and a made shot could improve the focus attribute. Extended-look footage reportedly shows Jason body-slammed in a pro-wrestling-style match, and Lucia practicing kickboxing. No organized fighting competition has been established.

The gym reportedly includes bench press, lat pulldowns, deadlifts, squats, treadmills and punching bags, which could affect health and stamina.

What remains unconfirmed?

Pool tables appear in footage, one reportedly at the Delight Strip Club, and a mini golf course was shown in Calhampton. A golf course was spotted through community mapping, but playable golf is unconfirmed. Tennis courts were reportedly seen, but no match has been shown.

Street racing would be the most varied activity, with several formats in the extended look. Yahoo Tech stresses that some activities rest on reported observations, not confirmed playable features. The July demo is only attested by the YouTuber himself.

This builds on the lifestyle systems covered in our article on Jason and Lucia after Edge. See also the release page.

Why it matters

Nothing is confirmed on the game side: these are hints, not announcements. But they sketch a Vice City where time can be spent without firing a shot, with effects on attributes. Which activities will be playable on November 19 is something Rockstar has not said.
