GTA 6 radio: eight stations confirmed, including Vice City FM and V-Rock FM

Rockstar has presented six GTA 6 stations on its official site; Xboxygen now counts eight, with Vice City FM returning and V-Rock FM joining.

來源 xboxygen.com · rockstargames.com

Eight radio stations, then. Xboxygen reports that Rockstar has officially presented six stations covering reggaeton, country, afro, metal, pop and Southern hip-hop. Two more had been spotted around Miami before being detailed, and the outlet updated its piece on October 20 to add Vice City FM and V-Rock FM.

The starting point is the page "The Music of Grand Theft Auto VI: In-Game Radio Stations" on Rockstar's Newswire. Previews of the first stations can also be heard in the Music section of the official game site.

Which radio stations are in GTA 6?

According to Xboxygen, the genres are reggaeton, country, afro, metal, pop and Southern hip-hop, plus Vice City FM and V-Rock FM. No track lists per station are given, and we will not guess: only the names and genres above are established.

Vice City FM is a name longtime fans know. Xboxygen calls it an emblematic station making a comeback; for Vice City fans the name alone brings back a city at night and radios on while driving.

A "dynamic" soundtrack is coming

Rockstar says more information is on the way, notably about the "dynamic" soundtrack. The studio has not explained the term. Xboxygen reads it as music that adapts to the situation, location or what Jason and Lucia are doing; that is an interpretation, not a confirmation.

We already reported that radio no longer stops at the car door: on-demand podcasts and headphones linked to the phone let players keep listening on foot. Our radio and music pages gather the rest.

Why it matters

The list is incomplete: eight stations are a first look, not the total. The music menu is taking shape before November 19, but we still do not know which artists will play on which station. Podcast content and how the dynamic soundtrack works remain unknown.
