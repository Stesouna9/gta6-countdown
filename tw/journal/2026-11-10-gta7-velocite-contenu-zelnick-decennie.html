Will GTA 7 come faster than GTA 6 ?

這個問題來自 IGN，它引用了Bloomberg一篇報道，其中Rockstar的開發負責人表示，該工作室的下一款遊戲不會需要八年時間，即GTA V和GTA 6之間的差距。 Zelnick回答很籠統：「我們將從包括GTA車手》在內的標誌性系列中看到的內容的性質和速度，在未來十年將呈現出巨大的不同」。

TweakTown reads this as the gap between two GTA games possibly shrinking, while noting Zelnick never named GTA 7 or a release window. "Velocity" could just as easily mean more frequent updates and online content as a faster sequel.

What Rockstar has already hinted

According to the outlet, Rockstar says the tools, engine and team built for GTA 6 should speed up development of the next game. Zelnick also ties the shift to high-quality entertainment, new ways of reaching players and keeping them engaged after launch.
