GTA 6 download time: which French ISP loads the 212 GB fastest?

Bouygues Telecom could download GTA 6 in 58 minutes in theory, SFR in 1 h 23, according to Journal du Geek: the gap stays under half an hour.

Which internet provider downloads GTA 6 fastest in France? According to Journal du Geek, Bouygues Telecom leads on paper: 212 GB in 58 minutes under ideal conditions, against 1 h 23 on SFR. The gap is under 30 minutes, and the box is rarely the weak link.

How long does 212 GB take on each French ISP?

The site relies on the yearly nPerf barometer, published in January from 2025 measurements. Average download speed: Bouygues Telecom 491 Mbit/s, Free 451, Orange 425, SFR 340. Journal du Geek works out theoretical times for 212 GB: 58 minutes on Bouygues, 1 h 03 on Free, 1 h 07 on Orange, 1 h 23 on SFR. These are national averages, not a promise for your home.

Preloading opened on November 10, as covered in our preload launch article. Unlocking stays set for November 19, at a time Rockstar has not announced.

Why switching provider is pointless

First, the console caps the speed. Per Journal du Geek, both PS5 and Xbox Series X have a gigabit Ethernet port limited to 1,000 Mbit/s, so 5 or 8 Gbit/s plans change nothing. Even on the best box, a PS5 would need about half an hour.

Second, Wi-Fi eats the speed. PS5 handles Wi-Fi 6 up to 1.2 Gbit/s, Xbox Series X stops at Wi-Fi 5, and a load-bearing wall or the 2.4 GHz band can ruin the maths. Finally, servers: millions of players will connect at once on opening night. Subscribing to a new plan makes no sense either, the installation would take longer than the wait for release.

What to do before November 19

An Ethernet cable between console and box remains the most effective move. Turning on automatic downloads in rest mode (PS5) or instant-on (Xbox) lets the console work overnight. Starting the preload this week avoids the crowd of latecomers on the evening of the 18th. Sizes vary by region: see the regional file breakdown. Rockstar has announced a launch patch, its size is unknown.

Why it matters

Nothing justifies rushing to a new provider: the theoretical gain between first and last is under 25 minutes. The real advice is the cable and planning ahead. For the calendar, the release page lists the confirmed facts.
