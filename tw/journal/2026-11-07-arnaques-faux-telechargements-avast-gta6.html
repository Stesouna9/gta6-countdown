GTA 6 scams ahead of November 19: Avast spotted 200 sites in 24 hours

Le Parisien warns of fake offers to play GTA 6 early: Avast found 200 game-related sites in 24 hours, and no official early access exists.

來源 leparisien.fr

J-12 GTA 6 還有 12 天發售 →

Twelve days before launch, scammers have found their bait. Le Parisien warns that "any downloaded file is info-stealing software", and says online offers to play GTA 6 early are multiplying ahead of the November 19 release.

The paper relies on a study published last month by cybercrime experts at Avast, the US antivirus company, when pre-orders opened. Within 24 hours the team spotted no fewer than 200 websites directly tied to the game. Le Parisien compares it to the Olympics: an event followed by millions draws fraudsters.

Can you play GTA 6 before November 19?

Not through any official channel. The confirmed facts: release on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, pre-load from November 12. According to Mashable there is no early access, as we covered in our piece on the review embargo. Any offer promising the game "in advance" deserves suspicion.

Why a file to download is a red flag

No PC version has been announced, as we explained in our GTA 6 on PC explainer. An "installer" or "crack" on an unknown site matches no official product. The same goes for supposed builds: Cyberleek's latest promises, which we followed in this article, remain unproven.

The Le Parisien text we could read is short and does not detail the techniques used, so we only report what it establishes: the Avast study, the 200 sites in 24 hours, and the warning about downloaded files.

Why it matters

Until November 19 the rule is simple: buy or pre-load through your console's store, at the prices on our price page. A link received by message, a "key" sold in advance or a file promising the full game has no reason to be opened. With the release close, these attempts should multiply.
