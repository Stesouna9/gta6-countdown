GTA 6 review copies: no Xbox copies and no codes, anonymous source claims

An anonymous source shared on X says Rockstar sent no Xbox review copies and no game codes to journalists. Not confirmed by Rockstar.

來源 x.com · x.com

No Xbox review copies, and no game codes for reviewers: that is what an anonymous source claims, shared at 6 p.m. by @GTAVI_Countdown on X (over 126,000 views) and then by @gtasix_. Rockstar has confirmed nothing, so treat this as unofficial.

What does the anonymous source say?

According to the post, there are "apparently" no Xbox review copies because Xbox's review process does not meet Rockstar's security expectations. Reviewers reportedly did not get traditional game codes. The second account adds that access is tied to specific PSN accounts and consoles. The first post is cut off after "Instead," so the rest of the distribution method is unknown.

Why would Rockstar be this strict?

The game launches on 19 November and the studio has already been through several leaks. A transferable code can circulate; access bound to an account and a console limits that risk. That is interpretation only: Rockstar has not explained it.

Why it matters

If true, the Xbox version would be reviewed later than the PS5 one, so Xbox players would get opinions after everyone else. Rockstar has neither confirmed nor denied; we will update. Prices and editions. The unlock time is still unknown.
