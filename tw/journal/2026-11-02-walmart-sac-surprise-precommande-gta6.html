GTA 6: Walmart reportedly adds a surprise bag to online pre-orders

Insider Gaming reports that GTA 6 pre-orders placed online at Walmart would ship with a "surprise and delight" bag; what is inside is unknown and nothing is confirmed.

來源 insider-gaming.com

GTA 6 pre-orders placed online at Walmart may come with a small extra. Insider Gaming reports that a "surprise and delight" bag would be tucked into orders at select US stores, with no word on what is inside. Neither Rockstar nor Walmart has confirmed it.

What do we know about the Walmart GTA 6 bag?

The claim comes from a social media post that Insider Gaming relays without having verified it. It describes a limited promotion between Rockstar Games and Walmart: each online pre-order would get a "surprise and delight bag". The outlet says only "select stores" are involved and that pick-ups would start on November 12.

The reporter has asked Walmart for details. He writes himself that the content is unknown: a poster, a badge, a keychain, anything is possible, nothing is stated. We stick to the facts: one bag, one date, one retailer, one original source.

Why November 12?

November 12 is the pre-load date, as Insider Gaming points out. The offer applies to in-store pick-ups, meaning a code in a box. The game still does not start before the November 19 release, and the unlock time remains unknown.

Pre-order extras keep piling up. We already covered the free month of GTA+ with digital pre-orders. Walmart does not operate in France, so this one targets US customers only.

Why it matters

For French players, nothing concrete. If it holds up, it shows Rockstar wants pick-up day to feel like an event well before launch. Follow our goodies page and the release page: we will update if Walmart answers.
