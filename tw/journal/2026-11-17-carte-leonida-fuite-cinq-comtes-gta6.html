GTA 6 map leak: Leonida's five counties and southern swamps

Two Leonida maps are circulating, said by Journal du Geek to come from the Vice City Collection; Rockstar has not confirmed them.

來源 journaldugeek.com

Two maps of Leonida are circulating hours before launch. According to Journal du Geek, they come from the double-sided poster in the Vice City Collection, the 399 euro box sold without the game. Rockstar has not confirmed anything, and their exact origin is not established.

What do the GTA 6 maps show?

One is a colourful geographic map with roads, islands and waterways; the other is an illustrated map split into five counties: Lummox in the north, Kelly in the west, Leonard in the east, Vice-Dale around Vice City and Mariana in the southern swamps. The ViceWire | Rockstar News account posted them on 13 November. They show toll roads, a large airport southwest of Vice City and an island chain in the south that recalls the Florida Keys, although the Leonida Keys do not appear.

The outlet finds the leak credible because the Leonida lettering and the sun at top left match the official image of the poster's back side released by Rockstar.

Is the map really twice the size of GTA 5's?

Journal du Geek relays "many sources" saying it covers close to 150 km², against 81 km² for GTA 5. Rockstar has not confirmed this: treat it as an estimate until the game is out.

Will there be a paper map in the box?

Rockstar traditionally included a paper map in every box. With preloading open since 12 November, the studio probably wanted to avoid spoilers, the outlet suggests. A free downloadable map is due at launch or shortly after, and a physical version would be sold on the site. On boxes, see our report on boxes reported without a code, and the goodies page for the collection.

Why it matters

If the maps are genuine, players already know where the five counties sit before taking a first step in Leonida. If not, the official map of 19 November will settle it. Either way, the map should not be treated as confirmed until Rockstar publishes it.
