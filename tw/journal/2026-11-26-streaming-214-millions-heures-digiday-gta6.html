GTA 6 streaming: 420 million hours watched in week one, per Digiday

Digiday, citing Streams Charts, says GTA 6 drew 420 million hours watched across major platforms in its first week, peaking at 7.91 million viewers.

來源 digiday.com

GTA 6 is crushing streaming too. According to Digiday, citing Streams Charts, the game logged 420 million hours watched across major platforms in its first week, with a peak of 7.91 million concurrent viewers.

What record did GTA 6 break?

Per Digiday, GTA 6 beat every other major game's full-week watch time in just two days: 214.6 million hours, against 150.48 million for Valorant over a whole week. Streams Charts' Nazar Babenko says a record "was never in doubt", as August's gameplay reveal already drew nearly 4 million concurrent viewers. Hours watched surprised him more than the peak.

What do the conversation numbers say?

Streams Charts also counts more than 3.2 million mentions in livestream chats across over 55,000 channels. Digiday says Twitch beat YouTube for live watch hours on launch day, possibly thanks to game giveaways announced at TwitchCon, which is the outlet's own speculation. On video, YouTube reports over 3.5 billion views of videos with GTA 6 in the title on release day; we covered it in this piece.

Were creators ready?

Yes. Night Media CEO Reed Duchscher says creator Andre "Typical Gamer" Rebelo (16.3 million YouTube followers) planned videos filmed away from his livestream, since most viewers will be busy beating the campaign. GTA 5 creators had been preparing for over a year.

These figures come from an analytics firm; Rockstar has published no streaming numbers. Another sign of scale: a million boxes sold in France.

Why it matters

For Rockstar it is free, continuous promotion, with the game visible every day. The numbers are a third party's and should be read as such. More on the journal.
