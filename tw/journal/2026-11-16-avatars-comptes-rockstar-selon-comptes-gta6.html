GTA 6 avatars reported for Rockstar accounts, with no PSN announcement

Several GTA community accounts report new Rockstar profile avatars. Their posts do not establish a PlayStation Network release.

圖片：Rockstar Games

來源 x.com · x.com

According to GTAVI_Countdown and gtasix_, GTA VI avatars have become available for Rockstar Games accounts. Both community news accounts posted the claim on Monday, November 16, within minutes of each other. Their reports agree on the service involved. We have not independently checked the selection inside a signed-in Rockstar account or reviewed an official announcement confirming its details.

Which profile can use the reported avatars?

The posts identify Rockstar Games accounts. They do not announce a release for every gaming profile a player might use. That distinction is particularly relevant to PlayStation owners: GTAVI_Countdown follows its report with a request for the images to come to PSN. A request from a fan account is evidence of interest, rather than confirmation that Sony has added them.

French accounts gta6hubfr and RockstarNewsFRA also reported the addition. Multiple matching reports make this a development worth flagging before a fuller announcement appears. They still leave questions unanswered. We cannot establish the complete selection, how many portraits are included, whether access has conditions, or how long the images will remain available from the material reviewed.

Vice City reaches another part of a player's identity

A profile picture is a modest way to mark the approaching launch. The reports do not describe an in-game item, a preorder reward or a new gameplay feature. They also provide no basis for treating a particular portrait as a clue about the story. Keeping those distinctions clear matters when even small account changes can spread quickly across fan feeds.

The broader promotional push has taken several forms this week. Our coverage of the NBA 2K27 collaboration examines a separate game partnership, while the Cactus Jack, Nike and Miami Heat collection concerns clothing. The reported avatars sit at the personal end of that spectrum: a change to how an existing account appears.

Why it matters

Players who already use Rockstar services can check their account's personalization options. The reports reviewed here do not mention a required purchase. They also do not alter console preparation or establish access to the game itself. Our GTA 6 release guide follows those separate questions. For now, the useful news is narrow: several community accounts report Rockstar profile avatars, while any equivalent PSN release remains unconfirmed. That is enough to investigate without promising a feature on a different platform.
