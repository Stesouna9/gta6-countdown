GTA 6: Miami Heat dress their court and arena as Vice City tonight

The Heat host the Bucks Wednesday at 7 p.m. Miami time, 1 a.m. Thursday in Paris, on a pink and blue GTA 6 court, according to GeeksLands.

Miami's Kaseya Center is changing its name for one night. According to GeeksLands, the Heat host the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday, November 18 at 7 p.m. Miami time, billed as "A Night in Vice City". Tip-off is 1 a.m. Thursday in Paris, so GTA 6 will already have been out for an hour in France, where it unlocks at local midnight.

What does the Vice City court look like?

The club showed a floor that keeps its light wood lines. The three-point arcs turn bright pink, the paint goes blue, a "VI" logo with palm trees sits on each half court and "Vice City" is written in cursive at center. Behind one baseline, a white "I ♥ Vice City" is painted. A giant sign welcomes fans from the roof.

What is planned inside the arena?

Cars from the game will be displayed on the west plaza. Inside, GTA 6 music, including tracks from its radio stations, will play all night, and Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell, the series' fast-food chains, will run some concession stands. Players wear a Nike x Cactus Jack jersey, Travis Scott's brand, with a "Vice City" version sold on site in limited quantities. We already covered the Cactus Jack x Heat collection: the Court Culture store lists a crop top at $45 and a satin jacket at $168. ESPN broadcasts the game in the US.

How does NBA 2K27 fit in?

The jersey will be playable in NBA 2K27. The game's Vice City Edition, sold digitally from November 27 to January 15, puts Heat center Bam Adebayo on the cover at a temporary console launch price of $39.99. No European price has been announced. GTA+ members can play the Standard edition of NBA 2K27 at no extra cost on PS5 and Xbox Series during the same period. Rockstar and 2K are both owned by Take-Two.

Why it matters

A regular-season game becomes one more showcase for GTA 6, after fashion, sneakers and drinks. Nothing changes for the game itself, but its launch day now reaches an NBA arena. More merchandise is on our goodies page.
