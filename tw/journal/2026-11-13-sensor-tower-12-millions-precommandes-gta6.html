GTA 6: 12.13 million digital pre-orders worth $1.18 billion, Sensor Tower says

Sensor Tower now estimates 12.13 million digital GTA 6 pre-orders worth $1.18 billion, up from 9.69 million on Tuesday, according to Clubic.

來源 clubic.com

Sensor Tower's tracker was updated on Friday, November 13: it now counts 12.13 million digital copies of GTA 6 pre-ordered, refunds deducted, for $1.18 billion in revenue. Clubic reports it six days before the November 19 launch.

How much did pre-orders grow in three days?

On Tuesday the tracker showed 9.69 million copies and $942 million, as we reported in our piece on the 10 million pre-orders. That is a gain of over 2.4 million copies and about $240 million. Clubic derives an average of roughly $79 million a day, almost three times the previous week.

The outlet attributes part of the acceleration to the preload opening on November 10. That is Clubic's reading; Sensor Tower does not explain it in the figures quoted.

PS5 or Xbox: who weighs what?

The split has not moved: PS5 accounts for 75% of sales, with 9.08 million copies and $880 million, against 3.06 million copies and $296 million on Xbox. The average basket is about $97 per copy, almost the price of the Ultimate edition, suggesting many buyers pick the priciest version. Prices are detailed on our price page.

Clubic adds that the game already clears Cyberpunk 2077's pre-order record by a wide margin. Worth remembering: these are estimates from an analytics firm, not data published by Rockstar or Take-Two, and they cover digital only, not boxes sold in stores.

Why it matters

These estimates feed analyst expectations, including Bank of America's $1.9 billion for the opening weekend. The real total will only be known when Take-Two reports its results.
