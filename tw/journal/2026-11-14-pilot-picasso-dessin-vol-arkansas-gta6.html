GTA 6 fan draws a tribute with an eight-hour flight over Arkansas

Pilot Benjamin McDaniel explains how a planned flight path became a GTA 6 drawing on November 5.

An eight-hour flight over Arkansas became an unusual tribute to GTA 6. Speaking to Rockstar Mag, pilot Benjamin McDaniel, who uses the name The Pilot Picasso, describes the artwork he made on November 5 after departing from Bentonville. The result was not written in smoke: the recorded route of his aircraft created the image when viewed as a flight track.

How can a flight become a drawing?

McDaniel tells Rockstar Mag that he planned the design in a flight application before taking off. The intended shapes had to become headings, distances and turns his aircraft could safely perform. In practical terms, the picture first needed to work as a route, with the final image emerging from the recorded movement.

The profile identifies his aircraft as a Cessna 182RG and places the flight over northwestern Arkansas. The reported eight hours refer to his account of the flying itself. They do not establish the full amount of preparation involved, nor do they provide a cost for the project. Neither figure should be guessed from the length of the route.

What did the GTA 6 tribute show?

Rockstar Mag describes a helicopter, a crocodile, a sports car, palm trees and a woman holding a gun, together with the Grand Theft Auto 6 name. These were elements assembled by a fan to evoke the game. They are not newly revealed game assets, and they should not be interpreted as clues about missions or characters.

The interview also explains how McDaniel developed the technique. Originally from the Birmingham area of Alabama, he discovered aviation in 2020, studied at Auburn and became an instructor after graduating in 2022, according to the profile. He started making flight-track drawings in 2023, initially working with simple shapes before attempting larger compositions.

Why it matters

The new interview adds a first-hand explanation of the work behind a community tribute. Rockstar Mag reports that the Instagram video attracted a comment from the Rockstar Games account. That reported response does not establish that the publisher commissioned or financed the flight, so the project remains a fan creation in our account.

With launch approaching on November 19, the story illustrates a form of participation built around someone's existing craft. Our community page follows the wider fan scene, while the news journal covers developments around the game. The official illustration accompanying this article shows aviation imagery from Leonida; it is not a photograph of McDaniel's flight or a reproduction of his route.
