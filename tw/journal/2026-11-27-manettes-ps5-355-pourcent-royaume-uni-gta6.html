GTA 6: PS5 controller sales jump 355% in the UK, GinjFo reports

NielsenIQ data cited by GinjFo shows PS5 controller sales up 355% in the UK between 15 and 21 November, GTA 6's launch week.

來源 ginjfo.com

GTA 6's launch lifted accessory sales in the UK, GinjFo reports, citing NielsenIQ data. Between 15 and 21 November 2026, PS5 controller sales by volume rose 355%, even though the game was only available for the last three days of that period.

What do the UK numbers show?

British specialist games retail reportedly generated an estimated 142 million pounds over the week, up 99% on the previous one. Gaming accessories account for 21 million pounds, up 123%. In detail, per GinjFo: controllers +194% by volume (all platforms), headsets +94%, batteries and charging stations +69%, gaming chairs +32%. External console SSDs rose 30%, their best week since the start of 2026.

What about console sales?

PlayStation 5 sales rose 46% and Xbox Series X and S 75%, according to the same data. That echoes the search surge seen in France, detailed in our Leboncoin article.

Mind the scope: GinjFo says the NielsenIQ data covers only the UK and excludes purchases made directly on platform digital stores, so it cannot be read as a global number. For the game's own sales, see Alinea's 53 million estimate, which remains an estimate.

Why controllers more than consoles?

GinjFo offers no figure-backed explanation. One hypothesis, to be taken as such: many players already own a console but not a spare or fresh controller for a long campaign. Nothing in the source confirms it.

Why it matters

The hardware market benefits from GTA 6, not only game sales. The NielsenIQ data is British and covers a single week, so later readings will show whether the rise holds. See our price page for deals around the game.
