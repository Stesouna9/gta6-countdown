GTA 6: PS5 listings at 300 euros on Vinted only sell a photo, France reports

With the PS5 out of stock after GTA 6's launch, French sellers on Vinted list a photograph of the console for 300 euros, 20 Minutes reports.

來源 20minutes.fr

PS5 consoles under 350 euros on Vinted look too good to be true, and they are. According to 20 Minutes, citing Actu.fr, sellers are exploiting the console's stock shortage since GTA 6 launched on November 19 by selling, for 300 euros, nothing more than a photograph of a PS5.

What exactly is being sold?

The wording sits inside the listing: "Attention, you are only buying a photograph of the PS5. No console is included or sold with this listing". 20 Minutes quotes an example spotted by TF1 Info. The seller covers themselves against disputes and bets that hurried buyers will not read to the end.

The listings are filed under the "consoles" category. Lawyer Elias Bourran, who specialises in deceptive commercial practices, calls the presentation "deliberately misleading" and a "staging meant to deceive the buyer", with criminal and civil action possible.

What do Vinted and the law say?

Vinted told Actu.fr that buyers have two days after delivery to confirm receipt or report a problem, with a refund possible if an item is not delivered, arrives damaged or differs significantly from its description. Consumer-law lawyer Grégory Rouland points to article 1165 of the French Civil Code: a judge can reduce an abusive price or cancel the sale, and fraud can be alleged.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for Rockstar, but the lesson is simple: a very low price on a console nobody can find deserves a full read of the listing. The source gives no figure on how many listings are involved.
