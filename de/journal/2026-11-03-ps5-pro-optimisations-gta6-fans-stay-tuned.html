GTA 6 on PS5 Pro: fans hope for optimizations, nothing official yet

Nothing is announced for GTA 6 on PS5 Pro, but some fans read a hint in the PlayStation Blog's "stay tuned" line, per Notebookcheck.

Foto: Rockstar Games

Quellen notebookcheck.net

J-16 GTA 6 erscheint in 16 Tagen →

The PlayStation Blog posted GTA 6's pre-load date and 212 GB size on November 3. The end of the post is what intrigues: a "Stay tuned for more" section promises further information before November 19. According to Notebookcheck, a fan account sees possible PS5 Pro optimizations there.

What is actually known about PS5 Pro?

Nothing official. Notebookcheck recalls that no performance details have been given for GTA 6 on PS5 Pro, leading many to assume it will match the base PS5 at 30 fps, which makes the upgrade look pointless to some.

Which theories are circulating?

The outlet cites three fan guesses: PSSR2, a performance mode reaching 40 or 60 fps, or 30 fps kept with a slightly higher resolution. None is confirmed by Rockstar or Sony, and the line could concern anything else.

A generic closing sentence is not proof; it is only a reason to watch the PlayStation Blog.

Auch lesen GTA 6 -Leak-Verbot: Berichten zufolge wurde das PlayStation-Konto wegen eines PS-App-Clips eingefroren

And storage?

The pre-load file reaches 212 GB depending on the device, opening November 10, see our pre-load article. Full dates are on the release page.

Why it matters

For now, nothing: anyone weighing a PS5 Pro for GTA 6 has no solid data. The safe move is to wait for an official announcement, which may come before November 19 or not at all.
