GTA 6: being a furry is illegal in Leonida, and hunters shoot on sight

In GTA 6, Leonida's government has declared war on furries: a TV spot, a news story and a random encounter, according to Numerama.

Quellen numerama.com

It is the find making the rounds among early players tonight: in GTA 6, being a furry is strictly illegal. Numerama reports that Rockstar built a whole parody campaign around the idea, from TV to street events.

What does Leonida TV say about furries?

According to Numerama, switching on the TV in their hideout, players can catch a spot paid for by a mysterious "Anti-Furry Association". In the tone of a paranoid public safety campaign, the video says any "furry activity" is illegal in Leonida and warns tourists that "hunting season is open". It asks residents to report suspicious behaviour, such as an "animal" saying "uwu", "rawr xD" or "hewwo". The final card lets licensed hunters shoot any furry on sight.

Are furries part of the gameplay too?

Yes, again per Numerama. On the heroes' phone, an article from the Vice City Horn, the local daily, reads: "Governor declares Leonida furry-free". The writers also invented an underground group, the Furverts, forced to operate in the shadows. Leonida TV also airs a cartoon starring an anthropomorphic alligator, Macca the Gator.

The outlet also describes a dynamic event: round a corner, Jason or Lucia can meet someone in a full costume chased by the police, and either ignore the scene or help the fugitive lose the cops. That is the reactive open world logic Rockstar already worked on in Red Dead Redemption 2, Numerama notes.

Satire is nothing new for Rockstar, but this entry changed tone elsewhere: we already noted that the game removes prostitutes, a choice Numerama itself cites. Numerama relies here on a player's video shared on X.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for progression: it is set dressing and dark humour, but it says a lot about the angle Rockstar chose for Leonida, a fictional Florida that mocks American moral panics. We now wait to see whether other outlets confirm the details of the encounters and what players do with them. For everything we know about the game, our release page is up to date.
