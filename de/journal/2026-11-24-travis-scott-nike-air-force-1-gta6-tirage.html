GTA 6: Travis Scott x Rockstar Nike Air Force 1 raffle is now open

Travis Scott opened a raffle on his site for his three GTA 6 Nike Air Force 1 Lows, including a multicolor pair limited to 600, per Sole Retriever.

Quellen soleretriever.com

Missing the sneaker drop does not mean losing out. According to Sole Retriever, Travis Scott's website opened a raffle on Tuesday, November 24 at 5:00 PM EST for all three pairs of his Rockstar Games x Nike Air Force 1 Low GTA 6 pack.

Which pairs are in the raffle?

Three colorways: an all-white patent leather pair, a triple-black pair with debossed map layouts, and a multicolor "friends and family" version, the rarest, limited to 600 pairs and sold only on the rapper's site. The pairs first showed up on launch day, November 19, alongside apparel, and stayed labeled "Soon" until now.

How do you enter?

You play a mini-game and collect six pairs of AF1s, then tick a box to enter for all three models. As with past raffles from Scott, the window lasts a few hours at most, per Sole Retriever, which says it is tracking the event live in its app. The source gives no price, so we do not state one.

Why it matters

The launch is spilling into real life in Miami: a Heat game, a music festival and a pop-up shop, the outlet notes. For merchandise, this raffle adds to what we track on the goodies page, with one difference: luck, not speed, decides. For the game itself, see release.
