GTA 6 empathy: Rockstar says Jason and Lucia start as small-time crooks

Rockstar's Rob Nelson told The Guardian GTA 6 encourages empathy for the first time, and that Jason and Lucia begin with petty crimes.

Quellen insider-gaming.com · insider-gaming.com

Will GTA 6 let you play the good guy? Partly, yes: Rockstar's Rob Nelson told The Guardian the game offers, "for the first time in a GTA", mechanisms that encourage empathy, according to Insider Gaming. The same interview says Jason and Lucia start out with small-time crimes, per Insider Gaming.

What does Rockstar say about empathy in GTA 6?

Asked about empathy, Nelson answered: "I would say that it's a product of the story we're trying to tell. We've tried for the first time in a GTA to provide mechanisms that encourage it, for those who want to." He added that crime remains a core pillar, that Rockstar does not want to punish players used to the formula, and that actions "will have some effect on how the game plays out".

Insider Gaming points to earlier cases: GTA V's random encounters, where you could help a victim or rob the robber, and Red Dead Redemption 2's honor system, which changed the ending. Rockstar has not detailed how these mechanisms work in GTA 6, so whether they reach the ending is unknown.

How do Jason and Lucia grow?

According to Insider Gaming, quoting the Guardian, the pair "can't just steal any car or bust their way into a bank" at the start. They begin by hotwiring cheap vehicles, picking basic locks and robbing convenience stores and gas stations before aiming bigger. The outlet ties this to what is already known: cars with alarms, trackers and immobilizers, shopkeepers who fight back, bystanders who film and leave a digital footprint.

Why it matters

Five days before the November 19 launch, Rockstar stresses progression and a world that reacts, not pure chaos. These are interview statements, not a demo, so the mechanics will be judged in play. See our release page and the journal for the latest previews.
