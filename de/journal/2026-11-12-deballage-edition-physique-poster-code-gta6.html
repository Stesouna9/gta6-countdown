GTA 6 physical edition unboxing: no disc, an A3 poster and a download code

First unboxing videos of GTA 6's physical edition show a box with an A3 poster and a single-use code, and no disc, on both PS5 and Xbox.

What is actually inside the GTA 6 box? No disc: an A3 double-sided poster and a download code, according to the first unboxing videos described by La Crème Du Gaming, including a widely shared PS5 one from @PythonSelkan.

What does the box contain?

Per La Crème Du Gaming, the shrink-wrapped PS5 box lists a single-player game, an online mode to come and a size of about 212 GB on the back. Inside sits a folded A3 poster: cover artwork on one side, a Vice City-style illustration on the other. The code, and the digital bonuses for Ultimate, are printed on it. The plastic tray meant for a Blu-ray is there but empty, on PS5 and Xbox alike.

The site sums up the Standard edition as three items: a box, a poster, a single code. No manual, nothing else. Ultimate looks identical; only the code differs.

Is the code region-locked?

On PS5, the same outlet says the code is tied to the PSN account's region, so it must be redeemed on an account matching the purchase zone. Once used it is spent, and the box becomes mostly a collector's item that is hard to resell.

What about the Leonida map?

No paper in the box. According to the same source, the map exists as a free PDF and a paid poster at $0.99 on the Rockstar Store. Upgrading from Standard to Ultimate would cost about $20 in the digital store. These figures come from the outlet, not from a statement cited here.

None of this is a complete surprise: we already covered the poster and the digital map and the box photographed in Germany. What is new is that videos confirm the contents on a real copy. Editions and prices are on the price page.

Why it matters

The physical edition does not spare you the 212 GB download, and preloading is already open. Buying the box means paying for a cover and a poster; players who want the same in-game experience can stay digital.
