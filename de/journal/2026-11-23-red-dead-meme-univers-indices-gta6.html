GTA 6 and Red Dead Redemption: same universe? Clues found in Leonida

Arthur Morgan's hat and a novel about Flaco Hernandez link GTA 6 to Red Dead Redemption 2, but Rockstar has confirmed nothing, Journal du Geek reports.

Quellen journaldugeek.com

Do GTA 6 and Red Dead Redemption share a universe? According to Journal du Geek, four days after launch players are finding clues that go beyond Rockstar's usual winks. The studio has confirmed nothing.

What clues existed before release?

The debate is old. In GTA 5, a book titled "Red Dead" by J. Marston sat in Franklin's house in Vinewood Hills, Journal du Geek recalls, and GTA Online hid a photo of the Reininger family. For GTA 6, the outlet cites the second trailer, where Leonida banknotes carry portraits of Thaddeus Waxman and Franklin Hardin, two presidents from Red Dead Redemption 2 cigarette cards. A M16 held by Jason bears the logo of Duke, a Red Dead weapons maker, and this summer's Extended Look used an RDR2 setting north of Annesburg.

What has been found since launch?

The best known is Arthur Morgan's hat, sold at a yard sale on Orchard Avenue, south of Port Gellhorn. In a western mini-golf course, a small dog also carries a cowboy doll that is Arthur Morgan. The strongest, per Journal du Geek, is a western novel readable early in the adventure. It tells of outlaw Flaco Hernandez found "dead in the snow" at Cairn Lake, where Arthur kills him in an RDR2 side quest. The book also names Blackwater, New Austin, Armadillo, Ambarino, Chuparosa and Diez Coronas.

A shared universe, really?

Officially nothing is settled and Rockstar has never confirmed a shared universe. Journal du Geek points to one obstacle: Red Dead mentions real cities like New York and San Francisco, while GTA uses fictional versions, Liberty City and San Andreas. The outlet still leans yes, since a hat at a yard sale is an easter egg while a novel recounting a specific event is lore. That is a reading, not an established fact.

Why it matters

Nothing in the game itself: these references block no mission. They feed the theory of a single Rockstar world, from the Wild West to Florida. While the studio stays silent, these are accumulating clues, not confirmation. If you find more, our community page is the place.
