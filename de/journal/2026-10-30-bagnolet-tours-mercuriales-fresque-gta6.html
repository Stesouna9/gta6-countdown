GTA 6: giant GTA VI mural on Bagnolet's Mercuriales towers is finished

The GTA VI banner covering both Mercuriales towers at Porte de Bagnolet near Paris is complete, 20 days before the November 19 launch, Le Parisien reports.

Quellen leparisien.fr

The mural is done. Le Parisien reports that the giant GTA VI banner has finished covering the Mercuriales towers at Porte de Bagnolet, just outside Paris, twenty days before the game launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

What does the Bagnolet mural show?

According to the paper, the game's cover art wraps one tower, while Jason and Lucia each take a face of the other. Six faces are covered in total. Each tower stands 114 metres tall, antenna excluded, and the banner can be seen from the Paris ring road.

Rope-access workers installed it strip by strip from the start of October.

Is it allowed?

The mayor's office of Bagnolet told the press the permit "was issued in line with the local intercommunal advertising regulation". Le Parisien gives no detail on how long the banner will stay up.

A launch campaign that is everywhere

It adds to the Miami highway signs and the Miami-Dade marketing deal we covered earlier. The paper recalls the timeline: release on Thursday November 19, third and last trailer on October 15, after an extended preview on Netflix at the end of August. Our release page has the full schedule.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for the game itself. But a 114-metre banner above the ring road shows how much visibility is being bought in French public space three weeks before launch.
