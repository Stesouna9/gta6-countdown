GTA 6: launch trailer reportedly 1440p native, without Sony’s PSSR upscaler

According to Génération NT, analysis of the GTA 6 launch trailer shows 1440p stretched to 4K, with no PSSR, the PS5’s AI upscaler.

The GTA 6 launch trailer runs at native 1440p and does not use PSSR, Sony’s AI-based upscaling technology. That is the reading Génération NT gives of technical analyses of the 90-second footage, which also confirms the November 19 release.

What does the video analysis show?

Per Génération NT, the image starts from a 1440p base and is then stretched to 4K without the PSSR that Sony promotes on its consoles. The site offers an explanation: running the algorithm would cost about 4 milliseconds out of a 33 millisecond frame budget, a heavy price for a marginal gain. That time would be better spent on physics and scenery detail, in this logic. It is the outlet’s interpretation, not a Rockstar statement.

Can we conclude anything about the game?

Carefully. A trailer is an encoded video and may be rendered differently from the final game. Rockstar has said nothing about GTA 6’s internal resolution on PS5, PS5 Pro or Xbox Series X|S, nor about PSSR. We do not know whether the final version will use it, or at what frame rate. On the same trailer, Génération NT notes that the animation stutter criticised in earlier videos seems gone, which we already covered with Digital Foundry.

A trailer that also talks music

The same article goes back to the soundtrack: French duo Justice composes the original score, and the launch list has 34 tracks, as we detailed in our piece on the soundtrack.

Why it matters

For anyone undecided on a console, nothing decisive: the gap between PS5 and PS5 Pro has to be measured on the game itself, after launch. Our console guide keeps the confirmed facts, and the release page the dates. Still unknown: the real resolution, the target frame rate and PSSR’s role.
