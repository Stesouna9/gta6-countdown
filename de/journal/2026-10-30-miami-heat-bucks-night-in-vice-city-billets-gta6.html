GTA 6: Miami Heat play as Vice City on November 18, tickets on sale

The Miami Heat host the Bucks on November 18 at 7pm ET, the night before GTA 6 launches, on a Vice City court and in Vice City uniforms; tickets start at $119.

Tickets for "A Night in Vice City" are on sale: according to Resell Calendar, the Miami Heat host the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday, November 18 at 7pm ET at Kaseya Center, the night before GTA 6 launches. The team plays on a Vice City themed court in a Vice City uniform.

What time is the game in Europe?

Tip-off is 7pm in Miami, which is 1am in mainland France overnight from November 18 to 19, DL Gaming calculates. The game lands a few hours before the November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. On broadcast, Resell Calendar reports national coverage on ESPN, while DL Gaming says Disney+ for French viewers. The two outlets differ, so check the official schedule closer to the date.

What changes on the court?

Per Resell Calendar, the club announces "The Vice City Court", a hardwood floor used for this one night only, plus a new Vice City uniform. Uniform details are not out: no word on a fan version, quantity or price. DL Gaming adds that food stands at Kaseya Center will carry the names Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell, two fictional chains from the series.

How much are tickets?

According to Resell Calendar, the cheapest Ticketmaster seats start at $119 before fees, and those are standing room or obstructed view. The resale-focused outlet points to two draws: Giannis Antetokounmpo's first home game against his former team (traded to Miami in June, it says) and the GTA 6 tie-in. It expects a busy resale market; that is its own projection, not sales data.

The Heat store already carries a 14-piece Vice City "Court Culture" collection, $45 to $110, shipping in five to six weeks per Resell Calendar. The game-worn uniform is not part of it.

Why it matters

Rockstar's promotion reaches well beyond screens: we already covered the $3.9 million Miami-Dade marketing deal and the highway signs in the city. This game is presented as the most visible event of that campaign. It says nothing about the game's unlock time, still unknown; for firm dates see the release page.
