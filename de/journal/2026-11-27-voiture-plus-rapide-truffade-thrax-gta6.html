GTA 6: Andrew Node's Truffade Thrax is reportedly the fastest car

The Truffade Thrax, Andrew Node's special version, rates 9.2 out of 10 for speed in fan databases and is obtained in chapter 3 via Randy Kozlov's list, according to La Crème du Gaming.

Quellen lacremedugaming.fr

What is the fastest car in GTA 6? According to La Crème du Gaming, it is the Truffade Thrax, in its special "Andrew Node's Thrax" version.

How is speed measured in GTA 6?

The outlet notes that Rockstar shows no km/h, only two ratings out of 10, Speed and Acceleration, through WAiNK and vehicle sheets. Figures in km/h, like the 276 km/h quoted for the Thrax, come from timed tests by specialist sites: community estimates, not official numbers.

The ranking

On the WAiNK sheet, the Truffade Thrax shows 8.7 for Speed, ahead of the Progen Emerus (8.3) and the Pegassi Vitello (8.2). Its sheet lists 8.6 acceleration, 7.5 road handling, 3.8 off-road and 3.0 durability: a road hypercar, fearsome in a straight line, much less comfortable once the terrain degrades. The Andrew Node version rises to 9.2 for Speed according to databases like gta.guide, which read internal stats, with no tuning.

How do you get it?

Reach chapter 3 and finish the tenth mission, a driver job that introduces Randy Kozlov. He hands over a key cloner and unlocks his "Randy's Wanted List": 20 cars to steal. Once it is complete, Randy says he has located the Thrax at the Dalton Island Club, near the Spa Splendido, reachable by ferry from Waxman Island. It has an alarm but no tracker, and two security guards. Registration at Moses costs $70,000, the first one being free, again per La Crème du Gaming.

Why it matters

Nothing official: the ranking rests on ratings read by fan sites, with gaps between sources (8.7 versus 9.2). For chapter 3 players, it is mainly a clear target for the next getaway.
