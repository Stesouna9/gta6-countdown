GTA 6: players hate using ATMs, according to Kotaku

Per Kotaku, depositing or withdrawing money at a GTA 6 ATM means scrolling amounts with the shoulder buttons, and many players want to type a number instead.

Quellen kotaku.com

The biggest gripe of the first days is neither a bug nor a mission. According to Kotaku, it is ATMs: withdrawing or depositing an exact amount in GTA 6 takes forever, and complaints keep piling up on Reddit.

Why are ATMs a problem?

Money matters in the game, writes Kotaku. Much of the adventure has you counting dollars, and banking your cash avoids losing a good part of it when you die or get arrested. But there is no field to type a number: you hold the controller's shoulder buttons to scroll the amount, and the scroll speeds up fast. The writer describes overshooting, then scrolling back slowly and overshooting again.

What do players say?

The outlet quotes several posts. A friend playing with him asks why he can't "just type in a number." On Reddit, one player counts "10, 20, 30, ... 300, 400 ... 10,000, 20,000" to withdraw $500, another says they spent two minutes trying to take out exactly $900. A third regrets that Rockstar kept Red Dead Redemption 2's system and would prefer GTA V's.

What did GTA V do?

In GTA V and GTA Online, preset amounts like $100 or $1,000 make banking easier. Not perfect, notes Kotaku, but much simpler. The writer is baffled that a game recreating so many everyday details, from sweat-damp shirts to realistic reflections, invents such a painful way to use an ATM.

Why it matters

Nothing official: Rockstar has not reacted in the sources we read, and no patch is announced. But this kind of ergonomic detail is often fixed after launch in games of this scale.
