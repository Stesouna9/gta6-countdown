GTA 6 publisher Take-Two rules out a sale after the EA buyout

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick tells Bloomberg the GTA 6 publisher will stay independent, after Electronic Arts' 55 billion dollar takeover.

Quellen worldissmall.fr

Take-Two is not looking to be bought. World Is Small reports that Strauss Zelnick, CEO of Rockstar's parent company, told David Rubenstein in a Bloomberg interview, days before GTA 6 launches on 19 November 2026. "I think we have proven we can do very well as an independent company," he said.

Why the question comes up now

The context, per the same outlet: Electronic Arts has come under the control of a consortium built around Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, Silver Lake and Affinity Partners, for 55 billion dollars. The deal closed on 4 August 2026, EA left the stock market and Andrew Wilson stayed at the helm. Asked about a similar scenario for Take-Two, Zelnick sees no strategic benefit.

The size argument

He explains that scale matters in big-budget games: a company under one billion dollars in yearly revenue can struggle to fund top creative teams, distribute worldwide or absorb a failed release. Beyond that threshold, as World Is Small reports his view, extra gains become less obvious. Take-Two therefore believes it is big enough, with solid finances and a catalogue of franchises.

For fiscal 2027, ending 31 March 2027, the publisher expects between 8 and 8.2 billion dollars in bookings, per the outlet. We could not read the original interview: the quotes come through that article.

A message timed with the launch

Zelnick is speaking often before launch. We already covered his remarks on physical games compared to vinyl. Here the message is aimed at investors: GTA 6 is framed as proof that independence is a tenable choice.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for players in the short term: the release stays set for 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The statement mostly says Take-Two plans to fund and publish its next projects alone. An executive's remark is not a guarantee: the market will judge on results in the coming months.
