GTA 6: players hunt real Miami locations inside Vice City, Polygon says

Polygon reports that players are spotting real Miami places in Vice City, from the Miami Marine Stadium to the Clevelander, and comparing them with real Florida.

출처 polygon.com

Two days after launch, some players have put the heists aside to look for Miami inside Vice City. Polygon describes the hunt for real-world spots as an old open-world habit, taken further than ever by Rockstar's game.

Which real Miami places show up in GTA 6?

Polygon names well-known landmarks such as the Adrienne Arsht Center and the E11EVEN nightclub, but also buildings nobody would notice. One viral clip shows Jason stopping on the patio of a South Beach apartment complex, then its creator walking out of their own door onto the same patio: "My apartment is in a video game," they wrote, per Polygon.

On the sports side, the game includes the Kaseya Center, home of the Miami Heat, and, in what the outlet calls a pixel-perfect copy, the Miami Marine Stadium. Built in the early 1960s as a regatta venue, it hosted concerts and rallies until Hurricane Andrew shut it down in 1992. Polygon adds that Miami voters passed a referendum in August that opens the way to a possible restoration.

Is the Clevelander really in the game?

Yes. The Polygon writer thought they glimpsed it for half a second in the second trailer in May 2025. After playing, they confirm the South Beach bar, flamingo pink included, is in the game. The bar had not answered their request for comment.

함께 읽기 Polygon에 따르면 GTA 6 : 트럼프와 베르사유 법안에 대한 고개를 끄덕이는 작품

Most spots players point out are in the Miami area, yet Vice City covers only a small part of Leonida's map. Polygon notes one glaring omission: Disney World. We covered that absence and a nod to a Trump Florida moment earlier.

Why it matters

Nothing changes for gameplay, but it stretches the life of the open world: every street corner becomes something to compare with reality. Our release page gathers what is known about the launch.
