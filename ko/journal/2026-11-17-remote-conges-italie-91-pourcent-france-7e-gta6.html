GTA 6 launch day leave: Italy up 91%, France ranks seventh

Remote says leave requests for November 19 jump 91% in Italy and 35% in France, seventh worldwide.

Offices will be emptier than usual on November 19. According to a study by HR platform Remote, reported by Le Parisien, leave requests for GTA 6 launch day are up 91% in Italy compared with the same day last year. France ranks seventh, at +35%.

Which country booked the most leave for GTA 6?

Italy leads the world ranking, ahead of India (+60%) and Ireland (+57%). Spain (+44%) and the United Kingdom (+42%) complete the top five, per Remote data cited by Le Parisien. Worldwide, leave booked for November 19 is up 32%, the biggest rise of 2026 for a working day outside school holidays.

A long weekend muddies the picture

The game launches on a Thursday, so part of the jump comes from the lure of a four-day weekend: the study says nearly one request in three covers both Thursday and Friday. Remote summed it up on social media by listing the four stages of a leave request: "family emergency", "personal appointment", "digital detox weekend", then GTA VI.

It is not an isolated story. We already covered the estimates of 1.5 million absentees and a billion dollars of lost productivity, and the workplace absence figures published before launch. Economists remain split: some expect the missed hours to be made up later in the year.
