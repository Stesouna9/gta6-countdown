GTA 6 TMNT Easter egg: where VICE places the turtles and green ooze

VICE describes a sewer reference in East Lummox County, best visited after 10 PM in game, plus a green ooze landmark to scan.

사진: Rockstar Games

출처 vice.com

Pizza boxes do much of the storytelling in one reported GTA 6 sewer discovery. According to VICE, four turtles and a rat gather underground in East Lummox County, creating a Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles reference. The outlet also describes green ooze that can be scanned with the in-game phone. Location and encounter details follow, so players hoping to discover the scene unaided should stop here.

Where should players look?

VICE places the sewer south of Lake Tallaloko and below the M7 Weed Grow Illegal Operation. Its recommended visit time is after 10 PM on the game clock. The guide says the sewer can be visited at other times, so that recommendation should not be mistaken for a claim that the entire area is inaccessible during the day.

The route described involves crossing a roadside guardrail and heading down an embankment. Look for a sewer opening with green water flowing out. The four turtles and rat reportedly gather on the east side of that opening, beside two empty pizza boxes. The official artwork accompanying this article illustrates Leonida generally; it does not depict the sewer entrance.

What can be scanned?

Approach carefully if you want to watch the group, VICE advises, because the rat can become startled and run away. A crack above the animals reportedly leaks green ooze. The outlet says using the Waink Me phone app to scan it registers the Mysterious Ooze landmark. This refers to the phone inside GTA 6, rather than an external application to install.

Turtles, a rat, pizza and green mutagen imagery make a recognisable combination. They do not establish a licensed mission, playable Ninja Turtles or an announced commercial partnership. What the source describes is an environmental reference, and keeping that distinction clear avoids turning a small discovery into a larger claim.

Why it matters

There are two separate reasons to visit: seeing the scene and recording the landmark mentioned by the guide. VICE does not promise a permanent ability upgrade or equipment reward here. Players looking for progression bonuses should therefore distinguish this encounter from hidden activities that explicitly improve a protagonist’s statistics.

Our community page offers a place to compare discoveries while keeping speculation visible. For this one, the useful advice is modest: arrive at the suggested in-game hour, avoid rushing the animals and look above them for the scan target. Anything beyond that would add a reward or condition the source has not established.
