GTA 6 PC ports: the Xbox GDK leak could help, Notebookcheck says

Leaked confidential Xbox GDK files could help GTA 6 PC port projects, mainly with shaders and ray tracing, according to Notebookcheck.

출처 notebookcheck.net

A second leak could help teams trying to run GTA 6 on PC: Microsoft's Xbox GDK. Notebookcheck says the files reveal confidential information about developing for Xbox Series X and S, which could help port projects built on the Xbox build Cyberleek released.

What is the leaked Xbox GDK?

The GDK is Microsoft's development toolkit. The part covering Windows games is public. Console-specific documentation and tools, however, go only to developers who sign an NDA. According to Notebookcheck, it is that confidential material that leaked.

How could it help a GTA 6 PC port?

The site names two areas. First, shader compilation: on Series X and S, games use programs written for RDNA 2 hardware, while PCs carry a wide range of Nvidia, AMD and Intel GPUs. So far, people adapting the build had to guess how to generate those shaders; the hope is a far more automated process. Second, ray tracing, which GTA 6 showcases even on the Series S: each graphics card handles it differently, and the GDK could keep it from wrecking performance on Windows.

Notebookcheck stays measured. Teams will still face fundamental console-versus-PC differences, memory included: Xbox uses unified memory while a PC splits CPU and GPU RAM, so projects must reproduce how Series X and S allocate resources.

Where do the ports stand?

Not at a playable game. We have already relayed the BBC look at the AI race to clone the game and Project Leonida carrying on despite takedowns. Per Notebookcheck, Take-Two is trying to slow these efforts; GitHub complied with DMCA requests, but similar projects survived. Neither Rockstar nor Microsoft comments in the piece.

Why it matters

The site says the leak "could" speed things up, not that it does: no project has announced GDK-related progress. Officially nothing moves, and our release page still lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
