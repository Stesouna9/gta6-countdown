GTA 6 preview describes 62 bird species and robbery preparation

A VICE report on The Guardian’s preview describes birdwatching, robbery preparation and relationship activities.

GTA 6 includes 62 kinds of birds to observe, according to VICE, which summarises The Guardian's visit to Rockstar North. The preview describes Jason using binoculars to identify a gull on a beach and adding it to a wildlife logbook. Beyond the opening crimes and empathy mechanics already covered, this is a more specific look at what players can do while spending time in Leonida.

What does birdwatching involve?

VICE describes a demonstration in which binoculars allowed Jason to identify the bird and record the discovery. The figure of 62 refers to birds, rather than the game's entire animal population. The report does not provide a complete species list, explain every observation requirement or identify rewards for finishing the logbook.

Red Dead Redemption 2 offers an obvious point of comparison, but a familiar idea does not establish identical rules. The supported details are narrower: binocular identification, a logbook and a stated number of birds. For players who enjoy exploring between missions, that is a concrete activity to look forward to without assuming a whole completion system that has not been described here.

How much can players prepare for robberies?

The same VICE report describes scouting a location for a rear entrance or a safe code. Players can reportedly disable security cameras or take their footage. These are options discussed in the preview, rather than evidence that every shop will provide exactly the same set of approaches.

Preparation also appears compatible with an operation going wrong. In the jewellery store robbery shown to The Guardian, a guard opened fire on Jason and Lucia, leading to a chase involving police vehicles and helicopters. That example matters because a plan and an outcome are separate things: having useful information does not mean every character will cooperate with it.

Why it matters

VICE also reports phone calls and texts between the protagonists when they are apart, shared outings and a button for holding hands. The precise consequences of neglecting their relationship remain unclear. There is no basis here for inventing relationship statistics, specific penalties or an ending requirement.

Together, the details add activities and decisions around the main criminal progression: observing wildlife, inspecting a target and spending time as a couple.
