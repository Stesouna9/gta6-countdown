GTA 6 Xbox download: 225 GB global package, smaller Japanese build

Xboxygen reports regional Xbox packages of around 225.34 GB globally and 224.14 GB in Japan. The size difference does not establish censorship.

GTA 6 appears to have slightly different Xbox download packages depending on the region. Xboxygen reports a global package of roughly 225.34 GB and a Japanese package of about 224.14 GB, based on Microsoft's distribution catalogue. Those figures describe reported package data; they are not a Rockstar announcement explaining regional content changes.

Is 209.86 GiB smaller than 225.34 GB?

Not in the way those numbers might suggest. Xboxygen cites a discovery by Cokymoky in Microsoft's DisplayCatalog API and pairs the global package's 209.86 GiB figure with approximately 225.34 GB. Binary gibibytes and decimal gigabytes express essentially the same amount of data using different scales. The lower number does not, on its own, identify a compressed download.

We covered that distinction in our earlier report on the Xbox file size. Today's additional detail is the regional comparison. For anyone clearing storage, the useful reference remains the free space shown by the console and the installation requirement displayed when the download becomes available.

What is different about the Japanese package?

Xboxygen lists approximately 208.74 GiB, or 224.14 GB, for Japan. That puts the reported difference at around 1.2 GB compared with the global package. The outlet also describes Australian and New Zealand packages as almost identical to the one distributed elsewhere. Separate package sizes establish a distribution difference, rather than explaining precisely what is inside each version.

Regional language files or audio are possible explanations mentioned by Xboxygen. None has been established as the cause. Nor does the previously reported Japanese CERO Z rating prove that material has been removed. A documented comparison or an official explanation would be needed before describing this as censorship.

Why it matters

For European Xbox Series X|S owners, roughly 225 GB is the practical scale suggested by the catalogue figures reported by Xboxygen. It is not a promise that the same amount of free space will cover every later update. There is also no reason to add the regional package sizes together: installing one version does not mean downloading all the regional variants discussed in the report.

GTA 6 is scheduled for November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Our release guide collects the broader launch information. This update concerns the Xbox distribution packages and their regional sizes; it does not establish different missions, scenes or gameplay features between those versions.
