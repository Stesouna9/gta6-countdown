GTA 6 Mount Chiliad-style mystery: a bloody cave near Leonida's gold mines

A cave with a blood trail leading to an unreachable platform near Leonida's gold mines puzzles players, says GAMINGbible. Rockstar has confirmed nothing.

출처 gamingbible.com

GTA 6 players may have their first Mount Chiliad-style mystery. GAMINGbible says it found, near Leonida's gold mines, a cave with a blood trail climbing toward an unreachable platform. There is no explanation yet.

What is in the cave?

According to the GAMINGbible writer, who claims more than 70 hours of play, the gold mine area hides man-made tunnels and caves that are mostly unremarkable. One stands out: walk to the end of the tunnel, to the bend, flashlight on because it gets very dark.

On the wall, a blood trail leads up to a platform out of reach. Above it, an opening leads to the surface, but dropping through from the top is fatal: the character does not survive the fall.

What theories are being floated?

The outlet first thinks of a wild animal such as a bear, then jokes about legends like skinwalkers, noting Rockstar's taste for the occult. It even tried driving a pick-up down the tunnel to reach the platform. These are the writer's own guesses, not Rockstar information.

Why the Mount Chiliad comparison?

In GTA 5, Mount Chiliad fed years of theories about its secrets. GAMINGbible says it knows of no mystery in GTA 6's mountains themselves, but the surrounding area is full of them. Rockstar has confirmed nothing: it is unknown whether this spot is ambient detail or a real secret to solve.

Finds like this add to others we relayed, such as the secret radios unlocked by scanning posters, or the state's wildlife in our 170 species piece.

Why it matters

Nothing is verified yet, but the community has a new hunting ground. If you find the cave, share what you see on our community page.
