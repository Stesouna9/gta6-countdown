GTA 6: alleged fired engineer accuses Rockstar of profiling players, no proof

An anonymous X account claims GTA 6 profiles players through a system called Lens; Clubic notes no verifiable document exists and Rockstar has not responded.

출처 clubic.com

An accusation is circulating on X, and it is unproven. According to Clubic, an anonymous account presenting itself as a fired Rockstar North engineer claims GTA 6 profiles its players. Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has responded, and Clubic stresses no verifiable document has been published. It should be read as a rumor.

What does the post claim?

Per Clubic, a long message in Chinese, posted November 20 by the account @make_claude, was relayed in English and seen over 10 million times. The author says he is a senior engine engineer working on RAGE optimization, fired on November 18, the day before launch. He describes a Take-Two telemetry system called Lens that would record every action to the millisecond, from buttons to the PS5's adaptive triggers, to build a "behavioral signature" per player. Profiles would then go to a subsidiary named Vantage Analytics, then to insurers and other firms, according to him.

What doesn't add up, according to Clubic?

Clubic notes Vantage Analytics appears in no Take-Two SEC filing, which list about 145 subsidiaries. No file, server address or network capture has been published. The account is anonymous, created in August 2026. The dismissal email shown in a photo, taken at an angle off a screen, allows no serious analysis. The author promises to release documents later.

It is not the first time the launch attracts dubious security stories: see the fake PDF guide hiding spyware, a documented danger this time. For player privacy, the safest course is to rely on what the publisher itself publishes.
