GTA 6: New Zealand's tourism board welcomes the Xbox region trick

New Zealand's tourist board is greeting players who switch their Xbox region to launch GTA 6 a few hours early, according to IGN.

The country where the sun rises first decided to laugh about it. According to IGN, New Zealand's tourist board posted on Instagram: "One perk of seeing the sun first? Getting GTA 6 first too. We heard a few of you are trying the New Zealand trick to join us. We get it, and we'd love to have you."

How does the New Zealand trick work?

GTA 6 unlocks at local midnight in each region. Midnight in New Zealand is 11am GMT this Wednesday, November 18, Pure Xbox explains. On Xbox Series X|S, players open Settings, System, Language and location, pick New Zealand and restart, per PC Guide, which adds there is no guarantee it works for GTA 6.

On PS5 it is harder: accounts are tied to the region where they were created, so you would need a New Zealand account and a purchase on the local PlayStation Store. Our November 16 piece covered the basics.

Is there a risk of a ban?

Xbox's terms say not to change region unless you actually moved, Pure Xbox reminds readers. Windows Central's Jez Corden and YouTuber Colt Eastwood discussed it during a visit to Xbox's Seattle headquarters. Others, such as The Verge's Tom Warren, call it a non-issue, and the site says past releases saw no bans. Nobody knows what Microsoft will do, and Pure Xbox would not recommend it. IGN says Microsoft has always stayed quiet about it.

Why it matters

Nothing official changes: release stays at midnight where you live. But when a country turns the trick into a tourism pitch, it has become a ritual. Regional times are on our release page. The risk stays with each player.
