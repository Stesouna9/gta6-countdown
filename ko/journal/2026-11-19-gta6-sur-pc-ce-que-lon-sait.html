GTA 6 on PC: no launch version, a Q1 2027 release reported but unconfirmed

Rockstar has announced no PC version of GTA 6, out on PS5 and Xbox Series; GamesBeat points to Q1 2027, unconfirmed.

사진: Rockstar Games

출처 mashable.com

No, GTA 6 is not on PC this November 19. Rockstar has confirmed the game launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S with no PC version announced, Mashable reminds readers in its launch-day roundup. Here is what is certain, what is reported and what remains unknown.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not yet. Rockstar and Take-Two have given no date, price or system requirements, and have not explained the choice. The game can be played through Xbox Cloud Gaming, but with strict monthly limits according to Mashable, which makes a full playthrough difficult.

A PC release in Q1 2027?

GamesBeat reported in October, citing anonymous sources close to Rockstar, a PC launch in the first quarter of 2027. Mashable credits the outlet with a good track record, but it is unconfirmed: treat it as a lead, not a date.

What does Rockstar's history say?

Past releases call for caution. GTA IV hit consoles in April 2008 and PC in December. GTA V arrived on PC 19 months after its September 2013 launch, in April 2015. Red Dead Redemption 2 took about a year. The one exception was the 2021 Definitive Edition trilogy, released everywhere at once.
