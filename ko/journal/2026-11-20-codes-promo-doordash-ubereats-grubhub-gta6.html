GTA 6: DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub launch discount codes in the US

Per Mashable, three US delivery apps offered discount codes on launch day, and overnight orders rose.

출처 mashable.com

Food delivery joined the GTA 6 launch. Mashable lists discount codes offered on 19 November by DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub. The offers are for the United States.

Which codes circulated?

According to Mashable, DoorDash gave $6 off Cheesecake Factory orders of $20 or more with CAKEGTA6, valid on 19 November only, and $15 off at Wendy's for the first 100 customers via codes on the chain's Twitch stream. Uber Eats offered ONAMISSION: $10 off $20, for launch day. Grubhub had OUTOFOFFICE: $10 off orders over $10, limited to the first 1,000 customers and exhausted in under an hour and a half.

What effect on sales?

Mashable cites the New York Times: DoorDash energy drink sales rose over 20 percent on Wednesday night during the midnight launch. DoorDash's overnight sales rose by double digits in several states, and Uber Eats' by 30 percent in Miami, the city behind Vice City. These figures are reported by media, not published by Rockstar.

Why it matters

Nothing for French players, as the codes are American and expired. It shows how far the launch spread beyond the game, after traffic spikes at ISPs. More in the journal.
