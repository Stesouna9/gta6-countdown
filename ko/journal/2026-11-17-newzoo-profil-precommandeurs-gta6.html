GTA 6 pre-orders: mostly high-spending male console players, says Newzoo

Newzoo says 84% of GTA 6 pre-orderers bought another premium game in the past year, and only 15% are women.

Who pre-ordered GTA 6? According to Newzoo data shared exclusively with GamesIndustry.biz, mostly high-spending console players, overwhelmingly men, nearly all of whom bought a big game last year.

During the pre-order period, which opened on June 24, 84% of buyers had purchased at least one other premium game in the previous 12 months. The typical pre-orderer spent $129 across three other titles, and a quarter spent over $290, excluding in-game spending, Newzoo says.

Is the audience more male than GTA 5's?

Yes, per Newzoo: women make up 15% of GTA 6 pre-orders, against 27% of all premium game buyers in the period. For GTA 5, the firm counted 22% women among buyers since 2018. Age is conventional: 56% of pre-orderers are under 35 (55% for premium games overall) and 22% are 45 or older.

Franchise veterans, but not only

52% of pre-orderers have spent on GTA 5 or GTA Online since 2018, and at least 37% on GTA Online. Newzoo calls these minimums, as it lacks earlier data. Conversely, 16% had not bought a full-price game in a year, 3% with no purchase found since 2018. For market intelligence director Emmanuel Rosier, "GTA 6 looks like genuinely new spending": it brings lapsed players back.

Newzoo expects a short-term hit to other releases, then extra demand, as some players "stretch their gaming budgets". It projects a 17.5% rise in console full-game spending. We covered its forecast in our piece on the $5 billion first week, and Sensor Tower's estimates in the one on $1.4 billion in pre-orders.

Why it matters

These figures describe pre-orderers, not all buyers: Newzoo expects a broader launch audience. They are a research firm's estimates, not Rockstar data. Prices and editions are on the price page.
