GTA 6 music: Justice's Flower In A Hurricane jumps to No. 7 in Germany

Zickma reports that Flower In A Hurricane by Justice, Tame Impala and SZA climbed from 70th to 7th in Germany and gives Justice its first UK Top 10.

출처 zickma.fr

GTA 6 is helping a Justice, Tame Impala and SZA track climb the charts. According to Zickma, Flower In A Hurricane rose from 70th to 7th place in Germany and from 32nd to 9th in the UK, giving Justice its first British Top 10.

What does Zickma say about the climb?

The song came out a month ago, meant to keep fans busy before launch, Zickma writes. It has been rising for several days across European charts. The outlet describes a familiar pattern for the series: each release lifts certain songs, because players drive around the game with the stereo loud. Since GTA 5, it adds, streaming has amplified the effect, to the point that tracks enter charts worldwide.

How is it doing in France?

Results are more modest there. Zickma says the best French position dates from early October, at 50th, followed by a fall of nearly 50 places and a rebound a few days ago. The outlet thinks the game's heavy presence could give the song a second wind. Nothing guarantees it; the next chart updates will tell.

For context, the game launched on November 19, and its soundtrack has its own story: GTA VI: The Album has been available since November 19, with 34 tracks. Flower In A Hurricane is a song by Justice, Tame Impala and SZA, and Zickma does not say how far it features in the game, so we do not either.
