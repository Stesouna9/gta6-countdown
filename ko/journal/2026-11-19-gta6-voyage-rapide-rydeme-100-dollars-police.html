GTA 6 fast travel: ride a RydeMe cab for $100, unless police are chasing you

GTA 6 has no map teleport menu: you call a RydeMe driver, and skipping the ride costs $100, unless the police are after you.

출처 journaldugeek.com

Leonida is huge, and some nights you just want to arrive. Good news, a shortcut exists, just not where you would expect: according to Journal du Geek, GTA 6 has no classic fast travel menu to teleport from safehouse to safehouse from the map.

How does fast travel work in GTA 6?

You go through RydeMe, the game's ride-hailing app, which works as a taxi. Per the outlet, you pull out the character's phone, open the app, pick a destination and call the driver. Once aboard you can ride normally, or hold L2 and Triangle to skip the trip and arrive directly. The skip costs $100, a trivial sum once you have emptied a few gang stashes, Journal du Geek notes.

The idea echoes GTA 5's taxi, which series veterans look for by reflex. It also fits the studio's taste for phones and apps, already visible in the Leonida map that only fully opens after 30% of the story.

When does RydeMe refuse a ride?

Two limits, Journal du Geek reports. First, you cannot order a driver while police are after you: lose the cops before heading home. Second, drivers dislike trouble: pulling a weapon during the ride is enough for them to cancel and drop you off on the spot.

It fits what Rockstar seems to want from this Vice City: a world where every shortcut has a social rule. The outlet also advises against overusing it early on, since the map's secrets are often found on the road. See also the explorable buildings of Vice City.

Why it matters

No free teleport and no menu: fast travel has a price and conditions, which keeps the map alive. One point still needs confirming, as the $100 figure comes from a single source; Rockstar published nothing on it in the material we reviewed. More launch info on our release page.
