GTA 6 PC release: ex-Rockstar tech director expects it sooner than thought

Rock Paper Shotgun reports ex-Rockstar North technical director Obbe Vermeij thinks a GTA 6 PC version could come sooner over fears of AI-made ports. Nothing is announced.

No PC version of GTA 6 is announced: the game launches November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. But according to Rock Paper Shotgun, former Rockstar North technical director Obbe Vermeij thinks it could arrive sooner than expected. He says himself he does not know what Rockstar is planning: this is a guess, not a leak.

Why would a PC version come earlier?

Vermeij's argument is financial. He worries someone could take the PS5 version and have AI convert it to PC. The result would be worse than Rockstar's own work, he says, "but it could still also hurt their sales". He points to AI-assisted recompilation tools that allow fast unofficial ports.

He thinks such tools are "probably" capable of it: it would take hours and cost "tens of thousands of dollars" in tokens, but "if it's GTA, I think people are gonna try it". Hence his view that he would not be surprised if releases were brought closer together.

What do we actually know about GTA 6 on PC?

Very little. No date, no announcement, no system requirements. Vermeij recalls the history: GTA 1 started on PC, then Rockstar flipped the order from GTA 3 onward, consoles first. He adds many publishers prefer launching every version together to pay for one marketing campaign, but GTA has always made its own rules.

Why it matters

Nothing concrete yet. The opinion comes from a former employee who left Rockstar, and he says so plainly. PC players still have only silence from Rockstar and Take-Two, so this scenario remains an opinion.
