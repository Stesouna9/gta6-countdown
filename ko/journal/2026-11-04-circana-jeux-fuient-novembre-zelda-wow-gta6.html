GTA 6 release: games that left November may not have needed to, says Circana

Circana analyst Mat Piscatella says many games moved out of November over GTA 6 fear, while Zelda and WoW benefit, GamesRadar+ reports.

Games that fled November because of GTA VI may have moved for nothing, says Mat Piscatella, games lead at Circana, in an interview published by GamesRadar+. He was talking about The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (remake) and World of Warcraft: Forever, both out this month.

What does the Circana analyst say?

Piscatella expects GTA VI to lead the holiday rush and lift spending across the industry. He thinks many publishers moved out of fear: "people are probably just scared of potentially running into this game". A cozy sim or a shooter, he argues, had no obvious reason to shift. "Everyone just kind of moved and perhaps they didn't need to," he sums up.

Are Zelda and WoW safe?

GamesRadar+ notes that the Ocarina of Time remake, due November 8, and World of Warcraft: Forever, November 4, enjoy a clear month. Piscatella points out that WoW is on PC, where GTA VI is not announced, and that the Switch 2 audience is not the PlayStation or Xbox crowd. The overlap is still notable: per Circana's tracking, about 15% of people planning to buy GTA VI also plan to buy the Zelda remake.

It adds to the record purchase intent Circana already measured. Piscatella also describes two games aimed at mature, nostalgic audiences, which explains the overlap.
