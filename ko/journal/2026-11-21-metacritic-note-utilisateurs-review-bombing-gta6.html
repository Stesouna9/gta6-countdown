GTA 6 user score on Metacritic falls to 5.1 while critics give it 99

Insider Gaming reports a 5.1 out of 10 Metacritic user score for GTA 6 from over 10,000 ratings, against a 99 Metascore, and raises possible review bombing.

사진: Rockstar Games

출처 insider-gaming.com

Three days after launch, players and critics are far apart on Metacritic. Insider Gaming reports a user score of 5.1 out of 10, labelled "Mixed", from more than 10,000 ratings since November 19. The Metascore holds at 99 out of 100 across 152 critic reviews. Other outlets, Notebookcheck and Mezha among them, headline a 5.2 opening score and talk of review bombing claims.

The two figures probably reflect different moments, since the score moves with every vote. We could not read the Notebookcheck piece, so the details below come from Insider Gaming.

What do the numbers show?

According to Insider Gaming, nearly half of user ratings are "Negative", meaning below 50 out of 100. It points to Backloggd, where the game sits at 3.9 out of 5 over 492 ratings, 15% of them at 0.5 out of 5. The gap with the critics' 99 is stark, but a user score is not a professional review: anyone can vote without proving they played.

Why such a score?

Insider Gaming lists several theories without quoting a named person. First, performance problems and lost progress, which it ties to a piece on autosave. Then complaints of substance, from "too much Red Dead Redemption influence" to "differing political views". It adds that in "some very unfortunate cases", some comments amount to "actual racism and sexism".

It does not settle the question, writing of a "possible reality that GTA 6 is currently being review bombed". Some negative reviews may be sincere (crashes, saves), others part of a campaign, and public data cannot separate them. Neither Metacritic nor Rockstar is quoted in the article.

What we already knew

Before launch, Metacritic had already removed fake user reviews from people who did not own the game, then blocked new ratings until release. On the technical side, players also report PS5 crashes (code CE-108255-1). See our price page and the journal for context.

Why it matters

For a hesitant buyer, nothing decisive: critics' 99 and players' 5.1 tell different stories, and user scores usually settle after the first days. For Rockstar, it is a signal: fixes for crashes and saves will be watched closely. We will follow the score and any official response.
