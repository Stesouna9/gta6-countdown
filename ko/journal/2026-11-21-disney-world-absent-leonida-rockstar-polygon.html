GTA 6: Rockstar explains why Leonida has no Disney World equivalent

Polygon reports that Rockstar saw no strong story reason to build a working theme park and lacked the production time for one.

사진: Rockstar Games

출처 polygon.com

One conspicuous part of real Florida did not make the trip to Leonida. According to Polygon, Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson addressed the lack of a Disney World equivalent by pointing to narrative priorities and the effort required to build a functioning theme park. Polygon considers the omission a missed opportunity, but its criticism and Rockstar’s explanation are different parts of the story.

Why did Rockstar leave a theme park out?

Polygon relays comments Nelson made to VGC. His explanation centres on whether the story supplied a good enough reason for the protagonists to spend time in such a location. A working park would also have demanded production effort the team did not have available. Rockstar focused on what supported the narrative and the feeling of being in Leonida.

That is a description of a development decision, rather than a claim that theme parks cannot work in an open world. It is also no commitment to add one later. The comments reproduced in the collected article contain no announcement of an expansion, update or future destination built around this idea.

Where the reporting ends and the argument begins

Polygon’s objection is that Disney is a major part of Florida’s economy, tourism and politics. A game satirising the state, it argues, leaves a substantial subject untouched by omitting an equivalent. Readers can assess that criticism without treating it as a factual account of what happened inside Rockstar during development.

The piece also suggests that avoiding a powerful company could have commercial advantages. That remains the publication’s interpretation. The collected text does not establish that Disney intervened, threatened Rockstar or imposed a legal restriction. Nor does it show Rockstar confirming that fear of a dispute drove the decision. Those claims would require evidence beyond the explanation attributed to Nelson.

Why it matters

For players comparing Leonida with Florida, the answer sets a useful boundary. This is a setting assembled around a story, rather than a promise to reproduce every landmark or industry. Whether that makes the satire less convincing is a fair discussion for our community page, without revealing mission events or guessing at unannounced content.

Our earlier coverage of explorable interiors and space retained for future content provides more context on the boundaries of the world. Neither report should be read as evidence that a Disney-style park is on the way. For now, the specific explanation reported here is about story needs and the work required to meet them.
