GTA 6: Travis Scott, Nike and the Miami Heat launch an apparel collection on November 18

The Cactus Jack x Miami Heat x GTA 6 x Nike collection (hats, T-shirts, hoodies) goes on sale November 18 at the Heat's arena, then online November 20.

After the Vice City uniforms and three Travis Scott Air Force 1s, the rapper's label is unveiling an everyday apparel line, reports Sole Retriever. The Cactus Jack x Miami Heat x GTA 6 x Nike collection includes hats, T-shirts, hoodies and long-sleeve tees.

When and where can you buy the Heat's GTA 6 collection?

According to Sole Retriever, the full collection is available on November 18 at the Miami Heat's arena during "A Night in Vice City", with doors opening at 5:00 PM ET. Two of the previewed items are said to be arena exclusives. The rest reaches Nike SNKRS, Fanatics and NBA Store on November 20, with Travis Scott's Cactus Jack site following shortly after. The article gives no prices.

What is in the collection?

The outlet describes a camo shirt with 3M reflective Vice City branding on the chest and a black hat with the Heat logo on the front, a Nike Air logo on the right and Cactus Jack and Rockstar logos on the left. Several pieces carry Vice City, Nike and Cactus Jack references on the back.

The Heat are a central launch-week partner: the Miami franchise also fronts NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition, and the city is already wearing the game's brands, as in our Wynwood festival piece.

Why it matters

For collectors the window is short: in-person access in Miami on the 18th, a wider sale on the 20th. Our goodies page tracks official products. Whether every item ships internationally, and at what price, is not stated by Sole Retriever.
